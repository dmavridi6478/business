# Tallyleaf (fictional vendor)

Tallyleaf sells invoice-matching and freight-audit software to mid-size logistics companies (200 to 2,000 employees) in Greece and the wider EU. Sender for outreach: Eleni Markou, Account Executive, Tallyleaf Ltd, 12 Example Street, Athens, Greece.

## Capabilities confirmed as real and demoable (the only ones a demo may show)
- Automatic matching of carrier invoices to shipment records.
- Exception queue for mismatches with reason codes.
- Carrier rate-card import (CSV) and rate checks.
- Monthly accrual report export (CSV, XLSX).
- Role-based access with SAML single sign-on.
