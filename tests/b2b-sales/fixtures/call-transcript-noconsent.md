# Discovery call, Harbourline Freight (fictional). Date: 2026-10-09

Participants: Eleni Markou (Tallyleaf, seller), Nikos Vamvas (Finance Director, Harbourline Freight), Sofia Rallis (Accounts Payable Lead, Harbourline Freight).

Eleni: Nikos, tell me what prompted you to take this call.
Nikos: Our month-end close takes eleven days. The board wants it under six. And our external auditors come in November.
Eleni: What is slowing the close down?
Sofia: Mostly carrier invoices. We get about 3,200 a month and we match them by hand against shipment records in spreadsheets.
Eleni: And when they do not match?
Sofia: About fifteen percent do not match. Each one takes roughly 25 minutes to chase. Our previous AP clerk left in August and nobody wrote down how she did the rate checks.
Eleni: How many people work on this today?
Sofia: Two clerks and me. I would say the two clerks spend most of their month on it.
Nikos: I do not want to add headcount. That is not going to be approved.
Eleni: Understood. What have you looked at so far?
Nikos: We had a call with Ledgerwise last week. They quoted lower than I expected. I have not seen a demo.
Eleni: What would a good result look like for you?
Nikos: Close in six days or fewer, and an audit trail I can show the auditors.
Eleni: Who else will be involved in the decision?
Nikos: Me, and our CEO signs anything above a certain amount. I have not asked IT yet. Sofia would use it every day.
Eleni: What is your timeline?
Nikos: Ideally live before the year-end close. I cannot promise that. Let us see how the audit goes first.
Eleni: I can send you a sample accrual report by Friday so you can see what the output looks like.
Nikos: Please do. And Sofia can send you a sample of carrier invoices, anonymised.
Sofia: I will send ten of them by Monday.
Eleni: Great. We have not set a next meeting. Shall I propose one after the audit?
Nikos: Yes, after the audit.
