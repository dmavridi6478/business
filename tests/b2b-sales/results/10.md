## Compliance checklist

Rules for business email differ by country and may treat business contacts differently from consumers. Have counsel review this checklist for Greece and, separately, Cyprus. Nothing below is settled law.

- Lawful basis for each recipient, recorded before sending.
- Source of each address recorded. No purchased personal data without a lawful basis.
- Sender identity and postal address in every email (included).
- A working opt-out in every email, honoured promptly, with a suppression list checked before every send. An opt-out overrides all other rules.
- Stop on any reply, bounce or opt-out.
- Check whether open tracking needs notice or consent. Open data is also unreliable as proof of reading.
- Cyprus contacts need their own check; the sending region given is Greece.
- You send these yourself; nothing here is sent for you.

## Test plan

Change one variable at a time. Suggested first test: the subject line of email 1, with the same body, send day and list.

The sample is small. If your Tier 2 list is about 40 accounts (`persona.md`), splitting it in two gives groups too small to show a real difference. Treat the result as anecdotal and repeat it before drawing conclusions. Do not change the ask or the offer during the same test.
