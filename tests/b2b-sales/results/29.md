- The buyer said the next meeting is "after the audit", with no date, so the calendar gives no reason to advance now.

## Risks if advancing

If you demo anyway, expect these risks:
- The demo is built on unwritten success criteria, so the buyer can judge it against a target you have not agreed.
- The CEO, IT and procurement are absent, so any positive reaction does not reach the people who approve.
- The competitor's quote ("lower than I expected") will be the only price reference the buyer has seen.
- The timeline is soft: the close date has moved twice and the buyer said "I cannot promise that."
- No added headcount is a hard constraint; a demo that implies new process work may meet resistance.

What to ask in the demo if it goes ahead:
- "What would you need to see here to call this a success?" (write the answer down, ask for agreement in writing)
- "Who else should see this, and what will they ask?"
- "What happens next in your decision, and by when?"
