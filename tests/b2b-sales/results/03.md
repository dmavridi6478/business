## Scoring rules

Points = 0 if lift is below 1.25; otherwise round((lift - 1) x 5), halves up [calc: points formula]. No negative points.

Fit points:
- Source referral: 3 [calc: (1.59-1) x 5 = 2.95]. Source inbound: 1 [calc: (1.27-1) x 5 = 1.35].
- Employees 500-1000: 5 [calc: (1.96-1) x 5 = 4.8].
- Industry 3PL: 2 [calc: (1.39-1) x 5 = 1.95].

Behaviour points (see data check: undated, possibly after the fact):
- Demo requested: 6 [calc: (2.26-1) x 5 = 6.3].
- Pricing page visits 3 or more: 3 [calc: (1.50-1) x 5 = 2.5, rounded up].

Decay: behaviour points halve every 30 days after the behaviour [assumption: N = 30, no behaviour dates in the data]. Decay is not applied in the test below for the same reason.

Total score = fit points + behaviour points. Maximum 19 [calc: 3+5+2+6+3].

## Monitoring plan

- Re-run quarterly. Compare conversion of the hot bucket with the rest, as counts. Alert if hot converts at less than twice the rest on at least 20 hot leads [assumption: 20 and 2x are my starting values].
- Re-fit once behaviour dates and lead creation dates exist, and once leads have aged past 120 days so non-conversion is final.
- Track the fit rules separately from behaviour rules, since the fit rules failed in the test.
