## Scoring rule

Stated before the scores. Score = fit points + relevance points + recency points.
- Fit gate: employees 250 to 1,000 and a location in Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania or Italy are both required. Accounts that fail the gate are listed as outside the ICP, not ranked.
- Fit points (max 6): employees in band 2; location in the five countries 2; industry 2 if the industry label contains "freight" (road, sea, air), 1 for 3PL, courier or cold chain, because "freight companies" in your ICP is not defined further [assumption].
- Relevance points: 2 for a clear match to your signal map, 1 for a partial match, 0 for no mapping.
- Recency points (only where relevance is above 0): 2 if the signal is 30 days old or less, 1 if 31 to 90 days.
- Ties: higher relevance first, then more recent.
