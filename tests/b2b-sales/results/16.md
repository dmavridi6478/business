## Element list used

The common seven-element MEDDICC:

1. Metrics
2. Economic buyer
3. Decision criteria
4. Decision process
5. Identify pain
6. Champion
7. Competition

Definitions of MEDDICC vary between teams (some use a compelling event, paper process or "implicate the pain" instead of, or on top of, these). Please correct this list if your team's differs.

## Gaps

- **Economic buyer:** not confirmed. The CEO signs above an unknown amount, the deal is 30,000 dollars, and the CEO has not been met (record: "Stakeholders not met: CEO, IT, procurement.").
- **Champion:** rests on the seller's label. It is not shown that Sofia can influence Nikos or the CEO.
- **Decision process:** no dated steps, and the audit outcome ("Let us see how the audit goes first") may decide timing.
- **Metrics:** no money value, no agreed written success criteria.
- **Competition:** Ledgerwise quote amount and scope unknown.
- **Stage evidence:** none of the four Evaluation exit criteria is evidenced as met: (1) no written success criteria agreed; (2) economic buyer "per the seller", not verified; (3) no technical validation, IT unasked; (4) no dated next step agreed by the buyer.
- **Close date:** the record shows 2026-12-15, moved from 2026-11-15 and 2026-10-31 (two moves); the notes contain no buyer-stated close date, only "I cannot promise that".
- **CRM next step:** "Send sample accrual report" has no date, and the log shows the report was sent on 2026-10-10, so the field is out of date.
- **Plan fit:** the record lists the Growth plan; the transcript contains no price or plan discussion.

Methodology note: MEDDICC fits this deal well overall. Its seven elements do not capture the audit in November as a compelling event, which in this deal drives both the pain and the timing; it is recorded under Decision process and Identify pain rather than forced into an element.

## Priorities

Three elements to advance, in order:

1. **Economic buyer.** Everything about the forecast depends on who signs and above what amount, and the CEO has not been met.
2. **Decision process.** The close date has moved twice and no buyer-stated dates exist; the audit, IT and procurement steps are undefined.
3. **Champion.** Sofia is the only buyer-side person who has acted for us, and she is the likeliest route to IT and to the CEO, but her influence is untested.

Metrics, Competition and Decision criteria come after these; Identify pain is already the best evidenced.
