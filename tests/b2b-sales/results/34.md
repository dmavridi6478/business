## Discovery questions

These surface the buyer's needs without naming or attacking the competitor.
1. When an invoice does not match, how do you want to categorise the reason, and do you need your own categories?
2. How much of the work of handling the mismatches would you want to see in one queue?
3. How do you compare cost across options: licence, number of users, implementation, and volume of invoices?
4. How quickly do you need to be live, and what has to be true for that to be realistic?
5. What do you expect from support, and what response time matters to you?
6. Which parts of the current process must not change?
