## Risks and exit criteria

Risks:
- Data access: real invoices need a data agreement first. Do not load real customer data without one.
- Data quality: the shipment records may not be in a format that can be loaded, or the rate cards may not be available as CSV.
- Availability of people: Sofia's team is already fully loaded, per her own words above.
- Disputed counts: if "mismatch" is not defined up front, flagged invoices can be argued about. Define it in C1.
- Scope creep: any added item needs a written change.
- Comparison: Nikos has spoken to Ledgerwise ("They quoted lower than I expected. I have not seen a demo."). The pilot may be a free comparison. Ask whether Harbourline is running a similar test elsewhere.

Exit criteria, stop early if:
- Criteria, decision commitment or data agreement are not signed before the start.
- The data has not arrived by the end of week 1, day 2 `[assumption]`.
- Harbourline cannot attend a checkpoint.
- A scope change is requested and not agreed in writing.
