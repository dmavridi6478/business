## Moves to create overlap

- Term: offer a 24-month term in return for a lower annual price. A 12-month term is already your minimum, so it is not a concession.
- Timing: annual prepayment in return for price. Billing or start timing aligned to the buyer's budget year may help if his "this year" budget is the constraint, but it is not in your supplied authority and needs approval.
- Scope and risk: the five capabilities discussed plus the 5-user Starter cap. Case-study rights and reference calls have value to you and can be traded for price; they also let the buyer see peers using it. Accepting written success criteria first reduces the buyer's risk.

Price moves, within limits:
