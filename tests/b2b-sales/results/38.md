## Moves to create overlap

Non-price as well as price. Each move is paired with something from the buyer, and none breaches the floor.

**Price (within authority):**

- Reduce the one-time implementation fee instead of the annual price (protects recurring revenue and the floor).
- A small Starter reduction, to a maximum of $2,000, only against a return.

**Terms:**

- 12-month term, as asked: it equals the minimum, so it costs nothing to accept, but trade it for annual prepayment.
- 24-month term in exchange for any further movement.
- Annual prepayment.

**Scope and timing:**

- Confirm that the Starter plan scope (up to 5 users) matches what they need; the call transcript names three people involved in the work (two clerks and the AP lead), which is within 5 [calc: 2 + 1 = 3, count of people named]. Do not shrink scope without checking with the user, since no scope-reduced price exists in the inputs.
- Start timing: the buyer wants to be live around the year-end close but "cannot promise that"; a start date linked to the audit outcome is a timing move. Do not create urgency from it.

**Risk sharing and non-price value:**

- Reference calls and case-study rights are tradeable value for the seller; they are asked of the buyer, not offered to them.
- Show the confirmed capabilities against the buyer's stated needs: automatic matching of carrier invoices, an exception queue with reason codes, rate-card import and rate checks, and a monthly accrual report export. Do not claim an "audit trail" or a guaranteed close time: neither appears in the confirmed list, so check with the product team before saying either. Do not claim that headcount is avoided.
- Security answers must come from the documents on file only (for example EU hosting in Frankfurt; ISO 27001 "in progress"; no SOC 2 report mentioned).
