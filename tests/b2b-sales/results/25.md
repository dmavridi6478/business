## Three improvements

1. **Follow the number or deadline the buyer drops.** After "The board wants it under six. And our external auditors come in November." try "Tell me more about the board's target." and then "What will the auditors be looking at?" Behaviour, not person: the seller moved straight to the next topic question.
2. **Stay with a stated constraint before acknowledging it.** After "I do not want to add headcount. That is not going to be approved." replace "Understood." with "What is behind that?" or repeat the key words: "...not going to be approved?" This keeps the constraint open for later.
