## Recommendation

Continue, with two gaps to close before putting in heavy effort.

- Need is confirmed in the buyer's own words with numbers, and there is an event behind the timeline (board target, November audit).
- Budget is unknown and authority is only implied. Each is a gap a single question can settle, so this is not a reason to pause or exit.
- The timeline is event-driven but not committed ("I cannot promise that"), and the only agreed next meeting is "after the audit" with no date.

If both answers come back unfavourable (no budget owner, or approval needed at a level the seller cannot reach), reassess as pause.
