## Questions to fill gaps

Ask the Finance Manager first:
1. How many carrier invoices do you handle in a month?
2. How long does one take to match, on average, and how long when it does not match?
3. How many people spend time on it, and about how much of their month?
4. What does the closing take in days now, and what do you need it to be?
5. What does a day of delay in the close cost you, or what does it hold up?
6. What have the auditors' complaints been about, how often, and what extra work followed?
7. What does an hour of that team's time cost the company?
