## Scenario plan

Three scenarios, each tied to a stated problem. Capabilities are only from the confirmed list in `company.md`.

**Scenario 1: Carrier invoices matched by hand, and the ones that do not match**
- Problem (Sofia): "we match them by hand against shipment records in spreadsheets"; "About fifteen percent do not match. Each one takes roughly 25 minutes to chase."
- What to show: a batch of carrier invoices matched to shipment records without anyone matching line by line; then the invoices that did not match arriving in a queue with a reason attached, ready for a clerk to work.
- Capability used: Automatic matching of carrier invoices to shipment records; Exception queue for mismatches with reason codes.
- Outcome the buyer should recognise: the matching they do in spreadsheets happens without a person, and the unmatched ones are in one place with a reason. Do not state a time saving or a match rate: none is on file.

**Scenario 2: The rate checks nobody wrote down**
- Problem (Sofia): "nobody wrote down how she did the rate checks."
- What to show: a carrier rate card imported from CSV, then an invoice checked against it, with a rate difference shown.
- Capability used: Carrier rate-card import (CSV) and rate checks.
- Outcome the buyer should recognise: the rate check is a stated rule in the system, not knowledge held by one person.

**Scenario 3: The month-end output**
- Problem (Nikos): "Our month-end close takes eleven days. The board wants it under six."
- What to show: the monthly accrual report exported as CSV and XLSX. Eleni sent a sample accrual report on 2026-10-10 (deal record), so show the live version of what they have already seen.
- Capability used: Monthly accrual report export (CSV, XLSX).
- Outcome the buyer should recognise: the figures they need for the close come out of the same matched data. Do not claim the close will fall to six days: no evidence supports that number.

Demo data: use data you have approved for demos. If Sofia's ten anonymised invoices (sent 2026-10-12) are to be used, ask her first. See "What not to show".
