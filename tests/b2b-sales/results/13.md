# Cadence: Tier 2 accounts

## Objective

Book a first conversation with the Finance Director at each of the 40 Tier 2 accounts (Finance Directors at road freight companies, 300 to 600 employees, Greece and Cyprus). Length per account: 21 days. Region: Greece (EU).

Two cohorts of 20 accounts, the second starting one week after the first. This keeps the weekly load under capacity (see the capacity check). The whole programme therefore takes 4 weeks `[calc: 3 weeks + 1 week offset]`.

Intensity for Tier 2: personalise touches 1 and 6 with one cited public fact where there is one; the rest are templated. All message content is draft, needs approval.

## Cadence table

A touch is one email sent, one call attempt or one LinkedIn message or request, by hand.

| Step | Day | Channel | Purpose | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Relevance: why this persona, one ask | Email 1 |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn connection request | Introduce yourself with one real reason to connect | Connection note |
| 3 | 6 | Phone | Reach the person; ask permission and one open question | Call opener and voicemail |
| 4 | 9 | Email | Show how the product works, since no authorised customer proof exists | Email 2 |
| 5 | 13 | LinkedIn message, only if the request was accepted | Offer help or an open question, no pitch | First follow-up |
| 6 | 16 | Email | A different angle (month-end and audit) | Email 3 |
| 7 | 21 | Email | Close the loop politely | Email 4 |

The channels used are the ones you allow: email, phone, LinkedIn by hand. `persona.md` says no customer has authorised being named, so no touch quotes a customer.

## Stop rules

Stop the whole cadence for an account on any of these:

- A reply. Hand it to a person to read and classify.
- A bounce.
- An opt-out or any request to stop.
- A "not now" with a date. Pause and restart from step 1 on that date.
- A do-not-contact entry. The do-not-contact list overrides everything else, and an opt-out on one channel counts for all channels.

Check the suppression list before every touch.

## Capacity check

Capacity: 2 sellers at 60 touches each per week `[calc: 2 x 60 = 120]`.

Touches per account: 7 `[calc: steps 1 to 7]`. Step 5 is sent only after an accepted request, so 7 is the upper bound.

Accounts: 40.

Average load: 40 accounts x 7 touches / 3 weeks = 93.3 per week `[calc: 40 x 7 / 3]`. That is below 120.

A single start for all 40 would put 120 touches in week 1 `[calc: 40 x 3]`, which is the whole capacity. So I split the accounts into two cohorts of 20, one week apart:

| Calendar week | Cohort A (20 accounts) | Cohort B (20 accounts) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 `[calc: 20 x 3]` | 0 | 60 |
| 2 | 40 `[calc: 20 x 2]` | 60 | 100 |
| 3 | 40 | 40 | 80 |
| 4 | 0 | 40 | 40 |

The weeks sum to 280 touches `[calc: 60 + 100 + 80 + 40]`, equal to 40 x 7. The peak is 100 per week, below 120, which is 50 per seller `[calc: 100 / 2]` against 60. In the peak week 20 touches remain `[calc: 120 - 100]`.

Not known: whether the 120 weekly touches are shared with Tier 1 and other work. If they are, Tier 2 gets less than 100 in the peak week. Please give me Tier 2's share and I will re-run the table.

## Compliance notes

- Email: rules for business email in Greece and Cyprus vary and may treat business contacts differently from consumers. Record a lawful basis and the source for each address. Include sender identity and an opt-out in each email.
- Phone: recording-consent rules and do-not-call registers vary. Check each number before calling.
- LinkedIn: send by hand. Automation and bulk tools may breach the platform's terms.
- Cyprus accounts need their own check; the region given is Greece.
- Have counsel review these notes. They are not stated as settled law.

## Measurement plan

- Reply rate per step: replies divided by touches sent at that step.
- Meetings per 100 accounts `[calc: meetings / 40 x 100]`.
- Unsubscribes and opt-outs, counted per step.
- Bounces, counted per email step.
- Review date: one week after the last cohort B touch, which is the end of week 5 `[calc: 4 weeks + 1]`. Set the calendar date yourself.

With 40 accounts, per-step rates rest on very few replies, so treat early results as anecdotal. No past reply data was supplied.

## Assumptions and what could be wrong

- `[assumption]` Seven touches per account over 21 days, my design.
- `[assumption]` The cohort split of 20 and 20, one week apart.
- `[assumption]` The Tier 2 definition is not given. I used only the count of 40 and the persona.
- `[assumption]` One contact per account, the Finance Director.
- The capacity of 120 may be shared with other tiers. This is the thing most likely to make the plan wrong.
- No contact restrictions beyond the region were given; no do-not-contact list was supplied.
- No past reply data was used, so nothing here is evidence that this cadence works.
