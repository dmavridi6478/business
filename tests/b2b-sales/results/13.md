# Cadence for Tier 2 accounts (skill 13)

All outward content below is **draft, needs approval**. Nothing is sent, posted or promised.

## Objective

Get a first conversation (a meeting booked) with the Finance Director at each of the 40 Tier 2 road freight accounts (300 to 600 employees, Greece and Cyprus), over a 21-day cadence, using email, phone and LinkedIn by hand, within a capacity of 2 sellers x 60 touches a week = 120 touches a week [calc: 2 x 60].

Tier 2 intensity: moderate personalisation (the first line of the email and the LinkedIn note are personalised; the rest is shared) `[assumption: the inputs do not define Tier 1 or Tier 2 intensity]`.

Message themes come from the persona only: slow month-end close, carrier billing disputes, audit pressure, and an offer of invoice matching that cuts the matching work. These are pains reported in public reviews and past deals for the persona, not facts about any one account, so every touch should ask rather than assert.

## Cadence table

Steps are laid out as two waves of 20 accounts, wave B starting 7 days after wave A, so the load stays under capacity (see Capacity check). Day offsets below are per account, counted from that account's Day 1.

| Step | Day | Channel | Purpose (different at every step) | Asset (draft, needs approval) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Introduce Tallyleaf and ask one question: who owns carrier invoice matching and month-end close | Email 1: short, personalised first line, one question, sender block and opt-out line |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn, by hand | Connect, no pitch | Connection note, one sentence, personalised |

| 7 | 21 | Email | Close the loop: offer to stop contact, or to come back on a date the account names | Email 3: three lines, opt-out line |

Touches per account: 7 [calc: count of table rows].

Content limits for every asset:

- Do not name any customer. The persona states that no customer has authorised being named in outreach.
- The persona's one customer data point is a stated *wish* ("wanting month-end close under 5 days"), not a delivered result. Do not present it as an outcome. Using even the unnamed wish in outreach needs the user's approval.
- Do not claim savings, percentages or results; the inputs contain none for Tallyleaf.
- Do not use false urgency or imply familiarity with the account.

## Stop rules

Any one of these ends the cadence for that account on all channels:

1. **Do-not-contact list: overrides everything.** Before every touch, check the list. An account or person on it is skipped, whatever step they are on.
2. **Opt-out on any channel** (an unsubscribe, a "do not contact me", a verbal request on a call): stop all channels at once and add the person to the do-not-contact list the same day.
3. **Reply** (positive, negative or redirect): stop the remaining scripted steps and handle by hand. A meeting booked ends the cadence.
4. **Bounce** (hard): stop email, remove the address, and do not try to guess another address; phone and LinkedIn steps may continue only if the contact is otherwise valid.
5. **"Not now" with a date:** pause and re-enter at Day 1 after that date. A "not now" without a date: ask once for a date; if none, stop.
6. **Wrong person:** stop and record the referral; start a new cadence only for the named person and only if they are not on the do-not-contact list.

## Capacity check

Stated capacity: 2 sellers x 60 touches a week = 120 touches a week [calc: 2 x 60].

Single wave, all 40 accounts together:

- Touches in total: 40 accounts x 7 touches = 280 [calc: 40 x 7].
- Average over the 3 weeks of the cadence: 280 / 3 = 93.3 a week [calc: 280/3], under 120.
- But the weekly profile is not flat. Week 1 holds Days 1, 3 and 7, that is 3 touches per account: 40 x 3 = 120 [calc: 40 x 3], exactly the capacity with no headroom. So a single wave is not recommended.

Two waves of 20 accounts, wave B one week later:

| Calendar week | Wave A [calc: 20 accounts x touches that week] | Wave B [calc: 20 accounts x touches that week] | Total [calc: wave A + wave B] | Within 120? [calc: total <= 120] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 x 3 = 60 | 0 | 60 | yes |
| 2 | 20 x 2 = 40 | 20 x 3 = 60 | 100 | yes |
| 3 | 20 x 2 = 40 | 20 x 2 = 40 | 80 | yes |
| 4 | 0 | 20 x 2 = 40 | 40 | yes |

Column totals `[calc: touches per week = accounts in wave x touches that week]`. Sum of weeks: 60 + 100 + 80 + 40 = 280 [calc], equal to 40 x 7, so the table is consistent.

Peak week is week 2 at 100, leaving 20 touches of headroom [calc: 120 - 100].

Per seller, if each seller owns 20 accounts (10 in each wave): week 1 = 10 x 3 = 30; week 2 = 10 x 2 + 10 x 3 = 50; peak 50, under 60 [calc: per-seller figures].

This check assumes the whole 120-touch capacity is available to Tier 2 `[assumption: the inputs do not say how many touches other tiers use]`. If other tiers share the capacity, reduce the Tier 2 share, or lengthen the stagger, before starting. It also counts a phone call and a LinkedIn message as one touch each, the same as an email, because the inputs state capacity in touches only.

## Compliance notes

These are flags for review by the user's counsel, not statements of what the law requires.

- **Email (Greece, EU):** rules on unsolicited commercial email and on processing business contact data differ between countries and may treat business contacts differently from consumers. Review before sending: the lawful basis for contacting each person, how long the contact data is kept, and how opt-out requests are honoured. Each email should carry the sender block from the inputs (Eleni Markou, Tallyleaf Ltd, 12 Example Street, Athens, Greece) and a working opt-out.
- **Phone:** rules on unsolicited calls, including calls to business numbers and any national do-not-call registers, vary by country. Counsel to confirm before step 3 and step 6.
- **LinkedIn:** manual use only, as the inputs allow. Do not scrape profiles, and do not use automation tools that breach the platform's terms.
- **Cyprus:** the persona covers Greece and Cyprus but the region line says Greece (EU). If any of the 40 accounts are in Cyprus, have counsel review Cyprus separately; the split of the 40 accounts by country is not given.
- **Source of the contact data** for the 40 accounts is not stated. Record it, and where and when each contact was obtained, before the first touch.
- **Data minimisation:** use business information only (company, role, business email, business phone). Do not add private details about individuals.

## Measurement plan

Per step: replies / touches made at that step [calc: replies at step / touches at step], and positive replies separately.

Also track:

- Meetings booked per 100 accounts [calc: meetings / 40 x 100].
- Unsubscribes and opt-outs: count, and per 100 touches [calc: opt-outs / touches x 100].
- Hard bounces: count and share of emails sent.
- Entries added to the do-not-contact list, and any touch made after an opt-out (target: zero).

Sample caution: 40 accounts is a small sample; each step reaches at most 40 contacts. Treat per-step reply rates as anecdotal, not as evidence that one channel or message works.

Review date: the end of wave B, Day 28 after wave A starts [calc: wave B starts Day 8; its Day 21 is Day 28 overall]. The calendar date depends on the start date, which the inputs do not give. Compare only against the team's own later cadences; no outside benchmark is used here.

## Assumptions and what could be wrong

Tagged assumptions:

- `[assumption]` Tier 2 means moderate personalisation (first line and LinkedIn note personalised, rest shared).
- `[assumption]` The full 120-touch weekly capacity is available to Tier 2.

Other assumptions and unknowns:

- One touch per channel action, whatever the channel; a call may take longer than an email, so real capacity may be lower than 120.
- The persona gives capacity as touches per week per seller; I assumed it applies to the working weeks of the cadence, with no holidays.
- No past reply data was supplied, so the channel mix and step order are a design choice, not a result.
- The split of accounts between Greece and Cyprus, the source of the contact data, and whether the contacts have phone numbers and LinkedIn profiles are unknown.
- The start date is unknown, so the review date is a day count only.
- The persona's pains come from public reviews and past deals; they may not apply to a given account.

The one thing most likely to make this plan wrong: other tiers using the same sellers' 120 touches a week, which would leave less capacity than the plan assumes. A close second is the legality of cold phone and email contact to these accounts, which counsel must confirm before step 1.
