## Scripts

All three are **draft, needs approval**, for use after the user confirms the limits.

**Discount request** ("We need 40 percent off.")
"Thank you for being direct. 40 percent is outside what I can do. Can you help me understand what the budget you mentioned covers: the annual subscription alone, or the first year including implementation? Once I know that, I can see what I can do, and if I can move on price, I would ask for something in return, such as payment up front for the year or a reference call."

**Longer payment term** (buyer asks for extended payment terms)
"I cannot agree payment terms on this call. I would need to check. What I can look at is the other direction: if you were able to pay annually up front, I may be able to adjust the price. What is behind the payment timing request?"

**Price match** (if the buyer cites a lower quote)
"Could you share what the other quote covers, so we compare like for like: users, features, implementation, term? I will not match a number I have not seen. If there are differences in scope, we can look at those."

Silence: if the buyer goes quiet after an offer, wait. Do not add a concession to fill the pause, and do not invent a deadline.

Buyer walks away: thank them, restate that the current offer stands as stated, ask what would have to change for it to work, and leave the door open. Do not add urgency and do not reveal the $16,000 floor.
