Timing signals count only if dated within the last 60 days from 2026-10-14, i.e. on or after 2026-08-15 [calc: 2026-10-14 minus 60 days]. I also required a source for a signal to count (skill rule). ## Tier rule

Tier 1: fit score 6 of 6 and at least one counted timing signal. Tier 2: fit score 6 of 6 with no counted timing signal. Tier 3: fit score 4 of 6 (exactly one criterion fails). Fit score 2 or lower: not worked this cycle. Fit score is the sum of the three criterion scores.
