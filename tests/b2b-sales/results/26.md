1. **Time chasing mismatched invoices.** Quantifiable now from buyer figures.
2. **Time matching all invoices by hand.** The buyer said the two clerks "spend most of their month on it" but gave no minutes per invoice or hours. Not quantifiable yet.
3. **Delay to the month-end close.** The buyer said the close takes eleven days and the board wants it under six. No cost of delay was given. Not quantifiable yet.
4. **Audit exposure.** The buyer said the external auditors come in November. No fee, finding or penalty was given. Not quantifiable yet.
5. **Undocumented rate checks.** The buyer said nobody wrote down how the previous AP clerk did them. The risk of errors or overcharges was not sized. Not quantifiable yet.
