## Formulas and inputs

**Driver 1, mismatch chasing (buyer-supplied inputs only):**

- Invoices per month: 3,200 (Sofia: "about").
- Mismatch share: 15% (Sofia: "About fifteen percent").
- Minutes per mismatch: 25 (Sofia: "roughly").
- Mismatches per month = 3,200 x 15% = 480 [calc: 3,200 x 0.15].
- Hours per month = 480 x 25 / 60 = 200 [calc: 480 x 25 / 60].
- Hours per year = 200 x 12 = 2,400 [calc: 200 x 12].
- Money value = 2,400 hours x loaded cost per hour. **The loaded cost per hour was not supplied**, so no money value is computed. See Questions.

**Driver 2, hand matching:** invoices per month x minutes per invoice. Minutes per invoice not supplied; not computed.

**Driver 3, delay:** the gap between the current close and the target is 5 days [calc: 11 - 6], measured against "six days or fewer" (Nikos: "Close in six days or fewer, and an audit trail I can show the auditors."). The board's wording is "under six", which would be a larger gap. No cost per day was supplied; not computed.

**Drivers 4 and 5:** no numbers supplied; not computed.

## Hard and soft costs

- **Hard (hours, money):** driver 1, 2,400 hours a year [calc: 200 x 12]. Driver 2 (hand matching time) is hard but not yet quantified. Driver 5 (clerk time) is hard but see double counting below.
- **Soft (excluded from totals):** pressure from the board on the 11-day close, audit readiness risk with undocumented rate checks, and dependence on one departed employee's knowledge.

Double counting check: Sofia's "the two clerks spend most of their month on it" describes the same working time as the 200 hours of mismatch chasing and hand matching, so driver 5 is not added to drivers 1 and 2; it is only a cross-check. Whether the chase time (driver 1) and the matching time (driver 2) overlap depends on whether the 25 minutes begins after a mismatch is found; Sofia's wording ("Each one takes roughly 25 minutes to chase") suggests it does, but this should be confirmed. The 480 mismatches are part of the 3,200 invoices, so they must not be counted again in driver 2's time.

## Assumptions and what could be wrong

Tagged assumptions: none are used in any total or figure above.

Unknowns that change the conclusion:

- Loaded cost per hour (without it there is no money figure).
- Whether the 25 minutes covers the whole chase, and whether matching time overlaps it.
- All inputs are rough buyer estimates, so 2,400 hours a year [calc: 200 x 12] could be materially higher or lower.
- The two target wordings ("under six" and "six days or fewer") give gaps of different size.

The one thing most likely to make this output wrong: the 15% and 25 minutes are Sofia's spoken estimates ("About", "roughly"). If either is off, the hours move in direct proportion.
