## Actions by band

Internal actions for the customer manager. Nothing is sent automatically; any message to a customer is a draft that needs approval.

- Green (0 to 1): normal cadence. Re-score at each review.
- Amber (2): customer manager reviews the ticket themes and who the sponsor is, and decides whether to book a check-in.
- Red (3 to 4): named owner within the account team, a plan to re-engage the sponsor through the existing contact, a review of open tickets, and a manager review of the account. Do not treat red as a prediction that the account will churn.

## Limits

- Small data: 40 accounts, 16 churn events, 12 test accounts, 5 test churn events.
- Time order is a stand-in (account ID). No dates were supplied.
- Signal timing is unknown. If tickets or utilisation were measured after the customer had decided to leave, the signals describe the exit, not warn of it.
- Cuts were selected on the training data, and some groups are small.
- No contract values, so every account counts equally.
- No information on product or pricing changes during the period, which can break the relationships.
- Association is not cause. Reducing tickets will not necessarily keep an account.
- Account-level business data only; no personal data about individuals is used or needed.

## Assumptions and what could be wrong

Assumptions tagged above:
- Column meanings (churned = 1 is churned; sponsor_active = 1 is active; ticket window is 90 days before the outcome). `[assumption]`
- Account ID order equals time order, so C1 to C28 are earlier than C29 to C40. `[assumption]`

Unknowns that change the conclusion:
- Dates and when each signal was measured relative to the outcome.
- Whether `sponsor_active` was measured before or after the sponsor decided to leave.
- Contract values and any product changes over the period.

The one thing most likely to make this wrong: the time split. If ID order is not chronological, or the signals were recorded after the churn decision, the test results above do not mean what they appear to mean. Please send the dates before anyone relies on the bands.
