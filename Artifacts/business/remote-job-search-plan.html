Scam and verification rules

  • Skip any role asking for payment, a starter-kit fee, equipment purchase, or bank or card details.
  • Be wary of offers without an interview, immediate moves to Telegram or WhatsApp, and companies with no verifiable site or team.
  • Check each listing against the company's own careers page; otherwise mark it unverified.
  • Do not pay any board without independent reviews.
