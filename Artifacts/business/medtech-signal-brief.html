MedTech Signal Brief

3-Tier MedTech Commercial Architecture — from clinical brand foundation through market strategy to execution. Apply in strict top-down order.

Before any marketing or sales activity, the clinical foundation and market-economic strategy must be solid. Jumping to execution without Tiers 1 and 2 produces a commodity sale. Each tier has a distinct failure mode if skipped.

The 3 Tiers

Tier 1 — The Foundation
Clinical Brand Strategy
  1. Vision & Standard of Care — What procedural outcome or patient safety paradigm does this device shift? State the clinical before/after in one sentence.
  2. Clinical Integrity — What peer-reviewed evidence exists or is planned? List publications, registries, white papers, and identify gaps.
  3. Paradigm Positioning — Specific clinical differentiation vs. the current standard of care (not vs. competitors). Build a Defensible Clinical Positioning Matrix: efficacy, safety, procedural efficiency, learning curve.
  4. Core Value Proposition — One tailored value prop per medical specialty affected.
Risk of skipping: commoditization, physician skepticism, inability to justify premium pricing.
Tier 2 — How We Compete
Market & Economic Strategy
  1. Stakeholder Ecosystem — Map every decision-maker in the hospital buying matrix: Surgeons/Users (procedural accuracy, ergonomic safety), Procurement/CFO (TCO, DRG optimization), OR Directors (turnover time, workflow integration), IT/BioMed (cyber compliance, PACS/EMR interoperability), VAC (evidence, economic dossier, formulary pathway).
  2. Economic Value Architecture — HEOR deliverables: length-of-stay reduction model, complication cost avoidance model, DRG/reimbursement mapping, TCO model (CapEx vs. lease-per-procedure vs. risk-share).
  3. Go-To-Market Route — Define the procurement pathway: capital placement, consumable model, or service SLA; tender strategy for public hospitals; risk-share or pay-per-use pilot to lower adoption barrier.
Risk of skipping: stalled tenders, blocked formulary approvals, prolonged sales cycles.
Tier 3 — How We Show Up
Execution: Branding & Marketing
  1. White-Glove Clinical Support — Proctorship program, on-site OR specialist coverage, hands-on cadaveric/simulation workshops.
  2. Peer-to-Peer Medical Education — KOL masterclasses, CME symposia, multi-center registry case studies.
  3. Executive Hospital Partnerships — C-suite roundtables, partnership agreements, joint outcome registries.
  4. Commercial Marketing — Peer-reviewed content, health economics briefings, congress presence with post-congress digital nurture.
Risk of skipping: high churn, buyer remorse, underutilized capital equipment.
