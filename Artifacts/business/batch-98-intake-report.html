8. Risks and recommendations

I disagree with the video's default of auto-replying to leads in 60 seconds and auto-sending invoice reminders. Here is what I would do instead: keep every agent at draft-only for at least 20 reviewed drafts, then promote one agent at a time with the reason written in rules/agent-permissions.md. The risk in the video's approach is a wrong, unconsented or regulated-claim message sent to a real person under your name, which no later fix can recall. Likely
