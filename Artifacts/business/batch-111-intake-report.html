Batch 111 Intake Report

Two archives (20 AI-visibility infographics; 14 TikTok cards from the iCloud export) and three infographics attached in chat were studied and converted into reusable assets: 20 skills, 24 commands, 10 agents, 8 design templates, 5 themes, 6 workflows, a verified repository table and plain-text prompts. Nothing was installed and no external account was changed.

Read this first

1. None of the 37 images contained an executable task. The only calls to action ("Comment BOOK", "Comment ASSET", "Follow", "Repost") are the authors' lead-generation hooks. They were not actioned. The two gated guides behind them (the 50-agent marketing book and the 16-page LinkedIn guide) were reconstructed only from what is visible on the images, and each skill says so.

2. This repository is becoming a collection rather than a tool. It now holds 1,239 skills, 734 commands and 273 agents. Every skill and agent description is read into session context, so each addition has a running cost and dilutes routing accuracy. Roughly half of this batch (the tool directories, the project-plan and interview skills) duplicates what a single prompt does. Recommendation: keep the eight skills that carry real logic (see section 9) and prune the rest after a month of observed use.

3. The headline saving of $8,621 a year is arithmetically correct (verified) and economically misleading. It assumes zero engineering time. Four of the twelve swaps need no new server (OSV-Scanner, Nuclei, Starlight, Better Auth); the others transfer cost from a licence to your hours. For a marketing-led business only OSV-Scanner is plausibly relevant.

4. Statistics in the AI-search infographics are unverified vendor claims (for example "61% chance of appearing in AI Mode"). They are labelled as such in the skill and must not be quoted to clients.

Plain-text prompts: the nine sales-email prompts (source wording)

Replace every [bracket]. Each result is a draft; compliance gates are in the skill.

A1. The Personalised Opener
Purpose: skip the "hope you're well" trap and prove you did your homework.
Here's my prospect's LinkedIn profile: [paste]. Write 3 opening lines for a cold email that reference something specific about their career, recent activity, or company. No flattery. Make it sound like I actually read their page.

A2. The Simplifier
Purpose: shorter emails get more replies.
Rewrite this sales email at a 5th grade reading level. Cut it to under 80 words. Remove every word that doesn't earn its spot. Keep one clear CTA: [paste draft]

A3. The Subject Line Factory
Purpose: test 10 options instead of guessing one.
Here's my email body: [paste]. Give me 10 subject lines using these angles: curiosity, a specific number, their company name, a pain point, and a bold claim. Keep each under 6 words.

A4. The Feature-To-Benefit Translator
Purpose: features describe your product; benefits describe their life after buying.
I'm selling [product] to a [prospect's title] at a [company type]. Here are my product's features: [list]. Rewrite each one as a benefit statement this buyer would actually care about. Start each with "You" or "Your team."

A5. The Objection Follow-Up
Purpose: address the real blocker instead of "circling back".
My prospect said: "[exact objection]". Their role is [title] at a [company type]. Write a follow-up email that acknowledges their concern without agreeing, reframes the value around their specific pain point, and ends with a soft CTA. Under 100 words.

A6. The Breakup Email
Purpose: remove all pressure with a final message.
I've sent [number] emails to [prospect's title] at [company] over [timeframe] with no reply. Write a final email that's honest, zero-pressure, and gives them an easy out. Make it feel human, not passive-aggressive. Under 60 words.

A7. The Case Study Snippet
Purpose: one case study becomes ammunition for every persona.
Here's a full case study: [paste]. Write a 2-sentence version for a [specific role] that leads with the metric they care about most. Then write a different 2-sentence version for a [different role]. No buzzwords.

A8. The Spam And Tone Audit
Purpose: catch spam-trigger language and robotic tone before sending.
Here's my sales email: [paste]. Flag any words or phrases likely to trigger spam filters. Rate the tone from 1-10 on warmth and confidence. Then rewrite it with your fixes applied.

A9. The Full Sequence Builder
Purpose: a working five-email draft in minutes.
I sell [product/service] to [ICP]. The main pain point I solve is [pain]. Build a 5-email outbound sequence. Email 1: pain-focused. Email 2: social proof. Email 3: insight or contrarian take. Email 4: direct ask. Email 5: breakup. Keep each under 80 words.
