6. Prompts in plain text

The graphics print almost no literal prompts, so these two were written by me from their content; brackets are your inputs.

Delegation matrix (written by me from the Ryze structure)
For our [channel] marketing, sort every task into four boxes: automate (agent does it, no approval), approve (agent drafts, a human approves before anything goes live or spends), draft (agent proposes, a human writes the final), and keep (human only). For each task name the data it reads, the output, how often it runs and the hard limit (for example a daily spend cap). Do not put anything that spends money or publishes into the automate box.
GTM waste audit (written by me from the Frontal graphic)
Here is our GTM plan: [paste]. List every activity, then mark each one as "matters" or "does not matter" using these tests: does it improve the list, the offer, deliverability, a target-account touch, or a pipeline number shared across channels? Flag open-rate reporting, one template to a large list, and extra tools added without a named bottleneck.
