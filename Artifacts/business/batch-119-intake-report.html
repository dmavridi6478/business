6. Prompts in plain text

The graphics print almost no literal prompts, so these three were written by me from their content; brackets are your inputs.

AI visibility test (written by me from the wheel)
Here are 30 prompts a buyer might ask about [category]: [paste]. For each, tell me which brands you would recommend and which sources you would cite. Run it three times and report where [brand] appears (never, sometimes, always), which competitors appear instead, and which of my pages would need to exist to be cited. Do not guess about sources you cannot see.
Forecast build (written by me from the 4 levels)
Build a [level 1, 2 or 3] forecast model for [business] in a spreadsheet. Inputs sheet: [price, volume, churn, headcount plan]. Level 1: revenue build segmented into existing, pipeline and new customers. Level 2: add a P&L with a headcount build. Level 3: add a balance sheet and cash flow with a cash check equal to zero. No hard-coded numbers in formulas; show base, low and high scenarios on separate columns.
RevOps vs GTM split (written by me)
Our CRM is [tool]. List the fields RevOps must control (with sign-off), the staging fields GTM tools may write to, the Slack alerts each team needs, and four metrics for each team. Flag every place an enrichment tool could overwrite a field RevOps relies on.

  • For AI visibility work, test a fixed prompt list weekly and report ranges, not single answers.
  • Pick one forecasting level that matches your stage; most early companies need Level 1 or 2.
