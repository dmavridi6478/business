3. Critique

  • Scope. Sections 7 and 8 describe months of work. The staged plan in the skill starts with a no-I/O kernel and tests, then one codec, then storage, then one proxy client.
  • Security. The cert-MITM launcher installs a local certificate authority so a proxy can read and rewrite client traffic, exposing keys and all prompts to that process. Use it only on machines and clients you control; prefer base-URL rewrite or plugin modes.
  • Terms of service. Rewriting client requests may conflict with provider or client terms and request signing; the prompt says to respect them.
  • Quality. Summaries replace history; a poor summary loses information. Measure task success, not just tokens.
  • No evidence. No benchmark or result is shown, and the roughly twenty client integrations were not verified.
  • Provenance. Credit is a watermark only. I did not republish the text on any external service; check with the author before doing so.
