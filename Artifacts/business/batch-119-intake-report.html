1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] I did not install the 40 add-ons and I recommend you do not. Each skill, plugin and MCP server loads context every session and is third-party code with your permissions. The list contains three memory systems, several token-savers and five workflow frameworks that do the same job. I built a catalogue with a verified path, stars, licence and risk note per item, and a rule: one add-on per job, at most about five, tested on a throwaway branch.
  • [Certain] I checked all 40 on GitHub. Three have moved or redirect: beads (now gastownhall/beads), graphify (now Graphify-Labs/graphify) and headroom (now headroomlabs-ai/headroom). Stars on the card were not shown, so none could be compared. Four repo pages (claude-mem, repomix, planning-with-files, the system-prompts repo) did not return a star count, so I did not quote one.
  • [Certain] Specific risks on the list: cc-switch says only ccswitch.io is official, so impersonation downloads are a real risk; claude-code-router and codex-plugin-cc send your code to other providers; firecrawl is AGPL-3.0; the system-prompts repo collects commercial products' prompts, some likely extracted without owners' consent; claude-mem stores your session content and overlaps the Cognee memory you already use.
  • [Likely] The "Million-Dollar Landing Page" is a sensible checklist with an unproven title. The graphic gives no evidence that the structure earns AI citations or revenue. Two points are wrong or risky: [Certain] since 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for well-known government and health sites, so FAQ markup on a commercial page will not get the rich result; and a "last updated" stamp changed without real edits is deceptive. Author, logos and proof must be real and permitted.
  • [Certain] The storytelling graphic is solid craft advice (tension, a human moment, images, the audience, stakes, a closed loop, a decision). The Steve Jobs quote at its centre is attributed without a source I could verify, and principle 5 (stakes) becomes manipulation if the loss is manufactured. I added a truth rule and an audit.
  • [Certain] There is no verbatim prompt in any of the three graphics, so the six prompts in the prompts file are original, built from their content.

4. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree that a list of 40 is something to install. The risk is context bloat, overlapping tools that conflict, and unvetted code with access to your files. I recommend one add-on per job and a measurement.
  • I disagree with "Million-Dollar" as a promise. The risk is publishing hype claims, fake freshness or unpermitted logos in the hope of AI citations. I made the audit check truth first.
  • I disagree with using stakes to push a decision. The risk is manufactured urgency. The skill requires a real, specific cost.
