MedTech Content Playbook

5-Stage SalesFlow Architecture for the modern digital MedTech commercial engine — Understand → Amplify → Verify → Expand → Nurture.

81% of physicians use AI/LLMs in clinical practice
90% of hospital decision-makers do ~12 online searches before contacting a rep
68% of searches end in zero-clicks due to AI Overviews
90% of addressable hospital buyers are currently out-of-market

The 5 Stages

Stage 1
Understand — Hospital Buying Matrix & Needs-Based Journeys

Create tailored one-page journey maps for each stakeholder persona. Do not pitch generic feature sheets.

Persona Primary Decision Criteria Content That Resonates
Surgeon/User Procedural accuracy, ergonomic safety, learning curve Technique videos, outcomes data, peer comparisons
Procurement/CFO TCO, DRG optimization, budget cycle Cost models, ROI calculators, budget impact analysis
OR Director Turnover time, workflow integration, staff training Workflow diagrams, implementation timeline, training plan
IT/BioMed Cyber compliance, PACS/EMR interoperability, uptime Tech specs, compliance certs, integration documentation
VAC Committee Clinical evidence, economic value, formulary pathway HEOR dossier, RWE data, peer-reviewed publications
Deliverable: One-page journey map per persona — 3–5 content touchpoints from awareness to active evaluation.
Stage 2
Amplify — Convert 3-Day Congress into 60-Day Digital Pipeline

Congress is a signal, not a strategy. Convert the 3-day window into a 60-day engine: Pre-congress paid search and LinkedIn targeting of registered attendees → During-congress hyper-local geo-targeting → Post-congress retargeting (Day 1–7: hot leads clinical data; Day 8–30: warm leads economic model; Day 31–57: all badge scans education content). Cost is 1/8th of live congress, CTR 10× higher than cold outreach.

Trap to avoid: Packing up the booth Sunday and relying on reps to cold-call badge scans weeks later.
Stage 3
Verify — Command Search & Generative AI Citation Share

If your clinical content is not indexed and cited by AI Overviews and LLMs, you don't exist for 68% of searchers. Fix this with: structured data (Schema.org markup: MedicalProcedure, MedicalDevice, FAQPage) on all clinical landing pages; LinkedIn as AI training corpus (mirror peer-reviewed publications as long-form articles); authority citations (Wikipedia + peer-reviewed journals, the sources LLMs cite most); technical SEO (Core Web Vitals, mobile rendering, crawlability).

Deliverable: Content audit identifying top 10 clinical queries where your device should appear in AI Overviews; gap analysis; schema implementation plan.
Stage 4
Expand — Precision ICP Reach & KOL Thought Leader Ads

Reach the 90% of addressable market that is out-of-market before they search. LinkedIn Boolean AND targeting by specialty, hospital seniority, institution type, and geography — avoid "Audience Expansion" (drains up to 95% of budget on bot traffic). Use matched audiences with hospital target lists, CRM contacts, conference attendee lists. For Thought Leader Ads (highest-trust format in MedTech): identify 3–5 KOL surgeons active on LinkedIn and sponsor their organic posts into specific hospital ICPs.

ICP segmentation: Tier 1 (named accounts, confirmed budget cycles, known VAC timelines) → Tier 2 (institutions matching Tier 1 profile) → Tier 3 (broad market, education only).

Deliverable: LinkedIn campaign architecture per ICP tier; KOL shortlist with engagement brief; monthly spend allocation by tier.
Stage 5
Nurture — 6–24 Month Multi-Stakeholder Automation

Capital medical device sales cycles run 6–24 months. Most teams abandon after 1–2 touches. Win by staying present. Persona-segmented streams: Surgeon (quarterly outcomes data, technique videos, peer case studies) → Economic buyer (updated TCO models, reimbursement news, health system benchmarks) → VAC (RWE registry updates, expanded indications, comparative effectiveness data). Trigger-based escalation: budget cycle signals, new procedure volume data, competitor activity.

RWE Engine: establish multi-center registry from first 10 implanting sites, publish interim data at 6, 12, 24 months, use hospital case studies as anchor content for all nurture streams.

Deliverable: 24-month nurture calendar by persona; trigger logic map; RWE publication roadmap.
