1. Result

The input was two different things: nineteen carousel slides from one creator about seventeen open-source projects, and three LinkedIn and sales infographics. Seventeen repositories were checked; five were already registered here, so twelve are new. Five skills, two commands, one design template and one theme were created. There was no agent guide and no workflow in the files, so no agent was built; the n8n server needs authorisation in this session, so no workflow was created.

The main findings, in order of importance. First, the interface pictures on the animation carousel are illustrations, not screenshots (three cards say so on their face). Second, the founder-versus-team infographic does not add up: its eight follower counts sum to about 154,900, not the "180K+" it claims. Third, the headline formula sheet numbers its parts differently in the examples than in the formula. Fourth, the "Head of Sales" poster shows no prompts and gates them behind a comment keyword, so the routines were built from the poster's one-line jobs, with data requirements added.
