1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] Most of the repo cards were already in the library. Duplicate check by search: OpenHands, Maxun, Openwhispr, Strix, OpenSEO, OmniRoute and ai-job-search each appear in an existing skill, so they were not rebuilt. Genuinely new: cleanyourtv, Filee, MacNativeGames, ruflo, Postiz, Trigger.dev, Open Notebook, Open Generative AI, Book to Skill, No AI Slop, I Have ADHD.
  • [Certain] Every star count on a card was out of date. Read from GitHub on 28 October 2026: cleanyourtv 41,347 (card 27,491), ai-job-search 66,618 (64,035), Filee 14,711 (10,947), MacNativeGames 5,393 (4,843), ruflo 79,407 (78,730). Two other figures were wrong in kind: the card's "78k awesome-claude-skills" matched no repo I found (BehiSecc/awesome-claude-skills has 10,308), and the course card says 12 lessons where the repo now says 18.
  • [Certain] The riskiest tool is cleanyourtv. It gives an AI agent control of a TV over ADB, its licence is "Other" and the repo is eleven days old. Its restore script is the safety net and it is worth reading first. Samsung, LG and Roku are not supported.
  • [Certain] MacNativeGames is not a coding question, it is a legal one. The repo ships no game, but you must dump your own copy and console keys, and that may breach law or terms where you live. I did not offer to clone it in the install script.
  • [Likely] ruflo is the one most likely to cost you time rather than money: it changes Claude Code settings, has 988 open issues and a very large surface. Try it in a spare project. Its "4 ms memory recall" is the author's own test.
  • [Certain] The finance graphic's method is sound (lock the budget, roll one forecast, compare both). Its footer is an advert for a spreadsheet add-in, which I excluded. It omits two real pitfalls: sign conventions, and a rolling forecast that hides slippage against the locked budget.
  • [Certain] Open Generative AI says "400+ models" in one place and "600+" in another, and most generation goes to a paid third-party gateway. "Self-hosted" is true of the app, not of most of the models.
  • [Certain] The carousel's "comment REPO and I will DM you the article" is an engagement hook. The repos are public; no DM is needed.

3. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree with ordering repos by weekly star growth. The risk is installing the newest viral repo, not the safest. A repo created ten days ago can have 40,000 stars and a licence marked "Other". Use the safe-trial protocol and read the install path first.
  • I disagree with "paste PROMPT.md into your AI agent" as an install step. The risk is that the agent executes whatever the file says on a device you own. Read the file first and keep the restore script.
  • I disagree with presenting open source as free of cost. Postiz is AGPL, Open Generative AI needs a paid gateway for most models, and Open Notebook sends your sources to whichever hosted model you enable.
