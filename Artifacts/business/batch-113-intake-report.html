1. Executive summary

The material is four sets: a 60-tool AI map, a 15-prompt Excel and PowerPoint library, a carousel of seven GitHub repos for building AI agents (with a pitch for a free Hermes guide), and a carousel of five trending repos. Most of the seven agent repos were already in the repository from an earlier batch, so I added only what that coverage lacked: licences, ownership and warnings.

The uncomfortable part: the repo carousels are promotion, and they leave out facts that matter. OpenViking is AGPL-3.0, a licence that can obligate you to publish your source if you run a modified copy as a network service; no slide mentions it. "Anthropic Cybersecurity Skills" is not made by Anthropic; the owner is an individual account. "Awesome Harness Engineering" is the name of at least three different repositories. Every star count is a screenshot I could not check, and one repo shows 44.9k stars with 14 commits.

4. The finance workbook (executed task)

Prompts 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are implemented as formulas in finance-models-starter.xlsx: customer lookup, CAGR, three-scenario revenue with a chart, DCF, profitability projection, anomaly detector and a six-month forecast with a chart. I recalculated it in LibreOffice and compared 18 values with an independent calculation; all matched. Examples: CAGR 12.91%, DCF enterprise value 3,191,388 for the sample inputs, forecast slope 7.34 a month. Sample numbers are illustrative.

Limit: the anomaly sheet uses the sample standard deviation including the outliers, so large outliers inflate it and can hide smaller ones. Review flagged and unflagged items, not only the flags.

7. Critique and risks

  • The 60-tool map has no criteria. Ten tools per category are alternatives, not a stack. Pick two per category at most, and check each vendor's data terms before sending client data.
  • Hermes guide. The carousel offers a free guide to commenters. It is a lead magnet, was not in the archive, and nothing here claims to reproduce it.
  • Tools that act on your behalf (orca, opencode, Browser Use) use your credentials and can change files or sites. Start with a clean git state and read-only tasks.
  • Stars are novelty, not quality. The trending ranking is stars gained in seven days.
  • Duplicates. The repository now holds two sources for the seven-repo carousel; the ledger points to the older entries rather than repeating them.
