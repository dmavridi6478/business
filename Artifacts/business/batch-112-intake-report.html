5. Critique and risks

  • Cards are marketing, not assessment. Check recent commits and open issues before depending on any of them.
  • FreshRSS is a server you must patch and back up, and AGPL-3.0 means offering a modified copy over a network requires offering its source.
  • The hardening guide can lock you out. The skill gives general safe practice (test machine, snapshot, second session, one change at a time); it does not summarise chapters I did not read.
  • OSSU courses are third-party. Content can move or require payment for certificates.
  • Badges are one-day snapshots (FreeCAD release v1.1.4, FreshRSS 41.27 EUR a week).
