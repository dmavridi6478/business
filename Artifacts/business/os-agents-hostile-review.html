OS Agent Hostile Review

Read-only review of the 25 os-* agents, the two commands and the rules files. Findings 1, 2 and 6 have since been fixed with hooks; the other eight are open.

Verdict

The "draft-only behind an approval gate" claim I made is true by instruction, not by enforcement. Certain The agents cannot send anything externally, because they hold no send, shell or edit tools. But every one can write any file in the repo, including its own rules, the approval record and other agents' definitions, and no hook or permission rule stops it.

Certain Do not promote any agent above tier 0 until findings 1 to 6 are fixed. The first two are what a hostile lead message would use.

What held up

  • Certain No agent has Bash, Edit, Agent or any MCP tool, so direct sending and code execution are not available to a subagent.
  • Certain WebFetch is limited to 4 of 25 agents. data/ai-os/* is gitignored, so lead and client data cannot be committed.
  • Likely The defense baseline is present in all 25 files and the model tiers are sensible.

Recommended order

  • Fix 1, 2 and 6 together in one hook set. They share a mechanism.
  • Then 3 and 4, which close the exfiltration and main-session holes.
  • Then 5, 7 and 8. Findings 9 to 11 matter only once you promote an agent.

Fix status for 1, 2 and 6

  • 1 Write scope: .claude/hooks/os_guard.py confines each os-* agent to its own .md paths, resolves symlinks and .., and fails closed.
  • 2 Approvals: scripts/os_approvals.py records approvals only from a real terminal, bound to the exact card text, expiring after 24 h, in a hash chain. The hook and a deny rule block every agent and session from writing the ledger.
  • 6 Evidence: .claude/hooks/os_log.py writes a log the agents cannot touch; a missing log is reported as CRITICAL, not as all clear.
