Already existed, not duplicated

The @ruby.creates1 series is the same six tools and advice as alinacoding-app-business-6; Excalidraw, Maxun, Heyform, OpenChatCut and Macshot already appear in earlier repo-card skills, so the new skill consolidates them rather than repeating the details.

Not installed, on purpose

No plugin, connector or clone was added. AnyPS5 is documented with its copyright and DRM risk and excluded from the script. Moli's installer pipes curl into sh and was not run. REA is flagged for use only on software you own or may inspect. The terminal slides show no repos or licences, so those apps need checking first.

Caveats

Star counts, benchmarks and "this week" gains are the slides' own and some look like viral spikes (PhotoCraft +32.7k on 33.5k). The two Strata slides disagree on RAM (32 GB vs 64 GB). Paid-tool icons on the replace.so slides were identified from logos only. The creators' lead magnets ("comment REPO", tool notes) were not in the files.
