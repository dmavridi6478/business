17 images in a zip plus four inline infographics processed with the seven-step intake. Result: three new skills, three commands, one merge, two templates, two themes, four workflows.

1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] The Perplexity-for-sales-research graphic is an exact duplicate of Batch 102 (perplexity-sales-research-10 and /sales-research, same ten prompts and three rules). I built nothing for it. Re-sending the same card costs you review time.
  • [Certain] The "Claude B2B Sales Stack" graphic is a cover with layer names and counts (500+ prompts, 150+ skills, 100+ agents, 25+ workflows) and a "comment B2B" gate. It contains no prompts or skill text, so nothing was extracted. I did not verify the counts and would not trust them.
  • [Certain] The Unsloth export card is wrong. It imports export_to_gguf; the documented call is model.save_pretrained_gguf(directory, tokenizer, quantization_method=...). I checked this against Unsloth's documentation and source and corrected it in the skill. The most common export failure (gibberish after moving to Ollama or llama.cpp) is a chat-template or end-of-sequence mismatch, which the cards omit.
  • [Certain] The Unsloth repo has changed: 78,530 stars now (the card showed 66.8k) and its description is now a local UI to run and train models, so the card's library-only picture is out of date.
  • [Likely] The sales-growth formula (leads x conversion x deal size x retention) is a useful diagnostic and not a model. The multiplicative effect is real (a 10% gain on all four is 1.1^4 = +46.4%), but retention does not belong in a first-sale formula, and the 70/30 and 3-yes rules are folk wisdom with no source. The 3-yes rule turns into manipulation when used as a pressure ladder.
  • [Certain] The six demo apps are three open and three paid. Searching GitHub as you asked: Cap, Screenity and VHS are open source; Screen Studio, Arcade and Tella are not. Screen Studio has open alternatives (OpenScreen, whose original repo is archived and now continued at getopenscreen/openscreen; Focra; Vuoom). For Arcade the open options are tiny personal projects (runthru 4 stars, showkit 0 stars), so I flagged them as trials only.
  • [Certain] The Excel/PPT prompts are sound but dangerous in finance: prompts 3, 4, 6 and 7 touch reconciliations, formatting of a P&L and eliminations. I added a tie-out check to each and a rule that an accountant reviews before use.

4. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree that the Unsloth cards are a safe copy-and-paste path. The risk is a script that fails at export or a model that misbehaves after export, with no evaluation to tell you whether training helped. I added an evaluation gate and the corrected API.
  • I disagree with treating the sales-growth formula as a model. The risk is optimising a factor in isolation (more leads, lower conversion). The command tests each lever and the combination and says where the formula does not fit.
  • I disagree with letting an assistant edit a finance workbook without a control. The risk is a silent formula change reaching a board pack. The skill requires a copy, live formulas and a tie-out.
