What you should know first

[Certain] One card claims "+54,706 stars today" for a reverse-engineering MCP (morluto/rea). That is not plausible for any repository in a day. Treat it as an error or a fake-star signal; the repo exists and is MIT-licensed.

[Certain] cursor/plugins has no LICENSE file at its root. A card called it "every official Cursor plugin, one repo", but you cannot assume an open licence; each plugin folder must be read.

[Certain] Star counts disagree across cards. Open WebUI is 140K on one card and 154k on another; Langflow at 194K and MarkItDown at 188k+ are unusually high. Check GitHub before quoting any.

[Certain] Licences are mixed, not "free". Six of the tools are AGPL-3.0 (RustDesk, Upscayl, Automa, Maxun, SiYuan, OpenStock), Open WebUI is a custom licence, and FFmpeg is LGPL with optional GPL parts.

[Certain] Two repos in the @freellmapi video are not named (the leaked system prompts and the free-LLM-API aggregator), so they could not be checked. Do not download a repo from a comment reply, and treat "leaked system prompts" as unverifiable.

5. Source problems

  • Implausible or conflicting star counts (rea +54,706 in a day; Open WebUI 140K vs 154k; Langflow 194K; MarkItDown 188k+).
  • cursor/plugins has no root licence; FFmpeg and Beekeeper have layered licences; Open WebUI is custom.
  • "Free" often means free core with paid tiers (Stirling PDF, RustDesk Pro, Beekeeper, SiYuan sync).
  • Two repos in the video were never named, and the leaked system prompts cannot be verified.
  • The Site Tusk headline "they don't want you to know" is clickbait; all five are well-known projects.
