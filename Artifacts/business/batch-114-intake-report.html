6. Prompts in plain text

Square brackets are your inputs. These seven prompts were written by me from the lesson titles and promises in the graphic (lesson 01 is a reading topic and has no prompt). They are not the course author's wording, which I could not find.

Lesson 02: sales emails
Write three personalised sales emails of under 120 words each for [product] to [buyer role] at [company type]. Use these facts only: [paste facts]. Each email needs one specific reason it is relevant to them, one clear ask and no invented claims or results.
Lesson 03: prospect research
Build a prospect research sheet for [product]. For each of these prospects, give the buying signal I can point to and the source link for it: [paste names or companies]. Mark anything you cannot verify and do not invent prospects.
Lesson 04: follow-ups
Write a four-touch follow-up sequence for a prospect who went quiet after [last interaction]. Give the timing of each touch, a message under 80 words, and one reason to stop. Include an easy way to opt out.
Lesson 05: objections
Here are the objections I hear most: [paste]. For each, give a short reply, one question that keeps the conversation going and the evidence I would need to back the reply. Do not invent customer examples.
Lesson 06: lead capture flow
Design a lead capture, qualify and nurture flow for [business]. For each step say what triggers it, what is sent, what data is collected and the lawful basis or consent needed. Keep a human review before anything is sent automatically.
Lesson 07: call review
Review this sales call transcript (names removed, shared with permission): [paste]. Report talk ratio, questions asked, objections raised, the agreed next step and the one thing to change next time. Quote the transcript for each point.
Lesson 08: 30-day plan
Build a 30-day AI sales plan for [product] aimed at [buyer]. I currently get [leads and closes per month] and have [hours per week]. Give four weeks of three tasks each with time estimates, one number to measure each week and a stop-or-continue rule. Do not promise results.
