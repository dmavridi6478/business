1. Result

The archive holds two five-tool lists: a carousel of agent and developer tools (@your.aimentor) and a video of security scanners (@cyberwithjohann). All ten repositories exist and each licence was read from the repository's own file. Three skills, two commands, one agent, two design templates and one theme were added, together with two action documents and a user-run script. No prompts, workflows or installable connectors were in the files.

The main finding is that the cards and the video present these tools as plain installs. Four of the ten carry a risk that the source omits: TruffleHog tests found secrets against the real service, Trivy had a supply-chain compromise in March 2026, Craft Anything is a reverse-engineering engine, and Scrapling advertises bot-protection bypass.
