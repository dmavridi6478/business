Half of this upload was already in the repo. The CV and LinkedIn prompts, the 17-skill Fable outbound chain, the pre-mortem, 5 Whys, SWOT and decision-tree commands, the GitHubNow and replace.so card styles and most of the repos existed. I added only what was new and wrote no duplicates.

The "50+ sales skills" slide gives you nothing to install. It names 15 file names out of 50+ and ends with "comment SEND to get it". I did not invent the rest. I mapped the 15 names to the 17 skills you already have. claude run --all-skills on that slide is not a real Claude Code command.

The 9 prompt-writing skills are my versions, not the author's. The infographic shows names and one-line purposes only. The seven new commands are written from those purposes. It also says "Opus 4.8" while this environment names Opus 5.5, so the Opus command is version-neutral.

Several statistics on the slides have no source. The agentic-marketing shares (32% orchestrate vs 8% autonomous) do not reconcile, and the LinkedIn "5X views, 7X requests" figure has no link. They are quoted and tagged, not endorsed.

Three of the repo owners were not on the slides. I found Capd, ChatbotX and alphai-tui by web search and confirmed each with git ls-remote. ChatbotX and cmux have custom licences; unsloth, athas and uptimepage are AGPL-3.0. Read the licence before you self-host or resell.

Sales research: 10 prompts (SalesDaily.co)

1. Company overview
Summarize what {company} does, who they serve, how they make money, and their 3 most recent strategic moves. Cite a source for each claim.
2. Recent news and triggers
List the 5 most important news items about {company} from the last 90 days. Focus on hiring, funding, product launches and leadership changes. Add dates and links.
3. Earnings insights
Pull the 3 biggest themes from {company}'s latest earnings call. What did the CEO emphasize? What did analysts push on? Works for public companies.
4. Buyer pain points
What are the top 5 business problems a {title} at a {size} {industry} company faces in 2026? Cite recent surveys, research and articles.
5. Competitive intel
Compare {product} vs {competitor}. What do customers say in G2, Capterra and Reddit reviews? Where does each win and where does each lose?
6. Stakeholder mapping
Who leads {function} at {company}? List names and roles found in press releases, news, conference talks and the company website, with links.
7. Industry trends
What are the 3 biggest shifts in {industry} right now that a {role} would care about? Include data points and cite each one.
8. Tech stack discovery
What technology does {company} use, based on their job postings, case studies, integration pages and engineering blog?
9. Buying signals
Has {company} posted jobs in {function} in the last 60 days? Any RFPs, vendor announcements, partnerships or expansion news?
10. Champion research
Research {first name last name}, {title} at {company}: podcast appearances, conference talks, interviews and articles they wrote or were quoted in.

Photoshoot: 7 prompts for your own photo (@theaiguyhere)

1. DSLR Photoshoot Mode
Transform my uploaded photo into a professional DSLR photoshoot. Keep my identity, facial structure, skin tone, and natural features same without any alteration. Improve the composition, lighting, depth of field, pose, background, wardrobe, and overall atmosphere while keeping everything physically realistic. Make it look like a professional photographer actually captured the image using a high-end camera and portrait lens. Avoid plastic skin, excessive retouching, distorted features, or an obvious AI-generated appearance.
2. Change Your Location
Use my uploaded photo as the identity reference and place me naturally in [LOCATION]. Don't simply replace the background. Reconstruct the entire photograph so my lighting, shadows, perspective, reflections, clothing, depth, and color temperature naturally match the environment. Make it look as though I genuinely traveled to this location for a professional photoshoot.
3. Luxury Photoshoot Mode
Transform my uploaded photo into a luxury editorial photoshoot. Give me a sophisticated [OUTFIT], elegant [LOCATION], flattering professional lighting, realistic skin texture, cinematic depth, premium composition, and subtle high-end color grading. Keep my face and identity recognizable. The final result should look expensive, sophisticated, and believable rather than overly edited.
4. Fashion Magazine Transformation
Transform my uploaded photo into a high-end fashion editorial photograph. Keep my actual identity and facial characteristics intact. Give me a professional model-like pose, [FASHION STYLE], sophisticated lighting, controlled shadows, realistic skin texture, carefully composed framing, and editorial-quality styling. Make the photograph look like it was captured by a professional fashion photographer for a premium magazine.
5. Fix an Ordinary Photo
Take my uploaded ordinary photo and turn it into an extraordinary professional photograph. Keep my face and body same without any alteration. Identify the weaknesses in the original image, including lighting, framing, background, pose, clothing, distractions, and composition, then correct them through a realistic photoshoot transformation. Preserve my recognizable features and natural appearance. The final image should look dramatically better but still believable.
6. Travel Influencer Photoshoot
Turn my uploaded photo into an authentic professional travel photograph taken in [DESTINATION]. Give me a natural, confident pose and appropriate [OUTFIT]. Match the sunlight, shadows, weather, environment, perspective, and color temperature to the location. Use realistic depth of field and professional travel photography composition. Make it feel spontaneous but professionally captured.
7. Social Media Profile Photoshoot
Transform my uploaded photo into a professional personal-brand photoshoot for my [INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN] profile. Create a polished but natural appearance that communicates [PERSONALITY/PROFESSION]. Improve the pose, wardrobe, lighting, background, framing, facial expression, and composition. Make it look professionally photographed while keeping my appearance authentic and recognizable.
