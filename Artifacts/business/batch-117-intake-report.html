Two archives holding 13 business and marketing infographics (10 images, 3 animated GIFs), turned into 12 skills, 2 commands and no installs. These are reference graphics, so the work was transcription, de-duplication against the repo, and honest labelling of what each one can and cannot support.

What you should know first

[Certain] The "89 Claude commands" are mostly not real commands. Typing /workflow or /forecast in Claude does nothing built-in. They work only as a convention, or after you save each as your own command or skill. The graphic also ends in a "comment AI Tools for the database" funnel.

[Certain] The "Claude Code marketing team" is an org chart, not a result. The 50 agent files were not in the upload (they sit behind "Comment BOOK"), and no cost, quality or conversion data is shown. I recorded the names and duties only.

[Certain] The LinkedIn acquisition sheet's numbers are decoration. Acceptance rates and lead counts change between animation frames. Automated connection requests and comment-to-DM bots can also breach LinkedIn's terms.

[Certain] The "50+ must-have AI tools" sheet is dated and internally inconsistent. The same sentence appears under several unrelated tools, and some names (Codey, Veed GPT, Replit Ghost Writer) have since been renamed. I kept names only.

[Likely] "How AI search finds your brand" is a simplified diagram, not a documented system. Treat its two tips (be crawlable, optimise for both SEO and AI retrieval) as sound basics, not as a way to guarantee citations.

6. Prompts in plain text

The graphics print almost no literal prompts, so these four were written by me from their content; brackets are your inputs.

Business review (written by me from the structure)
Build a 9-slide business review for [brand] for [period]. Use these facts only: [paste data]. Slide 1 scorecard table (this year, last year, plan, competitor, comment; write "needed" for unknown cells). Slide 2 SWOT. Slides 4 to 9 (marketplace, consumers, competitors, channels, brand, financials): exactly three numbered findings and one bold conclusion each. Slide 3 summarises the six conclusions and states one brand challenge. List the five data gaps that would change a conclusion.
Sales proposal (written by me from the 10 steps)
Draft a sales proposal for [buyer]. Title it with their desired outcome and date: [outcome]. Then in this order: recap how we got here, where they are now (problem, root cause, impact in their words: [paste notes]), where they want to go, the cost of doing nothing (show arithmetic), our solution mapped to their problems, a simple timeline, one case study [paste approved case], the investment in context with two or three options [paste prices], and one clear next step with a date. Do not invent numbers or quotes.
AI search (written by me from the two tips)
Audit [url] for AI retrieval: is the page crawlable and public (robots, noindex, login walls)? Are the brand name, product names and key claims stated consistently and in plain text? List five fixes ordered by effort, and say which affect Google SEO as well.
Excel data model (written by me from the steps)
I have these tables: [names and columns]. Propose the relationships (key column on each side, one-to-many direction), three DAX measures I will need, and the PivotTable layout. Flag any key that has duplicates or blanks.
