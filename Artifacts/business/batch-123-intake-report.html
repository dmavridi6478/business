1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] The sales-prep graphic is the only one of the three that prints usable prompts, and I quoted all ten verbatim. Its weakness is not the prompts but what they leave out: only prompt 2 separates verified facts from hypotheses, and prompt 8 will produce confident cost-of-inaction figures from thin context. I added a guardrail wrapper (prompt 11) and rules: label every claim with a source, never show a model's estimate to a customer as their cost, drafts only for anything sent or written to the CRM.
  • [Certain] The "107 strategy agents, zero consultants" graphic shows 21 skill names and one-line descriptions. It does not show the other 86, the five prompts per skill, or any evidence for the "$1,800,000 strategy study". I did not invent the author's prompts. The method cards in the new skill are original, built from the printed titles and descriptions. A search to verify the study claim failed on a rate limit, so it stays unverified.
  • [Certain] "Comment AGENTS" is a lead-magnet hook. If you hand over contact details for a pack, read each skill before installing and treat it as untrusted prompt text.
  • [Certain] The 60 AI tools graphic is a catalogue with no prices, data terms or evidence of growth. For a corporate reader the deciding question is data governance, which it never mentions. It also miscategorises: Cursor is a code editor, Clay is data enrichment, Browserbase is agent browser infrastructure, Poe is an aggregator of other models, Flux is a model.
  • [Likely] Some entries are stale. Limitless was reported acquired and its hardware wound down; "Motion" here is the scheduling app, not the creative-analytics connector in this account. Both are flagged for checking, not asserted.
  • [Certain] Duplicate check: the sales prompts overlap the plugin skills sales:account-research, sales:call-prep, sales:handle-objection and sales:deal-advance-gap, and the library skills perplexity-sales-research-10, claude-cowork-sales-prompts-10 and claude-sales-prompts-20. The new skill adds the ten as printed plus the guardrails. The 60-tool catalogue joins ai-tools-50-directory as a third reference; 60 tools from a different list were already there for builders. Strategy topics overlap mckinsey-frameworks-10 and product-strategy-session; the new skill is the flow that connects them.
  • [Certain] Connectors seen in this account for tools on the 60 list: Fathom, Fireflies, Canva, Figma, Notion, Zapier, n8n, Clay, Opus Clip and Adobe are connected; Granola needs sign-in.

3. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree with "let Claude do the prep" as a framing. The risk is that unsourced company facts and invented ROI numbers reach a customer in your name. Keep the model to drafting and require a source on every claim.
  • I disagree with "zero consultants". The risk is a board paper whose market size, margins and price elasticity are plausible fiction with no accountable owner. Keep a named human per skill and spot-check ten percent of figures.
  • I disagree with sixty tools as a corporate strategy. The risk is shadow adoption: confidential data in consumer plans with unknown training terms. Approve three tools per department and measure one pilot.
