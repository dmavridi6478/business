7. Prompts in plain text

Each prompt is ready to paste into any Claude session. Brackets mark what you supply. The matching skill or command is named under each title.

1. Role prompt for any named agent (skill roster-agents; command /roster-agent) 
Act as the [ROLE NAME] from the [LinkedIn-100 | GTM-200 | Lead-Gen-100 | Revenue-45] roster. Context: my business is [WHAT YOU SELL], my buyer is [ICP], my offer is [OFFER], my proof is [CASE STUDIES OR NUMBERS, or "none yet"]. Task: [WHAT YOU NEED]. Rules: use only facts I gave you; write [NEEDS PROOF] where a claim has no evidence; output a draft I can review in two minutes, not an essay; nothing is sent or posted by you. End with a list of what you assumed.
2. Build a list for a role (the Prosp 200 entry prompt, extended) 
Build me a list of the 10 specialist agent roles that a [ROLE, e.g. recruiter, coach, agency owner, B2B seller] would use on LinkedIn, grouped into 4 divisions. For each role give a one-line job, the input it needs, and the draft it produces. Do not invent tools or results.
3. GTM Ops Diagnostic (skill gtm-ops-diagnostic; command /gtm-diagnostic) 
Run the GTM Ops Diagnostic for [COMPANY]. Areas I run: [Pipeline Management / Inbound / Outbound / ABM / Partners / Renewals / Expansion]. For each area and each level (Fundamentals, Adoption, Optimization, Amplification) ask me for one line of evidence and score GREEN, YELLOW or RED; no evidence scores red. Then give me the heatmap as a table, the danger zones (optimising or amplifying on a weak base), the fix order with Fundamentals first, and a one-quarter roadmap with owner, metric and due date. List what you could not score.
4. First 100 customers (skill gtm-first-100-customers) 
I have [N] paying customers (not leads). I sell [PRODUCT] to [BUYER]. Using the four-stage first-100-customers roadmap (understand, 0-10 sell it yourself, 10-50 repeat what works, 50-100 scale what holds up), tell me which stage I am really in, what I must NOT do yet, my next two actions, the one metric that proves this stage is working, and the most likely way I am fooling myself. Ask me questions first if my answer would otherwise be a guess.
5. Sales call prep (skill ai-sales-prep-15min; command /call-prep) 
You are my sales-call prep analyst. Meeting: [GOAL, TYPE, ATTENDEES, DESIRED NEXT STEP]. Material below: [PASTE: company news, website text, attendee profiles, email thread, CRM notes, case studies]. Do six things in order and label each: (1) account snapshot: what changed, what they care about, 3 business priorities, 3 opening angles; (2) the 5 most likely pain points, strategic vs day-to-day, with symptoms and impact; (3) stakeholder map with priorities, likely influence and a messaging angle each; (4) a discovery plan: 10 questions, 3 hypotheses to test, 3 red flags; (5) objection talk tracks for price, timing, no-priority, competitor and "send me something"; (6) a final brief I can read in 2 minutes. Use only my material. Tag each claim [from input] or [hypothesis]. List anything I must verify before the call.
6. Interview answer rewrite (skill interview-10-donts; command /interview-reframe) 
I am interviewing for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Here is my planned answer: [PASTE]. Tell me which of these traps it matches: open to anything; knows little about the company; no weaknesses; asks about promotion; perfectionist; criticises a past boss; prefers to work alone; salary as the first question; time management as a weakness; no questions. Give the risk in one sentence, ask me for the true specifics you need, then rewrite it in my own voice using only those facts, and tell me how an interviewer might push back.
7. Idea to investor kit (skill startup-investor-kit; command /investor-kit) 
I want to build [IDEA IN ONE SENTENCE] for [BUYER]. First run a viability gate: score (0-2 each) named buyer, painful frequent problem with a current workaround, a way to reach 10 buyers this month without ads, a believable reason it beats the workaround, and workable unit economics. If the total is under 6/10, tell me why and stop. If it passes, research the market and competitors, then write: market_analysis, competitor_map, product_spec, business_model, product_roadmap, pitch_deck, investor_pitch and a one-file landing_page.html. Label every number [user], [sourced: URL] or [assumption]. Do not invent traction, customers or quotes.
8. Score the agent you shipped last (skill agent-eval-repos-7; command /agent-scorecard) 
Score my agent [NAME / PATH] from 0 to 3 on each line, citing evidence (file, trace or test): traces for every tool call; a golden set of 20+ cases; an automated hallucination or groundedness check; an adversarial or red-team pass; a production alert on a named bad behaviour; cost and latency tracking; weekly human review of a sample. No evidence scores 0. Give the total out of 21, my two weakest lines, and the smallest next step for each, naming which of opik, deepeval, phoenix, inspect_ai, giskard-oss, laminar or openllmetry fits and its install command.
