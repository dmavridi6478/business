What you should know first

The CEO KPI infographic has a wrong formula. It prints customer churn as (customers at end minus customers at start) divided by customers at start. That is net customer growth, not churn. The skill uses customers lost divided by customers at start and lists the other loose formulas.

Most of the repo cards are not plain open source. Hexabot, Directus and Screenpipe are source-available or commercial, seven are AGPL-3.0, and four are open core. I read each root licence file and recorded it. Read the licence before you self-host or resell.

wifit3 is a Wi-Fi attack toolkit. I registered it for reading only. It is lawful on networks you own or are authorised in writing to test, and unlawful otherwise. It is not in any install script.

Some source slides are incomplete or padded. The jek.notes cover promises 10 problems but shows 5. Card 12 of the "12 workflows" video repeats card 11. The last item of the Claude Checklist is hidden by a watermark. The 25-repo card gives titles only, so I matched 17 by title, marked them Likely, and left 8 unidentified.

I changed nothing outside this batch. The three chat agents are still not run live, and the price list and FAQ are still empty.
