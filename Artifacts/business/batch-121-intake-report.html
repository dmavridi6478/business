Batch 121 Intake Report

Four inline infographics processed with the seven-step intake. Result: three new skills, three commands, one reference, two templates, two themes, four workflows. No repositories, agents or connectors were referenced, so there is no install script this time.

1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] "Set up Claude for marketers" is a sensible order of work with unproven numbers. The graphic claims "12 roles replaced", a 4-hour setup, 100+ connectors and $20 a month. None is sourced. Its "skills" are saved prompt templates, not Claude's Skills feature (a SKILL.md package). I kept the four steps and added least-privilege rules: every connector gives an AI service access to company data, so start read-only on one folder and get approval for customer data.
  • [Certain] The TAM playbook is a vendor graphic. The six steps are sound and vendor-neutral, but the "70% unlimited lookups, 30% waterfall across 28+ providers" split describes the vendor's product. The graphic omits two real problems: your CRM only contains companies you already reached (survivorship bias in the ICP), and outreach to the DACH and UK regions it names is tightly regulated. I added a hand-check of 50 records per segment and a legal check per region. [Likely] B2B cold email is not consent-free in every country.
  • [Certain] "Where product marketing ends" is a clear ownership map from a PMM's point of view. It gives metrics only for PMM, so I added labelled suggestions for the other four and a rule to split by task, not title, in small companies.
  • [Certain] The five presentation frameworks are all standard (10-20-30 is Guy Kawasaki's investor-pitch rule; the Golden Circle is Simon Sinek's). Each has limits the graphic omits, and using all five in one deck reads as a template. I added when to choose which, and a rule to combine at most two.
  • [Certain] Duplicate check: the TAM topic overlaps build-and-fill-a-tam and /gtm-engineer-skills, so the new skill is a playbook that points to them. Golden Circle and STARR appear inside other skills, but there was no skill for choosing between the five.
  • [Certain] None of the four graphics contains a prompt, so all seven prompts in the prompts file are original.

3. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree with "12 roles replaced" as a headline. The risk is that a team treats a $20 tool as a headcount decision on an unmeasured claim. Measure time saved per task over two weeks first.
  • I disagree with building a target list from your CRM alone and sending cold email on a tiered score. The risk is a biased ICP and unlawful outreach in strict regions. Test one new segment, hand-check the data and get legal advice per region.
  • I disagree with connecting Claude to Drive, Gmail and the CRM in one step. The risk is giving an AI service broad access to company and customer data. Add one connector, read-only, after approval.

4. Not done, and why

  • No repositories, agents or connectors were referenced, so nothing was cloned or installed and there is no install script.
  • Two layouts were skipped rather than faked: the isometric staircase of the TAM graphic and the tall four-section setup guide both need hand-drawn art.
  • No n8n workflow: none of the four workflows is an automation. Ask if you want one.
