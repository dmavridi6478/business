What you should know first

[Certain] The Part 15 headline "Save $1,620/year" only holds if you replace all twelve tools. The twelve annual prices sum to $1,620, but the summary slide lists 8 swaps that sum to $1,434 under the same caption. This is now the fifth part of the series with this pattern (Parts 5, 10, 11, 15, 16).

[Certain] "744B on your laptop" hides the disk. The colibri engine is real (Apache-2.0, pure C), but streaming experts from disk means hundreds of gigabytes of fast SSD and, in my judgement [Likely], slow tokens per second. The slide's terminal and photos are staged graphics.

[Certain] Two licences are not what the slides imply. Open WebUI uses a custom licence with a branding clause, not MIT. Postal (the Mailgun replacement) had no root LICENSE file that my script could find, so its licence is unverified.

[Certain] Voice cloning and job-application automation need a human gate. The top-ten video promotes VoiceStudio (cloning) and ai-job-search (sends applications to real employers). I installed neither.

[Certain] The SalesDaily graphic asks you to paste three custom connector URLs. I did not add any: a custom connector gives that server a path into your session, and the URLs came from an image.

6. Prompts in plain text

Square brackets and sheet names are your inputs. The ten Excel prompts are as printed on the graphic; the four sales asks are examples from the SalesDaily graphic and need the connectors (not added).

01 Identify key sales trends
Analyze the sales data in columns A (Date), B (ProductCategory), and C (Revenue) from Sheet1. Identify and summarize the key sales trends observed over the last 12 months.
02 Suggest formula for a financial metric
I have annual revenue figures in cells B2:B10, representing revenue from Year 1 to Year 9. Suggest an Excel formula to calculate the Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) for this period and place it in cell C1.
03 Create a forecast chart from historical data
Based on the historical monthly expense data in columns A (Month) and B (Expenses) on 'ExpenseSheet', create a forecast chart projecting expenses for the next 6 months.
04 Highlight data anomalies or outliers
Review the 'Transaction_Log' sheet, specifically the 'Amount' column (D2:D500). Highlight any transactions that appear to be significant anomalies or outliers compared to the average transaction value.
05 Assist in building a financial model
Help me build a 3-year profitability projection model in a new sheet. I need input cells for: Year 1 Revenue, Annual Revenue Growth Rate (%), COGS as % of Revenue, and Fixed Operating Expenses. The model should output: Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Operating Expenses, and Net Profit for each of the 3 years.
06 Explain an existing Excel formula
Explain the formula currently in cell H25 on the 'ValuationModel' sheet. What does it calculate, and how do the different parts of the formula contribute to the result?
07 Identify data validation errors
Scan the active worksheet for any cells that currently violate data validation rules. List the cell addresses and the nature of the validation error for each.
08 Analyze a cash flow statement for issues
Analyze the data in the 'Monthly_CF_Statement' sheet, which contains columns for Operating Cash Flow, Investing Cash Flow, and Financing Cash Flow for the last 24 months. Identify any concerning trends or potential cash flow issues.
09 Assist with data reconciliation
I have an 'AR_Ledger' sheet with columns 'InvoiceID', 'Customer', 'Amount' and a 'Bank_Receipts' sheet with 'TransactionID', 'Payer', 'ReceivedAmount'. Help me identify invoices in 'AR_Ledger' that have a matching payment in 'Bank_Receipts' by comparing 'InvoiceID' with 'TransactionID' and 'Amount' with 'ReceivedAmount'. Highlight unmatched AR items.
10 Generate Python code in Excel for analysis
Using Python in Excel, analyze the customer churn data in table 'CustomerData'. Calculate the monthly churn rate and visualize it as a line chart.
