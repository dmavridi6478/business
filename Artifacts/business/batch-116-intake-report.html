1. Result

The archive is a mix of carousels and short-video stills from eight creators, not one series. Most of the 25 or so named projects were already in the repository, so only the new ones were added. Three skills, two commands, four design templates, four themes, a user-run script and two documents were created. There was no agent guide and no workflow in the files, so no agent or workflow was built, and no connector was installed.

The main finding: the strongest material is the security checklist (five ways a vibe-coded app gets hacked), not the repo lists. The weakest is the freelance pitch "use AI to undercut graphic designers", which has no evidence and is poor positioning. Creator promotions (Capacity, Omniscio, Slothy) sit inside several slides and were not assessed.

4. Critique of two pitches

  • Undercut the designers: competing on price attracts price-driven clients, blocks rate rises, and the same tools are available to the client. A narrow niche with a measurable outcome is the better route; the skill points to the positioning and first-100-customers skills.
  • "10x'd my website traffic": no baseline, timeframe or evidence. The five sites are a sensible free stack (timing, keywords, a channel, indexing, measurement), not a growth guarantee. The claim that Bing indexing helps ChatGPT search is the slide's and is unverified.
