Business Decision Framework

Free vs Paid Tool

When this applies Choosing between a free/open-source desktop tool and a paid SaaS subscription for a business function — notes, workspace, passwords, streaming, video editing, team chat, PDFs, or file transfer.
01
Count actual users, not just the founder

A solo founder or two-person team can usually run entirely on the free/local tool. Once a team needs shared real-time access, permissions, or admin controls across many people, the free tool's local-first design starts costing more in coordination overhead than the subscription would cost in cash.

02
Check for a hard sync/collaboration requirement

If the workflow needs real-time multi-user editing, cross-device sync without manual setup, or mobile apps with desktop parity, verify the free tool actually covers it. Some do — Joplin has sync options — but most don't have real-time co-editing. Don't assume feature parity with the paid tool it replaces.

03
Weigh data portability against support

Free/open-source tools generally win on data portability (local files, open formats, no vendor lock-in) and lose on support (no vendor SLA, community forums instead of a support line). For anything client-facing or revenue-critical, missing support is a real cost — weigh it explicitly, don't ignore it.

04
Flag security/compliance-sensitive data before defaulting to local

Password vaults and file-transfer tools keep data on your own machine by design — that's a feature for sensitive data, but confirm the maintenance burden is actually owned by someone: who backs up the vault file, who patches the app.

05
Re-run when the business's shape changes

A tool chosen correctly for a solo founder can become the wrong choice once a team forms, or once compliance or support requirements appear. Don't treat the initial choice as permanent — revisit it at each stage change, not only when something breaks.

06
Default for pre-revenue / solo stage

Favor the free tool from lean-software-stack unless step 2 or 3 above surfaces a concrete blocker. Cost control matters most before the business has revenue to validate spending against.

Decision shortcut

If the answer to "does this need real-time multi-user access?" is no, start free. If it's yes, price the paid option — the coordination tax on a local tool at team scale often exceeds the subscription cost within weeks.
