A zip of 33 images in three series (a ten-prompt due-diligence carousel, a Blotato nine-platform publishing tutorial, and a "room of billionaire advisors" series) processed with the seven-step intake. Result: three new skills, three commands, three templates, two themes, three workflows, seventeen prompts and a read-only install script. Nothing was installed or connected.

1. Executive findings

  • [Certain] "R.I.P. Big 4" is a screening aid, not a replacement. Its own last slide lists what AI cannot do: sign an audit opinion, interview management, see what is missing from the data room, take legal responsibility. The headline comparison ($50K and 2 to 4 weeks against one subscription and one afternoon) is unsourced; the slide calls it a "typical range". I quoted all ten prompts verbatim and added what the carousel leaves out: whether you may upload a seller's data room at all (NDA and data-room terms), arithmetic errors, missing documents, context limits, and the fact that "You are a Big 4 director" changes tone, not competence.
  • [Certain] The Blotato setup was checked against Blotato's own documentation. The connector URL is right and it is a remote Streamable HTTP server. The slide's "check that your plan includes MCP" understates it: MCP and API access need a paid Blotato subscription and are not in the free trial, and generating an API key during a trial ends the trial and starts billing. The docs also say a "Connected" badge alone does not prove a tool call works; verify by listing the connected accounts.
  • [Certain] The carousel omits what the connector can do beyond posting: per Blotato it can read comments and direct messages, send DMs and create reply automations. Connecting nine accounts gives a third party posting rights on all nine. Approval before scheduling is a rule you must state, not something the tool guarantees.
  • [Certain] The "billionaire advisors" series is one model playing seven parts. I disagree with the framing: the risk is false independence, and the output is not what those people think. The carousel's own fine print says "simulated perspectives, not the real people or their endorsement". The repository nateherkai/roast-room was read on GitHub: 1,271 stars, created 10 October 2026, one contributor, licence shown as "Other", needs Node 20 or newer, runs a local app on port 4730. The skill gives the three-round protocol with role lenses instead of named people.
  • [Likely] Step 8 of the advisor series was not in the zip, so the ten steps are covered from seven slides plus steps 9 and 10. The "If you want all resources, link in my community" slide is a lead-generation promotion and was not followed up.
  • [Certain] Duplicate check: nothing in the library mentioned Blotato, roast-room or the quality-of-earnings prompts. Neighbours exist and are linked: private-equity:dd-checklist, corporate-legal:diligence-issue-extraction, social-publishing, advisory-board, multi-agent-debate, marketing-council. The skills add the verbatim prompts, the verification against the vendor and repo, and the guardrails.
  • [Certain] Connectors: Blotato is a new connector and needs your own paid account and a browser sign-in, so it was not added. Postzen, Typefully, Taplio and SocialGPT were already visible in this account earlier in the session and cover part of the same ground. No .mcp.json was committed on purpose, so that opening this repository does not connect a third-party server by default.

3. Where I disagree with the source material

  • I disagree with "R.I.P. Big 4". The risk is a committee relying on a model's red-flag memo with unchecked arithmetic and no accountable signer, after uploading documents the NDA may not allow. Use it to shorten your advisers' first week and hand them the memo.
  • I disagree with "turn Claude into a room of billionaire advisors". The risk is treating one model's roleplay as seven independent minds, and attributing views to real people who never said them. Use role lenses and record the dissent.
  • I disagree with "post to nine platforms in one workflow" as a default. The risk is one mistaken schedule reaching nine audiences with a third party holding the keys. Start with one account, require approval of exact items, and keep DM automations off.

4. Not done, and why

  • Blotato was not connected and roast-room was not cloned or run. The script offers a read-only clone and prints the connector command; it runs neither without you.
  • No template for the advisor photo grid or the red "Step N" tutorial title: the first uses real people's photos and the second is covered by prompt-step-red-cream.
  • No separate n8n workflow: none of the three workflows is a scheduled automation.
  • Blotato's price and rate limit are the vendor's claims and were not independently checked.
