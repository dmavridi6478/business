1. The workflow

Nine steps from inspiration to a decision. The gates sit before any video is made.

  1. InspirationLatest discovered outliers
  2. Topic searchFaceless, monetised, under 50k subs
  3. TriageFlag AI-made and dramatised channels
  4. Breakout videosTopics and title patterns, not copy
  5. SaturationSimilar-channel count
  6. Revenue sanityUse the lower estimate
  7. OriginalityYour angle, script, footage, voice
  8. Policy and licenceMonetisation, synthetic content, licences
  9. Decide and saveSwipe file only after approval

A high outlier score is not a green light: it can come from a fabricated story or a tiny sample. The policy and originality gates come before the first video, not after.
