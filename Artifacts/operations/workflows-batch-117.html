1. Four-lever change check

Run with /mckinsey-framework (Influence Model) or prompt 8 in docs/batch-117-prompts.md. Source: the Influence Model graphic.

  1. Name the changeWho, what, by when
  2. Score 4 leversConviction, mechanisms, skills, role models; 1 to 5
  3. Ask the questionsFrontline explanation, KPI that rewards the old way, day-1 skill, informal leaders
  4. Find weakestOne lever
  5. Pick one moveOwner and date
  6. Re-scoreAfter 4 weeks

The "8 times more likely" figure on the graphic is the graphic's claim, not verified here. Use the levers as a checklist, not as proof.

2. Make to n8n migration

Run with /make-to-n8n. Source: the four Automated Digital cards.

  1. Export blueprintMake: three dots, Export blueprint
  2. Attach to ClaudeTarget is n8n; native nodes where they exist
  3. Mapping tableMake module, n8n node, gaps. Approve before any JSON
  4. Resolve gapsHTTP Request, Code node or redesign
  5. Import and reconnectImport from File; re-add credentials; new webhook URLs

3. Digital product in six prompts

Run with /digital-product-6. Source: the 6-prompts infographic.

  1. TransformationOne sentence: buyer, before, after
  2. Outline5 modules, 3 to 5 lessons each
  3. Cut and reorderRemove what does not serve the result
  4. Test with 5 buyersBefore drafting
  5. Draft modulesOne at a time
  6. Your voice and proofStories, numbers, screenshots
  7. Price and launchNo income claims

Do the transformation sentence yourself first, and spend most time on your voice and proof: the model drafts, you supply what makes it worth buying.

4. Strategy portfolio session

Run with /mckinsey-framework using Three Horizons and Portfolio of Initiatives. Source: the McKinsey graphics.

  1. List initiativesBudget, owner, expected payoff
  2. Tag horizonH1 core, H2 emerging, H3 options
  3. Plot on the mapDistinctive, shared or new knowledge
  4. Check balanceBig bets only where you have distinctive knowledge
  5. Set gatesEvidence that moves a bet up, or kills it
  6. Review quarterlyRe-tag and graduate

Fund all three horizons at once. A portfolio with big bets on unfamiliar ground is gambling, and options that never face a kill criterion quietly eat the budget.
