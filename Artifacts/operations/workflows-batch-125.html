3. Test a decision with an advisor room

Run with /advisor-room. Source: the room-of-advisors carousel and the roast-room repository.

  1. Write the briefDecision, options, numbers, limits, your lean
  2. Pick lensesRoles, not real people
  3. Round 1: independent opinionsNo peeking
  4. Round 2: challengeAssumptions, risks, trade-offs
  5. Round 3: reconsider and voteReason for any change
  6. Verify three factsBefore acting
  7. Decide and set a review dateRecord the dissent
