Prompt 01 · The audit

Paste your profile, then run this

"I am pasting my full profile below. Read it as my ideal client would. Why would they scroll past? What is missing? Do not rewrite yet. Just diagnose."
Prompt 02 · Ideal client

Make the model describe your buyer first

"Define my ideal client: title, company size, the problem, the words they search, the fear, the outcome they buy."
Do not skip this phase: every later rewrite is written for the client defined here.
