One line, three corners

Target audience, unique value, differentiation. If the line sounds like a CV, a stranger in your audience will not recognise it as theirs.

T

Target audience

The exact people you help and the problem they would pay to fix.

Weak "Business owners"
Strong "Founders who want LinkedIn bringing in leads"
U

Unique value

What they walk away with, in outcomes they recognise.

Weak "I help with personal branding"
Strong "Turn a founder's LinkedIn into steady inbound calls"
D

Differentiation

Why they pick you over someone making the same promise. Pick one and commit.

Four kinds of differentiator

  • A method
    a process others do not run
  • A view
    one you will defend
  • A proof point
    work nobody else can claim
  • A focus
    one niche, done properly
