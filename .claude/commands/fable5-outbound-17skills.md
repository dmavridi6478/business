# 17 Fable 5 Outbound Skills

Source: "17 Fable 5 outbound skills" infographic — "From prospect research to qualified pipeline in one outbound system." Each skill is one named, narrow capability; chain them in order for a full outbound motion.

## Phase 1 — Find and Prioritize Right Prospects (01–06)

01. **ICP Definition** — Target — defines the exact companies and buyers to target.
02. **ICP Research** — Research — builds a fact-based picture of the ideal customer.
03. **Account Research** — Context — finds context, priorities, and relevant company news.
04. **Buying Signals** — Intent — detects hiring, funding, and high-intent changes.
05. **Contact Enrichment** — Contacts — finds and verifies the right decision-makers.
06. **Lead Prioritization** — Score — ranks every prospect by fit, timing, and intent.

## Phase 2 — Write Outreach and Earn Replies (07–12)

07. **Personalization** — Angle — creates a relevant angle for every prospect.
08. **Cold Email** — Email — writes concise emails designed to start conversations.
09. **Subject-Line Testing** — Test — generates and compares high-clarity subject lines.
10. **LinkedIn Outreach** — LinkedIn — writes connection requests and direct messages.
11. **Follow-Up Sequences** — Follow-up — builds persistent follow-up without repetition.
12. **Reply Classification** — Replies — sorts interest, timing, objections, and referrals.

## Phase 3 — Qualify, Follow Up and Manage Pipeline (13–17)

13. **Lead Qualification** — Qualify — checks fit, need, urgency, and buying authority.
14. **Meeting Qualification** — Meetings — filters weak calls before they enter the calendar.
15. **Sales Pipeline** — Pipeline — tracks every lead, next step, and open opportunity.
16. **Weekly Report** — Report — summarizes outreach, replies, meetings, and gaps.
17. **Pipeline Optimization** — Shows what to improve in the next outbound cycle.

## Plain-text prompt to run the chain end to end

```
Run the 17 Fable 5 outbound skills on this target: [DESCRIBE ICP OR PASTE
A COMPANY LIST]. Phase 1: define/research the ICP, pull account context,
flag buying signals, enrich contacts, and rank by fit/timing/intent.
Phase 2: for the top 5 prospects, write a personalized cold email with 2
subject-line options, a LinkedIn connection note, and a 3-touch follow-up
sequence — then classify how you'd expect each reply type to sort.
Phase 3: define what "qualified" means for a lead and for a meeting here,
and sketch the weekly report format (outreach sent, replies, meetings,
gaps) I should track from week one.
```
