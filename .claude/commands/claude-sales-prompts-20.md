# 20 Claude Prompts for Sales

Source: SpeedChat.ai infographic, "Turn Claude into your sales thinking
partner."

Copy any prompt below as-is and fill in the bracket, or paste your own
notes/transcript/data in place of "this."

## Lead Qualification & Discovery

1. **Lead Qualification** — `Score this lead against our ICP and explain why.`
2. **Discovery Questions** — `Generate questions that uncover pain, urgency, and budget.`
3. **Pain Point Mining** — `Find the strongest business problems in these notes.`
4. **Account Research** — `Turn this company research into sales insights.`
5. **Buyer Persona** — `Identify likely buyers, priorities, and objections.`
6. **Objection Handling** — `Give 3 responses to this objection without sounding scripted.`

## Meeting Prep & Follow-Up

7. **Call Prep** — `Create a focused agenda and key talking points.`
8. **Call Summary** — `Turn these notes into actions, risks, and next steps.`
9. **Follow-Up Email** — `Write a concise follow-up tied to their priorities.`
10. **Personalization** — `Find 3 credible personalization angles for this prospect.`

## Positioning & Proposals

11. **Value Proposition** — `Turn our features into outcomes for this buyer.`
12. **Competitive Positioning** — `Compare us to this alternative by buyer priorities.`
13. **Proposal Review** — `Find gaps, risks, and unclear value in this proposal.`
14. **Deal Risk** — `Identify what could stall or kill this opportunity.`

## Deal & Pipeline Management

15. **Next-Step Planner** — `Suggest the strongest next step based on this stage.`
16. **Negotiation Prep** — `Map likely asks, trade-offs, and responses.`
17. **Pipeline Review** — `Find patterns and risks across these opportunities.`
18. **Win/Loss Analysis** — `Identify recurring reasons we win or lose.`
19. **Forecast Challenge** — `Stress-test this forecast and flag weak assumptions.`
20. **Sales Strategy** — `Build a practical plan to move this deal forward.`

---

This repo's existing `sales-enablement` skill and `sales.md` command already
cover pitch decks, one-pagers, and MEDDPICC/MEDDIC/BANT objection handling in
more depth — use these 20 as quick single-turn prompts inside a live deal
thread, and reach for `sales-enablement` when the ask is a full deliverable
(deck, battlecard, proposal).
