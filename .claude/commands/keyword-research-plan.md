description: Build a keyword research plan using the 7-step framework, 10 keyword types, 10 methods and 8 metrics, ending in a scored keyword-to-URL map
argument-hint: [business or site, audience, and goal]
---

Use the `seo` skill and its reference `references/keyword-research-framework.md`.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Write the offer, audience and goal in three lines (step 1). If missing, ask once.
2. Propose 10-20 seed keywords across the 10 keyword types (step 2).
3. Suggest which of the 10 research methods to use first for this business and why (step 3).
4. If Ahrefs or Semrush is connected, pull volume, difficulty and traffic potential for the seeds and say which tool the numbers came from. If not, label every number an estimate.
5. Score each keyword on relevance, traffic potential, difficulty, intent match and business value (0-2 each). State the cutoff first.
6. Output clusters and a keyword-to-URL map (new page, update or merge), then a monthly tracking list.

Draft only. Do not publish, edit the site or spend on tools.
