# 6 Claude Prompts to Fix Your CV and LinkedIn

Use these 6 prompts in sequence to position yourself strongly for any role: $ARGUMENTS

---

**PROMPT 1 — Build Career File**

Upload your CV, LinkedIn profile, 3 target job posts, 5 real wins, 3 projects, and 2 career goals. Then run:

> Act as my career positioning expert. Study these files and find the strongest version of my profile for [role]. Show me my best proof, weak points, unclear claims, and the exact story I should tell recruiters.

---

**PROMPT 2 — Create Role-Fit Map**

> Create a 4-column table: job requirement, proof from my background, missing proof, fix needed. Be strict. Do not praise me. Show what would make a recruiter pause.

*This shows what to improve before applying.*

---

**PROMPT 3 — Rewrite Weak CV**

> Rewrite each bullet using this format: action + skill + result + proof. Keep it short. Use numbers only if I gave them. If proof is missing, ask me one question before rewriting.

*This stops AI from inventing fake wins.*

---

**PROMPT 4 — Fix Keywords Without Sounding Robotic**

> Extract the most important keywords from the job post. Only use the ones I can prove. Show where each keyword should go: summary, skills, experience, or LinkedIn.

*Keywords help. Keyword stuffing hurts.*

---

**PROMPT 5 — Upgrade LinkedIn for Recruiters**

> Rewrite my LinkedIn headline, About section, and top 3 experience lines. Make it clear who I help, what problems I solve, and what proof I bring. Give 3 tones: clean, warm, bold.

---

**PROMPT 6 — Run Final Recruiter Test**

> Review my CV and LinkedIn like a busy recruiter. Tell me in 30 seconds what role I fit, why I look credible, and what still feels weak.

---

Usage: `/cv-linkedin-prompts` — run all 6 prompts in sequence with your career materials
