# 9 Claude Workflows for LinkedIn DMs

Source: "Claude Workflows Library" (LinkedIn Master Guide for Claude). Built after studying how the source booked 1,100+ calls from 17,000+ DMs sent (a 6.5% call rate) — same conversations, bigger opportunities: DMs go from cold intros to booked deals.

## The 9 workflows

1. **ICP Targeting Workflow** — define and filter for the exact companies/roles worth messaging.
2. **Connection Request Workflow** — the request note itself, written to earn an accept.
3. **Profile Research & Personalisation Workflow** — pull specifics from a prospect's profile/posts to make message #1 not generic.
4. **Opening DM Workflow** — the first message after connecting (the sample shown: reference their recent post, offer a quick idea, ask for a short chat).
5. **Follow-Up Sequence Workflow** — what to send if there's no reply, and when.
6. **Signal-Based Outreach Workflow** — trigger outreach off a real event (funding, hiring, a post) rather than cold-blasting a list.
7. **Objection Handling Workflow** — scripted responses to the predictable "not interested"/"send more info" replies.
8. **Qualification Framework Workflow** — decide, mid-conversation, whether this DM thread is worth a call.
9. **Call Booking Workflow** — convert a qualified conversation into a scheduled call.

## Plain-text prompt to build workflow #4 (Opening DM) as a worked example

```
Write my opening LinkedIn DM to [PROSPECT NAME], [THEIR TITLE] at [THEIR
COMPANY]. They recently posted about [TOPIC FROM THEIR PROFILE/POST].
Reference that post specifically, offer one relevant idea related to
[YOUR OFFER], and close by asking if they're open to a quick chat this
week. Keep it under 40 words, no pitch, no links.
```
