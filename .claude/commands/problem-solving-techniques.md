# 12 Problem-Solving Techniques

Apply these 12 frameworks to solve business and partnership problems: $ARGUMENTS

---

**1. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS**
Most problems fail because you solve the symptom, not the problem underneath.

**Action:** Ask why 5 times before you decide anything.

---

**2. FIRST PRINCIPLES THINKING**
Strip the growth strategy back to what's actually true. Stop copying what worked for someone else and rebuild from the ground up.

**Action:** Remove every assumption and start from what you know to be fact.

---

**3. REVERSE THINKING**
Start with the outcome you want to avoid and work backwards from there.

**Action:** List the 3 most likely failure points before you start anything.

---

**4. THE PRE-MORTEM**
Before you launch a program or partnership, assume it fails. Map the most likely reasons why and fix them first.

**Action:** Run a 10-minute pre-mortem with your team before every major launch.

---

**5. SCENARIO PLANNING**
Build 3 versions of what happens if your top traffic source disappears tomorrow. If you have no answer, that's your problem.

**Action:** Map best case, worst case, and most likely before committing to any single strategy.

---

**6. THE IMPACT VS. EFFORT MATRIX**
Not every partner, problem, or opportunity deserves equal energy. High impact, low effort moves first.

**Action:** Plot your current to-do list on a two-by-two grid and cut or delegate the bottom half.

---

**7. THE TRUST AUDIT**
Before you bring on any partner, ask 3 questions: real audience, real trust, real alignment. If the answer to any is no, move on.

**Action:** Run every potential partner through these 3 questions before you sign anything.

---

**8. THE LOYALTY CHECK**
When did you last reach out to your top partners with no agenda? If you cannot remember, you already have a retention problem.

**Action:** Schedule one no-agenda check-in with a top partner this week.

---

**9. THE STABILITY TEST**
If your top 3 partners stopped promoting tomorrow, what would happen to your revenue? If the answer is significant damage, you have a dependency, not a program.

**Action:** Identify your top 3 revenue dependencies and build a contingency for each.

---

**10. THE BOTTLENECK FINDER**
A lot of scaling problems are people and process problems. Find the one constraint slowing everything else down.

**Action:** Map your current workflow and identify the single step where everything slows or stops.

---

**11. THE RIGHT ROOM FILTER**
Not every opportunity or relationship is worth your time. Before you say yes, ask whether this environment moves you closer to the people and deals that matter.

**Action:** Before your next commitment, ask whether it gets you closer to your top 3 goals. If not, decline.

---

**12. THE RELATIONSHIP ROI REVIEW**
Every quarter, look at where your best opportunities, referrals, and revenue came from. Let the data tell you where to double down.

**Action:** Do a quarterly review of your top 10 relationships and where real value came from. Invest more there.

---
