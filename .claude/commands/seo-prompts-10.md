If no argument is given, display all 10 prompts and ask which to run.

## THE 10 PROMPTS

### 1. SEO Audit
```
You are an SEO expert. Conduct a comprehensive SEO audit of [website URL]. Analyse the following areas and provide specific, actionable recommendations: Technical SEO: Site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, indexing issues, Core Web Vitals, and HTTPS security. On-Page SEO: Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, keyword usage, internal linking, and content quality. Content Strategy: Identify content gaps, thin content, and opportunities for new, high-ranking content. Backlink Profile: Assess the quality and quantity of backlinks, identify toxic links, and suggest link-building opportunities. User Experience: Evaluate site navigation, design, and how it impacts SEO performance. Create a prioritised action plan with specific solutions for each issue found.
```

---

### 2. TASM — Total Addressable Search Market
```
What is the total addressable search market for [your niche]? Identify: (1) High volume, low-competition keywords (2) Long-tail opportunities often overlooked by competitors (3) Questions people are searching for related to [your topic] (4) Emerging trends or topics with growing search interest. Provide estimated monthly search volumes and difficulty scores where possible.
```

---

### 3. Search Intent Reverse Engineering
```
Analyse the search intent behind [keyword or topic]. Determine: (1) Is the intent informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional? (2) What does the user actually want to achieve? (3) What format of content best satisfies this intent? (4) What questions should the content answer to fully satisfy the searcher? Use this to outline the perfect piece of content.
```

---

### 4. Programmatic SEO Template Generator
```
Create a programmatic SEO template for [niche/industry] targeting [keyword pattern, e.g. "best [product] in [city]"]. Include: (1) A scalable page template structure (2) The key variables to swap out for each page (3) Unique value-add elements to avoid thin content penalties (4) Internal linking strategy between pages (5) Example of a completed page using the template.
```

---

### 5. Entity-Optimised Article Creator
```
Write an SEO-optimised article on [topic] that incorporates entity-based SEO. Include: (1) The primary entity and its attributes (2) Related entities and how they connect (3) Semantic keywords and LSI terms (4) Schema markup recommendations (5) A clear E-E-A-T signal strategy. The article should be [word count] words and target the keyword [primary keyword].
```

---

### 6. Topical Authority Builder
```
Create a topical authority content plan for [your niche]. Design a content cluster around [main topic] that includes: (1) A comprehensive pillar page outline (2) 10 supporting cluster content pieces (3) Internal linking structure between all pieces (4) Keyword targets for each piece (5) Publishing priority order. The goal is to establish [website] as the go-to resource for [topic].
```

---

### 7. SERP Competitor Teardown
```
Analyse the top 5 ranking pages for [target keyword]. For each competitor provide: (1) Content length and structure (2) Key topics and subtopics covered (3) Unique angles or data they use (4) Backlink profile highlights (5) Gaps or weaknesses in their content. Then tell me exactly how to create a page that outranks them.
```

---

### 8. BOFU Content Finder
```
Identify bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) content opportunities for [product/service/niche]. Find: (1) Comparison keywords (e.g. [brand] vs [competitor]) (2) Review and testimonial keywords (3) "Best [product category]" searches (4) Alternative and substitute searches (5) Price and deal-related searches. For each opportunity, suggest the ideal content format and angle to convert high-intent searchers.
```

---

### 9. LLM SEO — Optimise for AI Search
```
How do I optimise [website/content] to appear in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews? Provide a strategy that includes: (1) How to structure content for AI citation (2) What makes sources trustworthy to LLMs (3) How to target conversational and question-based queries (4) Schema and structured data recommendations (5) How to track and measure visibility in AI search results.
```

---

### 10. Untapped Demand
```
Find untapped keyword opportunities in [niche] that have: (1) Decent search volume (500+ monthly searches) (2) Low keyword difficulty (under 30) (3) High commercial or informational value (4) Limited competition from authoritative domains. Provide a list of 20 such keywords with estimated volume, difficulty, and a suggested content angle for each.
```
