# 9 Ways to Use AI to Learn Anything Faster

Use these 9 AI learning techniques to master any topic faster: $ARGUMENTS

---

**1. EXPLAIN LIKE I'M 5**
Reduce complexity by forcing plain-language explanation with familiar everyday terms.

> Explain [topic] like I'm 5 using a real-life analogy and 3 key takeaways.

---

**2. EXAMPLES & ANALOGIES**
Anchor new concepts to known domains to accelerate understanding.

> Give 3 analogies for [topic]: one work-related, one personal-life, one visual/metaphor.

---

**3. MOTIVATION & PURPOSE**
Sustain effort by clarifying why it matters and what 'good' looks like.

> Show why [topic] matters for [goal] and design a 7-day momentum plan.

---

**4. ROLE-PLAY PRACTICE**
Build skill through realistic simulation, feedback, and iterative reps.

> Act as [role]; run a scenario on [topic], then critique my response and suggest improvements.

---

**5. STUDY PLAN (MILESTONES)**
Convert a vague goal into a structured path with checkpoints and measurable outputs.

> Create a 2-week plan for [topic] with daily tasks, milestones, and a checkpoint quiz schedule.

---

**6. QUIZ & FEEDBACK LOOP**
Use retrieval practice to reveal gaps and strengthen long-term retention.

> Quiz me on [topic] (10 questions). After each answer, explain and give a follow-up mini-question.

---

**7. MIND MAP THE DOMAIN**
Organize concepts into a hierarchy to see relationships and dependencies.

> Create a mind map for [topic]: pillars → subtopics → key terms; note prerequisites.

---

**8. EXPERT ROUNDTABLE**
Compare perspectives to understand trade-offs, edge cases, and decision criteria.

> Simulate 3 experts debating [topic] (pragmatist, skeptic, specialist) and summarize consensus + risks.

---

**9. MENTAL ASSOCIATIONS (MNEMONICS)**
Encode key ideas with memory hooks to improve recall under time pressure.

> Create 5 mnemonics for [topic] and a 60-second recall drill I can repeat daily.

---

Usage: `/ai-learning-prompts [topic]` — pick the technique(s) that fit your learning goal
