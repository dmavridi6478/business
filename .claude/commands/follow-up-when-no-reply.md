# What to Write When Someone Doesn't Reply

Source: Mo Bunnell (Bunnell Idea Group), "What to Write When Someone
Doesn't Reply." A complete, ready-to-use follow-up system — reproduced in
full below with the exact message templates.

## The premise

Silence rarely means "no." Most non-replies aren't rejection — they're
timing, inbox overload, or a note that didn't quite land: it landed on a
busy day, they meant to reply and forgot, they're waiting on someone else,
or the last note didn't give them enough reason to respond.

## The cadence

A rough rhythm, not a rulebook — space notes out so every touch is worth
opening on its own, whether or not they ever reply:

| Day | Stage | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 3 | Light touch | Zero pressure — a gentle re-surface |
| Day 9 | Add value | Give something useful, don't just nudge |
| Day 16 | Easy yes or no | Make it effortless for them to answer either way |
| Day 30 | Open door | A warm exit that protects the relationship |

## The exact messages to send

| Stage | Message template | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Light touch | "No reply needed. Floating [thread] back up in case it slipped." | Zero pressure reads as respect, not chasing |
| 2. Add value | "Saw [resource] on [their topic] and thought of you." | A gift beats a nudge every time |
| 3. Easy yes or no | "Still worth exploring, or should I check back [later]?" | An easy no gets you the truth |
| 4. Open door | "I'll stop nudging, the door's open whenever you're ready." | A warm exit protects the relationship |

## Add value, don't just nudge

| Just nudging (avoid) | Adding value (do this instead) |
|---|---|
| "Just checking in again" | Share a useful article or idea |
| "Did you see my email?" | Offer a quick, specific next step |
| "Following up on this" | Send a short answer to their problem |

## Know when to pause

Not every non-reply deserves the same effort. Match how hard you follow up
to relationship value (low/high) and their past interest (lukewarm/keen):

| | Low relationship value | High relationship value |
|---|---|---|
| **Lukewarm past interest** | Let it rest — little interest, little upside | Change your approach — the relationship matters, but you haven't hooked them yet |
| **Keen past interest** | Keep it light — they liked you, but there's not much upside | Keep the rhythm going — worth your best effort |

## Plain-text prompt

```
Someone hasn't replied to [WHAT THEY HAVEN'T REPLIED TO]. It's been
[N] days. Their past interest was [lukewarm/keen] and this relationship
is [low/high] value to me. Using Mo Bunnell's 4-stage cadence (light
touch → add value → easy yes/no → open door), tell me which stage I'm
at, draft that exact message for this situation, and tell me whether I
should even keep following up given the relationship-value/interest
match.
```
