# Verified Research (PromptBuilder.cc)

Research $ARGUMENTS for me.

Rules:
- Use only sources you can name and link.
- Put every claim on its own line with the source and the date.
- Label each one as fact, estimate, or opinion.
- If you are not sure, write UNVERIFIED instead of filling the gap.
- If you cannot find a source, say so and stop.
- End with the three things you could not confirm.
- No summaries of your own confidence.

---
Usage: `/verified-research [your question]`
