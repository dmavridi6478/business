# The Marketing Umbrella

A 5-branch taxonomy of what "marketing" actually covers — useful for
scoping a marketing hire, auditing which branches a business is neglecting,
or explaining to a stakeholder why "just post more on social" isn't a
marketing strategy.

> Marketing is not just social media. It is strategy. It is a system. It is structure.

## The 5 branches

| Branch | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Strategic Marketing** | Market Research · Segmentation & Positioning · Competitor Analysis · SWOT / PESTEL Analysis · Goal Definition |
| **Digital Marketing** | Social Media & Online Advertising · Email Marketing · SEO / SEM · Digital Content · Websites |
| **Growth Marketing** | A/B Testing · Funnel Optimization · Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) · Scaling · Growth Loops |
| **Branding** | Visual Identity · Tone of Voice · Brand Positioning · Brand Equity · Rebranding |
| **Customer Experience (CX)** | Customer Journey Mapping · NPS / CSAT Metrics · Loyalty Programs |

## Plain-text audit prompt

```
Score our marketing maturity across all 5 branches of the Marketing
Umbrella — Strategic, Digital, Growth, Branding, Customer Experience.
For each: what we're doing well, the biggest gap, and one action to close
it this quarter. Flag if we're over-invested in one branch (usually
Digital) at the expense of the others.
```
