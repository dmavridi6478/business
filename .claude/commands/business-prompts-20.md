You are a business strategy assistant. These 20 prompt templates cover the core operational, marketing, and strategic needs of any business owner. Each prompt is fill-in-the-bracket — replace the bracketed sections with your specifics and run.

If no argument is given, display the full list and ask which to run.

## THE 20 PROMPTS

### 1. Content Calendar Development
```
Create a comprehensive content calendar for our [product/service] targeting [specific audience] for the next [timeframe, e.g., quarter, year]. Include post types (blog posts, social media, email, newsletters), themes (seasonal, industry trends), and optimal posting times based on audience engagement patterns. Ensure the calendar aligns with our brand voice and messaging strategy.
```

---

### 2. Press Release for Product Launch
```
Generate a press release for the launch of our new [product/service], highlighting [key features, e.g., innovative technology, unique design] and [unique selling points, e.g., cost-effectiveness, sustainability]. Keep the press release concise, under 500 words, with a professional tone. Include a quote from our CEO or relevant executive and provide contact information for media inquiries.
```

---

### 3. Email Sequence for Customer Engagement
```
Design an email sequence for [customer segment, e.g., new subscribers, inactive users] who [specific action/behavior, e.g., signed up but haven't made a purchase]. The sequence should include subject lines and CTAs that will increase [desired outcome, e.g., conversion rates, engagement]. Tailor the content to address [specific pain points] and offer [relevant incentives, e.g., discounts, free trials].
```

---

### 4. Social Media Copy for Product Promotion
```
Write social media copy for [platform, e.g., Facebook, Instagram] promoting our [product/service] that addresses [pain point, e.g., time management, budget constraints] for [target audience, e.g., young professionals, families]. Include relevant hashtags and tag any influencers or partners involved. Ensure the tone is engaging and aligns with our brand's voice.
```

---

### 5. Competitor Analysis Framework
```
Develop a competitor analysis framework for the [industry] focusing on [specific aspects, e.g., pricing strategy, marketing channels]. The framework should help us identify market gaps and opportunities by comparing [key metrics, e.g., market share, customer satisfaction]. Include a template for data collection and analysis.
```

---

### 6. Handling Customer Objections
```
Create a script for handling [common customer objection, e.g., "too expensive"] about [our product/service] that emphasizes [key benefit, e.g., long-term savings, quality] and addresses [specific concern, e.g., budget constraints]. Ensure the script is empathetic and provides solutions or alternatives that meet the customer's needs.
```

---

### 7. Follow-Up Email Sequence for Leads
```
Design a follow-up email sequence for leads who [specific action, e.g., downloaded a guide but haven't converted]. Focus on [value proposition, e.g., exclusive offers, expert insights] and include clear CTAs that encourage them to take the next step. Tailor the content to address [specific pain points] and offer [relevant incentives].
```

---

### 8. Customer Satisfaction Survey
```
Generate a customer satisfaction survey for our [product/service] that measures [specific metrics, e.g., overall satisfaction, likelihood to recommend]. Include questions about [key aspects, e.g., product quality, customer support]. Ensure the survey is concise and easy to complete, with options for open-ended feedback.
```

---

### 9. Customer Support Inquiry Responses
```
Craft responses to these common customer support inquiries for [product/service]: [list 3–5 common questions, e.g., "How do I reset my password? What is your return policy?"]. Ensure the responses are clear, concise, and provide direct solutions or point to relevant resources.
```

---

### 10. Sales Pitch Template
```
Create a sales pitch template for our [product/service] that highlights [key benefits, e.g., efficiency, cost savings] and addresses [common pain points, e.g., complexity, integration challenges] for our [target audience]. Include a section for customizing the pitch based on the prospect's specific needs and interests.
```

---

### 11. SWOT Analysis Template
```
Develop a SWOT analysis template for our [business/product/role] that emphasizes [area] with a specific focus on [particular aspect, e.g., market trends, internal capabilities]. The template should help identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats by including sections for data collection and analysis.
```

---

### 12. Onboarding Checklist for New Employees
```
Create an onboarding checklist for new employees [specific role] that helps them understand [key processes, e.g., company policies, software tools] and company culture within [timeframe, e.g., first week, month]. Include tasks for HR, supervisors, and the new employee to complete during the onboarding process.
```

---

### 13. Project Management Framework
```
Design a project management framework for [specific type of project, e.g., software development, marketing campaign] that includes milestones, deliverables, and risk assessment methods. Ensure the framework is adaptable to different project sizes and complexities.
```

---

### 14. Meeting Agenda Template
```
Generate a meeting agenda template for [meeting type, e.g., team meeting, client meeting] that maximizes productivity and ensures all key topics are covered within [timeframe]. Include sections for introduction, main discussion points, action items, and next steps.
```

---

### 15. Business Continuity Plan Outline
```
Create a business continuity plan outline for [potential disruption scenario, e.g., natural disaster, supply chain disruption] that prioritizes [critical functions, e.g., customer service, production] and minimizes impact on [key operations]. Include strategies for communication, resource allocation, and recovery.
```

---

### 16. Financial Forecast Template
```
Develop a financial forecast template for our [business type] that projects [key metrics, e.g., revenue, expenses, cash flow] over the next [timeframe, e.g., quarter, year] based on [assumptions, e.g., market growth, inflation]. Ensure the template allows for adjustments to assumptions and provides scenarios for different economic conditions.
```

---

### 17. Pricing Strategy Analysis
```
Create a pricing strategy analysis for our [product/service] considering [market factors, e.g., competition, demand elasticity], [competitor pricing], and [customer willingness to pay]. Include recommendations for pricing tiers, discounts, and promotions to optimize revenue and profitability.
```

---

### 18. Fundraising Pitch Outline
```
Generate a fundraising pitch outline for our [business/startup] highlighting [unique value proposition], [market opportunity], and [growth potential]. Include sections for introduction, product demonstration, financial projections, and investment ask.
```

---

### 19. Cost-Cutting Framework
```
Design a cost-cutting framework that identifies inefficiencies in [department/process] without compromising [quality/service standards]. Include methods for analyzing current expenses, potential cuts, and implementing cost-saving measures while maintaining operational effectiveness.
```

---

### 20. Market Expansion Strategy
```
Create an expansion strategy for entering the [new market/location] with our [product/service], addressing [potential challenges, e.g., regulatory hurdles, cultural differences] and leveraging [competitive advantages, e.g., brand recognition, technology]. Include a timeline for market entry and key performance indicators for success.
```
