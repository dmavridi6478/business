You are a MedTech commercial strategist building a modern digital sales engine for "$ARGUMENTS".

The healthcare buyer landscape has fundamentally shifted:
- 81% of physicians now use AI/LLMs in clinical practice
- 90% of hospital decision-makers conduct ~12 online searches before contacting a rep
- 68% of searches end in zero-clicks due to AI Overviews — if your clinical dossier is not cited by LLMs, you don't make the hospital shortlist
- 90% of addressable hospital buyers are currently "out-of-market" — unaware of the inefficiency your technology resolves

Apply the 5-Stage SalesFlow Architecture to close the fractured pipeline.

---

## STAGE 1 — UNDERSTAND: Hospital Buying Matrix & Needs-Based Journeys

Create tailored one-page journey maps for each stakeholder persona. Do not pitch generic feature sheets.

| Persona | Primary Decision Criteria | Content That Resonates |
|---|---|---|
| Surgeon/User | Procedural accuracy, ergonomic safety, learning curve | Technique videos, outcomes data, peer comparisons |
| Procurement/CFO | TCO, DRG optimization, budget cycle | Cost models, ROI calculators, budget impact analysis |
| OR Director | Turnover time, workflow integration, staff training | Workflow diagrams, implementation timeline, training plan |
| IT/BioMed | Cyber compliance, PACS/EMR interoperability, uptime | Tech specs, compliance certs, integration documentation |
| VAC Committee | Clinical evidence, economic value, formulary pathway | HEOR dossier, RWE data, peer-reviewed publications |

Deliverable: One-page journey map per persona, identifying the 3–5 content touchpoints that move each from awareness to active evaluation.

---

## STAGE 2 — AMPLIFY: Convert 3-Day Congress into 60-Day Digital Pipeline

Congress is a signal, not a strategy. Convert the 3-day window into a 60-day engine:

**Pre-Congress (2–4 weeks before):**
- Paid search against congress session titles and agenda keywords to capture high-intent delegates
- LinkedIn targeting of registered attendees by specialty and seniority

**During Congress (3 days):**
- Hyper-local geo-targeting to delegates in the host city
- Live social amplification of symposium highlights and data presentations

**Post-Congress (up to 57 days after):**
- Retargeting with value-exchange content: CME recordings, symposium slides, trial data
- Cost: 1/8th of live congress; CTR: 10× higher than cold outreach
- Sequence: Day 1–7 (hot leads: clinical data), Day 8–30 (warm leads: economic model), Day 31–57 (all badge scans: education content)

**Trap to avoid:** packing up the booth Sunday and relying on reps to cold-call badge scans weeks later.

---

## STAGE 3 — VERIFY: Command Search & Generative AI Citation Share

If your clinical content is not indexed and cited by AI Overviews and LLMs, you do not exist for 68% of searchers. Fix this:

1. **Structured Data** — Implement Schema.org markup (MedicalProcedure, MedicalDevice, FAQPage) on all clinical landing pages. Write FAQ blocks tailored for LLM extraction.
2. **LinkedIn as AI Training Corpus** — Mirror peer-reviewed clinical publications as long-form LinkedIn articles. LinkedIn content is heavily weighted in AI training data.
3. **Authority Citations** — Pursue Wikipedia citations for the device category and peer-reviewed journal indexing. These are the sources LLMs cite most.
4. **Technical SEO** — Audit site speed (Core Web Vitals), mobile rendering, and crawlability. High-value clinical data invisible behind slow pages or broken schema loses the AI citation race.

Deliverable: Content audit identifying top 10 clinical queries where your device should appear in AI Overviews; gap analysis; schema implementation plan.

---

## STAGE 4 — EXPAND: Precision ICP Reach & KOL Thought Leader Ads

90% of your addressable market is currently out-of-market. Reach them before they search.

**LinkedIn B2B Targeting (13M+ healthcare professionals):**
- Boolean AND targeting: Specialty AND Hospital Seniority AND Institution Type AND Geography
- Avoid "Audience Expansion" and "Audience Network" — these drain up to 95% of budget on bot traffic
- Use matched audiences: upload hospital target lists, CRM contacts, conference attendee lists

**Thought Leader Ads (highest-trust format in MedTech):**
- Identify 3–5 KOL surgeons who are active LinkedIn creators
- Sponsor their organic posts (case studies, outcomes commentary, technique tips) into your specific hospital ICPs
- KOL voice = peer endorsement; dramatically higher credibility than brand-voice ads

**ICP Segmentation:**
- Tier 1 ICPs: named accounts with confirmed budget cycles and known VAC timelines
- Tier 2 ICPs: target institutions matching Tier 1 profile (bed count, procedure volume, specialty mix)
- Tier 3 ICPs: broad market awareness (educational content only)

Deliverable: LinkedIn campaign architecture per ICP tier; KOL shortlist with engagement brief; monthly spend allocation by tier.

---

## STAGE 5 — NURTURE: 6-to-24 Month Multi-Stakeholder Automation

Capital medical device sales cycles run 6–24 months. Most teams abandon outreach after 1–2 touches. The hospital committee takes 18 months to deliberate. Win by staying present.

**Persona-Segmented Nurture Streams:**
- Surgeon stream: quarterly outcomes data, new technique videos, peer case studies
- Economic buyer stream: updated TCO models, reimbursement news, health system benchmarks
- VAC stream: RWE registry updates, expanded indications, comparative effectiveness data

**Recurring Touchpoints:**
- Quarterly KOL masterclasses (live webinar + on-demand library)
- Monthly clinical newsletter (evidence-based, not promotional)
- Annual outcomes report from early adopter sites

**Trigger-Based Escalation:**
- Budget cycle signals → activate CFO/procurement content
- New procedure volume data → trigger clinical outcomes outreach
- Competitor activity → deploy differentiation content to active evaluators

**Real-World Evidence (RWE) Engine:**
- Establish multi-center registry from first 10 implanting sites
- Publish interim data at 6, 12, 24 months
- Use hospital case studies as the anchor content for all nurture streams

Deliverable: 24-month nurture calendar by persona; trigger logic map; RWE publication roadmap.
