# LinkedIn Profile Audit

Upload your LinkedIn PDF (Profile → More → Save to PDF on desktop) and run this prompt:

---

Act as a LinkedIn strategist. Audit this profile. Find what's stopping people from hiring, following, or buying from this person. Identify gaps in: Headline, About, Experience, Skills. Ask clarifying questions about my goals. Then optimize each section using:
- XYZ formula for Experience (I accomplished X by doing Y, resulting in Z)
- 3 bullet points max per role
- ATS-friendly keywords.
