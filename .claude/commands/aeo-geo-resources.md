## Prompts — copy and paste

### Brand AEO audit prompt
```
Audit my brand's current AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) posture. Tell me:
1. What types of questions would AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Mode) answer about my category?
2. What would make my brand more likely to be cited in those answers?
3. What content gaps am I likely missing?
Brand/product: [describe your brand, product, and target audience]
```

### Content-for-AI-citation prompt
```
Rewrite this content so it is more likely to be cited by AI answer engines. Optimise for: clear factual claims, concise definitions, structured data, direct answers to specific questions. Original content: [paste your content]
```

My product is [describe]. What would I need to do to appear in ChatGPT Shopping results? List the technical requirements, content requirements, and the 3 highest-leverage actions I should take first.
```

Compare AEO and traditional SEO strategy for my business. Where do they overlap? Where do they conflict? Given that I have limited resources, in what order should I prioritise them? My business: [describe]
```
