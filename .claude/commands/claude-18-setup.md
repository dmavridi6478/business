# 18 Things to Set Up in Your Claude

Source: @waelmando.ai

---

## THE FOUNDATION (without it, the rest falls apart)

**1 – Projects**
One for each area of your life: work, studies, freelance. Loose chats are lost knowledge.

**2 – Projects Instructions**
Tell it who you are, what you do, how you want answers. Write it once, it lasts forever.

**3 – Personal Preferences**
The global tone in settings: direct, no fluff, with examples. All of Claude follows it.

---

## MAKE IT REMEMBER YOU

**4 – Memory Enabled**
It remembers context between conversations. Stop introducing yourself every time.

**5 – Search Past Chats**
"What did we decide about that dashboard?" — it just knows. Your account becomes a searchable archive.

**6 – Personal Preferences (Voice)**
Paste examples of your own writing and it starts answering in YOUR voice.

---

## TURN ON THE SUPERPOWERS (they come switched OFF)

**7 – Web Search**
Without it, it answers what it knows. With it, it researches what it doesn't.

**8 – Code Execution**
What separates "AI that opines" from "AI that calculates" — it runs real code on your spreadsheets.

**9 – Personal Preferences (Artifacts)**
Dashboards, apps, and documents built right alongside the conversation.

---

## GET IT OUT OF THE BROWSER — PUT IT IN YOUR LIFE

**10 – Desktop App**
A global shortcut: Claude opens on top of any window.

**11 – Mobile App + Widget**
The coffee line is where the best questions show up.

**12 – Voice Mode**
Speak instead of typing. Catch the idea before it slips away.

---

## CONNECT IT TO WHAT YOU ALREADY USE

**13 – Connectors**
Google Drive, calendar, email — it works with your files without you attaching a thing.

**14 – Claude For Excel**
The official add-in puts it INSIDE the spreadsheet, editing cells right in front of you.

**15 – API Key**
The hard-mode level: Claude talking to other tools like Power BI, writing its own analysis.

---

## THE FINAL 3 THAT SEPARATE A USER FROM A PROFESSIONAL

**16 – The Right Model For Each Task**
The fast one for daily work, the heavy one for hard problems. Using the same one every time is driving in first gear.

**17 – Reusable Instructions**
The prompt you use every week becomes a permanent instruction. Never rewrite it again.

**18 – The Brain Project**
One project with EVERYTHING about your work. Becomes a second brain that knows you.
