# Live Research Prompts (@earchoe)

Use these prompts with Claude web search ON for real-time research: $ARGUMENTS

## Prompts (copy and use with web search enabled)

**1. COMPETITOR RESEARCH:**
Search for [competitor name] and give me their current pricing, services, positioning, and any recent changes in 2026. What are customers saying about them?

**2. MARKET RESEARCH:**
What is the current state of [your niche] market in the UK in 2026? Search for recent stats, trends, and growth areas.

**3. TOOL COMPARISON:**
Search for and compare [Tool A] vs [Tool B] as of 2026. What are the latest pricing, features, and user reviews?

**4. NEWS TO CONTENT:**
Search for the 3 most recent significant developments in [niche] this week. Summarise each and suggest a TikTok angle for each one.
