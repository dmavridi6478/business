# Granola MCP — Meeting Prompts

Source: 51ultron.com

6 Claude prompts designed for use with the Granola MCP server.
Granola records and transcribes meetings; these prompts pull from that data inside Claude.

**Prerequisite:** Granola MCP must be installed and connected.
Setup: https://granola.ai → Settings → MCP

---

## PROMPT 1 — Open Action Items

Pull open tasks from recent meetings.

```
Pull my last 5 meetings from Granola + list every open action item, grouped by owner.
```

**Use when:** Monday morning or before a team sync — get a full picture of what's outstanding and who owns it.

---

## PROMPT 2 — Walk In Prepared

Brief yourself before a call using past notes.

```
Before my next call, summarize what we've covered from my past Granola notes.
```

**Use when:** Jumping on a call with someone you've met before. Surfaces relevant context without digging through notes manually.

---

## PROMPT 3 — Draft the Follow-Up

Turn meeting notes into a follow-up email.

```
Read my Granola notes from today's call + draft a follow-up in my voice with the next step.
```

**Use when:** Immediately after a call. Keeps momentum without writing from scratch.

---

## PROMPT 4 — What Got Decided

Extract decisions and open questions from a project.

```
From my Granola notes on this project, list every decision + what's still open.
```

**Use when:** A project has multiple meetings and you've lost track of what's settled vs. still debated.

---

## PROMPT 5 — Patterns Across Calls

Find recurring themes in sales or client conversations.

```
Analyze my last 10 sales calls in Granola + surface the top 3 objections I keep hearing.
```

**Use when:** Reviewing sales performance or preparing for a pitch. Replaces manual note review.

---

## PROMPT 6 — Call to Deliverable

Convert discovery notes into a proposal outline.

```
Turn my Granola notes from the discovery call into a first-draft proposal outline.
```

**Use when:** After a discovery call. Skips the blank-page problem.
