You are a high-ticket sales strategist. Run all 10 agents for "$ARGUMENTS":

**Agent 01 — Discovery Call Preparation**
Build a 45-minute discovery call framework:
- Pre-call research checklist (5 items)
- Opening (first 3 minutes, puts them at ease)
- Diagnostic questions (10 questions, ranked by insight value)
- Transition to offer (exact words)
- Close (how to ask for the decision without pressure)

**Agent 02 — Irresistible Offer Architecture**
Design the core offer:
- Primary outcome (what transformation do they buy?)
- Key deliverables (max 5, named memorably)
- Risk reversal / guarantee (specific, not generic)
- Bonuses (2–3 that amplify the core promise)
- Price anchoring narrative (justify investment before stating price)

**Agent 03 — Proposal Writing**
Write a client-specific proposal template:
- Their Situation (as you understood it)
- The Cost of Inaction (quantified)
- Our Approach (3–5 steps, outcome for each)
- Evidence (1 case study headline + result)
- Investment options (2 tiers)
- Next Steps (clear, dated, one action)

**Agent 04 — Objection Neutralisation**
List the top 7 objections for this offer. For each write: the hidden fear behind it, a reframe, and the response script (max 3 sentences).

**Agent 05 — Value Ladder Development**
Map a 4-tier value ladder:
- Free: what, purpose, how it feeds the next tier
- Low-ticket (£50–£500): what, who it's for
- Mid-ticket (£1k–£5k): what, who it's for
- High-ticket (£5k+): what, who it's for

**Agent 06 — High-Ticket Closing**
Script a non-pushy closing sequence for discovery calls. Include: the transition phrase, how to present the offer, how to handle silence (3 seconds, 10 seconds, 30 seconds), and the "next step" close.

**Agent 07 — Pricing Structure**
Recommend 3 pricing models: retainer, project-based, performance-based. For each: monthly/total fee range, payment structure, ideal client profile, and one-line sales pitch for that model.

**Agent 08 — Engagement Letter**
Draft a 1-page engagement letter template covering: scope summary, deliverables list, timeline, fees & payment terms, IP ownership, confidentiality, and how to proceed.

**Agent 09 — Follow-Up Sequence**
Write a 5-touchpoint follow-up for prospects who expressed interest but didn't commit. Touchpoints at Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30. Each message: medium (email/DM), subject line, body (max 4 sentences), CTA.

**Agent 10 — No-Show Recovery**
Write 3 escalating recovery messages for missed discovery calls: (1) same-day empathetic reschedule, (2) day 3 value-add check-in, (3) day 10 graceful close that keeps the door open.

Deliver each agent clearly labelled. All scripts ready to copy-paste with [BRACKETS] for personalisation.
