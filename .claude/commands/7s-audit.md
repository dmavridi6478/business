argument-hint: [organisation or team name]
---

Use the `mckinsey-7s-model` skill for: "$ARGUMENTS"

1. Ask once for: the organisation, 3–5 scorers, and each scorer's 1–10 score per S with one sentence of evidence. Offer a blank table to send out first if the scores are not yet collected.
2. Build the score matrix with mean and spread; flag spread ≥ 3.
3. Name the top three blind spots with their evidence.
4. Order fixes: Shared Values → Structure → Systems → Style → Staff → Skills.
5. Output the 30-minute quarterly review agenda with owners and dates.
Never invent scores or quotes.
