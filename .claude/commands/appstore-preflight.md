---
description: Pre-submission check of a web-based app against common App Store rejection reasons
argument-hint: <app description or repo path>
---

Use skill `app-store-toolchain-5`. For "$ARGUMENTS" check: is it a thin web wrapper (add real native value), Sign in with Apple if other social logins exist, in-app account deletion, privacy labels and policy URL, paywall and restore-purchase flow, test account for reviewers, screenshots, and permission strings. Give pass, fail or unknown per item with the evidence, then the three fixes that matter most. Do not submit, change prices or touch certificates.
