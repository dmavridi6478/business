Use the skills `avoid-ai-writing` and `humanizer` (step 07). Draft: "$ARGUMENTS".

1. Read both skills and apply them to the draft. Cut stock openers and closers, empty hedges, forced three-item lists, and vague intensifiers; prefer concrete nouns, specific numbers and plain verbs.
2. Never change a fact, name, number or claim. If the draft makes a claim it cannot support, flag it instead of smoothing it over.
3. Output the cleaned text, then a short list of the patterns removed.
