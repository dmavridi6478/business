# Brand Health Framework

Source: Gartner infographic (© 2026 Gartner, Inc.). A 6-stage funnel for
measuring brand health end-to-end, from strategic differentiation through to
business outcomes.

| Stage | Metrics |
|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Differentiators are unique and valuable |
| **Reach** | Aided awareness · Unaided awareness |
| **Perception** | Brand equity and personality perceptions |
| **Consideration** | Purchase intent |
| **Experience** | Brand experience satisfaction |
| **Business outcomes** | Revenue · Profit · Customer lifetime value |

## How to use it

Each stage needs its own measurement, and a weak result at any stage caps
what's achievable downstream — high awareness with poor perception won't
convert to consideration, and strong consideration with a bad experience
won't reach retained revenue. Diagnose bottlenecks stage by stage rather
than optimizing one metric (usually Reach) in isolation.

```
Score our brand across Gartner's 6-stage brand health framework (Strategy,
Reach, Perception, Consideration, Experience, Business Outcomes) using
[whatever data you have: surveys, NPS, funnel conversion, revenue]. For each
stage: current signal, biggest gap, and the one action most likely to move
it this quarter.
```

---

This is a distinct framework from this repo's own `brand-guidelines`
(Anthropic's visual brand colors/fonts — not a health-measurement
framework) and `brand-brain` skill (check `brand-brain` for overlap before
running both on the same brand review). Cross-reference: `atm-framework`
(Audience Growth → Trust & Engagement → Monetization) is a narrower,
single-track version of the same reach → perception → outcome idea; this
Gartner framework is the fuller 6-stage measurement model to reach for when
the ask is specifically "how healthy is our brand," not "how do we grow
audience."
