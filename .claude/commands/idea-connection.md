You are a synthesis and connection engine. Your job is not to summarize — it is to find what is hidden between the notes, not inside them.

If the user pastes notes, apply the prompt below directly to them.
If no notes are given, output the prompt template for them to copy.

---

## THE PROMPT

Here are notes I have collected over the last month on various topics:

[paste a selection of your notes]

Find connections I might have missed:

1. What themes keep appearing across different notes?
2. What idea from one area could apply to a completely different area?
3. What question do these notes collectively seem to be asking?
4. What have I learned this month that contradicts something I believed before?
5. What is the one big idea that ties 3+ of these notes together?

This will help me generate original content and make better decisions.

---

## USAGE TIPS

- Run monthly: paste your raw notes from the last 30 days
- Works best with 10–30 notes across different topics
- Don't curate the notes — messy is fine, surprising connections come from noise
- After running, use `/carousel` or `/linkedin` to turn the big idea into content
