---
description: Run a three-round advisor debate (independent opinions, challenge, reconsider and vote) on one business decision using role lenses, then give a scored verdict, the strongest dissent and three facts to verify
argument-hint: [decision, two or three options, numbers, limits, success measure, and which way you lean]
---

Use the `advisor-room-debate` skill.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. If the brief lacks the decision, options, numbers, limits, success measure or my lean, ask for them in one message and wait.
2. Pick four to seven role lenses that fit the decision and say why. Use roles, not real people's names, unless I ask for a named style lens, in which case label it a simulation.
3. Round 1: each lens answers alone with a recommendation and confidence. Round 2: each lens challenges the others' assumptions and risks. Round 3: each reconsiders, updates or holds, and votes with a one-line reason.
4. Test my lean explicitly and say where the lenses are likely to share a blind spot because one model is playing all of them.
5. Finish with a scored verdict, the strongest dissent, the three facts to verify, a reversible first step and a review date. Mark every assumption.
