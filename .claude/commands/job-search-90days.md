You are a senior executive career strategist. Run all 5 steps of the 90-day plan for "$ARGUMENTS":

---

## STEP 1 — Target the Right Roles (Days 1–14)
*Why this matters: Senior searches collapse without a focused list. Best-fit roles close faster and pay better.*

**Deliver:**
1. Build a list of 30 target companies (criteria: size, industry, culture fit, growth stage). Use the role title and industry provided.
2. Identify 10 specific roles to track on LinkedIn/company sites.
3. Red-line criteria: what 3 things would make you say no? (Helps cut noise in Week 1.)
4. Week 1 priority action: what is the single most important thing to do before Day 7?

**Outcome**: A focused target list of senior-fit roles and companies.

---

## STEP 2 — Position Yourself to Be Found (Days 15–28)
*Why this matters: Recruiters search ATS and LinkedIn. Resume keywords and LinkedIn presence decide who they find. Senior candidates lose roles they could have won here.*

**Deliver:**
1. Extract the top 10 keywords from 5 JDs in this target role category.
2. LinkedIn headline rewrite: "[Function] leader who [specific outcome] for [company type]"
3. LinkedIn About section rewrite: first 3 lines must hook a recruiter in under 10 seconds.
4. Content strategy: 2–3x/week posts — what 3 topics demonstrate authority in this role?
5. Daily 30-min engagement ritual: which 5–10 target-company contacts to engage with this week?

**Outcome**: Discovered by recruiters and hiring managers within 2 weeks.

---

## STEP 3 — Build a Warm Pipeline (Days 29–56)
*Why this matters: The best £200K+ roles get filled before they're posted. Warm intros land faster than cold applications. Networking from strength beats networking from desperation.*

**Deliver:**
1. Map second-degree connections at 10 target companies using LinkedIn.
2. Write 3 outreach templates to warm connections (not asking for a job — leading with insight):
   - Template A: Former colleague / mutual connection
   - Template B: Company insider (no prior relationship)
   - Template C: Recruiter or talent partner
3. Weekly cadence: reach out to 25 second-degree contacts per week. How to prioritise?
4. Coffee chat agenda (30-min informational): 5 questions that build rapport and uncover hidden opportunities.

**Outcome**: 5–10 warm conversations and 1–2 referral pipelines open.

---

## STEP 4 — Master the Interview (Days 57–80)
*Why this matters: Senior interviews come down to 2–3 behavioural answers. Hiring managers screen for ownership and clarity. A reframed question reads more senior than a quick answer.*

**Deliver:**
1. Run the PCAR framework on 3 key experiences:
   - **P**: Problem (specific, quantified)
   - **C**: Constraint (what made it hard)
   - **A**: Action (what you specifically did)
   - **R**: Result (business outcome with metrics)
2. Write the answers for these 3 questions at senior level:
   - "Tell me about a time you drove significant change."
   - "Describe a high-stakes decision you made with incomplete information."
   - "Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority."
3. Bring 3 questions hiring managers rarely hear (not salary, benefits, or "what does success look like in this role").
4. How to reframe a question to buy thinking time without sounding evasive.

**Outcome**: Final-round invites at 2–3 of your target companies.

---

## STEP 5 — Negotiate the Full Offer (Days 81–90)
*Why this matters: Most senior candidates leave £20K–£80K on the table. Comp is 6+ levers, not just base salary. The right phrases unlock what hiring managers can approve.*

**Deliver:**
1. Map all 6 compensation levers: base, sign-on bonus, equity/options, performance bonus, benefits, title/scope.
2. For each lever: how to ask for it, what a strong ask sounds like, and what most candidates miss.
3. Write the negotiation opener (after offer received, before accepting): exact phrasing that creates room without creating tension.
4. 3 specific phrases that get hiring managers to reveal their budget ceiling.
5. How to negotiate scope, not just pay — and why getting the bigger title/responsibility now is worth more than a bigger salary.

**Outcome**: A signed offer 20–30% above the initial number across total comp.
