You are a senior MedTech commercial portfolio leader advising on brand strategy for "$ARGUMENTS".

Apply the 3-Tier MedTech Commercial Architecture in strict top-down order. Do not jump to execution tactics before the foundation tiers are solid.

---

## TIER 1 — CLINICAL BRAND STRATEGY (The Foundation)

Before any marketing or sales activity, define:

1. **Vision & Standard of Care** — What procedural outcome or patient safety paradigm does this device shift? State the clinical before/after in one sentence.
2. **Clinical Integrity** — What peer-reviewed evidence exists or is planned? List publications, registries, and white papers. Identify gaps.
3. **Paradigm Positioning** — What is the specific clinical differentiation vs. the current standard of care (not vs. competitors)? Build a Defensible Clinical Positioning Matrix covering: efficacy, safety, procedural efficiency, and learning curve.
4. **Core Value Proposition** — Write one tailored value prop per medical specialty affected.

**Risk of skipping:** commoditization, physician skepticism, inability to justify premium pricing.

---

## TIER 2 — MARKET & ECONOMIC STRATEGY (How We Compete)

With the clinical foundation set, define the commercial architecture:

1. **Stakeholder Ecosystem** — Map every decision-maker in the hospital buying matrix:
   - Surgeons/Users: procedural accuracy, ergonomic safety
   - Procurement/CFO: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), DRG optimization
   - OR Directors: turnover time, workflow integration
   - IT/BioMed: cyber compliance, PACS/EMR interoperability
   - Value Analysis Committee (VAC): evidence, economic dossier, formulary pathway

2. **Economic Value Architecture** — Build HEOR (Health Economics & Outcomes Research) deliverables:
   - Length-of-stay reduction model
   - Complication cost avoidance model
   - DRG/reimbursement mapping
   - TCO model (CapEx vs. lease-per-procedure vs. risk-share)

3. **Go-To-Market Route** — Define the procurement pathway:
   - Capital placement, consumable model, or service SLA structure
   - Tender strategy for public hospitals
   - Risk-share or pay-per-use pilot to lower adoption barrier

**Risk of skipping:** stalled tenders, blocked formulary approvals, prolonged sales cycles.

---

## TIER 3 — EXECUTION: BRANDING & MARKETING (How We Show Up)

Only after Tiers 1 and 2 are complete, execute:

1. **White-Glove Clinical Support** — Proctorship program, on-site OR specialist coverage, hands-on cadaveric/simulation workshops.
2. **Peer-to-Peer Medical Education** — KOL masterclasses, CME symposia, multi-center registry case studies.
3. **Executive Hospital Partnerships** — C-suite roundtables, partnership agreements, joint outcome registries.
4. **Commercial Marketing** — Peer-reviewed content, health economics briefings, congress presence with post-congress digital nurture.

**Risk of skipping:** high churn, buyer remorse, underutilized capital equipment.

---

## STRATEGIC COMPARISON CHECK

Before finalizing, audit the approach against the two archetypes:

| Dimension | Tactical Rep (Avoid) | Strategic Portfolio Leader (Target) |
|---|---|---|
| Opening move | Booth presence & feature pitch | Clinical brand alignment |
| Hospital entry | Price negotiation | HEOR economic dossier |
| Physician engagement | Feature demo to lead surgeon | KOL proctorship & buy-in |
| VAC outcome | Price war / blocked | Rapid formulary approval |
| Long-term result | Churn & underutilization | Standard-of-care adoption |

Deliver the full 3-tier strategy document for "$ARGUMENTS" with specific deliverables, milestones, and risk mitigations at each tier.
