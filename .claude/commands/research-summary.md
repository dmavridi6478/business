# Research & Document Summarizer (SkillDrop AI)

Analyze the following document, notes, or feedback and extract the key takeaways: $ARGUMENTS

Provide:
1. TL;DR (2–3 sentences max)
2. Key findings or decisions (bullet points)
3. Action items with owners if mentioned
4. Open questions or unresolved points
5. Recommended next steps

Be concise and ruthlessly prioritize the most important information.

---
Usage: `/research-summary [paste document, notes, or describe what needs summarizing]`
