---
description: Explain the idea back and expose what is still unclear
argument-hint: [idea, concept, or explanation to test for clarity]
---

Take the idea or explanation in "$ARGUMENTS" and teach it back — explain it as if speaking to someone who has not seen it before.

Then identify what is still unclear after the explanation:
1. **Assumptions baked in** — things the explanation relies on the reader already knowing
2. **Gaps** — steps, transitions, or claims that are asserted but not demonstrated
3. **Ambiguities** — places where reasonable people could interpret the same words differently
4. **The weakest link** — the single part of the explanation that would lose the most readers

End with a sharper version of the one sentence that explains what this is and why it matters.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what idea or explanation to teach back before proceeding.
