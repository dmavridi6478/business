## PROMPTS

### /garage-sale — Garage Sale Flipper
Watch local marketplaces for underpriced items and flag them.

```
Watch Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist in my city for
[cameras / furniture / bikes] listed way under market, and
text me the second one's mispriced with the link.
```

### /agency-to-software — Turn an Agency into Software
Reverse-engineer a service business into a sellable AI product.

```
Pick one service business in [niche] and reverse-engineer the
exact workflow they sell to clients. Break it into steps, tools
used, inputs, outputs, human judgment points, and places where
the work gets slow or expensive. Then design the simplest AI
product that could replace the first version of that service
and charge $500-$5,000/month.
```

---

### /dashboard — Create My 1-Person Company Dashboard
Turn scattered business data into a weekly operator briefing.

```
Look at my docs, notes, Stripe exports, analytics, customer
calls, and project list, then build a weekly operator
dashboard. I want to know what is making money, what is
wasting time, what customers are asking for, what I should
stop doing, and the three highest-leverage actions for next
week. Be blunt and show your work.
```

---

### /competitor-spy — The Competitor Spy
Keep a standing monthly watch on what competitors ship and change.

```
Sign up for my top 3 competitors, sit inside their product and
their emails, and send me a monthly report on every new
feature, price change, and things they do better than us.
```

---

### /qa-team — The Whole QA Team
Nightly regression pass across your app's core flows.

```
Every night, open my app on a real phone, go through signup,
checkout, and the main flows, and screenshot anything that's
broken or confusing.
```

### /browser-operator — Be My Browser Operator
Turn a one-off manual workflow into a documented, repeatable automation.

```
Use the browser to complete this workflow: [workflow]. As you
go, click through the actual sites, collect the data, fill the
forms where appropriate, and keep notes on what broke or
slowed you down. When you're done, give me the output, the
repeatable SOP, and the automation plan so this can become an
agent.
```

---

### /browser-game — A Browser Game People Actually Play
A lead magnet with real hooks, not a cute tech demo.

```
Build a browser game around this mechanic: [mechanic]. Don't
just make a cute demo, add progression, tension, scoring,
failure, polish, and one reason someone would send it to a
friend. Then add lead capture (email/sms), it needs to tie
into my core product which sells [XYZ].
```

---

### /audit-opportunities — Audit My Company for Agent Opportunities
Find what to automate before hiring, ranked by payoff.

```
Look at how this business works and find the tasks we should
give to agents before hiring another person. For each task,
estimate the current human time, the cost of mistakes, the
tools involved, the difficulty of automating it, and the first
safe version we could deploy. Prioritize things that save
money or create revenue within 30 days.
```

---

### /bill-negotiator — The Bill Renegotiator
Chase down recurring bills for savings without sitting on hold.

```
Go through my internet, phone, and software bills, jump into
each provider's chat support, and negotiate them down or
cancel what I'm not using.
```
