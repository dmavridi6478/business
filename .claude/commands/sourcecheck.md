Review the text or response in "$ARGUMENTS" claim by claim. For each factual assertion:

1. **SOURCED** — identify what backs it (named study, known standard, widely documented fact)
2. **INFERRED** — identify it as a reasonable inference rather than a stated fact, and from what
3. **UNSUPPORTED** — flag it explicitly if it cannot be backed up; suggest whether to remove it, caveat it, or go verify it

At the end, give an overall reliability verdict (High / Medium / Low) and the single change that would most improve the document's credibility.

Do not invent sources. If a claim is likely true but you cannot name a source, say "likely true, unverifiable here" and flag it rather than presenting it as confirmed.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what text or claims to source-check before proceeding.
