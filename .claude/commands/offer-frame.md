---
description: Frame an idea as a complete offer — audience, problem, outcome, proof
argument-hint: [idea or service to frame as an offer]
---

Turn the idea in "$ARGUMENTS" into a structured offer using the four-part frame:

1. **Audience** — who this is specifically for; be narrow enough that the right person recognizes themselves
2. **Problem** — the specific, named pain or frustration they have right now; not a category, a real situation
3. **Outcome** — the concrete result they get; stated in their terms, not your process or features
4. **Proof** — why they should believe the outcome is real; the most credible signal available (case study, result, mechanism, track record)

After the four parts, write a one-sentence offer statement that combines them: "[Audience] who [Problem] get [Outcome] because [Proof]."

Then identify the weakest element — which of the four parts is currently least convincing — and what would make it stronger.

Note: `/offer` (also in this repo) designs a priced offer that sells outcomes over deliverables. This command is the audience/problem/outcome/proof framing step.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what idea or service to frame before proceeding.
