## Prompts — copy and paste

### Build your AI marketing team prompt
```
Act as a CMO building an AI-powered marketing department from scratch. Using Claude as the engine, design a complete marketing system for my business that covers: lead generation, content, SEO, email nurture, paid ads, and analytics. Give me: (1) the 5 most important AI agents to build first, (2) the workflows they each run, and (3) the tools/integrations needed. My business: [describe product, audience, current marketing]
```

### Content engine setup prompt
```
Build me a complete Content Engine using Claude. It should: (1) take a core topic or keyword, (2) generate a content calendar for 30 days across blog, LinkedIn, email, and social, (3) produce the first piece of content for each channel. Topic: [your topic]. Audience: [describe]. Tone: [describe].
```

### Lead generation system prompt
```
Design a Lead Finder + Outreach system using AI. Include: (1) ICP definition, (2) where to find leads, (3) research workflow per lead, (4) personalised outreach sequence (email + LinkedIn DM), (5) follow-up cadence. My product: [describe]. Target buyer: [describe].
```

### SEO flywheel prompt
```
Build an SEO flywheel for my business using Claude. Include: (1) keyword research process, (2) content brief template, (3) on-page optimisation checklist, (4) internal linking strategy, (5) a 90-day publishing plan. Business: [describe]. Current organic traffic: [describe].
```

### Performance reporting system prompt
```
Design a weekly marketing performance dashboard. Include metrics for: Content (views, engagement, shares), SEO (rankings, traffic, backlinks), Email (open rate, CTR, unsubscribes), Paid Ads (CPC, ROAS, CTR), Pipeline (leads, SQLs, conversion rate). Format as a table with: metric, target, last week, trend, and action trigger.
```

### Full marketing audit prompt
```
Audit my current marketing across these 9 areas: Outreach & Sales, Content & Social, SEO & Organic, Paid Advertising, Email & Lifecycle, Brand & Positioning, PR & Partnerships, Product Launches, Analytics & Operations. For each area, score me 1–5 and give the single highest-leverage improvement I can make this month. My current marketing: [describe what you're doing today]
```
