# Build Your Claude Workflow in 5 Minutes

Use this structure for every Claude prompt to get better results every time: $ARGUMENTS

---

**THE SIMPLE FORMULA (use for every prompt):**

- **Task:** What do you want?
- **Context:** Give background or details
- **Format:** How should it look?
- **Tone:** How should it sound?
- **Goal:** What result do you expect?

**Quick prompt template:**
```
I want you to [task].
Context: [details].
Format: [output style].
Tone: [voice].
Goal: [result].
```

**Example:**
> Write a short Instagram caption (task) about AI tools (context). Keep it casual and friendly (tone). Use bullet points (format). Goal is to get engagement.

---

**BREAK BIG TASKS INTO SMALL STEPS**

Instead of asking everything at once:
1. Give me content ideas
2. Turn this into captions
3. Rewrite for Instagram tone

Think: step-by-step, not one big ask.

---

**GIVE EXAMPLES (SUPER POWER)**

If you want a specific style:
> "Write like this example: [paste text]"

Claude will match the tone + structure.

---

**DEFINE YOUR OUTPUT FORMAT**

Tell Claude exactly how to respond:
- Bullet points
- Table
- Short paragraph
- Step-by-step guide
- Script
- Caption

Example: *"Give me 5 tips in bullet points. Each tip must be 1 sentence only."*

---

**CONTROL THE TONE & STYLE**

Your first result is just a draft. Follow up with:
- "Make this shorter"
- "Simplify the words"
- "Make it more engaging"
- "Add examples"

Treat Claude like a **collaborator**, not a one-time tool.

---

**USE "IMPROVE THIS" METHOD**

Paste your content and say:
> "Improve this. Make it clearer, more engaging, and easy to read."

---

**BE SPECIFIC (this changes everything)**

Bad prompt: "Write about marketing."

Better prompt: "Explain 3 simple marketing strategies for small businesses using basic English. Add examples."

The more specific = the better the output.
