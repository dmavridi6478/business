## THE 9-ELEMENT SYSTEM

### 1. Your North Star
Pick one clear direction for the next 90 days. Clarity beats breadth.

**Ask yourself:** What does success actually look like 12 weeks from now?

**Prompt:**
```
Help me define my LinkedIn North Star for the next 90 days.
My background: [YOUR BACKGROUND]
My current goals: [YOUR GOALS]
1. What ONE direction should I commit to for the next 90 days?
2. What does success look like at week 12? (specific, measurable)
3. What do I need to stop doing to stay focused on this direction?
```

---

### 2. The Positioning Triangle
Personal brands work when 3 things line up:
1. What you're actually good at
2. A problem your market genuinely feels
3. Your take on it, specifically

**Ask yourself:** Why should someone listen to me over the 10,000 people saying something similar?

**Prompt:**
```
Help me complete my Positioning Triangle.
What I'm good at: [LIST YOUR SKILLS/EXPERTISE]
Problems I see in my market: [LIST PROBLEMS]
1. Where do my skills intersect with a real market problem?
2. What is my specific take or angle that differentiates me from others in this space?
3. Write my positioning statement: "I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] by [HOW] — unlike others who [CONTRAST]."
4. Why would someone choose me over the next 10,000 people saying the same thing?
```

---

### 3. The Audience Clarity Map
You're not talking to "everyone." You're talking to 3 groups with different needs.

| Group | Who they are | What they need |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Buyers** | The people who will pay you | Evidence, proof, ROI |
| **Aspirational Followers** | People who amplify you without buying | Inspiration, behind-the-scenes |
| **Learners** | Early stage, watching from the sidelines | Frameworks, step-by-step |

**Prompt:**
```
My personal brand focus is [FOCUS]. Map my audience:
1. Who are my Buyers? What do they need to see before they pay?
2. Who are my Aspirational Followers? What content makes them share?
3. Who are my Learners? What content builds their trust over time?
4. For each group, give me one content type that speaks directly to them.
```

---

### 4. The Content OS
This is the system behind every content plan. You need 4 lanes, always running:

| Lane | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **Teach** | Frameworks, tactics, step-by-step | Build authority |
| **Think** | Opinions, observations, hot takes | Build personality |
| **Build** | Behind the scenes, mistakes, lessons | Build trust |
| **Sell** | Results, stories, a clear offer | Build pipeline |

**Prompt:**
```
My niche is [NICHE]. Build my Content OS for the next 4 weeks:
1. Give me 2 Teach post ideas (frameworks/tactics)
2. Give me 2 Think post ideas (opinions/hot takes on trends in my space)
3. Give me 2 Build post ideas (behind the scenes, what I'm working on)
4. Give me 1 Sell post (a result, story, or clear offer)
Format each as a post title + 2-sentence hook.
```

### 5. The Attention Engine
Consistency needs a system to become repeatable. This weekly rhythm puts personal brand growth on autopilot.

```
WEEKLY RHYTHM:
→ Idea sourcing:     60 minutes (weekly)
→ Batch writing:     2 hours (weekly)
→ Daily engagement:  15–20 minutes (Mon–Fri)
→ End of week review: 20 minutes (Friday)
```

**Prompt:**
```
Help me set up my Attention Engine for the week.
My schedule constraints: [TIME AVAILABLE]
My content niche: [NICHE]
1. Design my idea sourcing session (where to find ideas for my specific niche)
2. Structure my 2-hour batch writing session (how many posts, what order)
3. What should my 15-minute daily engagement routine look like?
4. What do I review at the end of the week?
```

### 6. The Trust Ladder
People buy from you because trust gets built in a specific order. Skip a step and the whole ladder gets shakier.

```
5 — ACTION     ← Buying / booking / hiring
4 — PROOF      ← Case studies, results, testimonials
3 — AUTHORITY  ← Consistent expertise over time
2 — RELEVANCE  ← "This person gets my situation"
1 — AWARENESS  ← "I've heard of them"
```

**Prompt:**
```
My current LinkedIn presence: [DESCRIBE WHERE YOU ARE NOW]
My offer: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Apply the Trust Ladder:
1. Which rung am I currently at with most of my audience?
2. What content moves people from rung 1 → 2 → 3?
3. What is my proof? (case studies, results, testimonials I should be sharing)
4. What does my "action" ask look like? (CTA, offer, booking link)
```

### 7. The Content Feedback Loop
Your audience tells you exactly what to make next. Use a weekly review to close the loop.

**Each week, check:**
- Your best performing post (What worked? Why?)
- Your worst performing post (What didn't? Why?)
- What topic actually resonated (not just liked — commented, shared, DM'd)
- What format's worth repeating

**Loop:** Measure → Learn → Improve → Repeat

**Prompt:**
```
Here are my last week's LinkedIn posts and their stats:
[PASTE POSTS + STATS]
Run the Content Feedback Loop:
1. What was my best performer and why did it work?
2. What was my worst and what would I do differently?
3. What topic or angle got the most genuine engagement (comments, DMs, shares)?
4. What format should I repeat next week?
5. Give me 3 post ideas based on this data.
```

---

### 8. The 3-Level Personal Brand Funnel
Your brand should be built to sell, not just to entertain.

```
TOP OF FUNNEL:    Content built for reach (broad appeal, educational, shareable)
MIDDLE OF FUNNEL: Deeper stuff — cheat sheets, carousels, guides (builds trust)
BOTTOM OF FUNNEL: Proof and real transformations / case studies (drives action)
```

**Prompt:**
```
My offer is [OFFER]. Build my 3-level funnel content plan:
TOP: Give me 3 post ideas designed for maximum reach in my niche.
MIDDLE: Give me 2 deeper content ideas (carousel, guide, cheat sheet) that build trust.
BOTTOM: Give me 1 proof/case study post that drives people toward [OFFER].
For each, write the opening hook line.
```

---

### 9. The Growth Scorecard
You only need 5 numbers to know if this is working. Check weekly.

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|------------------|
| **Views** | Reach — are people seeing your content? |
| **Follower growth** | Magnetism — are viewers choosing to follow? |
| **Engagement rate** | Resonance — are followers interacting? |
| **Inbound leads** | Pipeline — is your brand generating business interest? |
| **Newsletter signups** | Owned audience — are you building off-platform? |

**Prompt:**
```
Here are my LinkedIn numbers this week:
Views: [X], Follower growth: [+X], Engagement rate: [X%], Inbound leads: [X], Newsletter signups: [X]
Analyze my Growth Scorecard:
1. Which metric is my biggest bottleneck?
2. What's likely causing it?
3. What's one specific change I can make next week to move that number?
4. What does a healthy benchmark look like for each metric at my stage?
```
