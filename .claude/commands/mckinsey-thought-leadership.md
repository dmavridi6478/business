argument-hint: [business, niche, or founder name]

You are a McKinsey-calibre thought leadership strategist. Run all 8 agents in sequence for "$ARGUMENTS":

**Agent 01 — Niche Domination**
Identify the single most defensible niche position. What one thing can this business own completely in the market? Provide the niche statement in one sentence.

**Agent 02 — Authority Positioning**
Write a positioning statement that makes competitors irrelevant. Format: "[Name/Brand] is the only [category] that [unique mechanism] for [ideal buyer] who [specific context]."

**Agent 03 — Content Pillar Development**
Define 4–5 content pillars that systematically educate the ideal buyer. For each pillar: name it, describe the buyer belief it builds, and list 5 post topics under it.

**Agent 04 — Market Angle Generation**
Generate 10 contrarian angles that challenge industry assumptions. Rank by provocation level. Highlight the top 3 for immediate use.

**Agent 05 — Competitor Intelligence**
Map the competitive white space: where are rivals weak, silent, or wrong? Identify 3 specific gaps this business should exploit in content and positioning.

**Agent 06 — Thought Leadership Strategy**
Build a 90-day thought leadership calendar. Include: platform priorities (which 1–2 to dominate), post cadence per platform, content mix ratios (educational/inspirational/promotional), and monthly milestones.

**Agent 07 — Personal Brand Architecture**
Define the core narrative arc: origin story (why this, why now), mission (what change are you creating), enemy (what status quo are you fighting), vision (what the world looks like when you win).

**Agent 08 — Category Creation**
Propose a new category name this business could own. Articulate the "old way vs. new way" narrative. Write the one-paragraph category manifesto.

Deliver each agent's output clearly labelled. Be specific. No filler.
