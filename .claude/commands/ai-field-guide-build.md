# AI Field Guide — BUILD Habits

Source: @thesocialalpha (The AI Field Guide / BUILD carousel)

8 habits for people who build seriously with AI.

---

## Habit 01 — CONNECT AI TO REAL TOOLS

Don't run AI in isolation. Connect it to:

- Web
- Files
- APIs
- MCP
- Databases
- Code
- Automation

**The principle:** AI with no connections is a chat toy. AI with connections is a work system.

---

## Habit 02 — LEARN ENOUGH CODING TO BE DANGEROUS

You don't need to be a developer. You need enough to direct one.

Core skills:
- Python
- JSON
- APIs
- Git
- Terminal basics

**The principle:** You don't build everything — you understand enough to unstick yourself and review what AI produces.

---

## Habit 03 — STUDY YOUR OWN BOTTLENECKS

The quality of AI output depends on the quality of your input.

Three levers to audit:
1. **Your Brief** — are you giving AI enough context?
2. **Your Judgment** — can you tell good output from bad?
3. **Your Thinking** — is your reasoning actually clear?

Process: Diagnose your weakest input → Improve that input → Output improves.

---

## Habit 04 — VERIFY IMPORTANT OUTPUTS

AI is fast and often wrong. Your job is the verification layer.

Check every important output for:
- **Facts** — traceable to source?
- **Numbers** — reproducible?
- **Citations** — do they support the claim?
- **Assumptions** — what was taken for granted?
- **Logic** — does the conclusion follow from the premises?

---

## Habit 05 — BUILD CONSTANTLY

You learn AI by using AI — not by watching others use it.

Build things across every format:
- Apps
- Ads
- Agents
- Research Systems
- Automations

**The principle:** Usage compounds faster than watching tutorials.

---

## Habit 06 — REVERSE-ENGINEER YOUR WINS

When something works, don't move on. Extract the pattern.

Three-step loop:
1. **Spot the Win** — what worked better than expected?
2. **Find the Cause** — why did it work?
3. **Save the Pattern** — store the prompt, workflow, or structure for reuse

---

## Habit 07 — TRACK NEW CAPABILITIES

AI capabilities change every few weeks. Your workflows go stale.

When something new ships:
1. Notice the change
2. Test it against your existing workflow
3. Keep what helps, discard what doesn't

**The principle:** Yesterday's best approach is often not today's best approach.

---

## Habit 08 — DEVELOP JUDGMENT

This is the hardest habit. Judgment cannot be automated.

Three components:
- **ASK** — pick a problem worth solving (not every problem deserves AI)
- **REJECT** — spot what does not hold up under scrutiny
- **SHIP** — decide what is ready to use vs. what still needs work

**The principle:** You are not a prompt engineer. You are a decision-maker who uses AI.
