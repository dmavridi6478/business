# 5 Websites Every Small Business Should Know

---

## 01 — Hunter · hunter.io

**What it does:** Find professional email addresses for any company or domain.

**Use it when:** You want to reach out to a specific person but don't have their contact details.

**How:**
1. Go to [hunter.io](https://hunter.io)
2. Enter a company domain (e.g. `stripe.com`)
3. Get a list of verified emails + confidence scores

**Best for:** Cold outreach, partnership pitches, journalist contacts, recruiter searches.

---

## 02 — SERPtag · serptag.com

**What it does:** Track your SEO keyword rankings and see where you appear in Google search results.

**Use it when:** You publish content and want to know if it's actually ranking.

**How:**
1. Go to [serptag.com](https://serptag.com)
2. Enter your domain and target keywords
3. Track ranking changes over time

**Best for:** Content strategy validation, identifying which pages to optimise next.

---

## 03 — Microsoft Clarity · clarity.microsoft.com

**What it does:** Session recordings, heatmaps, and scroll maps showing exactly how visitors use your website — free, unlimited.

**Use it when:** You want to understand where users drop off, what they click, and what they ignore.

**How:**
1. Go to [clarity.microsoft.com](https://clarity.microsoft.com)
2. Add one tracking script to your site
3. Watch real session recordings within hours

**Free for:** Up to 2 million monthly sessions. Used by 2M+ sites worldwide.

**Best for:** Landing page optimisation, UX audits, finding friction in checkout flows.

---

## 04 — Calendly · calendly.com

**What it does:** Let people book meetings directly into your calendar — no back-and-forth emails.

**Use it when:** You spend time coordinating meeting times via email.

**How:**
1. Go to [calendly.com](https://calendly.com)
2. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar
3. Share your booking link — people pick a slot that works for both of you

**Best for:** Sales calls, discovery calls, client check-ins, team interviews.

---

## 05 — BuiltWith · builtwith.com

**What it does:** Discover the technology stack powering any website — CMS, analytics, hosting, payment processors, CDN, and more.

**Use it when:** You want to know what tools a competitor or prospect is using.

**How:**
1. Go to [builtwith.com](https://builtwith.com)
2. Enter any domain
3. Get a full tech stack breakdown

**Best for:** Competitive intelligence, sales prospecting (know what stack a lead is on before you pitch), partner research.
