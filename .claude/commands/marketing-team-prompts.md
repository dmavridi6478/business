# 10 Prompts That Replace Your Marketing Team

Use these standalone prompts (one at a time) to handle every marketing function: $ARGUMENTS

---

**1. CONTENT STRATEGIST**
Act as a senior content strategist. Analyse [my business/product] and give me a 30-day content plan for [platform]. Include: content pillars, post frequency, and 5 specific post ideas per pillar. My niche: [niche]. My audience: [audience].

**2. COPYWRITER**
Act as a direct-response copywriter. Write [landing page / product description / ad copy] for [product/service]. Use the PAS framework (Problem, Agitate, Solution). Keep it under [word count]. Tone: [e.g. conversational, authoritative, witty].

**3. EMAIL MARKETER**
Act as an email marketing specialist. Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [my business]. Email 1: welcome + quick win. Email 2: my story. Email 3: most common mistake. Email 4: case study. Email 5: soft pitch for [offer].

**4. SEO SPECIALIST**
Act as an SEO specialist. Give me 10 long-tail keywords for [topic/niche] with low competition and high intent. For each: explain the intent, suggest a blog title, and outline a 5-section article structure.

**5. AD CREATIVE**
Act as a paid ads creative director. Write 3 Facebook/Instagram ad variations for [product]. Each needs: a scroll-stopping first line, a clear benefit, social proof element, and CTA. Target audience: [audience].

**6. LANDING PAGE WRITER**
Act as a conversion copywriter. Write a full landing page for [offer]. Include: headline, subheadline, 3 key benefits, how it works (3 steps), testimonial placeholder, FAQ (3 questions), and CTA button text.

**7. BRAND STRATEGIST**
Act as a brand strategist. Define the brand voice for [my business]. Give me: brand personality (3 words), tone of voice guide, 5 things we always say, 5 things we never say, and a one-line brand positioning statement.

**8. MARKET RESEARCHER**
Act as a market research analyst. Analyse [my niche/market] and give me: top 3 customer pain points, 3 emerging trends, 2 underserved gaps, and what the top competitors are getting wrong.

**9. SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER**
Act as a social media manager. Create a week of content for [platform] for [business/brand]. Include: 3 educational posts, 2 engagement posts, 1 promotional post, and 1 behind-the-scenes post. Add hooks and CTAs.

**10. ANALYTICS TRANSLATOR**
Act as a data analyst. I have this marketing data: [paste data or describe metrics]. Tell me in plain English: what is working, what is not, what I should do more of, and what I should stop immediately.
