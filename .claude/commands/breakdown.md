---
description: Turn a complex goal into clear step-by-step actions
argument-hint: [goal to break down]
---

Turn "$ARGUMENTS" into a concrete, ordered list of steps a person could start executing today. Each step should name what to actually do (not a vague phase name), roughly how long it takes, and what it depends on finishing first. Flag any step that's a genuine blocker (needs a decision, budget, or someone else) versus one that can just be done.
