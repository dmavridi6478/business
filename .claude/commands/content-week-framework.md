# 1 Idea → A Week of Content

Source: Josh S. (LinkedIn)

One strong idea, repurposed across 7 formats over 7 days.
Each format serves a different audience behavior and platform moment.

---

## Monday — The Full Post

The anchor piece. Everything else extends from this.

- Best hook
- Best structure
- Stop-mid-scroll opener
- Full argument, insight, or story

**Goal:** Earn attention and establish the idea.

---

## Tuesday — The Carousel

Break the Monday post into slides.

- 7–10 slides
- One point per slide
- No slide does the job of another

**Goal:** Give the skimmers a scannable version. Carousels get saved and shared.

---

## Wednesday — The Infographic

Strip the idea down to its visual skeleton.

- Remove all prose
- Screenshot-worthy composition
- Before/after contrast where possible

**Goal:** The single most shareable format. If Wednesday's image gets shared, it earns followers who never saw Monday's post.

---

## Thursday — The Story Version

Make it personal and concrete.

- Prove it happened to you
- Specific detail beats general principle
- End on the change — what's different now?

**Goal:** Trust. Stories that name a real event outperform abstract advice.

---

## Friday — The Cheat Sheet

Reduce the idea to its executable core.

- Exact steps only
- Numbered
- Screenshot before finishing — does it still hold?

**Goal:** Utility. Cheat sheets get bookmarked and returned to.

---

## Saturday — Behind the Scenes

Show the process, not the result.

- What didn't work
- What changed mid-way
- Honest about the gap between plan and execution

**Goal:** Relatability. The human version of the polished Monday post.

---

## Sunday — Conversion

Ask for something.

- One clear next step
- No new content — just the CTA
- Arrives after 6 days of proof

**Goal:** Action. Sunday earns the right to ask because the week built the trust.

---

## Weekly Structure

| Day | Format | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Full Post | Anchor — establish the idea |
| Tuesday | Carousel | Scannable — reach skimmers |
| Wednesday | Infographic | Shareable — reach strangers |
| Thursday | Story | Trust — earn belief |
| Friday | Cheat Sheet | Utility — earn bookmarks |
| Saturday | Behind the Scenes | Relatability — earn connection |
| Sunday | Conversion | Action — earn the click |

---

## The Principle

Don't create 7 ideas. Create 1 idea well, then serve it 7 different ways to 7 different audience states.
