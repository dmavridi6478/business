# 30 KPIs for VP / C-Suite Leaders
*Source: Cicely Simpson — "What actually needs measuring, beyond the P&L"*

---

## Category 1: Your Business (15 KPIs)
*Monitor how the business itself is performing.*

| # | KPI | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Growth Rate | Increase in sales over time |
| 2 | Net Profit Margin | Percentage of revenue kept after expenses |
| 3 | Gross Margin | Revenue left after cost of goods sold |
| 4 | Operating Margin | Revenue left after operating costs |
| 5 | Customer Retention Rate | Percentage of customers who stay |
| 6 | Churn Rate | Rate at which customers leave |
| 7 | Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Cost to win a new customer |
| 8 | Lifetime Value (LTV) | Total revenue from a customer over time |
| 9 | Cash Flow | Money moving in and out of the business |
| 10 | Working Capital | Ability to cover short-term obligations |
| 11 | Employee Productivity | Output per person or team |
| 12 | Overhead Rate | Indirect costs relative to direct costs |
| 13 | Debt to Equity Ratio | Balance of debt versus ownership |
| 14 | Market Share | Your position relative to competitors |
| 15 | Net Promoter Score (NPS) | How likely customers are to recommend you |

---

## Category 2: Your Leadership (8 KPIs)
*Monitor how you are leading, not just what you are producing.*

| # | KPI | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trust Score | How safe people feel raising problems with you |
| 2 | Decision Speed | How quickly sound decisions get made |
| 3 | Talent Retention | How many of your best people stay |
| 4 | Succession Readiness | How prepared your team is without you |
| 5 | Communication Clarity | How well your message lands the first time |
| 6 | 360 Feedback | How you are seen from every direction, not just above |
| 7 | Team Growth Rate | How many people are developing under you |
| 8 | Delegation Effectiveness | How much only you are still doing |

---

## Category 3: Yourself (7 KPIs)
*Monitor the parts of you that fund everything above.*

| # | KPI | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Levels | Whether you are running on reserves or running on empty |
| 2 | Focus | How much of your attention is where it needs to be |
| 3 | Boundaries | How well you protect your time and energy |
| 4 | Self-Awareness | How clearly you see your own strengths and blind spots |
| 5 | Presence | How fully you show up in the moment you are actually in |
| 6 | Perspective | Whether you are choosing your outlook or reacting to it |
| 7 | Recovery | How well you rest and reset between demands |

## Prompts — copy and paste

### Full KPI audit prompt
```
Act as a senior executive coach. Using these 3 categories — Business KPIs, Leadership KPIs, and Personal KPIs — audit my current performance. I'll describe my situation. For each category, tell me: (1) which KPIs I'm likely neglecting, (2) which are most urgent, and (3) one specific action for each. Context: [describe your role, team size, current challenges]
```

### Dashboard design prompt
```
Design a weekly CEO/VP dashboard that tracks the most important KPIs from these 3 categories: Business (revenue, margin, retention, NPS), Leadership (trust score, delegation effectiveness, team growth), Personal (energy, focus, recovery). Format as a table with: KPI name, target range, current status (RAG: Red/Amber/Green), and one-line action trigger.
```

### Leadership KPI self-score prompt
```
Score me 1–10 on each of these 8 leadership KPIs, based on what I tell you. Then identify my weakest two and give me a 30-day plan to improve them. KPIs: Trust Score, Decision Speed, Talent Retention, Succession Readiness, Communication Clarity, 360 Feedback, Team Growth Rate, Delegation Effectiveness. My context: [describe]
```
