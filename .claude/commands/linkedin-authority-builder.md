# How to Build Authority on LinkedIn

Source: @theromanknox / Chris Donnelly framework

---

## Step 1 — Define Your Expertise
Own one specific lane. You need to figure out:
- What problem do you solve best?
- Who is this expertise NOT for?
- What stage of buyer needs this?
- What makes your approach unique?

## Step 2 — Understand ICP Problems (SPIN Framework)
Use the SPIN method to go deep:
- **S** — Situation: "What's your current struggle?"
- **P** — Problem: "What's not working for you?"
- **I** — Implication: "What does this cost you daily?"
- **N** — Need-payoff: "What changes if this is solved?"

## Step 3 — Create Unique POV (Authority Formula)
Authority = (Specific Expertise × Proven Results) / (Generic Advice × Inconsistency)

## Step 4 — Build Content System
**Awareness** → Authority content, Strategic engagement, ICP targeting
**Trust** → Case studies, Client wins, Lead magnets
**Conversion** → Clear CTAs, Booking funnels

## Step 5 — Prove With Results
Share proof in this order: Results → Client names → Specific numbers → Timeframes
- Add context: Who they were + What changed + How long it took
- Use screenshots that show real outcomes, not testimonials
- Make wins specific: "$11.6K in 3 weeks" not "increased revenue"

DON'T:
- Share vague wins without numbers
- Use "trust me" instead of showing receipts
- Talk about process before proving results

## Step 6 — Make CTAs Clear
End posts with:
- "Join my newsletter for more."
- "Follow for daily breakdowns."
- "Book a call if this resonates."

## Step 7 — Leverage Client Wins
**Document** → Capture measurable wins, get permission, screenshot conversations
**Share** → Post client transformations, tag clients when appropriate, show before/after
**Amplify** → Ask happy clients to share their wins, make testimonials easy to give, feature stories in content
