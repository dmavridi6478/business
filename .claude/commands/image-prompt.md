# AI Image Prompt Generator (99 Slash Commands — @sdv_stm)

Generate a detailed AI image prompt for: $ARGUMENTS

Structure the prompt with:
- **Subject**: what is in the image (person, object, scene)
- **Style**: artistic style (photorealistic, oil painting, anime, cinematic, etc.)
- **Lighting**: lighting conditions (golden hour, studio, neon, dramatic shadows)
- **Composition**: camera angle, framing, depth of field
- **Mood/Atmosphere**: emotional tone
- **Technical specs**: aspect ratio, render quality (8K, HDR, RAW photo)
- **Negative prompt**: what to exclude

Output 3 prompt variations: short (1 sentence), medium (3 sentences), full (detailed paragraph).

---
Usage: `/image-prompt [describe the image you want]`
