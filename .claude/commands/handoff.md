---
description: Package work so another person can continue without asking questions
argument-hint: [work or project to hand off]
---

Create a handoff document for the work described in "$ARGUMENTS" structured so that someone who knows nothing about it can continue without coming back to ask questions.

Include:
- **Context** — why this work exists and what it's trying to achieve
- **Where things stand** — what is done, what is in progress, what is not started
- **Key decisions made** — what was decided and why (especially anything non-obvious)
- **Next steps** — the specific actions to take next, in order, with any known blockers named
- **Things to watch out for** — gotchas, constraints, or assumptions that would bite a new person
- **Resources** — where to find anything needed (files, tools, contacts, access)

Write it to be read cold by someone under time pressure. No padding. If something is genuinely unclear or undocumented, say so rather than leaving gaps that look filled.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what work to package for handoff before proceeding.

Source: @theusefulshift "20 Claude Commands That Feel Illegal" — Command 08 (handoff).
