---
description: Separate what we know, assume, and still need to verify
argument-hint: [topic or question to map]
---

Map the knowledge state of the topic or question described in "$ARGUMENTS" into three clean columns:

**KNOW** — facts we can assert with confidence, with a brief source or basis for each.

**ASSUME** — things we are treating as true but have not verified; label each assumption as High / Medium / Low confidence.

**NEED TO VERIFY** — open questions, gaps, or claims that would change the direction if wrong; for each, note the fastest way to close the gap.

Be honest about which column something belongs in. Do not move an assumption into KNOW because it feels stable. The value of this map is its accuracy, not its reassurance.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what topic or question to map before proceeding.
