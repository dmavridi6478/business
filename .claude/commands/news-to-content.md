# News-to-Content Workflow (@earchoe)

Run every Monday morning with web search ON to turn niche news into content: $ARGUMENTS

Search for the most significant AI or [your niche] news from the last 7 days. For each of the top 3 stories:
1. Summarise it in 2 sentences (plain English, no jargon)
2. Why it matters to [your audience — e.g. UK solo founders / creators]
3. A TikTok carousel angle: hook line + 3 slides of practical takeaway
4. Rate how time-sensitive this is (post today / this week / evergreen)

My niche: [what your content is about]
My audience: [who follows you]

---
Usage: `/news-to-content [your niche, your audience]`
