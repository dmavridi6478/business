## FULL PLAYBOOK

### Phase 1 — Listen Without an Agenda
Goal: Understand the team before forming opinions.

1. **Open With a Question** — Start every conversation asking "What would you want me to know?"
2. **Meet Everyone Once** — 1:1 with each person in the first two weeks, no exceptions.
3. **Sit In as a Guest** — Join meetings as an observer; do not lead or redirect.
4. **Read Before You Ask** — Review existing docs, OKRs, and past decisions before questioning them.
5. **Track the Contradictions** — Write down every time two people describe the same thing differently.

---

### Phase 2 — Learn How Work Actually Gets Done
Goal: Map reality, not the org chart.

6. **Follow the Handoffs** — Trace one deliverable end-to-end and watch where it slows.
7. **Learn the Workarounds** — Ask "What do you do when the official process breaks?"
8. **Find the Real Bottleneck** — The loudest complaint is rarely the real constraint.
9. **Watch the Actual Work** — Sit with someone doing their job. Don't advise. Just watch.
10. **Name the Unwritten Rules** — List the norms nobody documents but everyone follows.

---

### Phase 3 — Map the Team Behind the Org Chart
Goal: Understand informal power and trust networks.

11. **Find the Informal Leader** — Who do people go to when they're stuck or uncertain?
12. **Spot the Steady Contributor** — Find who delivers quietly without recognition.
13. **Ask About the Last Leader** — "What worked? What didn't?" reveals the team's unmet needs.
14. **Watch Who People Follow** — In meetings, whose ideas get picked up and whose get ignored?
15. **Learn What Changed Last Time** — The last major shift reveals what the team fears repeating.

---

### Phase 4 — Diagnose Before You Decide
Goal: Resist the urge to fix before you understand.

16. **Separate People From Systems** — Is this a skill gap, a process gap, or a motivation gap?
17. **Test Your First Theory** — State your hypothesis to one trusted person. See if it holds.
18. **Ask What They'd Fix First** — "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
19. **Follow the Recurring Complaint** — A pattern that surfaces 3+ times is a real problem.
20. **Hold Your Verdict** — Wait until day 30 before deciding anything is broken.

---

### Phase 5 — Build Trust Through Small Moves
Goal: Earn credibility before you spend it.

21. **Fix One Small Thing Fast** — Find a low-stakes, visible pain and remove it in week one.
22. **Give Credit in Public** — Name the person behind every win in team settings.
23. **Deliver One Ask** — If someone asks for something reasonable, make it happen.
24. **Show Up on Time Every Time** — Punctuality signals reliability before you've proven anything else.
25. **Own Your Blind Spot** — Say "I don't know X yet" before someone discovers it for you.

---

### Phase 6 — Earn the Right to Change Something
Goal: Make your first move count.

26. **Name What You Learned** — Share your observations with the team in a summary conversation.
27. **Pick One Change First** — One focused change lands better than a broad initiative.
28. **Explain the Why** — People accept change when they understand the reasoning.
29. **Involve the Closest Person** — The person most affected by a change should help design it.
30. **Set the Next Checkpoint** — Announce when you'll revisit the change and what success looks like.

Use this prompt when coaching yourself or someone else through a specific phase:

```
I'm in [PHASE NAME / PHASE NUMBER] of leading a new team.
Current situation: [what you're observing or struggling with]
Team size: [number]
Industry/context: [brief description]

Help me:
1. Identify which of the 5 actions in this phase I should prioritize.
2. Give me a specific script or question I can use today.
3. Flag any risk I should watch for in this phase.
4. Suggest the one thing I should NOT do right now.
```
