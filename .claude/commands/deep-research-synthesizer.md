description: Pull scattered research notes and sources into one clear summary with every claim sourced and the gaps stated
argument-hint: [paste notes, links or files, and the question to answer]
---

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Use `deep-research`, `research-summary` and `research-skeptic`.

1. State the question and the scope. List the sources you were given with date and type.
2. Group findings by theme. Each claim carries its source; claims from one source only are marked single-source.
3. Show where sources disagree and which is stronger and why.
4. End with: the answer in five lines, confidence (high, medium, low) with reasons, and what is missing. Never add a claim that is not in the sources.
