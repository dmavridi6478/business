# The Chain Prompting Method (@earchoe)

Turn a single draft into polished, publish-ready content with 4 chained prompts: $ARGUMENTS

---

**THE CONCEPT:**

One prompt is a draft. Three prompts is a piece of content.

Most people stop at the first output. The chain is what makes the difference.

---

**THE 4-STEP CHAIN:**

**Step 1 — DRAFT**
Run your persona prompt (see `/persona-prompt-formula`) for the full piece.

**Step 2 — SHARPEN**
Follow up with:
> "Make the opening line more provocative. Make it a bold claim under 8 words."

**Step 3 — COMPRESS**
Follow up with:
> "Cut 20% of the words without losing any meaning. No filler."

**Step 4 — VOICE CHECK**
Follow up with:
> "Read this back and flag any line that sounds like generic AI content — not like the expert persona."

---

**THE QUALITY TEST:**

Before you post anything AI helped you write — read it aloud.

- If it sounds like something you would say to a friend who asked for honest advice → publish it.
- If it sounds like a LinkedIn post from 2019 → run the chain prompts again.
