description: Choose the smallest action that removes the most uncertainty
argument-hint: [situation or goal]
---

Given the situation or goal in "$ARGUMENTS", identify the single next move — the smallest, cheapest action that removes the most uncertainty.

Your output:
1. **The move** — one specific, concrete action (not a category of action)
2. **What uncertainty it removes** — what you won't know until you take this step, and will know after
3. **Time and cost** — how long it takes and what it costs (money, attention, or social capital)
4. **Why not a bigger move first** — what makes this the right unit of action given current unknowns
5. **What follows** — the decision or next question that emerges once this move is made

Resist the pull toward comprehensive plans. The point is to find the cheapest way to learn what the next decision actually requires — not to plan past it.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what situation or goal to analyze before proceeding.
