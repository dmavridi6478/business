# Proposal & Offer Writer (SkillDrop AI)

Act as a senior sales consultant. Write a tailored business proposal for: $ARGUMENTS

Structure the proposal with:
1. Executive summary (the problem you solve)
2. Proposed solution (specific to this client/situation)
3. Deliverables and scope
4. Timeline
5. Investment / pricing
6. Why us (differentiators)
7. Clear call to action

Write persuasively but honestly. Focus on client outcomes, not features.

---
Usage: `/proposal-writer [client name, their problem, your service/offer]`
