## THE 8-MODULE NICHE PROSPECTING SYSTEM

### MODULE 01 — KEYWORD SELECTION
**What:** Find niches based on what your ideal companies are trying to rank for.
**Playbook:** ICP Targeting Guide
**Benefits:** Find specific niches · Start from search intent · Avoid random prospecting · Build a sharper ICP

**Prompt:**
"My product/service is [describe]. My ideal customer is [role/industry/size]. Suggest 10 Google keywords my ideal customer would search for to find solutions to the problems we solve. Focus on commercial and transactional intent. Output as a table: keyword, search intent, company type searching it, and why it signals buying intent."

---

### MODULE 02 — GOOGLE DISCOVERY
**What:** Turn a Google keyword into a list of companies ranking for that search.
**Playbook:** Complete Prospeo Benchmark Report
**Benefits:** Discover niche companies · Skip manual Google research · Find relevant domains · Build lists faster

**Prompt:**
"Take this keyword: [keyword]. What types of companies rank for it? What do they have in common — size, business model, tech stack, location? Help me build a targeting filter for Prospeo to find similar companies at scale."

---

### MODULE 03 — BUSINESS FILTERING
**What:** Filter companies by business model, size, location and more.
**Playbook:** ICP Targeting Guide
**Benefits:** Remove bad-fit accounts · Tighten your list · Improve targeting · Focus on better prospects

**Prompt:**
"I have [N] companies from Google Discovery. Apply these ICP filters: (1) business model [B2B/B2C/agency/SaaS], (2) headcount range [X–Y], (3) revenue range [$X–$Y], (4) location [country/region], (5) tech stack [if relevant]. Score each 1–10 for ICP fit. Remove anyone below 6. Output a filtered list with scores and a brief reason per company."

---

### MODULE 04 — BUYING SIGNALS
**What:** Add signals to identify companies worth contacting first.
**Playbook:** Complete 600-Lead Benchmark Report
**Benefits:** Spot active companies · Find warmer accounts · Improve outreach timing · Prioritise better leads

**Prompt:**
"From my filtered list of [N] companies, identify which have active buying signals. Check: (1) recent job postings relevant to our solution, (2) funding events in last 90 days, (3) tech stack changes, (4) G2 review activity, (5) LinkedIn engagement with relevant content. Rank by signal strength and label each warm/cool/cold."

---

### MODULE 05 — DECISION-MAKER TARGETING
**What:** Find the exact person to reach inside each company.
**Playbook:** 9 Cold-Call Openers
**Benefits:** Reach the right buyer · Skip manual research · Reduce wasted outreach · Start more conversations

**Prompt:**
"For each company on my list, identify the right decision-maker for [our solution]. Title pattern: [e.g. Head of Marketing / VP Sales / Founder]. For each person: name, title, LinkedIn URL. Write a cold call opener referencing something specific about their company's buying signal. Give me 9 opener variations: hiring signal, funding event, competitor switch, tech stack change, G2 activity, LinkedIn engagement, content download, web visit, and referral name drop."

---

### MODULE 06 — VERIFIED EMAILS
**What:** Find verified contact information before launching outreach.
**Playbook:** 6-Email Sequence
**Benefits:** Get outreach-ready contacts · Reduce bad data · Launch faster · Run structured follow-ups

**Prompt:**
"I have [N] decision-maker names and companies. Build a 6-email outreach sequence for [ICP + solution]. Email 1: trigger-based opener (why now). Email 2: value prop + social proof. Email 3: case study or specific result. Email 4: objection handler. Email 5: different angle or use case. Email 6: breakup / last attempt. Each email: subject line, 3-sentence body, CTA. Under 100 words each."

---

### MODULE 07 — OUTREACH SYSTEM
**What:** Turn the finished lead list into an actual client acquisition campaign.
**Playbook:** 3 Agency Retainer Structures

"Design the outreach campaign for my [N]-lead list. Channel: [email/LinkedIn/multichannel]. Sequence: [N] touchpoints over [N] days. For each touchpoint: channel, message angle, timing. Then present 3 retainer structures I can offer prospects who respond: (1) starter/low-commitment, (2) standard/growth, (3) full-service/premium. Include monthly price, deliverables, and the one-line pitch that closes each."

## FULL SYSTEM PROMPTS — PLAIN TEXT
