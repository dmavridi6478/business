---
description: List why a skeptical buyer says no, then improve the offer to address each objection
argument-hint: [offer or pitch to stress-test]
---

Act as a skeptical buyer considering the offer in "$ARGUMENTS". List every reason you would say no — the real objections, not the polite ones.

For each objection:
1. **State the objection** as the buyer would think it, not as the seller would hear it
2. **Rate it** — Core (would kill the deal) / Serious (meaningful hesitation) / Minor (background noise)
3. **Diagnose the source** — is this a trust gap, a value gap, a risk concern, a timing issue, or something else?
4. **Suggest the fix** — the specific change to the offer, message, or evidence that would neutralize this objection

End with the one thing that would do the most to move a skeptical buyer from "no" to "maybe" — and the one claim in the current offer that is hardest to believe.

If "$ARGUMENTS" is empty, ask what offer or pitch to stress-test before proceeding.
