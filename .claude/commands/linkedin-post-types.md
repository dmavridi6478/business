# 7 Types of LinkedIn Posts

Source: Chris Donnelly's $100M personal brand content mix

---

**1 – Awareness Posts**
Goal: Reach new people and spark interest.
Pre-validated ideas, opinion-led. "Opening the door."
Tip: Keep it simple. You're just opening the door.

**2 – Educational Posts**
Goal: Help your audience make better decisions.
Teach one useful idea someone can apply today.
Tip: Teach one useful idea your ICP can act on immediately.

**3 – Personal Story Posts**
Goal: Build trust and emotional connection.
Tell it like you'd tell a friend over a beer.
Tip: Build deeper relationships through vulnerability and relatability.

**4 – Proof of Work Posts**
Goal: Turn followers into leads or buyers.
Walk through the problem, decision, and outcome.
Tip: Show your methodology delivers results with specific numbers.

**5 – Building in Public Posts**
Goal: Create transparency and long-term trust.
Share the big decision you made and what changed.
Tip: Honest updates and lessons as you build — messy is good.

**6 – Lead Magnet Posts**
Goal: Turn attention into owned audience.
Solve a specific problem so clearly the value is obvious.
Tip: Offer something valuable in exchange for an email.

**7 – Newsjacking Posts**
Goal: Tap into existing attention for reach.
Move fast — relevance has a short shelf life.
Tip: Ride trending news and tie it to your expertise.

---

## Content Prompt Templates

**Awareness:** "Congrats on your failed [Business / Job / Side-Hustle]. Most people don't even try."

**Educational:** Write a carousel teaching [one actionable concept] that your ICP struggles with.

**Personal Story:** "I posted on LinkedIn once this week and here's what happened..."

**Proof of Work:** "How I built [specific result] in [specific timeframe] — the exact steps."

**Building in Public:** "We just [major decision]. Here's why and what we learned."

**Lead Magnet:** Comment "[KEYWORD]" for the full [resource name].

**Newsjacking:** "[Breaking news/trend] — here's what this means for [your niche]."
