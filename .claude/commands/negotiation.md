Use `salary-negotiation-kit` (method), `objection` and `deal-blocker-maneuvers`.

1. Set walk-away, target and opening numbers and the reasoning; mark market figures as assumptions to check.
2. Map the other side's interests, alternatives (their BATNA) and likely first moves.
3. List what you can trade (term, scope, payment, timing) in value order, and what you will not trade.
4. Write the opening sentence, the three likely objections with replies, and the close. Do not suggest bluffing or false claims. Note local legal limits.
