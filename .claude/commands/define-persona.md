# Define Your Expert Content Persona (@earchoe)

Build your AI content persona in 5 minutes: $ARGUMENTS

---

**PROMPT — paste into Claude:**

Help me define my content persona for AI and side hustle content.

About me: [your background, skills, what you have built]

My audience: [who I want to attract]

3 creators I admire and why: [list them]

The gap I see in my niche: [what most creators are not saying]

Words that describe my ideal tone: [give 5]

Give me:
1. My persona statement (2 sentences — who I write as)
2. My 3 non-negotiable style rules
3. 5 phrases I should use often
4. 5 phrases I should never use
5. The one POV that makes me different from everyone else in this space

---

**HOW TO USE YOUR PERSONA:**

Once Claude gives you your persona, save it to your Claude Project Instructions so it applies automatically to every chat — no re-pasting needed.

1. Open your Content Creation project in Claude
2. Paste the persona into the Project Instructions at the top
3. Every chat inside the project now starts from your persona — automatically

The compound effect: after 30 days of prompting with your persona, your content has a recognisable voice. That is what builds a following.

---
Usage: `/define-persona [your background, audience, admired creators, niche gap, tone words]`
