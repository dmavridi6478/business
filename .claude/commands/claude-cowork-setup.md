# How to Configure Claude Cowork in 2 Hours

Source: AIForLeaders.com infographic, "10 practical steps to get your Claude
Cowork ready and organized."

This is a setup guide for Claude Cowork specifically (the desktop/consumer
product), not this Claude Code environment — steps 1, 2, 7, and 9 describe
actions inside the Claude desktop app that this sandboxed environment has no
way to click through. Documented here as a reference procedure; steps 3-6
and 10 (folder structure, About Me files, global instructions, templates)
are things you can ask Claude Code to draft the *content* for even though it
can't install them into the Cowork app itself.

## The 10 steps

1. **Download the app** — Claude app, not browser.
2. **Subscribe to Pro** — $20/mo; use Opus for complex tasks.
3. **Create the Cowork folder** — subfolders: `ABOUT ME`, `OUTPUTS`, `TEMPLATES`.
4. **Add About Me files** — `about-me.md`, `company.md`, `style.md`.
5. **Have Claude interview you** — your answers become your context file.
6. **Define global instructions** — Settings → Cowork → Edit.
7. **Choose the model** — Opus for deep work, Sonnet for short tasks.
8. **Install Obsidian.md** — open the Cowork folder as a vault.
9. **Do your first session** — "Read my folder. Help me with [task]."
10. **Create templates** — save useful structures for reuse.

## Draftable now, inside this repo

Ask Claude Code to produce the three About Me files (step 4) and the
interview questions (step 5) right now, then copy the output into your local
Cowork folder once it exists:

```
Draft about-me.md, company.md, and style.md for [my name/role/business],
following the "About Me files" pattern from Claude Cowork setup: about-me.md
covers who I am and how I work, company.md covers what the business does and
who it serves, style.md covers tone/formatting preferences for anything
Claude writes for me.
```

```
Interview me the way Claude Cowork's onboarding does — ask me one question
at a time about my role, my business, my typical tasks, and my working
style, then compile my answers into a single context file I can drop into a
Cowork folder.
```

This repo's `claude-start-business` skill already documents a related but
distinct workflow (validating and launching a new business via Claude); this
guide is narrower — it's about configuring the Cowork *app environment*
itself, not what to build inside it.
