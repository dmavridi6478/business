## 4-TOOL PROMPTING CHEAT SHEET

---

### CHATGPT — Prompt like an instructor
**Goal:** Plan a task and get clear, useful steps.
**Best for:** Planning, drafting, and broad help across almost any task.

**7 Tips:**
1. Start with a role — "act as a coach"
2. Say exactly what you need help with
3. Ask for a step-by-step plan
4. Add background so it sees the full picture
5. Ask for the format you want, like a table or list
6. Read the reply and ask follow-ups to sharpen it
7. Ask it to suggest tools or next moves

---

### CLAUDE — Prompt like a careful teammate
**Goal:** Get deep writing, coding, and real work done, not just answers.
**Best for:** Writing, coding, careful reasoning, and tasks built on your own work.

**6 Tips:**
1. Pick the strongest model and turn on extended thinking
2. Tell Claude who you are and how you work — save it once
3. Use action words, like "find the risks" or "rewrite this"
4. Set the length, format, and tone up front
5. Hand it real jobs — it can build, code, and edit files, not just talk
6. Connect your apps so Claude reads your mail, drive, and calendar

---

### PERPLEXITY — Prompt like a research analyst
**Goal:** Get facts you can trust, with sources shown.
**Best for:** Market research, fact-checking, and answers backed by links.

**7 Tips:**
1. Ask one clear, specific question
2. Add the names, dates, or place that matter
3. Ask it to show sources so you can check the answer
4. Keep your words plain and direct
5. Ask for short summary points for fast review
6. Use deep research mode to read many pages at once
7. Follow up to dig deeper or pull more sources

---

### GEMINI — Prompt like a project planner
**Goal:** Plan work and pull from your Google apps.
**Best for:** Project plans, Google app workflows, and turning media into text.

**6 Tips:**
1. Say what kind of output you want, like a plan or doc
2. Name who it is for and what it should do
3. Ask for a clear breakdown, like day-by-day steps
4. Ask for a table, list, or chart if it helps
5. Have it pull from Gmail, Drive, and Calendar
6. Use it to turn long videos or audio into text

## AI PROMPTS — PLAIN TEXT

**ChatGPT instructor prompt template:**
Act as a [role, e.g. business coach]. I need help with [specific task]. Here's the background: [context]. Give me a step-by-step plan in [format], and suggest tools or next moves at the end.

**Claude careful teammate prompt template:**
I'm a [role], working on [project/company]. My preferred output is [format]. Please [action verb: rewrite/find/build/analyse] [specific thing]. Keep it [length/tone]. Use [specific constraints].

**Perplexity research analyst prompt template:**
What is [specific factual question] as of [date/year]? Focus on [specific angle]. Show your sources. Give me a 5-point summary I can verify.

**Gemini project planner prompt template:**
I'm planning [project/campaign]. Who it's for: [audience]. Pull relevant info from [Gmail/Drive/Calendar if connected]. Give me a [plan/table/timeline] broken down by [day/week/phase].

**Cross-tool workflow:**
I need to [goal]. Route this across tools: (1) use Perplexity to research [fact or market], (2) use Claude to [write/build/analyse] based on what Perplexity found, (3) use ChatGPT to [plan/structure] the output, (4) use Gemini to [schedule/organise/connect to Google]. Give me the complete cross-tool workflow.
