argument-hint: [business stage or current bottleneck]

You are a revenue performance analyst. Run all 6 agents for "$ARGUMENTS":

**Agent 01 — Content Performance Tracking**
Define the 5 metrics that matter most for content performance at this stage (not vanity metrics). For each: metric name, how to measure it, what a good/bad number looks like, and the weekly review question it answers.

Build a weekly content review ritual (15 minutes): what to check, what to record, what decision it triggers.

**Agent 02 — Conversion Rate Analysis**
Build a pipeline conversion dashboard. Define the full funnel stages from content impression to signed client. For each stage: conversion rate benchmark, current estimate, gap, and one lever to improve it.

Identify the one stage where improving conversion by 10% would have the biggest revenue impact.

**Agent 03 — Pipeline Velocity**
Calculate pipeline velocity using: (Number of Opportunities × Average Deal Value × Win Rate) ÷ Sales Cycle Length.

Walk through how to measure each variable, what the current velocity estimate is, and the single biggest constraint to increasing it. Propose one specific action to reduce sales cycle length by 20%.

**Agent 04 — Revenue Attribution**
Map which content types, channels, and activities are generating revenue. Build a simple attribution model: for each pipeline deal, which touchpoints contributed (first touch, last touch, assist)?

Identify what to stop (low attribution, high time cost), what to scale (high attribution, low time cost), and what to test (high attribution potential, not yet proven).

**Agent 05 — Engagement Quality Scoring**
Create an engagement quality scoring system (not just likes/comments). Score each post on: depth of comments (surface vs. substantive), commenter quality (ICP or not), DMs generated, profile views driven, and leads initiated.

Assign a quality score (1–10) and define what a score of 8+ looks like vs. a score of 3 or below.

**Agent 06 — Weekly Performance Report**
Design a one-page weekly performance report template covering all 6 modules. Include:
- Module name and RAG status (Red/Amber/Green)
- One key metric per module
- One win this week
- One action next week
- Overall system health score (1–10) with commentary

The report must take under 20 minutes to complete and under 5 minutes to read.

Deliver each agent clearly labelled. Make every output immediately actionable.
