Use the `executive-presence-8` skill on: "$ARGUMENTS"

1. If nothing is supplied, ask for the text and the meeting context (audience, stakes) in one message.
2. Score the 8 habits 0–2 with a quoted line as evidence for each.
3. Count undermining phrases (just, kind of, I think maybe, sorry to bother).
4. Rewrite the three weakest lines, keeping the speaker's meaning.
5. Finish with one habit to practise this week and its exact phrase.
Do not invent quotes or judge the person; judge the text.
