## THE 5 MENTORING FRAMEWORKS

---

### 01 | SOAR
**Best for:** Strengths-based career conversations. Shifts focus from problems to possibilities and aspirations.

| Letter | Stands For | Key Question |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| S | Strengths | What do you do brilliantly? |
| O | Opportunities | What's possible for you? |
| A | Aspirations | What do you want to create? |
| R | Results | How will you measure success? |

**AI prompt to use:**
"Based on what I know about [person]'s recent work, help me identify 3 specific strengths to open this conversation with."

**Run SOAR — deliver:**
1. 3 specific strengths to name at the start (evidence-based)
2. 2–3 genuine opportunities relevant to their current role/stage
3. A sharp aspiration question tailored to where they want to go
4. A measurable success definition they can track over 90 days

---

### 02 | CLEAR
**Best for:** Formal mentoring relationships where contracting and trust-building matter most.

| Letter | Stands For | Focus |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| C | Contracting | Agree ground rules |
| L | Listening | Deep active listening |
| E | Exploring | Examine the situation |
| A | Action | Plan next steps |
| R | Review | Reflect and close |

**AI prompt to use:**
"Help me draft 3 ground rules to propose at the start of this mentoring relationship."

**Run CLEAR — deliver:**
1. 3 proposed ground rules for the mentoring contract (confidentiality, honesty, commitment)
2. 5 active listening questions to explore the mentee's situation fully
3. A structured action plan template (what, by when, who supports)
4. A closing reflection prompt to end each session with intention

---

### 03 | GROW
**Best for:** Goal-setting sessions with a clear gap between current and desired state.

| Letter | Stands For | Key Question |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| G | Goal | What do you want to achieve? |
| R | Reality | Where are you now? |
| O | Options | What could you try? |
| W | Will/Way forward | Commit to action |

**AI prompt to use:**
"Given this person's current situation and stated goal, help me think through 3 realistic options they might not have considered."

**Run GROW — deliver:**
1. A sharpened goal statement (specific, time-bound, personally meaningful)
2. An honest current-state assessment with 3 gap areas to name
3. 5 options they may not have considered (creative, practical, and contrarian)
4. A commitment script: the exact words to help them commit to a first action by a specific date

---

### 04 | OSCAR
**Best for:** Solution-focused coaching where the mentee leads the conversation.

| Letter | Stands For | Key Question |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| O | Outcome | What do you want? |
| S | Situation | Where are you now? |
| C | Choices | What options exist? |
| A | Actions | How did it go? |
| R | Review | Reflect and close |

**AI prompt to use:**
"Help me prepare open-ended questions for each OSCAR stage so I lead with questions, not answers."

**Run OSCAR — deliver:**
1. 3 open-ended Outcome questions (future-focused, not problem-focused)
2. 3 Situation questions (non-judgmental, curious)
3. 4 Choices questions that expand thinking without directing it
4. 2 Actions review questions (what happened, what did you learn)
5. 2 Review/close questions to cement the insight

---

### 05 | FUEL
**Best for:** Performance-focused mentoring where outcomes and accountability drive results.

| Letter | Stands For | Focus |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| F | Frame | Set the conversation context |
| U | Understand | Current state and gap |
| E | Explore | Desired future state |
| L | Lay out | A clear success plan |

**AI prompt to use:**
"Summarize this person's last 3 performance conversations so I walk in with full context on where we left off."

**Run FUEL — deliver:**
1. A session framing statement (2 sentences that set context and purpose)
2. 4 diagnostic questions to understand the current performance gap
3. A future-state visioning exercise (where does great look like in 90 days?)
4. A written success plan: 3 commitments, 3 milestones, 1 accountability check-in
