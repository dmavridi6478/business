# Why Your Prompts Keep Failing — 4 Fixes

Source: @learnaiwithdoyin. Vague requests get vague answers — 4 fixes that make AI actually understand what you want.

## Fix 01 — Say What Good Looks Like

"Make it better" means nothing to AI. Describe the outcome you actually want.

```
Rewrite this email to sound more confident and 30% shorter. Keep the same
key points and a friendly closing.
```

## Fix 02 — Give It The Missing Context

AI cannot read your inbox or your mind. Hand over the details it actually needs.

```
Here is the background: [paste]. Given that, draft a reply that addresses
the client's main concern directly.
```

## Fix 03 — Tell It What Format You Want

A wall of text is not always the answer. Specify the shape of the output.

```
Answer in a table with 3 columns: option, cost, and time required. No
extra explanation outside the table.
```

## Fix 04 — Push Back On The First Answer

The first draft is rarely the best one. Treat it as a starting point, not the finish line.

```
That is too generic. Make it more specific to my situation and cut
anything that could apply to any business.
```

## Which fix explains your last bad AI answer?

If none of your last few prompts specified an outcome, context, format, and a follow-up push, you were probably missing 2–3 of the 4 at once — check each prompt against all four before blaming the model.
