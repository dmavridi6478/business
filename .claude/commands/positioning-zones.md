Use the `brand-positioning-zones` skill. Brand and category: "$ARGUMENTS"

1. If competitors or the target segment are missing, ask for them first (one message).
2. Build the candidate-claim list (8–15), score Want / Best / Competitor strength for each with evidence, and label every score "verified" or "assumed".
3. Show the zone table. A claim with no proof for "Best" cannot be Winning.
4. Pick one lead claim + up to two supporting claims, write the positioning statement, and list the research needed to convert "assumed" into "verified".
