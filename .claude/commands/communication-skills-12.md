# 12 Communication Skills of High Performers

Source: Gav Blaxberg (@WOLF_Financial).

A reference checklist for what separates high-performer communication from
average — useful as a self-audit before a high-stakes call, pitch, or
negotiation, or as a coaching rubric for a team.

| Skill | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cultural Awareness** | Understand and respect cultural differences; communicate effectively across diverse groups |
| **Active Listening** | Listen attentively and empathetically; ensure full understanding before responding |
| **Clarity and Conciseness** | Convey thoughts clearly and concisely; avoid unnecessary jargon |
| **Storytelling** | Use narratives to illustrate points and engage the audience; make complex information relatable and memorable |
| **Nonverbal Communication** | Read and use body language, facial expressions, and eye contact; reinforce messages and understand others better |
| **Conflict Resolution** | Handle conflicts calmly and diplomatically; aim for win-win outcomes, maintain positive relationships |
| **Open-Mindedness** | Be open to new ideas and perspectives; foster collaborative and innovative environments |
| **Adaptability** | Adjust communication style to suit different audiences and situations |
| **Empathy** | Show genuine interest and concern for others' feelings and perspectives; foster trust and build strong relationships |
| **Persuasiveness** | Use compelling arguments and evidence to influence others; find common ground and present appealing solutions |
| **Constructive Feedback** | Provide and receive feedback constructively; focus on growth and improvement rather than criticism |
| **Confidence** | Speak assertively and with confidence; persuade and influence others effectively |

## Plain-text self-audit prompt

```
Score me 1-5 on each of these 12 communication skills based on this
[transcript/email/pitch I'm about to give]: Cultural Awareness, Active
Listening, Clarity and Conciseness, Storytelling, Nonverbal Communication,
Conflict Resolution, Open-Mindedness, Adaptability, Empathy, Persuasiveness,
Constructive Feedback, Confidence. For anything below 4, tell me the
specific thing to change, not generic advice.
```
