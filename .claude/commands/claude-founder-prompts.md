## THE 11 FOUNDER PROMPTS FOR CLAUDE

### 01 | FULL EFFORT
**When to use:** When you need Claude's best thinking, not a safe, hedged answer.
**Prompt:** "Give me your full, unfiltered best thinking. Don't hold back."
**Why it works:** Removes the default tendency to hedge. Claude gives you the sharper, more opinionated output you actually need.

---

### 02 | SOUND HUMAN
**When to use:** When Claude's output sounds like an AI wrote it — stiff, generic, overly polished.
**Prompt:** "Write this so it sounds like a person wrote it, not an AI."
**Why it works:** Shifts register from formal to natural. Removes "delve", "certainly", and hollow filler. Better for emails, LinkedIn posts, sales copy.

---

### 03 | BUILD IT AS AN ARTIFACT
**When to use:** When a wall of text is the wrong output and a visual, table, or interactive element would serve better.
**Prompt:** "Turn this into a visual or interactive format instead of plain text."
**Why it works:** Triggers Claude to produce HTML artifacts, comparison tables, flowcharts, or structured layouts — not just prose.

---

### 04 | ARGUE THE OTHER SIDE
**When to use:** Before committing to a strategy, plan, or decision. Stress-test your thinking.
**Prompt:** "Argue the opposite side of this before I commit to it."
**Why it works:** Forces you to see the strongest objection to your plan. Prevents confirmation bias and surfaces risks you haven't considered.

---

### 05 | THINK BEFORE ANSWERING
**When to use:** For complex, multi-step problems where the first answer is usually wrong.
**Prompt:** "Think through this step by step, out loud, before answering."
**Why it works:** Activates extended reasoning. You see the thinking process, can catch errors, and get a more reliable final answer.

---

### 06 | COMPRESS IT
**When to use:** When Claude's output is too long and you need the core idea fast.
**Prompt:** "Compress this to 3 lines max. No filler."
**Why it works:** Forces Claude to prioritize ruthlessly. The compression reveals what actually matters and removes everything decorative.

---

### 07 | COMPARISON TABLE
**When to use:** When evaluating options, tools, approaches, or strategies.
**Prompt:** "Give me this as a comparison table."
**Why it works:** Structured comparison is faster to read than paragraphs. Reveals gaps and trade-offs that prose obscures.

---

### 08 | REVERSE-ENGINEER IT
**When to use:** When something is working well (a competitor's email, a great ad, a product feature) and you want to understand why.
**Prompt:** "Reverse-engineer why this works."
**Why it works:** Claude breaks down the mechanism — the psychological trigger, structural choice, or timing — not just the surface features. Gives you replicable frameworks.

---

### 09 | BRUTAL HONESTY
**When to use:** When you want real feedback, not a polished validation of your existing plan.
**Prompt:** "Give me brutally honest feedback. No sugarcoating."
**Why it works:** Drops the diplomatic wrapper. Claude names the actual weaknesses, not buried-in-paragraph-three soft notes.

---

### 10 | SKIP THE EXPLANATION
**When to use:** When you already understand the context and just need the deliverable — not a lesson.
**Prompt:** "Just do the task. Skip the explanation."
**Why it works:** Removes the preamble, the "here's what I'll do," and the summary. Cuts response time in half for repeat tasks.

---

### 11 | STAY ON TASK
**When to use:** When Claude starts solving adjacent problems, adding unsolicited context, or drifting from your specific ask.
**Prompt:** "Stay focused only on this task. No tangents."
**Why it works:** Keeps Claude scoped. Prevents scope creep in long tasks where additional "helpful" context derails the core deliverable.

## COMBINATION RECIPES

**For a fast decision brief:**
"[Context]. Argue the other side. Then compress your conclusion to 3 lines max. No filler."

**For high-quality copy:**
"[Draft or brief]. Give me your full, unfiltered best thinking. Write so it sounds like a person wrote it, not an AI. Stay focused only on this task."

**For analyzing what's working:**
"Here is [example: email, ad, post, pitch]. Reverse-engineer why this works. Give me a comparison table of the key elements vs. the generic version. No tangents."

**For stress-testing a strategy:**
"Here is my plan: [plan]. Think through this step by step, out loud, before answering. Then argue the opposite side. Then give me brutally honest feedback."

**For fast execution:**
"[Task]. Just do the task. Skip the explanation. Compress the output to what's actually needed."
