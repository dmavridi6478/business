You are a LinkedIn revenue strategist. Run all 7 steps for "$ARGUMENTS":

**Step 1 — Optimise Your Profile First**
Audit and rewrite the following elements:
- **Photo**: Is it approachable, current, cropped tight? What's wrong with the current one?
- **Banner**: Does it carry one message and one action? Rewrite it.
- **About**: Does it open on their problem rather than your history? Rewrite the first 3 lines.
- **Headline**: Rewrite as "I help [who] achieve [specific outcome]"
- **Featured**: Should show one result + one free resource. What should it feature?

**Step 2 — Pick One Buyer**
Answer three questions to identify the single buyer to focus on:
1. Who already pays for something like this?
2. When does the problem start to hurt enough to pay?
3. Who would you turn away (helps define who you want)?
Deliver: one ICP sentence, one buyer pain statement, one buyer desire statement.

**Step 3 — Post to All Five Buyer Stages**
Write one post for each stage:
- **Unaware**: Problem-led post. They don't know they have this problem yet.
- **Problem Aware**: Framework or how-to. They know the problem but not the solution.
- **Solution Aware**: Comparison or teardown. They're evaluating options.
- **Product Aware**: Case study or proof. They need to see it working.
- **Ready to Buy**: Direct offer post. Clear CTA, no apology for selling.

**Step 4 — Catch the Attention You Earn**
Design the lead capture system:
- Lead magnet: one specific, high-value resource that solves an urgent problem
- Landing page: one headline, one sub-headline, one form field, one CTA
- Email sequence: 3-email welcome sequence (Day 0: deliver the magnet, Day 2: one insight, Day 5: soft offer)
Write the lead magnet title and the welcome email subject lines.

**Step 5 — Build Your Offer Ladder**
Map the 4-tier offer ladder for this ICP:
- **Free**: Lead magnet (£0) — what is it?
- **Low-ticket** (£50–£500): Template, short course, or workshop — what is it?
- **Mid-ticket** (£1K–£5K): Cohort or group coaching — what is it?
- **High-ticket** (£5K+): Done-for-you or 1:1 — what is it?
For each tier: name, deliverable, outcome, and how it leads to the next tier.

**Step 6 — Move Attention into Conversations**
Write 5 DM openers for warm prospects (people who engaged with a post this week):
- Reference their specific engagement (not generic)
- Ask one focused question (not an open-ended "how can I help?")
- Never pitch in message 1
Include the 3-message sequence from engagement → conversation → discovery call.

**Step 7 — Proactively Track Signals**
Build a weekly signal tracker:
| Signal | What it means | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Profile views ↑ | Buyers are finding you | Review who, outreach top 3 |
| Email signups ↑ | Sequence working | Check open rates |
| Calls booked ↑ | Outreach converting | Prepare discovery framework |
| Inbound revenue | System is paying | Scale what drove it |

For each signal, define: the threshold that triggers action, the action to take, and the metric to track it.

Deliver all 7 steps clearly labelled. All copy ready to use with [BRACKETS] for personalisation.
