3. Score each practice 0–3 from the evidence. "We sort of do it" = 1. Show the scoring table.
4. Find the innermost ring under 2.0. Explain in two sentences why the outer rings must wait.
5. Give exactly three 30-day actions for that ring, each with owner, date and evidence of done.
6. List the outer-ring ideas I raised as "parked", with the condition that unparks each.
