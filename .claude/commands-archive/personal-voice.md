description: Rewrite text so it sounds like you, using your voice profile or 2-3 samples of your writing
argument-hint: <text to rewrite> | <2-3 samples of your own writing, or 'use my profile'>
---

Use the skill `prompt-writing-9-skills` (step 06) and the voice profile from `/voice-builder` if one exists. Input: "$ARGUMENTS".

1. If no profile or samples are given, ask for 2-3 samples (about 150 words each) before rewriting.
2. Describe the voice in 5 bullets (sentence length, vocabulary, punctuation habits, what the writer never says, typical openings), then rewrite the text in it.
3. Do not change facts, numbers or commitments. Output the rewrite first, the 5-bullet voice description second.
