# Shadow Work

Use a birth date to explore blind spots, defense mechanisms, and self-sabotaging patterns.

## Prompt

```
Use only my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] to explore my potential blind spots.

Identify internal conflicts, defense mechanisms, and patterns that could sabotage my relationships or decisions.

For each pattern, explain:

what triggers the cycle, how I react → what need is behind it, how I could break it

I don't want you to label me. I want you to show me what I might not want to admit about myself.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/shadow-work` for deep self-reflection work — examining patterns you keep repeating, relationship blind spots, or behaviors that undermine your own goals. Replace `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your birth date before sending.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude, replacing `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your actual date of birth
3. Claude will identify recurring patterns and explain the trigger → reaction → need → break cycle for each
