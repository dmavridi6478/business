Hallmark is a large repo (CLI, a marketing site under `site/`, docs, and a
roadmap) built around one portable skill directory. The entire
`skills/hallmark/` tree — SKILL.md plus its full `references/` library
(anti-patterns, component cookbook, 21 named themes, 21 macrostructures,
typography/color/motion/layout guidance, and the `audit`/`redesign`/`study`
verb protocols) — is vendored here verbatim, since SKILL.md depends on those
reference files to function. Nothing outside `skills/hallmark/` (the CLI,
site, docs, roadmap) is included.
