# GTM in 2026, then and now (Monika Grycz graphic, animated)

Source: an animated GIF; the final frame shows both columns. Opinion, no data behind it.

**Then, "a CRM and a sequencer":** Salesforce (CRM), ZoomInfo (contact data), Sales Navigator (prospecting), Outreach (sequences), Gmail (cold email), Excel (lead lists), Gong (call recording).

**Now, "a stack per job":**
| Job | Tools and role on the graphic |
|---|---|
| AI and research | Claude (writing and strategy), ChatGPT (ideation), Perplexity (account research), Clay (enrichment, workflows) |
| Data | Apollo (B2B database), Prospeo (email finding), FullEnrich (phone finding), RB2B (website visitors) |
| Outreach | Smartlead (cold email), HeyReach (LinkedIn outreach), Nooks (AI dialer) |
| Ops and CRM | HubSpot (CRM), Claude Code (internal tools, scripts), n8n (automation), Fireflies (call insights) |
| Channels | LinkedIn (founder content), YouTube (video), X (short-form), Webinars (live education), Luma (in-person events), WhatsApp / Skool (community) |

## Read critically
- **[Certain]** "Then" tools are still the core of many large sales teams; the graphic describes a small-team or founder-led stack.
- **[Certain]** Cold email, mobile-number finding and visitor de-anonymisation (RB2B) have legal exposure in the EU and some US states; get legal review first.
- **[Likely]** Fewer tools is not automatically better: seven separate point tools create integration and data-quality work.
