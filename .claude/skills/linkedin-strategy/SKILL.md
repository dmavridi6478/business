# LinkedIn Strategy 2026

**Invoke:** `/linkedin-strategy`

## Purpose
Apply the 2026 LinkedIn algorithm reality to content planning, post auditing, and engagement strategy. Based on confirmed platform behavior, not speculation.

## The 7 Algorithm Shifts

### 1. Reach Got Redistributed
- The median profile barely moved — the floor held
- Virality became significantly harder for large, established creators
- Going viral used to be the goal. Now, **consistent reach matters more**

### 2. The Follower Moat Is Gone
- Accounts with 1–5K followers now see the highest engagement rates
- Accounts over 100K sit at just 1.53% engagement
- Your size stopped protecting you — **positioning does the work now**

### 3. LinkedIn Is Now Interest Media
- LinkedIn confirmed its feed runs on LLMs
- It builds a topic profile per user, then matches them to strangers outside their network
- **Your topic consistency matters more than your connections**

### 4. The AI Slop Penalty Is Real
- LinkedIn confirmed it can detect generic, repetitive content
- That content gets throttled on purpose — not banned, just buried
- **Original thinking beats recycled frameworks every time**

### 5. Hooks Matter, Formulas Don't
- Posts that open in first person see 20% higher engagement
- No single post type or template wins consistently on its own
- **Real copywriting beats copy-paste formulas every time**

### 6. Four Myths That Died This Year
1. Hashtags used to be a growth lever. Now they barely register in the algorithm.
2. Post length stopped predicting performance — short and long both work.
3. Nobody agrees on the best time to post.
4. Links used to hurt reach on principle. Now they're just a coin flip.

### 7. Impressions Aren't The Metric
- A viral post can still bring in zero leads
- Views and impressions don't pay the bills — they just feel good to look at
- A million views means nothing if none of it turns into a conversation
- **Optimize for conversations, not impressions**

## Usage

When invoked, ask the user what they need:
- **Post audit** → apply all 7 shifts to critique a draft
- **Content strategy** → build a positioning-first plan using shifts 2–5
- **30-day calendar** → plan consistent-reach content avoiding dead myths (shift 6)
- **Engagement audit** → identify why a post underperformed using shifts 1, 4, 5, 7

Then deliver structured output and offer to run the next logical step.
