# Your marketing metrics can lie

Source: six metric pairs, each "what you report" and "what checks it". The review procedure is added.

| # | What you report | How it misleads | What checks it | What the check shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost per lead | Drops fastest with wider, weaker targeting | Leads sales accepts | Cheaper leads with the same acceptance rate = real progress |
| 2 | Click-through rate | A clickbait headline brings the wrong clicks | Landing-page sign-ups | Did the page keep the ad's promise? |
| 3 | ROAS | Looks high on people about to buy anyway | Revenue from new customers | Customers you did not have before |
| 4 | Conversion rate | Big discounts push it up | Still customers at 90 days | Revenue that stays, not just checkouts |
| 5 | Email open rate | Many opens are now recorded automatically (machine opens) | Clicks and unsubscribes | Real interest vs sending too much |
| 6 | Website traffic | Grows with readers who will never buy | Visits to pricing or demo | Fewer, better-informed visits can win |

## Review procedure

1. List every headline metric in the report.
2. For each, find its checking metric in the data. If it is absent, write "unchecked" beside the headline.
3. Compare direction: a headline improving while its check is flat or falling = **suspect**; both improving = **credible**; headline flat but check improving = **under-reported win**.
4. Quantify with the user's own figures only (e.g. cost per accepted lead = spend / accepted leads). Show the arithmetic.
5. Conclude per metric: credible, suspect, unchecked. Recommend the one change to the report: add the check next to the headline.

## Rules

- Never name a "good" threshold; none is in the source. Compare with the same account's prior period.
- For ROAS, state the attribution window and whether branded and retargeting spend are included; incrementality needs a holdout test, not a ratio.
- For open rate, note that Apple Mail Privacy Protection and security scanners inflate opens; prefer clicks, replies, conversions.
- Report counts and definitions with each figure.

## Output

A table: metric | headline value | check value | direction | verdict | one-line reason. Then the three most urgent report fixes.
