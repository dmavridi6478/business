# Agentic marketing in one page (Ryze AI sheet)

Source: one 16-card sheet ("how to run Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO and AI search with a team of AI agents"; header counts 40 agents, 16 metrics, 24 guardrails, 1 real run). **The body text of each card was too small to read**, so only titles and the visible structure are recorded. The sheet ends "Comment Agents to get the guide" (Ryze AI, get-ryze.ai); nothing was commented, and the agent definitions behind it were not provided.

| 3 | The anatomy of an AI agent | Six parts: Role, Data, Rules, Output, Cadence, Approval, with a sample agent prompt |

| 15 | The agent guardrails checklist | Four groups: money, access, content, records |
| 16 | The agentic CMO checklist | Checklists by horizon (0 to 10, 11 to 20, 21 to 30 and beyond) |

## Using the structure
- **[Likely]** The most useful ideas are the ladder (do not jump to autonomy), the six-part agent definition and the delegation matrix: automate reporting, require approval for spend and publishing, keep strategy human. This repo already has equivalents: `claude-code-marketing-team-50-roles`, `governed-marketing-team`, `os-approval`, `agentic-marketing-levels`, `claude-six-levels`.
- **[Certain]** Agents that change ad budgets or publish content must have hard spend limits, read-only access first, and an approval step; the sheet's own "money" and "access" guardrails say the same.
- **[Guessing]** The "real run" is one example, not evidence of results.
