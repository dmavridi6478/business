## Reading the list
- Use cases 6, 7, 12, 13, 28, 42, 43 and 50 depend on specific products or plans; the others work in plain chat. Do not buy a plan for one line on an infographic.
- Nothing on the list is a claim about quality. For 26 (contracts), 27 (financial models) and 8 (formal documents) a qualified person must review the output.
