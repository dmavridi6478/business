# 15 marketing terms (Alec Rickard sheet)

Source: one infographic. Definitions are the sheet's; the corrections, examples and tests are my additions.

| # | Term | Sheet's definition | My note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CAC | What you spend to win one new customer. Payback = CAC / average monthly profit per customer | Include all sales and marketing cost for the period, not only ad spend |
| 2 | LTV | Total revenue a customer brings over time = average purchase value x purchase frequency x customer lifespan | That is revenue. Most teams use gross-profit LTV; compare LTV to CAC only on the same basis |
| 3 | Conversion rate | Percentage of visitors who take the wanted action | Define the action and the denominator (sessions, visitors, leads) |
| 4 | Attribution modelling | Tracking which channel drove the sale | Models (first touch, last touch, multi-touch) give different answers; none is the truth |
| 5 | A/B testing | Two versions, see which wins (sheet: A 4.2% vs B 6.1%) | A gap needs enough traffic and a planned stopping point to be believed |
| 6 | Marketing flywheel | Growth model where happy customers fuel more growth | Needs real referral or repeat data to claim |
| 7 | Demand gen vs lead gen | Long-term brand building versus capturing ready buyers now | Both matter; budget split depends on stage |
| 8 | Intent-based marketing | Content matched to where buyers are in the journey | Intent signals are often probabilistic |
| 9 | Omnichannel | One consistent brand across every channel a buyer touches | Consistency of data and message, not presence everywhere |
| 10 | EEAT | Google's ranking standard: Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust | It is a quality-rater guideline, not a single ranking factor |
| 11 | UGC | Real content from real customers, strongest proof | Get permission to reuse it |
| 12 | Content velocity | How often and consistently you publish | Consistency matters more than raw volume |
| 13 | Repurposing | One strong post into 8 or more assets | The "8" is illustrative |
| 14 | Generative AI | Tools that draft text, images, audio and video from prompts | Review for accuracy and rights |
| 15 | Prompt engineering | Sharper inputs for sharper outputs, e.g. "Write an email to [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC] with a [TONE] tone" | Add context, examples and a format |

## Worked examples (my numbers, for practice)

- CAC: 4,000 spent on sales and marketing, 40 new customers, CAC = 100.
- Payback: monthly profit per customer 25, payback = 100 / 25 = 4 months.
- LTV (revenue): 60 average purchase x 4 purchases a year x 3 years = 720; at 40% gross margin, profit LTV = 288, so LTV:CAC = 2.9.
- Conversion rate: 3,000 visitors, 90 sign-ups = 3.0%.
