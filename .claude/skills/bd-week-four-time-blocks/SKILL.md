# 80 minutes a week of business development
| Day | Block | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Mon, 15 min | **Plan Your Week** (Snowball System) | Pick the 3 most important relationship tasks; make them about people, not paperwork; block the time now, before the calendar fills |
| Wed, 30 min | **Give Something** (The Four Gifts) | Choose 3 people who matter; give each something useful (article, introduction, advice, invite); send with no pitch and no favour attached |
| Thu, 30 min | **Deepen One Relationship** (Relationship Ladder) | Pick one important person; decide the next step that brings you closer; take that one step this week |
| Fri, 5 min | **Review and Reset** (Psychological Momentum) | Count what you got done; celebrate small wins; decide next week's focus |
The infographic wraps these around fixed meetings/client work/delivery; only the four BD blocks are the method.
Run it: `/bd-week` builds the week as calendar blocks (draft events only; Google Calendar connector creates events only after you confirm). Pair with `linkedin-signal-outreach`, `client-follow-up`, `new-client-system`.
