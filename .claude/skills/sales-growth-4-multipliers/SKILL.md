# How to accelerate your sales growth

Source: an infographic by Eric Partaker (Batch 118). It is a heuristic summary, not research; the percentage rules on it are folk wisdom and carry no cited source.

## The 4 multipliers

**Sales = Leads x Conversion rate x Average deal size x Retention rate**

| Multiplier | Lever on the card | Ways to move it |
|---|---|---|
| Leads | Increase high-quality prospects | Better targeting, more channels, referrals |
| Conversion rate | Improve sales effectiveness | Qualification, objection handling, case studies |
| Average deal size | Upsell, bundle, price strategically | Packaging, tiers, expansion offers |
| Retention rate | Keep customers engaged and buying again | Onboarding, success reviews, renewals |

**Why multiplicative matters (checked):** a 10% gain on each of the four compounds to 1.1^4 = 1.464, a 46.4% increase in sales, not 40%. A 10% gain on one only gives 10%. Conversely a zero in any factor zeroes the result, so fix the weakest multiplier first.

**Caveat (added):** treat the formula as a diagnostic, not accounting. Retention drives repeat and lifetime revenue, not first-sale revenue, so for a subscription business use customer lifetime value instead (see `saas-growth-efficiency-metrics`). The factors are also not independent: pushing leads up usually lowers conversion.

## 3-step sales acceleration
1. Speed up lead qualification: lead scoring to prioritise high-value prospects.
2. Shorten sales cycles: pre-empt common objections early.
3. Increase win rates: case studies, testimonials, ROI-focused pitches.

## What separates top sales teams
Process-driven (a structured process followed consistently), data-driven (track key metrics and adjust), customer-centric (build trust, solve real pain points).

## Closing rules
- **70/30 rule:** let the prospect talk about 70% of the time and listen carefully. A rule of thumb; use it as a reminder to ask more than you tell.
- **3-yes rule:** get three small "yeses" before asking for the big one. Use it as a way to confirm real agreement (need, budget, timing). Used as a pressure ladder it becomes manipulation and it backfires with informed buyers.

## 5 ways to generate leads
Inbound (SEO, LinkedIn, content), outbound (cold email, LinkedIn outreach, strategic prospecting), referral programs, partnerships and affiliates, paid (LinkedIn and Google ads). "Diversify your lead sources; do not rely on one channel."

## Key sales metrics
| Metric | Why it matters | Formula (added) |
|---|---|---|
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | What you spend to acquire each customer | Sales and marketing spend / new customers |
| Lifetime value (LTV) | Total revenue per customer over time | Average revenue per period x gross margin x expected lifetime |
| Win rate | Share of deals closed versus total | Deals won / deals worked |
| Sales cycle length | Average time to close | Mean days from opportunity creation to close |
| Churn rate | Share of customers who stop buying | Customers lost / customers at period start |

Pro tip on the card: reduce CAC while increasing LTV. Healthy ratios and payback benchmarks are in `saas-growth-efficiency-metrics` and `ceo-kpi-framework`.
