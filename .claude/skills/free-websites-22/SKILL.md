## Rules of thumb
1. Free web tools are paid for with data, ads or upsell; read what is uploaded and kept.
2. Do not put confidential documents, personal data or client material into any online tool unless the terms allow it and your policy permits.
3. Ad clones exist: type the address yourself rather than following a search ad.
4. Prefer a local tool for sensitive files (a desktop PDF editor, a local image editor).
5. The graphic ends with a call to "learn AI for free" on the author's own site; treat it as promotion.
