# Modern Marketing 2026 — Taxonomy

Source: infographic by Searchable.com, "How businesses get found & grow in 2026." Five branches, each with five sub-items. Use this to structure a marketing plan, content calendar backbone, or to check a plan's coverage before presenting it.

## 1. AI Search Visibility
- AI Citations (AEO/GEO)
- Query Fanout Mapping
- Schema & Technical Setup
- Share of Voice Tracking
- ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini (platform-specific presence)

## 2. SEO & Organic Search
- Keyword & Fanout Research
- On-Page Optimisation
- Technical SEO
- Backlinks & Authority
- Content Indexing

## 3. Brand & Positioning
- Positioning & Differentiation
- Brand Voice & Tone
- Visual Identity
- Reputation Management
- Trust & Authority Signals

## 4. Content Strategy
- Founder-Led Content
- Educational Posts & Guides
- Lead Magnets & Playbooks
- Video & Short-Form
- Repurposing Across Channels

## 5. Paid & Performance
- Paid Social (Meta, LinkedIn)
- Google Ads & PPC
- Retargeting Campaigns
- CAC & ROAS Tracking
- Funnel & Attribution Setup

## Using this as a coverage check

When reviewing a marketing plan, map every planned activity onto one of the five branches. A plan concentrated entirely in branches 2 and 4 (classic SEO + content) with nothing in branch 1 (AI Search Visibility) is optimizing for a search landscape that's already partly obsolete — flag this gap rather than silently filling it in. Branch 1 is the newest and least mature category most marketing teams have not yet built a process for; it's the one most likely to be missing, and worth checking first.
