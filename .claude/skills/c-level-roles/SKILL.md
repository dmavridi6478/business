# C-level leaders (Eric Partaker)
- **CEO - Vision Leader:** sets company vision and long-term goals; makes big decisions quickly; aligns teams around mission and values; builds ties with board and key partners; adjusts course fast when markets shift.
- **COO - Operations Leader:** turns strategy into daily measurable actions; oversees operations across departments; improves processes for speed, quality and cost; solves work problems before they slow tasks; tracks KPIs and drives constant improvement.
- **CFO - Finance and Accounting Leader:** plans budgets and predicts future cash needs; monitors spending against targets; protects money and reduces company risk; guides investment and funding decisions; reports financial health clearly.
- **CMO - Marketing Leader:** creates the marketing plan to reach target customers; builds and protects brand across markets; runs campaigns for awareness and demand; tracks results and tweaks tactics; keeps messaging consistent across channels.
- **CTO - Technology Leader:** creates tech plan for long-term business goals; oversees software, hardware and system designs; evaluates new tech; ensures security and reliability of digital tools; leads engineers toward scalable solutions.
- **CHRO - People Leader:** recruits talent aligned with culture and strategy; designs fair pay, benefits and recognition; develops training that grows skills and careers; manages worker relations and conflicts; builds an inclusive culture.
- **CIO - IT Leader:** creates IT and data plans to support company goals; oversees IT systems, networks and essential services; safeguards data with strong security; provides data insights guiding decisions; updates tools to boost productivity.
- **CPO - Product Leader:** shapes product vision for customer and market fit; prioritises features on data and feedback; guides teams through the product lifecycle; tests, iterates and improves; measures success and drives innovation.
