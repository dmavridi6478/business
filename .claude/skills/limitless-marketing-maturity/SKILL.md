Use when scoring a marketing team's maturity, justifying a move from hiring to AI/partners, or writing a CMO operating-model roadmap.

# Stages
| Stage | Description | Locked costs |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Trapped | Ad hoc execution under severe constraint; reactive; default response "we need to hire"; minimal AI | 75%+ |
| 02 Aware | Recognises the resource paradox; freelancers/agencies as stopgaps; vendor sprawl and coordination overhead; still forced to choose programs vs capabilities | high fixed costs |
| 03 Systematic | Operating model intentional; strategic partners integrated; execution largely offloaded; internal team focuses on strategy and orchestration | 50-60% |
| 04 Optimized | Performance-driven allocation; AI embedded across workflows; execution engine optimised; partner ecosystem governed by data | 40-50% |
| 05 Limitless | Self-optimising system; continuous improvement loops; AI-driven optimisation and experimentation; maximum budget flexibility; new initiatives launched in under two weeks | minimal |
Percentages are the author's heuristics, not benchmarks. Score by evidence (share of budget fixed, time to launch a new initiative, where AI is used) and name the one constraint that blocks the next stage.
