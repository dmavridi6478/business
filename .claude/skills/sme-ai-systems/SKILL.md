# AI systems for any SME (Thomas Krypas, "8 AI systems", slides number 10)
| # | System | What it does | Repo agent / skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead Intelligence | website signals, LinkedIn insights, CRM context, data, next best action | `os-prospect`, `os-qualify` |
| 2 | Sales Follow-Up | transcript, key points, objections, opportunity summary, follow-up draft, CRM update, next task | `os-followup`, `os-close` |
| 3 | Customer Support Intelligence | incoming tickets, customer history, policy guidance, suggested reply, smart routing | `os-support`, `customer-support` |
| 4 | AI Content and Authority | ideation, planning, creation, repurpose, performance insights | `os-seo`, `os-email-sms`, `linkedin-*` skills |
| 5 | AI KPI and Reporting | data integration, automated reporting, AI insights, alerts, share | `os-business-analyst` |
| 6 | AI Growth Execution | goal setting, action planning, workflow automation, progress tracking, optimisation | `sme-growth-execution` (new) |
| 7 | Company Knowledge | centralise knowledge, ask in natural language, consistent answers, less dependency | `sme-knowledge-assistant` (new) |
| 8 | Meeting Intelligence | transcription, key decisions, action items, CRM/tool integration, follow-up reminders | `sme-meeting-intelligence` (new) |
| 9 | Executive Copilot | connect tools, AI analysis, weekly management brief, priorities and decisions, proactive recommendations | `sme-executive-copilot` (new) |
| 10 | Systems compound | pick the one system with the biggest impact and start | `/find-bottleneck` |
The cover promises 8 systems; the carousel shows 10. Build 1, 2 and 8 first if unsure; each result line on the slides is a target, not a measured outcome.
