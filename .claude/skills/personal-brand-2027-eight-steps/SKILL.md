# Your 2027 personal brand blueprint

Source: one infographic by Nader Alnajjar (LEVER). The eight steps and numbers are the sheet's; the plan and checks are my additions. No results data is shown.

| # | Step | Sheet's content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide your goal | Pick the outcome first: audience (people who'd follow you anywhere), authority (the name people think of), or leads (conversations with people who can buy). "You can have all three, but you can't start with all three" |
| 2 | Establish positioning | Target audience, unique value proposition, differentiation. "If people can't say what you do after three posts, the posts aren't the problem" |
| 3 | Profile as a landing page | Banner: one message, one next step. Headline: who you are + how you help + the result. About: their problem first, your story second. Featured: your proof, then your lead magnet |
| 4 | 3 to 4 content pillars | Topics from your buyers' problems, not your interests. Each pillar has a job: gets you found, earns trust, starts conversations |
| 5 | Post 5 times a week across the funnel | Top of funnel 70% (broad, shareable), middle 20% (frameworks and proof), bottom 10% (case studies and offers) |
| 6 | Comment where your buyers are | List accounts buyers follow, comment with something worth reading, DM people you would want as clients |
| 7 | Repurpose your best posts | This week's best post becomes next week's newsletter; rewrite with a new hook; redesign the visual |
| 8 | Give every reader a next step | Soft CTA, a lead magnet that solves one problem fast, a newsletter; ask new subscribers what describes them best |

## One-quarter plan (my addition)

Week 1: choose the goal, write the positioning line (`positioning-triangle`), rewrite headline (`linkedin-headline-six-parts`) and Featured. Weeks 2 to 12: five posts a week (about 3 top, 1 middle, 1 bottom), 10 meaningful comments a day on a list of 20 buyer-followed accounts, one repurposed post a week, one live next step. Measure weekly: profile views, search appearances, inbound messages, newsletter sign-ups, conversations with target buyers.

## Checks

- Five posts a week is a heavy load; start at three you can sustain.
- 70/20/10 is the sheet's rule of thumb, not a tested ratio; compare your own results.
- Do not mass-DM; personalise or skip. Do not use automation to comment or message under your name without your review of each one.
- Keep claims you can prove; do not invent proof for the Featured section.
