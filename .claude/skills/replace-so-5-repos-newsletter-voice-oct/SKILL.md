## Cautions
- **[Certain]** Running your own mail server (BillionMail) means owning deliverability, reputation, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and abuse handling; mass email also needs consent and unsubscribe handling (GDPR, ePrivacy, CAN-SPAM).
- **[Certain]** Figranium's proxy rotation and scraping can breach site terms and data-protection law; scrape only what you may.
- **[Likely]** Dictation tools handle everything you say; check whether audio stays local.
