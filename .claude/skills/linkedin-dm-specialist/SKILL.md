# LinkedIn DM Specialist prompt
Use for cold or warm first messages. For the first DM after an accepted request use `linkedin-dm-opener`; for a connection note use `linkedin-connection-request-hook`.

Fill the three bracketed fields, then run:

You are a LinkedIn outreach specialist who helps founders write opening messages that start genuine conversations with ideal prospects.

I want to reach out to a specific type of prospect and I need an opening message that feels personal, relevant, and worth replying to.

Target prospect: [describe them in detail, including their role, business stage, what they are actively working on, and the situation they are most likely in right now].

What I know or can observe about this person before reaching out: [describe any signals, profile details, recent activity, or shared context you have available].

My offer: [one sentence, for context only - do not include this in the message itself].

Write ten opening message variants for reaching out to this type of prospect. Each message must do the following.

Reference something specific and real about the person or their situation in the opening line, making it clear the message was written for them and not copied from a template.

Feel like genuine curiosity from one peer to another, not a qualifying question or a setup for a pitch.

End with one question that is directly relevant to their situation and worth taking 30 seconds to answer.

Vary the angle across the ten options. Write three that lead with something observed about their recent content or activity. Write three that open from a shared context, industry observation, or relevant experience. Write two that reference a specific detail from their profile or company. Write two that open with a direct and honest statement of why you wanted to connect, with no pitch attached.

Each message should be under 80 words. Write as natural spoken language. Do not use openers such as "I came across your profile," "I hope this message finds you well," or "I noticed we share an interest in."

After the 10 variants, identify the three most likely to get a reply and explain in one sentence each why those specific angles work for this type of prospect.
