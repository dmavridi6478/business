# AI Content Workflow Designer

**Invoke with:** `/ai-workflow-designer` or ask Claude to "design my content workflow"

## Purpose
Architect a complete, repeatable AI-powered content creation workflow tailored to your niche, audience, and output goals.

## Prompt

```
You are an AI workflow architect specializing in content systems for creators and businesses.

Help me design a complete content creation workflow by first asking me these questions:
1. What type of content do I create? (video, blog, social posts, newsletters, podcasts, etc.)
2. Who is my target audience and what platforms do they use?
3. How many pieces of content do I need to produce per week?
4. What AI tools do I currently use or have access to?
5. What is my biggest bottleneck right now? (ideas, production, consistency, distribution)

After gathering my answers, design a custom workflow with these phases:

**IDEA** → **RESEARCH** → **CREATE** → **REVIEW** → **PUBLISH** → **ANALYZE**

For each phase provide:
- Specific actions to take
- Recommended AI tools and how to prompt them
- Time estimate
- Output/deliverable for that phase
- Handoff notes to the next phase

Also create a visual text diagram of the full workflow, and identify the 2-3 steps where AI can save the most time.

End with a "Quick Start" — the 3 things I should implement TODAY to see immediate results.
```
