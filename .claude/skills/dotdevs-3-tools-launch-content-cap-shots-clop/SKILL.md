## Read critically
- **[Certain]** "Idea to App Store in 11 chapters, no sign-up" is a claim about a tutorial; App Store publishing needs an Apple developer account, review and fees.
- **[Likely]** Real screenshots are the safest launch content; do not publish mockups that show features the product lacks.
- **[Likely]** Clop is a macOS app; check platform before relying on it.
