# Build a personal brand in 90 days

Source: an infographic by Nader Alnajjar (Lever), "Build an Unforgettable Personal Brand in 90 Days" (Batch 127). Phases and day ranges are the graphic's; the numbers (70/30, 70/20/10, five posts a week) are the author's rules of thumb, not measured benchmarks. Companion to `linkedin-magnetic-brand-9-rules` (the nine rules graphic, already in this library) and `linkedin-authority-two-engines`.

| Phase | Days | What to do (summary) | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish positioning | 1 to 7 | Decide who you are for before you post: pick one ICP, define the specific problem you solve, hone in on what makes you different | One-sentence positioning, tested on five people |
| 2. Optimise your profile | 8 to 14 | Treat the profile as your landing page: banner (one message and a call to action), headline (who you are, how you help, the result), About (lead with their problem, not your CV), Featured (proof and results) | Profile that says the same as the positioning |
| 3. Choose content pillars | 15 to 21 | Pick three or four themes you will be known for; split them 70% reach, 30% trust. Reach posts pull attention; trust posts move people closer to working with you | Pillar list with two example posts each |
| 4. Post across the funnel | 22 to 45 | Aim for five posts a week spread across the funnel: top 70% (broad, shareable, grows reach), middle 20% (frameworks and proof that build trust), bottom 10% (case studies, calls to action, offers). Every post has a job | A calendar tagged top, middle or bottom |
| 5. Engage and distribute | 46 to 70 | Posting is half of it: get into the comments of accounts your buyers already follow; repurpose your best posts into your newsletter and other channels to reach the same people twice | Daily comment routine; one repurposed piece a week |
| 6. Turn attention into inbound | 71 to 90 | Give people a next step: a soft call to action, a lead magnet, a newsletter to join. Attention, then nurture, then monetise | One live lead path and a log of where inbound came from |
Day 90 message on the graphic: you are the name that comes to mind and on the road to becoming the go-to in your industry.

## Reading the graphic honestly
- **70/30 and 70/20/10 are conventions, not findings.** Two different splits appear (reach versus trust, and top versus middle versus bottom). Use one as a starting hypothesis and change it after you see which posts lead to conversations.
- **Five posts a week is a lot.** Volume you cannot sustain for 90 days is worse than three good posts a week. Choose the cadence you can hold.
- **Ninety days gets you started, not "unforgettable".** Reputation compounds slowly; plan to continue after day 90.
- **Phase order matters, day counts do not.** Do not post before the positioning exists; the 22-to-45 stretch is the longest because the learning happens there.
- **Selling too early or too late.** Phase 6 starts at day 71; a bottom-of-funnel offer in week 1 usually fails, but never offering anything leaves attention unmonetised.

## Weekly review (15 minutes, same day each week)
1. Which two posts earned the most qualified conversations, not likes? 2. Which funnel layer did each serve? 3. Who did I comment under, and did any reply? 4. How many inbound messages and from which source? 5. One change for next week.

## Measures
Qualified conversations per week, meetings booked, profile views to connection requests, newsletter sign-ups, and inbound by source. Impressions and likes are context only.

## Rules
Be honest in claims and case studies; get permission to name clients; do not automate comments or DMs in ways that mislead or breach LinkedIn's terms (check the current User Agreement); disclose AI help where the platform requires it.
