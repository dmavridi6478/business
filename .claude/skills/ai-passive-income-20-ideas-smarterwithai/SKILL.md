## Read critically
- **[Certain]** Nothing here is passive at the start: each needs research, quality control, marketing and support. The graphic gives no earnings evidence.
- **[Certain]** Platform rules differ: Kindle Direct Publishing, Etsy and stock sites require disclosure of AI-generated content, and some ban or limit it; AI art and music may not be copyrightable and training-data disputes affect licensing. Check each platform's current policy.
- **[Likely]** Ideas 1, 5, 7, 8 and 19 are saturated markets where low-quality AI output sells poorly; services (4, 10, 12, 16) with a named client are likelier to earn than product dumps.
- **[Likely]** "Voiceover products" (11) must not clone anyone's voice without consent.
