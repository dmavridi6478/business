# Method cards for the 21 strategy skills (original)

Written from each skill's printed title and one-line description. Not the author's prompts. Each card: the question it answers, inputs to supply, the output to demand, and the failure to check for.

| # | Skill | Question | Inputs | Output to demand | Failure check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | problem-framing | What is the real question? | Situation, decision owner, deadline | One-sentence question; an issue tree two or three levels deep, mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive | Branches overlap or leave a gap; the question has no owner or deadline |
| 2 | fact-base | What do we know, and how well? | Documents, data, interviews | Evidence table: claim, source, date, confidence | Claims without a source; stale data presented as current |
| 3 | assumption-audit | Which guess, if wrong, breaks the plan? | Plan, fact base | Ranked list of assumptions with impact, evidence and the cheapest test | Assumptions disguised as facts; no test attached |
| 4 | market-sizing | How big is the market? | Segment definition, price, volume drivers | Top-down and bottom-up estimates side by side, with the gap explained | Top-down only; one source; no definition of who counts |
| 5 | competitive-intel | What are rivals doing? | Rival list, public sources | Table of moves with dates and links; what we do not know | Marketing copy treated as strategy; no dates |
| 6 | customer-segmentation | Who actually pays? | Customer and revenue data | Segments cut by behavior, with size, margin and retention | Segments by demographics only; no revenue per segment |
| 7 | profit-pools | Where does the margin sit in the chain? | Cost and price data by stage | Margin by stage with sources and an uncertainty range | Averages hiding the real pools; no cost allocation rule |
| 8 | strategic-options | What are three real paths? | Problem, constraints, fact base | Three distinct options, cost, benefit, risk, what must be true for each | Options that are one option with different names; no "do nothing" baseline |
| 9 | pricing-strategy | What price move, and why? | Costs, willingness to pay, competitors | Price scenarios with volume response, margin and the formula | Elasticity invented; no sensitivity range |
| 10 | business-cases | Does it pay back? | Costs, benefits, timing | Model with inputs, formulas, scenarios, payback and NPV | Hard-coded numbers; benefits counted twice; no downside case |
| 11 | portfolio-review | Keep, fix or kill? | Units, revenue, margin, strategic fit | Decision per unit with reasons and the trigger that would change it | Sunk cost driving "keep"; no criteria stated first |
| 12 | operating-model | Who does what after the decision? | Decision, current org | Roles, decision rights, handoffs, interfaces | Org chart with no decision rights; no transition path |
| 13 | initiative-priority | What first? | Initiatives, value and readiness estimates | Ranked list scored by value and readiness with dependencies | Scores made up; dependencies ignored |
| 14 | transformation-roadmap | What happens in which wave? | Priorities, capacity, constraints | Waves with owners, dates, dependencies and gates | Owners are teams, not people; dates ignore capacity |
| 15 | risk-mitigation | What could go wrong, and what do we do? | Plan, assumptions | Register: risk, likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation, trigger | Generic risks; mitigation equals "monitor" |
| 16 | war-gaming | How will competitors respond? | Our move, rival profiles | Move and counter-move rounds for each rival, with our next response | One-step thinking; rivals assumed passive |
| 17 | kpi-architecture | What proves progress? | Strategy, objectives | Metric tree from outcome to driver, with definition, source, owner, cadence | Vanity metrics; metrics with no owner or definition |
| 18 | stakeholder-alignment | Who must say yes, and what is their price? | Decision, stakeholder list | Map: interest, influence, concern, what they need, who engages them | Stakeholders listed by title only; no plan to engage |
| 19 | executive-narrative | What is the story the numbers carry? | Fact base, decision, audience | Message-title outline, one source per number, the ask on the first page | Numbers not traceable to the model; no explicit ask |
| 20 | board-memos | What decision are we recording? | Decision, options considered, rationale | Memo: decision, options, reasons, risks, conditions, next steps, for the minutes | Rationale missing; conflicts of interest not noted |
| 21 | operating-system | How do we keep this alive? | Roadmap, KPIs, owners | Cadence: meetings, inputs, decisions, owners, review of KPIs and assumptions | Meetings with no decisions; no retirement rule |

Rule for every card: attach source and date to each figure; label the rest as assumption; list what could not be checked.
