11-20 **Crawl & index:** index coverage reader, sitemap auditor, robots checker, crawl budget analyser, orphan page finder, duplicate content finder, parameter handler, internal link mapper, click depth auditor, index bloat finder.
21-30 **Keywords:** keyword clusterer, intent classifier, page type matcher, longtail miner, competitor gap analyser, cannibalisation finder, seasonality reader, brand vs non-brand splitter, zero-click assessor, priority scorer.
31-40 **Content:** content brief writer, content verdict giver, SERP gap finder, content refresher, anti-slop editor, originality adder, topic cluster planner, readability checker, content decay finder, CTA matcher.
41-50 **On-page:** on-page auditor, title writer, meta description writer, heading structure fixer, schema generator, image optimiser, internal link adder, above-fold auditor, featured snippet optimiser, page experience checker.
51-60 **E-E-A-T & authority:** E-E-A-T auditor, author page builder, first-hand evidence finder, citation checker, brand mention finder, trust signal auditor, review presence checker, YMYL checker, about page auditor, authority gap reader.
61-70 **Links:** backlink profile reader, backlink gap finder, link opportunity qualifier, broken link finder, unlinked mention converter, digital PR angle finder, internal equity router, competitor link velocity, toxic link assessor, link report writer.
71-80 **AI search & GEO:** citability scorer, first-forty-words rewriter, prompt alignment checker, AI crawler checker, llms.txt builder, entity clarity checker, source attribution auditor, AI share of voice, FAQ coverage checker, GEO vs SEO reconciler.
81-90 **Local & international:** GBP auditor, NAP consistency checker, local pack analyser, review velocity tracker, service area mapper, multi-location auditor, hreflang auditor, international structure reader, translation quality checker, currency and format checker.
91-100 **Reporting & ops:** SEO diagnostician, baseline recorder, change logger, SEO drift watcher, ranking reporter, traffic attributor, forecast builder, weekly SEO report, quarterly roadmap, client SEO reporter.

# Modern Marketing 2026 (5 pillars, 5 items each)
AI search visibility (AI citations AEO/GEO, query fan-out mapping, schema & technical setup, share-of-voice tracking, ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini); SEO & organic (keyword & fan-out research, on-page, technical, backlinks & authority, indexing); Brand & positioning (positioning, voice & tone, visual identity, reputation, trust signals); Content strategy (founder-led content, educational posts, lead magnets, video & short-form, repurposing); Paid & performance (paid social, Google Ads/PPC, retargeting, CAC & ROAS, funnel & attribution).

# Pick rule
Run a baseline first (technical auditor, index coverage reader, keyword clusterer, citability scorer, baseline recorder). Add agents only where the baseline shows a gap; one agent = one SKILL.md = one output you review. Related: `claude-seo`, `seo-audit`, `ai-search-visibility`, `ai-seo`, `aeo-diagnostic-metrics`.
