# Google Ads in 7 steps

Source: infographic "Google Ads, Pay Per Click (PPC) Advertising" by Marketing With Rishika (Batch 115). A checklist, not data: it gives no benchmarks. Write your own targets before you spend.

| # | Step | Decide | Checklist from the card |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keyword research | Which searches signal intent to buy | Understand search intent; use relevant, high-performing keywords; analyse volume and competition; use match types (broad, phrase, exact); identify negative keywords |
| 2 | Campaign setup | Campaign type and structure | Types: Search, Display, Shopping, Video, Performance Max; structure campaign -> ad group; set location, language and devices; choose bidding strategy; set budget and audience targeting |
| 3 | Ad creation | What the ad promises | Effective headlines and descriptions; ad extensions (sitelinks, callouts, snippets); align copy with the landing page; high-quality creatives; follow Google Ads policies |
| 4 | Landing page and conversion | Does the page keep the ad's promise | Relevant and clear page; fast loading; mobile-friendly; clear call to action; simple form or checkout |
| 5 | Tracking and analytics | How you will know it worked | Conversion tracking (Google Ads + GA4); Google Tag Manager; key events and goals; UTM parameters; campaign reports |
| 6 | Budget and bidding | How much, and who decides | Manual or automated bidding; understand CPC, CPA and ROAS; bid adjustments (device, location, audience); allocate budget across campaigns; monitor and optimise |
| 7 | Optimization and growth | What to change next | Analyse CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS; find high and low performers; A/B test ads, pages and audiences; pause underperformers; scale what works |

## Order matters
Do step 5 before step 6: without tracking, automated bidding learns from nothing. Do step 4 before spending on step 3: sending paid clicks to a weak page wastes the budget.
