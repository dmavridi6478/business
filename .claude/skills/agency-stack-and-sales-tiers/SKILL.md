# Agency stack (slides 1-5)
1. Notion - client info, SOPs, projects. 2. SERPtag - rankings, keyword opportunities, SEO monitoring. 3. Claude - research, proposals, copy, strategy. 4. Stripe - invoices and payments without chasing clients. 5. Calendly - clients pick a slot, it is confirmed.
Connectors available here: Notion, Calendly, Claude. Stripe: skill `stripe-integration` (code) only; SERPtag has an entry in `oss-tools-part3-checked`.

# AI sales tools tier list 2026 (51ultron.com)
S "pipeline on autopilot": 51Ultron (free + credits), Apollo, Clay. A "strong at one job": HubSpot, Instantly, Attio. B "good with the right setup": Lemlist, Smartlead, Gong. C "overhyped, expensive": ZoomInfo, Seamless.AI.
**Bias warning:** 51Ultron published the list and put itself in S; no criteria, pricing or data are shown. Treat it as marketing. Use `sales-research`, `revenue-agent-pick` or your own trial scores (reply rate, cost per meeting, data accuracy) before deciding. Connectors in this environment: Clay, Apollo, Calendly, HubSpot, Vibe Prospecting.
