# 9 rules for a magnetic personal brand (LinkedIn)

Source: one infographic, three lines per rule. Scoring and the audit below are added.

| # | Rule | What to do | Top tip (from the source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focus on one topic | Pick the one problem you want to be known for solving; come at it from new angles | Write your bio around that topic first, then let content follow |
| 2 | Study the outliers | Find posts in your space doing 2x better than the rest; study hook, format, angle | Use SayWhat to spot and break down outlier posts |
| 3 | Reuse your top performers | The best post from 3 months ago still works; swap the numbers, redo the graphic, repost | Turn an old carousel into a fresh infographic |
| 4 | Test the format before the topic | One idea, three formats; see what people respond to | Run the same idea as a carousel, then a text post, and compare |
| 5 | Build strategy before you execute | Know your pillars, funnel and goal before opening the doc | Map topics across TOFU, MOFU, BOFU before writing |
| 6 | Observe before you post | Watch what works in your niche this week; build the post around what you notice | Keep a running note of 3 posts you liked and why |
| 7 | Post the failures as well as the wins | The flop teaches more than the hit; sharing both feels human | Add one line under any flop on what you would change |
| 8 | Track leads, not just likes | Likes show enjoyment; leads show intent to buy | Tag every lead-magnet click and build on what works |
| 9 | Turn comments into pipeline | Reply is step one and a signal for more: DM it, qualify it, move it forward | Reply publicly, then DM within 24 hours; make it about them |

## Weekly audit (score each rule 0 = absent, 1 = partial, 2 = in place)

Evidence required per score (a link, a number, a note). Total /18. Under 9: fix rules 1, 5, 8 first - topic, strategy and measurement - before any posting tactic.

## Guardrails

- Rule 9: DMs follow the LinkedIn terms and GDPR - one relevant message, no scraping, no bulk automation of connection requests. Approval gate before anything is sent.
- Rule 7: never fabricate a "failure" for engagement; only post a real one.
- Rule 8 needs a tracked asset (UTM link or lead magnet); if none exists, say "no lead data yet" and stop.
