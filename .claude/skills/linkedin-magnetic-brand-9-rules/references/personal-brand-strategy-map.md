# Personal brand strategy map: who you are, how you grow

Source: infographic "Personal Brand Strategy" (Nader Alnajjar, Lever) (Batch 116). A tidy two-level map; it gives no evidence that following it produces results. Use it as a checklist, not a forecast.

## Level 1: Who you are (seven parts)

| Part | Sub-items on the card |
|---|---|
| Niche | Expertise, audience, platform |
| Positioning | Unique angle, category, authority |
| Story | Origin, turning point, mission |
| Voice | Tone, language, energy |
| Content pillars | 3-5 themes, depth, range |
| Proof | Results, testimonials, case studies |
| Offer ladder | Free, low-ticket, high-ticket |

## Level 2: How you grow (seven parts)

| Part | Sub-items on the card |
|---|---|
| Content engine | Formats, frequency, repurposing |
| Distribution | Organic, paid, collaborations |
| Community | Comments, DMs, inner circle |
| Lead magnets | Triggers, opt-ins, nurture |
| Social proof | Milestones, features, endorsements |
| Monetisation | Products, services, partnerships |
| Optimisation | Analytics, testing, iteration |

## Order that works
Finish level 1 before level 2: growing before you can state your niche, angle and proof produces attention with nothing to convert. One sentence per part is enough to start. Score yourself 0-2 on each of the 14 parts and fix the lowest level-1 part first.
