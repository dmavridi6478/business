# SEO in 2026, five optimisations (Lego wheel graphic)

Source: one infographic with five wedges. Opinion; no data.

| # | Type | Definition on the graphic | Example | How to rank higher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEO, search engine optimisation | Earning organic rankings on Google through keywords, links and site health; the base layer every AI engine still pulls from | A local gym ranks for "24-hour gyms in Manchester" | Build a strong entity (identity, topics, clusters); refresh pages (freshness beats sheer volume); audit schema, content decay and Core Web Vitals |
| 2 | AEO, answer engine optimisation | Structuring content so it is served as the direct answer inside Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and chat responses | A tax firm's short explainer becomes the cited answer on UK capital gains allowances | Lead with the answer in 40 to 60 words; question-style H2s and H3s; FAQ and HowTo schema |
| 3 | GEO, generative engine optimisation | Getting your brand cited or summarised by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok and Claude | A fintech is named for "best budgeting apps for freelancers" | Factual, entity-rich content that earns E-E-A-T; get cited on forums, datasets and trusted publications; publish your own data and case studies |
| 4 | AIO, AI optimisation | Making sure your brand is included and represented accurately in the data AI models train on and pull from | A software brand becomes part of a model's working knowledge via well-structured docs and an accurate Wikipedia presence | Publish structured, well-formatted content on credible sources; keep messaging factually consistent; get featured where AI pulls from (Wikipedia, structured data repositories) |
| 5 | SXO, search experience optimisation | Optimising the full on-site experience so visitors convert once search or AI sends them your way | An e-commerce brand aligns page speed, layout and CTAs to buying intent on AI-referred traffic | Fast, mobile-friendly pages; align content to user intent; clear, persuasive CTAs |

## Read critically
- **[Certain]** "AIO" also commonly means Google's "AI Overviews" and "SXO" is an older UX-plus-SEO term; the graphic's definitions are one author's. Use the plain description in client work.
- **[Likely]** Types 2 to 4 overlap heavily and all depend on type 1 (crawlable, accurate, well-structured content). You cannot directly control what models "train on".
- **[Likely]** The 40 to 60 word answer length is a heuristic, not a published rule.
