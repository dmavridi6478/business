# Content calendar builder skill (SkillDrop AI demo)

Source: a 16-second demo video. The slides show **content-calendar-builder.md** ("Content operations: monthly calendars aligned to business goals"); the skill file was **not provided**, so this is a re-creation of the workflow from what was visible. The end card says "Free beginner guide, comment GUIDE" (skilldropai.com); nothing was commented.

## The workflow shown
1. Drop the skill file into Claude.
2. Add your context: **business goal** (example: generate leads), **audience** (freelancers), **channels** (LinkedIn plus email), **team capacity** (2 people, 6 hours a week).
3. Click "Build my calendar". Output: a **30-day monthly content system** in a grid of Monday to Sunday, with four weekly themes in order: **1 Educate, 2 Show proof, 3 Solve pain, 4 Invite**; entries tagged post, tip, email, guide, recap; and three lines underneath: **Assign** (writer then review), **Promote** (post then email), **Measure** (reach, leads). Rule on the sheet: every piece has an owner, a promotion and a metric.

## Run it here
`/content-calendar-30 <goal, audience, channels, capacity>`. Related: `ai-smart-publishing-planner`, `content-week-framework`, `instagram-content-calendar-gr`, `skilldrop-ai-customer-support`.

## Cautions
- **[Certain]** The example calendar is a template with placeholder cadences, not a tested plan; check the volume against your real hours.
- **[Likely]** "Show proof" weeks need real results or testimonials; do not publish invented ones.
