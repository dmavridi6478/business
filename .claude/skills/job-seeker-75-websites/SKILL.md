## What the headline hides

- **The real count is below 75.** Pramp, Interviewing.io, Big Interview and Exponent appear in both 4 and 8; Coursera appears twice (7 and 10); Glassdoor appears three times (jobs, interview questions, salaries); AngelList's domain is today Wellfound's, which is already in category 1 [Likely]. About 66 distinct services.
- **Strong India skew.** Naukri, Foundit, Freshersworld, TimesJobs, Hirect, Internshala, AmbitionBox, Great Learning, Cutshort and Jobaaj are India-focused; USAJOBS is for US federal jobs. For Greece and the EU the useful ones are LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, the remote boards, EU Careers and UN Careers; add local Greek boards yourself (not in the graphic).
- **Free is not uniform.** FlexJobs and several resume and mock-interview tools charge; check the plan before uploading a CV.
- **Privacy:** a CV holds personal data; do not paste it into tools whose data use you have not read.
- **No quality ranking.** The graphic lists, it does not compare. Pick by role and country.

## Shortlist method (8 sites)
1. One or two general boards for your country. 2. One remote board if relevant. 3. One resume/ATS check against a real posting. 4. One interview practice tool. 5. One salary source. 6. One portfolio place (GitHub or Behance). 7. A career-growth resource. Track applications in one sheet.
