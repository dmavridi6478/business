# 5 Claude prompts for a brilliant first impression

Source: @stackfloai ("StackFlo") carousel, 7 slides: cover, five prompts, "The Claude prompt I'd save", plus a follow slide. Transcribed 15 October 2026. Square brackets are your inputs.

## Prompt 01 - Nail your introduction
"I need to introduce myself at: [EVENT / MEETING / NEW JOB]. Here's some background about me: [YOUR BACKGROUND]. Write 3 natural introductions: 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 60 seconds. Make me sound confident, approachable and interesting without sounding rehearsed or self-important."

## Prompt 02 - Never run out of things to say
"I'm attending [EVENT] where I'll meet [TYPE OF PEOPLE]. Give me 10 interesting conversation starters that feel natural rather than forced. Avoid boring questions like 'What do you do?' Include follow-up questions that encourage people to share stories and experiences."

## Prompt 03 - Sound confident without showing off
"I want to talk about my experience with: [SKILL / ACHIEVEMENT / PROJECT]. Here's how I'd normally describe it: [YOUR WORDS]. Rewrite it so I sound confident and capable without bragging. Keep it conversational, specific and believable. Give me 3 versions for different situations."

## Prompt 04 - Handle questions you weren't expecting
"I'm preparing for [SITUATION]. Act as someone I'm meeting for the first time. Ask me 5 challenging but realistic questions ONE AT A TIME. After each answer, give me feedback on how I came across. Help me improve my clarity, confidence and ability to respond naturally."

## Prompt 05 - Make people remember you
"I'm meeting [PERSON / GROUP] for the first time. My goal is [GOAL]. Help me identify 3 memorable things I could communicate about myself. Focus on: relevant experiences, interesting stories, shared interests, genuine strengths. Avoid gimmicks, exaggeration and anything that sounds like a sales pitch."

## The prompt to save (before any important first meeting)
"Act as my personal communication coach. I'm about to meet [PERSON / GROUP] in [SITUATION]. Ask me 5 questions ONE AT A TIME about the meeting, my background and what I want to achieve. Then help me prepare: a natural introduction, 3 good conversation starters, 2 interesting things to share, 3 mistakes to avoid. Keep everything authentic to my personality."

Slide captions: "No more awkwardly explaining what you do." / "The best conversations rarely start with the most obvious questions." / "There's a difference between being impressive and trying to impress." / "Practise the uncomfortable questions before someone asks them." / "Being memorable doesn't mean being the loudest person in the room." / "You don't need to become someone else to make a great impression."

## Keeping it honest

- Give Claude true background only. A polished line about an achievement that is not yours is a lie you have to maintain in the room.
- Prompts 01, 03 and 05 return text you will memorise: read it aloud and cut anything you would not say to a friend.
- Prompt 04 is the highest-value one: it is practice with feedback. Run it with a real question list for the situation (interview panel, board, client).
