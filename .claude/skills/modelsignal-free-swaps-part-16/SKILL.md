## Arithmetic (checked)
- **[Certain]** The twelve annual prices sum to **$10,689**, which matches the cover. The headline is true only if you replace **all twelve**.
- **[Certain]** The summary slide lists 8 swaps (DevCycle 6,000; Prefect 1,200; Inngest 1,188; Zencoder 540; Pulumi 480; Liveblocks 300; AppSignal 279; Codacy 216), which sum to **$10,203**, yet it is captioned "Save $10,689 a year". Same pattern as Parts 5, 10 and 11.
- **[Certain]** "Free" excludes your hosting, database, on-call time and upgrade work. At $5 to $25 a month the paid tool is often cheaper than the hours spent running the free one.

## Safe use
- Never cancel a subscription because of a slide; confirm the free tool covers the feature you actually use, then migrate one service at a time.
- AGPL-3.0 (Worklenz, MegaLinter, Uptrace, Unleash) matters if you modify the code and expose it to users.
- The cover claims a site "modelsignal.dev" with direct links; it was not visited and nothing was commented or followed.
