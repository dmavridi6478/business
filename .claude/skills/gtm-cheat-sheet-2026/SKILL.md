# The 2026 GTM cheat sheet (@janivrancsik, growthtoday.co)
## 1. SaaS GTM motions
| Motion | How it works | Channels | Example on the card |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product-led (PLG) | Users try it alone before sales steps in | Free trial, freemium, in-app onboarding | (logos only) |
| Inbound-led | Buyers find you through content and raise a hand | SEO + SEM, blog, social, webinars | HubSpot |
| Outbound-led | You reach the right accounts first, when a signal fires | Cold email, LinkedIn, signal-based selling | (logos only) |
| Sales-led | A hands-on process closes big, complex deals | Custom demos, exec briefings, procurement | Workday |
| Community-led | Growth comes from active user communities | Slack groups, events, user forums | (logos only) |
| Partner-led | Partners sell and distribute for you | Resellers, co-marketing, referrals | (logos only) |
| Ecosystem-led | Integrations keep customers around longer | App marketplaces, APIs, integrations | (logos only) |
## 2. GTM iceberg 2026 ("everyone sees the top; the GTM that wins is built under the waterline")
Above: **GTM execution** = inbound (ads, blogs, homepage), outbound (landing pages, paid content), engagement (LinkedIn content, nurturing, community).
Below, in order: **What we create** - plans, playbooks and alignment; value proposition and offer. **What we profile** - buyer personas and process; ideal customer profile. **What we analyse** - market data; customer data; GTM strategy (the base).
## 6. Headcount planning
Pre-AI model: SDRs + AEs, high tool cost (pricey per-seat licences, overlapping tools, lots of manual work). AI-first team: strategist, GTM engineer, AEs, SDRs, low tool cost (smaller team, fewer licences, one place for all data, more output per person).
- **Capacity:** how much pipeline can your SDRs really make, how many deals can your AEs really work; keep both in balance.
- **Market:** your TAM runs out as each new rep gets a smaller patch; space touches by months, not weeks.
- **Timeline:** reps ramp in about 5.7 months; about 45% of SDRs and about 30% of AEs leave each year (card figures, no source printed).
