## Cautions
- **[Certain]** The slide itself says Joplin has an optional paid cloud service and Upscayl needs a compatible GPU; "zero price tag" covers the software only.
- **[Likely]** LocalSend moves files on your LAN; keep it off public Wi-Fi, and check the receive-confirmation setting.
- **[Likely]** AI upscaling invents detail; do not use it on evidence, medical or document images.
- Nothing was installed. These are well-known tools, so download only from the project site or its GitHub releases.
