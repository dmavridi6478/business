# Prompts (replace [brackets]). Coaching-style prompts, not therapy or medical advice; the neuroscience and relationship ones are generic discussion prompts, so check any factual claims they produce.
1. **Self-Sabotage Autopsy** - You are a self-sabotage forensic analyst. I consistently self-sabotage around: [area]. Perform a complete autopsy: what is the payoff of the sabotage, which part of me benefits from failure, and what does this part fear more than failure?
2. **Neuro-Learning Hacks** - Research-backed discussion on how binaural beats, meditation, and other neuro-techniques influence learning outcomes, and provide actionable tips for implementation.
3. **Identity Time Capsule** - You are a future self specialist. Have me write a letter from my future self at [age/achievement level] back to today. This future self should tell me what they regret not doing sooner, what they are grateful for starting, and what I should prioritize.
4. **Remix Machine** - You are a remix strategist. Take [existing successful work/concept] and give me the complete remix blueprint: what to keep, what to change, what to amplify, what to subvert. I want to create something derivative that feels original.
5. **Emotional Mirroring Technique** - You are a rapport specialist. Teach me the advanced emotional mirroring and labeling technique: how to reflect someone's emotional state back in a way that creates instant connection and trust. Apply it to [context].
6. **Emotional Labor in Romantic Relationships** - Examine the concept of emotional labor in romantic relationships and its effects on mental health.
7. **Uncomfortable Truth Engine** - You are a brutal truth accelerator. I will describe my current situation: [describe]. You will give me the 5 uncomfortable truths I am avoiding, why I am avoiding them, and exact steps to face each one.
8. **Quantum Leap in Personal Growth** - If you could instantly integrate the knowledge, skills, and experiences of someone 10 years older and wiser, who would it be, and what lessons would you apply to your life?
9. **Imaginary Perfect Day** - Describe your perfect day in vivid detail, what activities, relationships, and habits would you include, and how can you integrate these elements into your real life?
10. **Transition Navigator** - You are a life transition specialist. I am transitioning from [current state] to [desired state]. Map the psychological, practical, and social dimensions of this transition. Give me the complete navigation protocol so I emerge stronger on the other side.
11. **Dopamine Deception** - Describe the neurotransmitter-based differences between romantic love and lust, and discuss the implications of these findings for modern dating and long-term relationships.
12. **Forbidden Creativity Session** - You are a permission-slip destroyer. I am afraid to create [describe idea/project] because [fear]. Walk me through a session that destroys that fear using psychological reframing, then give me a 3-day creation sprint plan.

Prompt 7 asks for harsh output: add "be direct but not cruel, and mark what is inference" if you want it usable. Use with `/life-prompt <number or name> <details>`.

# Added in batch 113
13. **Confront Your Antifragile Self** - I want to tap into my antifragile nature. Teach me how to use the power of chaos to forge mental resilience.
14. **The Social Energy Manager** - You are a social energy specialist. Design my complete social energy management system: which people drain me, which energize, how to recharge after draining interactions, and the optimal social diet for maintaining peak social energy.
15. **Impromptu Genius Protocol** - You are an impromptu speaking specialist. Design the complete mental framework for speaking brilliantly on any topic without preparation: how to access deep knowledge, how to structure on the fly, and the confidence technique to never freeze.
16. **Dark Mode Productivity** - Develop a nighttime productivity routine for a night owl writer, assuming a 2 am - 5 am work block, to maximize creative output while minimizing the risks of burnout.
17. **Neuroplasticity Accelerator (do not run as written)** - original: "You are a neuroplasticity engineer. I want to rewire my brain for [skill/trait]. Map out the exact neural pathways I need to strengthen and weaken. Then give me a 7-day intensive protocol using sensory deprivation, cold exposure, and focused visualization." Problems: an LLM cannot map "exact neural pathways", and sensory deprivation plus cold exposure can be harmful for some people (heart conditions, anxiety disorders, pregnancy, medication). Safer version: "I want to build [skill/trait]. Using established learning science (spaced practice, deliberate practice, sleep, feedback), design a 7-day practice plan with daily 30-minute sessions. Do not include cold exposure or sensory deprivation. Note what is well supported by evidence and what is speculative."
