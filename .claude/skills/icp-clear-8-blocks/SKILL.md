# How to build a clear ICP
1. **Customer data** - your best ICP is already in your CRM: industry, team size, tools they use, pains they say out loud.
2. **Pain points** - read call recordings, then find the real pain: money at stake, cost pressure, time lost, risk of doing nothing.
3. **Extra data points** - you need 4 to 10 to lock an ICP: company facts, headcount growth, buying signals, fresh funding.
4. **Vague vs sharp** - "B2B SaaS companies" and "mid-market SaaS with a sales team" are vague. Sharp: "mid-market SaaS on HubSpot, 10-person revenue team, with growing GTM channels".
5. **Micro-segments** - split the market into 3-6 groups of 100-300 companies; ask what they sell, how they sell, whether they are growing or hiring.
6. **Target list** - start with 100-300 accounts; only your best segments; check it with sales; re-cut it every quarter.
7. **Questionnaire** - title and who they report to; do they decide or use; who owns the budget; what are they judged on.
8. **Refine the persona** - once the account fits, pick the person: what are they trying to fix, what blocks them, what does a win look like.
