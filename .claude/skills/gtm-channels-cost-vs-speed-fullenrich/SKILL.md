## How to read it
- **[Certain]** Cost per SQL is a median across companies; your own cost depends on deal size, ICP and execution. Compare it with your **customer lifetime value**, not with other channels alone.
- **[Certain]** The chart comes from a data-enrichment vendor, uses third-party benchmark sources and midpoint estimates, and a log scale compresses large differences.
- **[Likely]** Referrals are cheapest and highest quality but not scalable on demand; ads and cold outreach are fastest but costly. A common mix is one fast channel for pipeline now plus one slow compounding channel (SEO or referrals).
