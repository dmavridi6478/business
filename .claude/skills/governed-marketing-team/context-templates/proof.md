## Rules
- Numbers, superlatives ("leading", "best", "first"), awards, certifications, customer names and testimonials all require a row.
- Badges and awards must carry the year (failure mode #2: a badge with no date).
- Regulated claims (clinical efficacy, CE/MDR class, indications) also require a regulatory reviewer sign-off in the "Approved by" column.
