# Founder-led vs team-led LinkedIn content

Source: one infographic by Dan Rosenthal for Workflows.io, comparing its founder-led setup with a team-led setup. Names, follower counts, topic lists and audience layers below are transcribed from the sheet; the arithmetic, critique and pilot plan are my additions. Follower counts are the sheet's and were not checked on LinkedIn.

| | Founder-led | Team-led |
|---|---|---|
| People posting | 2 (Dan Rosenthal 52,800+, Fivos Aresti 39,500+) | 8 (adds Lorenzo Amadei 4,800+, Levi Born 6,000+, Marios Charalampous 15,500+, Pari Tomar 13,000+, Angelee Carandang 9,300+, Jan Brochwicz 14,000+) |

The design idea worth keeping: each person owns four topics, and each topic maps to a buyer layer, so the company covers the whole buying group instead of only the top.

## Checks on the sheet's numbers

- **The team total does not add up.** The eight follower counts sum to about 154,900, not 180K+. The gap of roughly 25,000 is unexplained: it may include a company page or rounding up, but the sheet does not say. The founder total is consistent (52,800 + 39,500 = 92,300, shown as 90K+).
- **Followers are not the same as unique people.** Audiences overlap, so adding follower counts overstates reach.
- **No outcome is shown.** There is no pipeline, meetings booked, revenue or even impressions. "Touchpoints" counts posts, not responses.
- **Who wrote it.** The sheet is by a company founder promoting his own company's approach; treat it as one case, not evidence that team-led beats founder-led.
- **Scale effect confounded.** Eight people posting 18 times a week also have more total effort; the sheet cannot separate "team-led" from "more output".

## One-quarter pilot (my addition)

1. Week 0: list your buying group (decision maker, champion, influencer, end user) and the four questions each asks.
2. Week 1: choose 3 to 4 posters whose roles fit those layers; give each four topics and a posting rhythm they can sustain (start at one to two posts a week each).
3. Week 1: write the safeguards (below) and get each person's written agreement. It is their personal account.
4. Weeks 2 to 12: publish; each week log impressions, comments by buyer-layer job titles, profile views and inbound messages per person.
5. Week 12: compare against the founder-only baseline from the previous quarter; keep what produced replies from the right titles, drop the rest.

## Safeguards for employee advocacy

- Participation is voluntary; no quotas tied to performance reviews.
- Rules on confidential information, customer names, unreleased products and competitors.
- Disclose affiliation when you promote the company; follow platform and advertising rules.
- Agree what happens to the content and the audience when a person leaves; accounts belong to the individual.
- Do not use AI tools to post under someone's name without their review of each post.
