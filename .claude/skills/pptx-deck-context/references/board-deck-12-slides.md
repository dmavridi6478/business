# Board deck from supplied materials (12 slides)

Batch 113, from "Claude for Boardroom Decks" (Claude + PowerPoint; the graphic ends with a comment-for-access offer, so no skill files were provided).

Workflow shown: upload the business plan, financial model and market research; Claude reads the source files, checks the slide titles against your materials, reviews plan assumptions (budget and financial model), then drafts a **12-slide** deck covering a clear narrative, performance, market position, strategic priorities, the investment plan, financial projections and the decisions required. It stays concise and board-ready using the data in your documents; you refine by section.

Slides visible in the example: "Where we stand" (market position, TAM/SAM/initial market, competitor comparison table); "Decisions we need from the board" (use of proceeds with category, amount, share of total, three workstreams, milestones Q1-Q4); "Evidence behind our plan" (sources of validation, customer interviews).

Rules: every number on a slide traces to a named source file; mark estimates as estimates; the human reviews before it goes to a board. See `board-dashboard-3-statement` for the finance side and `investor-materials` for investor decks.
