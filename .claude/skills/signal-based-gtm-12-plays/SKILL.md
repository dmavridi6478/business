# 12 signal-based GTM plays (Shivam Sethi, Growth Today)

Source: one infographic; tools named are examples. The "30% come back" figure on the closed-lost card is the author's, with no source.

| # | Play | Flow as drawn | Tools shown | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Website visitors | People (RB2B) and companies (Factors.ai), ICP check (Clay), Tier 1 LinkedIn DM, Tier 2 email, not a fit goes to nurture | RB2B, Factors.ai, Clay | Meeting booked |
| 02 | LinkedIn engagement | Likes, comments, reposts, profile views on your post, captured by Trigify and Clay, enrich and ICP filter, DM | Trigify, Clay, Expandi | Warm conversation |
| 03 | Hiring-signal outbound | Signal (role, seniority, location) from TheirStack, find buyer (Clay), email on the role (Instantly) | TheirStack, Clay, Instantly | Meeting booked |
| 04 | Champion job changes | Champion moves from old account to new, caught by UserGems, match account (Clay), first touch (HubSpot) | UserGems, Clay, HubSpot | Warm intro |
| 05 | Closed-lost revival | Lost on timing or budget: re-engage, refresh data, wait and watch signals ("30% come back") | HubSpot and others | Back into pipeline |
| 06 | Email waterfall | Provider 1 (Prospeo), then 2 (BetterContact), then 3 (Apollo) if not found, then verify (Clay) | Prospeo, BetterContact, Apollo, Clay | Verified email |
| 07 | Lookalike expansion | Best customers, lookalikes (Ocean.io, Apollo), tiering (Clay), tiers 1 to 3 | Ocean.io, Apollo, Clay | Prioritized list |
| 08 | Competitor engagement | Followers or engagers of competitors A and B on LinkedIn (Trigify), enrich and dedupe (Clay), DM (Expandi) | Trigify, Clay, Expandi | In-market prospect |
| 09 | Inbound orchestration | Form fill, enrich and score (Clay, Claude), ICP fit? yes routes to a rep (HubSpot), no goes to nurture | Clay, Claude, HubSpot | Rep in minutes |
| 10 | Awareness scoring | 01 Identified (website visit), 02 Aware (content engagement), 03 Interested (repeat visits), 04 Considering (pricing page), 05 Selecting (demo request), scored in HubSpot and Clay | HubSpot, Clay | Stage score |
| 11 | Webinar follow-up | Registrants (Luma): attended gets recap and next step within 24 h (HubSpot), no-show gets replay link within 24 h | Luma, HubSpot | Follow-up in 24 h |
| 12 | AI call prep | Inputs from Clay, HubSpot, LinkedIn, website; Claude writes a brief (company, buyer, signals, talking points) | Clay, HubSpot, LinkedIn, Claude | Call-ready brief |

## Cautions
- **[Certain]** Plays 01, 02, 03 and 08 use personal data about people who did not contact you (visitor de-anonymisation, engagement scraping). GDPR, ePrivacy and LinkedIn terms apply; get legal review and an opt-out path before running them.
- **[Likely]** Plays 02 and 08 rely on scraping LinkedIn engagement and automated DMs, which can breach LinkedIn's terms and restrict accounts.
- **[Likely]** Play 06's three-provider waterfall raises coverage but also bounce risk; always verify before sending.
