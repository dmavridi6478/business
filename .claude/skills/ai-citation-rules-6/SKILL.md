# The rules of AI citations are changing

Source: an infographic (Batch 124) from a vendor selling an AI-citation tracking service (it ends "get your invite"). It reports ChatGPT citation shares for August 2026, credited to Promptwatch, and six rules for brands.

## What the graphic claims, and what was checked (30 October 2026)

| Claim on the graphic | Check |
|---|---|
| Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from 3.83% (18 Jul to 7 Aug) to 0.52% (14 to 17 Aug), an 86.4% relative fall | [Certain] Matches Promptwatch's own pages: "averaged 3.83% from July 18 to August 7, 2026" and "the August 14 to 17 average is 0.52%, an 86.4% relative drop" (data page dated 18 August 2026; blog post dated 20 August and updated 8 September). The method counts Reddit's daily share of all citations in ChatGPT responses that carry at least one citation, from UI monitoring. No sample size or model version is stated, and the data page says a data-collection issue "cannot be ruled out, so treat the size of the drop as provisional" |
| G2 + Capterra + Trustpilot combined: 7% on GPT-5.1, 0% on GPT-5.6 | [Certain] Not found on any Promptwatch page that was read. Trustpilot is not in the review-site post. No source read names those model versions. Treat as unverified. A later Promptwatch post (22 October 2026) gives different figures for a different window (28 September to 4 October): G2 0.73%, Capterra 0.32%, and 1.57% for G2, Gartner, Capterra and Software Advice together, from "one prompt over 30 days, so treat it as directional" |
| Losing ground: Reddit, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot. Gaining: company sites, documentation, help centres, pages answering the exact question | [Likely] Directionally consistent with the Reddit data. The "gaining" half is the graphic's inference; no gain figure is shown |

Consequence: do not quote "-86.4%" as a lasting change or "0%" for review sites. Quote the Reddit drop as "a reported fall in a three-week window, flagged by its source as provisional", and watch whether it persists.

## The six rules (summary, with how to apply)
1. **Own your expertise.** Keep your best answers on your own site, not only on third-party platforms. Action: list your ten most-asked buyer questions and make sure a page on your site answers each.
2. **Answer real questions.** Cover costs, comparisons, alternatives, limits and FAQs. Action: mine support tickets, sales calls and search queries for the exact wording; one page per question.
3. **Show proof, not hype.** Original research, data, methods, expert input. Action: publish one dataset, method note or benchmark per quarter with the numbers and how you got them.
4. **Keep it easy to parse.** Clear headings, short answers, named experts and dates. Action: lead each section with a direct answer, add author and updated date, use descriptive headings.
5. **Earn trust off-site.** Press coverage, expert mentions, trusted industry voices. Action: pitch your data to the publications your buyers read; do not buy fake reviews or mentions.
6. **Track AI visibility.** Who appears, who is cited, where you are missing. Action: a fixed list of 20 to 30 prompts run monthly across assistants; log mention, citation, sentiment, source.

Closing line of the graphic: "More content won't win AI citations. Useful, authoritative, verifiable content will." [Likely] Sound as advice, unproven as a causal claim: citation behaviour changes with model updates, so treat any single month as noise.

## Cautions
- Citation share is not traffic or revenue. Measure assisted pipeline, branded search and direct-answer visits alongside it.
- Models change often; a rule that worked last quarter may not. Re-run your prompt set after major model releases.
- Do not astroturf Reddit or review sites. It breaches platform rules, and the evidence above suggests those sources are volatile anyway.
- Vendors of tracking tools have an interest in the problem looking urgent.

## 30-day action list
Week 1: build the 20 to 30 prompt set and take a baseline. Week 2: write or fix five question pages. Week 3: add dates, authors and structured answers to existing pages. Week 4: publish one proof asset and pitch it; re-run the prompt set.
