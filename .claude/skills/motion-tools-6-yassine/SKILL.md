# 6 tools that make your work move (@yassinezaanouni)

Source: an 8-slide dark carousel (cover "Screenshots in, motion out. Every one of them is free to try", six tool slides 01 to 06, a "Save this for the next post" summary). Prices, free tiers and limits were **not verified**; the cover says "free to try", which is not the same as free.

| # | Tool | URL | Use | Check before relying on it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Reelfolio | reelfolio.io | Drop in messy screenshots and screen recordings and get a polished showreel video (page says "Create a video in 10 seconds", "Commercial use") | Export limits, watermark, and what happens to client screenshots you upload |
| 02 | Rotato | rotato.app | 3D device mockup images and movies | Free vs paid export; Mac and Windows app |
| 03 | Jitter | jitter.video | Motion design in a browser tab, now with AI agents; page cites "over 20,000 creative teams" | Free-plan limits; that count is the vendor's |
| 04 | Shots | shots.so | Screenshots into animated mockup frames for social and sites | Watermark and licence for commercial use |
| 05 | Rive | rive.app | Interactive animations that react to clicks, for mobile, desktop, web and games; page cites Spotify Wrapped and Duolingo | The page shows a `curl ... \| sh` CLI installer: read the script before running it |
| 06 | Motionity | motionity.app | Free browser motion editor, nothing to install | Check the project's licence and whether files stay in the browser |

## Use
- For product demos pair Rotato or Shots with a template from `design-templates`; for code-driven video use `hyperframes` or `remotion`.
- Uploading unreleased product screens to any web tool shares them with that vendor; use test data or a local tool for confidential work.
- Slide counters say "N / 06" but the summary asks "What did I miss?": it is an engagement prompt, not a complete list.
