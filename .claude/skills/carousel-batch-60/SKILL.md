# 60 carousels in 60 minutes (@ai_slacker)
| Minute | Step | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0-15 | Batch the hooks, not the posts | One prompt, 60 hooks sorted by angle ("Give me 60 carousel hooks for [audience]"). Delete the weak ones, keep 40. |
| 15-30 | Turn each hook into slide copy | Feed it your past posts so the voice is yours. Run it in batches of 10, not one at a time. |
| 30-35 | Lock one template | Colours, fonts, layout decided once. This is why slide 6 takes minutes instead of hours. |
| 35-50 | Render all 60 at once | Same template, 60 sets of copy poured in. No designing. |
| 50-60 | Queue the whole month | Schedule everything in one sitting. |

**4 rules:** separate thinking from making (hooks first, copy second, design last); never batch in one pass (ten at a time keeps quality); feed it your old posts (without them everything sounds the same); keep 40 of 60 (the cut is the quality control).
**The honest part:** you get 60 drafts, not 60 finished posts, so you still edit. The first batch is slowest. Volume without a point is noise; hooks still have to be good.
Tools here: `/carousel`, `carousel-writer`, `linkedin-carousel-builder` (Taplio), Canva connector for rendering, Typefully or Taplio for queueing. Takeaway: "Batch once. Post for a month."
