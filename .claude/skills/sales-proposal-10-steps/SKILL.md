# 10 steps to master your sales proposal (Sollo graphic by Michael)

| # | Step | Question it answers | Rule on the graphic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead with their desired outcome | What do they want to achieve, and by when? | Make it the title: "Acme: Doubling qualified leads by March" beats "Proposal for Acme" |
| 2 | Recap how you got here | Why are you having this conversation? | Summarise the trigger and the key points discussed |
| 3 | Show where they are now | What is happening today? | Problem, root cause and impact in their words from discovery |
| 4 | Show where they want to go | What does success look like? | Outcome, targets and timeframe they gave you |
| 5 | Make the stakes clear | What happens if they do nothing? | Cost of the problem in money, time or missed opportunity; put a number on it |
| 6 | Map your solution to their problems | How will you solve what they told you? | "You told us X, so we'll do Y"; leave out anything unconnected |
| 7 | Make the timeline simple | What happens after they say yes? | Key stages, when, how quickly you can start |
| 8 | Use one relevant case study | Who have you helped with a similar problem? | Where they started, what you did, result, how long |
| 9 | Put the investment in context | What will it cost compared with the problem? | Price after the stakes; two or three options where sensible |
| 10 | Prescribe the next step | What exactly should they do next? | One clear action: sign, choose an option, book onboarding, agree a start date |

Run `/sales-proposal-10 <deal facts>` to draft one. Related: `proposal-writer`, `claude-for-sales-course-map`, `/objection`.

## Cautions
- **[Certain]** Steps 3 to 5 depend on real discovery notes; without them any number in step 5 is invented.
- **[Likely]** Never put a case-study result in front of a prospect unless the customer approved it.
- The footer of the graphic promotes the author's product; nothing was signed up for.
