# Personal Brand Strategy map

Source: an infographic by Thomas Pearce (image supplied in chat, 12 October 2026). The framework is a two-tier tree; nothing in it is data-backed. It is a checklist, not research.

## The map

**Who you are (positioning)**

| Element | Sub-points on the infographic | The question to answer |
|---|---|---|
| Niche | Expertise, Audience, Platform | Who exactly do you help, with what, and where do they already read? |
| Positioning | Unique angle, Category, Authority | Why you, in one sentence a stranger repeats correctly? |
| Story | Origin, Turning point, Mission | What happened that made this your work? |
| Voice | Tone, Language, Energy | Which five words sound like you, which five never? |
| Content pillars | 3-5 themes, Depth, Range | Which 3-5 topics will you be known for? |
| Proof | Results, Testimonials, Case studies | What can a doubter verify? |
| Offer ladder | Free, Low-ticket, High-ticket | What is the next step for each level of trust? |

**How you grow (distribution)**

| Element | Sub-points | The question to answer |
|---|---|---|
| Content engine | Formats, Frequency, Repurposing | What do you publish, how often, from one source idea? |
| Distribution | Organic, Paid, Collaborations | Where does it reach people who do not follow you? |
| Community | Comments, DMs, Inner circle | Who do you talk to every week, one to one? |
| Lead magnets | Triggers, Opt-ins, Nurture | What do you give for an email address, and what follows? |
| Social proof | Milestones, Features, Endorsements | What do others say about you in public? |
| Monetisation | Products, Services, Partnerships | What is sold, to whom, at what price? |
| Optimisation | Analytics, Testing, Iteration | Which two numbers do you review each month? |

## Method

1. Ask the 14 questions one at a time. Do not answer for the user; reflect their words back.
2. Score each element 0 (not started), 1 (idea), 2 (written down), 3 (in use), 4 (producing results with evidence).
3. The weakest **Who** element gates everything else: do not work on distribution above a 1 in Niche, Positioning or Proof.
4. Output: a one-page brand sheet (the 7 answers), a score table, the single weakest element, and a 30-day plan.

## 30-day sequence

- Week 1: Niche, Positioning, Voice (words only; no design).
- Week 2: Story, Pillars, Proof inventory (collect, do not invent).
- Week 3: Content engine (one idea to five formats), Lead magnet draft.
- Week 4: Distribution plan, Community routine, two metrics, Offer ladder draft.

## Honesty rules

- Proof must be real and permitted. No invented results or testimonials.
- Existing repo skills cover parts of this: `linkedin-personal-brand-2026`, `linkedin-positioning-statement`, `linkedin-profile-funnel`, `hook-writer`, `one-person-offer-architect`. Use them for the detailed work; this skill is the map and the scoring.

## Command

`/brand-map` starts the interview and returns the scored map. The design template for the map is `Artifacts/templates/mind-map-tree-brand-blue.html`.
