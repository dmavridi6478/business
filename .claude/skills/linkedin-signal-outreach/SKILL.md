# LinkedIn Signal Outreach

Source: reviewed from an uploaded infographic — "The LinkedIn Outreach
Matrix Nobody's Using" (Josh Sanders). No repo, but 3 real example
messages, quoted verbatim below. Complements this repo's existing
`outreach-channels` skill (which covers LinkedIn's 2-DM-max volume rule,
plus cold-call and video-outreach) rather than duplicating it — this
skill is about *who to message and when*, not the volume ceiling.

## 1. Know your 3 types

Every LinkedIn prospect is exactly one of three types, and each needs a
different opening:

- **Warm** — already knows the sender exists.
- **Cold** — fits the ICP, has never heard of the sender.
- **Dream** — the one prospect who'd change the business if they said
  yes.

## 2. Message warm prospects like you've met

They already know the name — don't restart the introduction. Reference
what they've already seen or done, and skip the formal opener entirely.

```text
Hey [name], saw you'd been around my page. What made you stop and look?
```

## 3. Slow down with cold prospects

They've never heard of the sender, so the reply has to be earned before
anything is asked. Reference their content specifically instead of a
generic compliment, and aim for a real conversation over 2-3 messages
rather than a pitch on message one.

```text
Hey [name], loved your post on [topic], I learnt [XXX]. How are you handling [challenge] right now?
```

## 4. Earn dream prospects with value first

The hardest to reach, least likely to reply, worth every message if they
do. Never pitch on message one — show up with something useful and no
ask attached.

```text
Hey [name], saw the news on [funding/launch/hire]. Happy to share what's worked for others in your spot.
```

## 5. Watch the signals

The stronger the signal, the faster the reach-out should follow:

- **Profile views** — they want to know more about the sender specifically.
- **Thoughtful comments** — real engagement, not "great post" waffle.
- **Recent moves** — a new role, funding, or hire all mean they're more
  open right now than they will be later.
- **Public problems** — they're posting the exact thing the offer solves.
- **Connection requests** — they made the first move; don't waste it with
  a generic thank-you.

## 6. Know where to look

Signals aren't hidden, and most outreach misses them because nobody's
checking: the notifications tab, comments under bigger creators in the
relevant space, and company pages when chasing specific accounts.

## 7. Follow the 3-step flow

Every message follows the same sequence: **spot the signal** → **match
the message to the type** (warm/cold/dream, per sections 2-4) → **send
within 24 hours** — signals go cold fast, so speed matters as much as
the message itself.
