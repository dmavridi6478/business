## Pick by goal

- Tech, design, marketing: WeWorkRemotely, Remotive, Himalayas, RemoteOK, Working Nomads.
- Startups with salary transparency: Wellfound.
- Curated or vetted (paid or screened): FlexJobs, Remotive.
- Aggregator that points to other boards: JobBoardSearch.
- Freelance writing: FreelanceWriting. Customer service and accounting: SkipTheDrive.
- Network-driven search: LinkedIn (see the `linkedin-*` skills).

## Weekly routine (draft-only)

1. Pick five boards that match the role; save the search with filters.
2. Spend 20 minutes per board, twice a week. Log role, company, link, salary range, date in a sheet.
3. Shortlist at most ten; tailor the CV and cover note per role (`job-application-5-prompts`, `dream-job-10-prompts`).
4. Never pay a board or "fee to apply" without checking reviews. Treat any role asking for payment or bank details as a scam.

The agent `remote-job-scout` runs steps 1 to 3 and only drafts; it never applies.
