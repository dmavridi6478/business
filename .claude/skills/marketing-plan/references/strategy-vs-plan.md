# Marketing strategy is not a marketing plan

Batch 113, from the "Marketing Strategy ≠ Marketing Plan" diagram (Nehal Kazim).

| | Marketing strategy | Marketing plan |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | A connected system: customer, positioning, offer, channel and brand all linked, producing momentum, then goals A, B, C | A sequence: start, campaign, then ads / content / posts, then goals A, B |
| Question it answers | Why will this customer choose us, and what keeps compounding? | What do we do, in what order, by when? |
| Failure mode | Never reaches execution | Activity with no positioning behind it |

How to use: write the strategy first (customer, positioning, offer, channel, brand and how they reinforce each other), then derive the plan from it. If a plan item cannot be traced to a strategy link, cut it. Related: `brand-positioning-zones`, `marketing-org-operating-model` (cascade mandates into workflows).
