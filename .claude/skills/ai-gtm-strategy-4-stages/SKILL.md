# How to build an AI GTM strategy
| Stage | Goal | Do it right | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Problem discovery and ICP clarity | Know your buyers before you automate anything | Look at your 20 best customers; use their real words from calls; write one sharp ICP before automating | Bad ICP in = bad pipeline out; a clear ICP lets AI personalise at scale |
| 2 Validation and message testing | Test hooks and angles before you scale | Test new ideas on a smaller sample first; change one thing at a time; keep what gets replies, kill the rest | Small tests protect your domain; relevance wins replies, not volume |
| 3 Personalised multichannel outreach | One story, three channels, one owner per account | Go 1:1 on accounts with a live signal; same story across email, LinkedIn, ads; one owner per account | Multichannel books more meetings than one channel; warm them first with content and ads |
| 4 Generation and optimisation loop | Feed results back in every week, not every quarter | Feed won deals back into targeting; retry no-reply accounts after 60 days; track one number per layer | Every deal sharpens the next round; AI only compounds when you train it weekly |
**Operationalise each layer:** turn every AI tool into a system; one flow (research, outreach, follow-up, CRM); one owner per automation.
**Weekly AI rhythm:** check what AI did every week; refresh hooks and prompts every 2 weeks.
**Close the feedback loop:** feed wins back into prompts; cut what flops fast.
