# 21. Champion Identifier

Stage 3 of 7: Qualification. Skill 21 of 50 in the B2B sales set. The title comes from the Prosp "Claude Skills for B2B Sales" graphic; the method below is original to this repository (the graphic's own skill file was not obtained). Follow `b2b-sales-standard` for evidence tags, drafts-only and privacy rules.

## When to use

Use for a live deal when the user names a likely champion and wants evidence rather than hope.

## Inputs

- Required: the candidate's role and what they have done in the deal, with dates
- Required: the economic buyer, if known
- Optional: other candidates
- Optional: the internal politics the user has heard about
- If a required input is missing, ask for it. Do not guess or fill it with an example.

## Method

1. List what the candidate has done: shared internal information, arranged access, argued for the solution when you were absent, spent effort or political capital.
2. Test five things, each pass, fail or unknown with evidence: access to the economic buyer, credibility inside the company, personal win from success, willingness to share information, willingness to act.
3. Classify: champion, coach (helps you, lacks influence), friendly contact, or unknown.
4. Choose tests that reveal the truth cheaply: ask for an introduction to the economic buyer, ask for the internal evaluation criteria, ask them to review a draft joint plan.
5. Plan how to equip a real champion: what they need to sell internally, in their words.
6. State the risk if the champion is wrong, and a second candidate to develop.

## Output

Use these headings, in this order:

- `## Behaviour evidence`
- `## Five tests`
- `## Classification`
- `## Tests to run next`
- `## Champion enablement`
- `## Risk and backup`
- `## Assumptions and what could be wrong`

## Quality checks

The output fails if any of these is false:

- Each of the five tests has a verdict and evidence.
- The classification follows from the tests.
- At least one test action is concrete and low-risk.
- A backup candidate or plan is named.
- Every number not taken from the inputs carries a `[calc: ...]` or `[assumption]` tag (see `b2b-sales-standard`).
- Nothing is sent, posted, priced or signed; anything outward is labelled "draft, needs approval".

## Do not

- Do not count friendliness as influence.
- Do not ask the candidate to take a risk you would not explain.
- Do not describe the person's motives beyond the evidence.
