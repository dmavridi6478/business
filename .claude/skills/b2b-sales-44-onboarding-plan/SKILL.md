# 44. Onboarding Plan

Stage 7 of 7: Onboarding and expansion. Skill 44 of 50 in the B2B sales set. The title comes from the Prosp "Claude Skills for B2B Sales" graphic; the method below is original to this repository (the graphic's own skill file was not obtained). Follow `b2b-sales-standard` for evidence tags, drafts-only and privacy rules.

## When to use

Use at hand-over from sales to customer success, with the agreed scope and the buyer's success measures.

## Inputs

- Required: the signed scope and success measures in the buyer's words
- Required: the customer's team and roles
- Required: the start date and any hard dates
- Optional: the sales notes on concerns and promises
- Optional: the customer's technical environment
- If a required input is missing, ask for it. Do not guess or fill it with an example.

## Method

1. Restate the success measures and every promise made during the sale. List the promises so they are kept.
2. Define the first-value milestone: the earliest point at which the customer sees a result they care about.
3. Break the path into milestones with an owner on each side and a relative date; use calendar dates only where supplied.
4. List what the customer must provide (data, access, people) and by when, and what happens if it is late.
5. Plan training by role.
6. List risks to first value and the early-warning signs.
7. Write the kick-off agenda and the welcome message as drafts needing approval.

## Output

Use these headings, in this order:

- `## Success measures and promises`
- `## First-value milestone`
- `## Milestone plan`
- `## Customer inputs`
- `## Training`
- `## Risks and warnings`
- `## Kick-off draft`
- `## Assumptions and what could be wrong`

## Quality checks

The output fails if any of these is false:

- Every promise from the sale is listed.
- The first-value milestone is defined.
- Every milestone has an owner on each side.
- Drafts are labelled.
- Every number not taken from the inputs carries a `[calc: ...]` or `[assumption]` tag (see `b2b-sales-standard`).
- Nothing is sent, posted, priced or signed; anything outward is labelled "draft, needs approval".

## Do not

- Do not drop a promise made in the sale.
- Do not plan only seller tasks.
- Do not use customer data in training without permission.
