# SEO vs AEO vs GEO (CiteRank graphic)

Source: one infographic from a vendor (citerank.com, "get your invite"). Headline claim: "**2/3 of today's search ecosystem goes missing when you only do SEO**". **[Certain] No source or method is given for 2/3**; treat it as marketing.

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Search engine optimisation | Answer engine optimisation | Generative engine optimisation |
| Goal | Top of classic organic results | Your expertise quoted in Google's AI Overviews and knowledge panels | Named in detailed recommendations on ChatGPT, Gemini and Llama |
| Best for | Steady, long-term traffic to high-intent service pages | Voice, mobile and conversational queries, no click needed | Being a trusted name inside AI training models |
| Gain | Proven, steady traffic; authority compounds | Instant authority in the answer; strong on voice and mobile | Early-mover edge in AI search; framed as the trusted name |
| Watch | Slow to show; exposed to algorithm updates | Many users never click; snippet positions shift often | Direct impact hard to measure; needs presence on many platforms beyond your site |
| How it works | Map high-intent keywords to service pages; earn authoritative backlinks | Precise direct answers; FAQ and How-To schema | Structured thought leadership with data and citations; show up on credible sources AI draws from |
| Rank higher | Target keyword clusters; audit technical health, refresh quarterly | Answers of 40 to 60 words under a clear heading; wrap FAQ and How-To in schema | Factual, entity-rich content on E-E-A-T principles; feed the sources AI cites most (industry reports, forums, publications) |

## Read critically
- **[Certain]** The three overlap and share the same base: crawlable, accurate, well-structured pages. They are not three separate channels.
- **[Likely]** "Being in AI training models" cannot be controlled or measured; focus on being cited by sources models retrieve.
- **[Likely]** The "40 to 60 words" rule is a common heuristic, not a published threshold.
