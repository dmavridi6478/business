# The insights quadrant of unified intelligence

Source: a Gartner graphic. It states the structure only; it contains no data, benchmark or scoring method. The audit and router below are added and are **not** Gartner's.

## The four quadrants

| Quadrant | Capabilities listed |
|---|---|
| **Data insights** | Advanced analytics; marketing analytics; data sciences; customer segments; test optimisation |
| **Customer insights** | Consumer trends; consumer research; voice of the customer; category opportunity sizing; buyer trends |
| **Market insights** | Global trends; market intelligence; market research; brand research; business opportunity sizing |
| **Business insights** | Business intelligence; competitive intelligence; product intelligence; industry trends; pricing research |

Centre: **informed decisioning**.

## Router - which quadrant answers which question

| Question | Quadrant | Typical first source |
|---|---|---|
| Which segment converts, and does the test win? | Data | CRM / analytics export |
| What do buyers say they want and why do they leave? | Customer | calls, reviews, surveys |
| How big is the category and where is it going? | Market | market reports, brand tracker |
| What are competitors doing and what should we charge? | Business | competitor pages, pricing research |

## Maturity audit (score each quadrant 0-3)

0 = nobody owns it. 1 = ad hoc requests. 2 = a named owner and a recurring deliverable. 3 = findings are combined with the other quadrants and change a decision each quarter. For every quadrant give one piece of evidence. A quadrant scoring 3 whose output never meets another quadrant's is really a 2: the point of the model is synthesis.

## Synthesis rule

For any decision, require one finding from at least two quadrants, and state where they disagree. (Example: customer insight says price is a barrier; business insight shows competitors charge more - the disagreement is the finding.)

## Rules

- Do not present the audit as a Gartner score.
- Name the owner of each quadrant, even if it is one person.
- Use only data the user supplies or connected sources (Ahrefs, Semrush, HubSpot, Amplitude, Fireflies); label gaps "not measured".
