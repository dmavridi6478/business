# 49 PM processes and the 13-phase road map (Template22)

Sources: "49 Project Management Processes: PMBOK Process Groups and Knowledge Areas at a Glance" (mind map) and "Project Management Road Map". Both carry a TEMPLATE22.COM mark.

## The mind map
Five groups around "Project Management Processes (49)": **Initiating** (Develop Project Charter, Identify Stakeholders), **Planning** (plans for management, scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, communications, risk, procurement and stakeholders, plus Collect Requirements, Define Scope, Create WBS, Define Activities, Sequence Activities, Estimate Durations and Costs, Determine Budget, Identify and Analyse Risks, Plan Risk Responses), **Executing** (Direct and Manage Project Work, Manage Project Knowledge, Acquire Resources, Develop Team, Manage Team, Manage Communications and similar), **Monitoring and Controlling** (Monitor and Control Work, Perform Integrated Change Control, Validate Scope, Control Scope, Control Schedule, Control Costs, Control Quality, Monitor Communications, Monitor Risks, Control Procurements) and **Closing** (Close Project or Phase).

Errors in the graphic: "Peveop Project Management Plan", "Create WBS" twice, "Develop Project Charter" twice, "Control Goulity", "Estimate Activity Durations" misspelt, and "Monitor Risks" placed oddly. The graphic's 49 matches the PMBOK Guide 6th edition structure (2 + 24 + 10 + 12 + 1) [Likely]. PMBOK 7 (2021) replaced process groups with principles and performance domains, so check which edition your employer or exam uses [Likely].

## The 13-phase road map
| # | Phase | Deliverables on the graphic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Initiation | Business case; project charter; project assessment; identify stakeholders; approval and go/no-go |
| 2 | Project Planning | Project management plan; scope statement; WBS; schedule plan; cost plan; resource plan; risk management plan; communication plan |
| 3 | Project Scheduling | Activity list; task sequencing; duration estimation; Gantt chart; milestone plan; schedule baseline |
| 4 | Resource Management | Resource planning; allocation; RACI matrix; team development plan; training plan; resource tracking |
| 5 | Risk Management | Risk identification; assessment; register; response plan; monitoring; contingency plan |
| 6 | Procurement Management | Procurement plan; make or buy analysis; supplier evaluation; contract management; purchase order; procurement tracking |
| 7 | Stakeholder Management | Identification; analysis; engagement plan; communication plan; stakeholder register; feedback management |
| 8 | Quality Management | Quality planning; standards; assurance; control; inspection checklist; quality metrics |
| 9 | Project Execution | Task execution; team collaboration; deliverable development; progress monitoring; issue management; status reporting |
| 10 | Cost Management | Cost estimation; budget planning; cost tracking; earned value management; variance analysis; cost control measures |
| 11 | Change Management | Change request; impact assessment; change log; approval workflow; implementation plan; change monitoring |
| 12 | Monitoring and Controlling | Performance tracking; KPI dashboard; variance analysis; risk and issue monitoring; quality monitoring; corrective actions; project reporting |
| 13 | Project Closure | Final deliverables; closure report; lessons learned; stakeholder sign-off; release resources; archive documents |

The order is a reading order, not a schedule: execution (9) runs alongside cost, change and monitoring (10 to 12) and planning areas repeat. The tracker workbook turns this table into a status sheet. Use `/pm-roadmap`.
