# Drug-coated balloon (DCB): indication evidence wheel

**Scope and limits.** One unsourced infographic. It carries no trial names, effect sizes or product names, so it cannot support a promotional claim. Clinical decisions belong to the treating cardiologist and current guidelines; commercial use needs the company's medical/legal/regulatory review and EU MDR-compliant claims (see `medtech-brand-strategy` and the proof-row gate in `docs/marketing-context/proof.md`).

## Evidence levels as drawn

| Level (legend) | Meaning in legend | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| High level of evidence (green) | "Should be considered" | Small vessels (< 3.0 mm); in-stent restenosis (ISR); bifurcations - DCB for side branch (SB) |
| Intermediate level of evidence (orange) | "May be considered" | Bifurcations - DCB for main vessel (MV); long / diffuse lesions and multivessel disease; calcified lesions and nodules; chronic total occlusion (CTO); diabetes mellitus; high bleeding risk (HBR); chronic kidney disease; young patients with long life expectancy; elderly / frail patients |
| Work in progress (grey) | "Evidence evolving" / trials ongoing | Vulnerable plaques; acute coronary syndromes / AMI |

## Technique principles (centre of the wheel)

DCB alone, or the hybrid / blended approach with a drug-eluting stent (DES) - optimal lesion preparation - intravascular imaging - management of dissections. Tagline: "Leave nothing behind".

## How to use it in business work

1. **Training slide**: the three tiers as a one-page overview; keep the legend wording ("should / may be considered") exactly.
2. **Gap analysis**: for each setting, list which of your products has a labelled indication, which has published data, and which has neither. Use `Unknown` rather than guessing.
3. **Congress plan**: orange settings with ongoing trials are the likely abstract topics; grey settings are "watch" topics, never claims.
4. **Greek market access**: map each setting to the reimbursement or tender category it would fall under (with a local specialist); the infographic says nothing on this.
5. **Content guard**: every statement shown to HCPs must be traceable to a labelled indication and a cited study; mark the wheel itself "illustration - source not stated" if reused.
