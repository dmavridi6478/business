## Read critically
- **[Certain]** Open WebUI shows 140K here and 154k on another card in this repo's notes; Langflow at 194K is unusually high. Check stars on GitHub before quoting.
- **[Certain]** Maxun is AGPL-3.0, and Open WebUI's licence is custom; read before offering them to clients.
- **[Likely]** "Works fully offline, zero data leaves your box" is only true with a local model and no outbound plugins.
- **[Likely]** "Challenging paid software" means partial overlap; hosting, security and updates are your job.
- Browser Use and OpenHands act on real systems; sandbox them. Related: `dotdevs-5-github-repos-for-ai-app`, `replace-so-oss-repos-10`, `self-hosted-docker-stack`.
