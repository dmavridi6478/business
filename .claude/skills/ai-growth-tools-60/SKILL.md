## Choosing from it

- Pick the job first, then at most two tools per category; ten tools in a category are alternatives, not a stack.
- Prefer a tool you already have a connector for; it can be driven from here without copying data.
- The map has no pricing, data-handling or accuracy information. Check each vendor's terms before sending client or personal data.
- Use `/ai-tool-pick [business task]`.
