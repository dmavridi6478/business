## Weekly routine (`/linkedin-week`)

Mon: write the week's posts (45 min). Tue: 20 meaningful comments. Wed: 20 DMs to the shortlist. Thu: follow-ups. Fri: update the six numbers and choose the one fix for next week. Output a Friday summary: six numbers vs last week, leakiest step, one change.

## Guardrails

- 20 connection requests and 20 DMs a day is the source's illustration; LinkedIn applies its own limits and terms. Stay under them, no scraping, no bulk automation tools that breach terms.
- Under GDPR, message only people with a plausible legitimate interest and honour opt-outs immediately.
- Proposals and testimonials are drafts until the user approves; never fabricate proof.
- The comment-keyword mechanic should be run by a human or a terms-compliant tool.
