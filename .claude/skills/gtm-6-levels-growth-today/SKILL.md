# The 6 levels of a GTM strategy (Growth Today, Jani Vrancsik)

Source: one graphic of six stepped panels with example tools. The tool names are examples, not recommendations; no pricing or results are given.

| Level | Job | Categories and example tools on the graphic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Traffic generation | Get attention | Content (LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Instagram, Reddit, podcasts); Outbound (Instantly, HeyReach, Smartlead, Nooks, Lemlist, Apollo); Ads (LinkedIn, Reddit, Google, YouTube, Meta, X); Partnerships (Crossbeam, Zapier, PartnerStack, affiliates, G2, events) |
| 2 Lead capture | Turn visitors into leads | Lead magnets (Tally, Notion, Lovable); Webinars (Luma, Google Meet); Community (Skool, Slack); Newsletter (beehiiv, Kit); Social engagement (Trigify, HeyReach) |
| 3 Lead nurture | Keep warm | Email flows (ActiveCampaign, HubSpot, Instantly); Retargeting ads (LinkedIn, Meta, Google); SDRs (Nooks, Apollo, HeyReach); Website intent (RB2B, Koala, Trigify); Content (LinkedIn, YouTube, X) |
| 4 Conversion | Win the deal | Website (Webflow, Lovable); Proof (G2, Trustpilot, case studies); Free tools (Replit, calculators, templates); Demo video (Loom, HeyGen, Synthesia); Booking (Calendly, Chili Piper, HubSpot) |
| 5 Qualification | Prioritise | Enrich (Clay, FullEnrich, Prospeo); Score (HubSpot, MadKudu, Pocus); Route to CRM (Chili Piper, Salesforce, Attio); Tiered outreach (Tier 1 Nooks, Tier 2 HeyReach, Tier 3 Instantly) |
| 6 Retention and expansion | Keep and grow | Support (Intercom, Pylon, Slack); Champions (Champify, Common Room); Referrals (PartnerStack, Crossbeam); Offers (sprint projects, recurring services, courses) |

## Use
Audit your own stack by level: name one owner, one metric and one tool per level, and delete tools that do not feed a metric. Pair with `gtm-5-stages-data-layer`, `gtm-funnel-2026`, `gtm-guide-map`, `gtm-outbound-engine`.

## Cautions
- **[Certain]** Pairing Level 5 enrichment (mobile numbers, emails) with Level 1 outbound at scale carries GDPR, ePrivacy and telemarketing risk; confirm a lawful basis.
- **[Likely]** Six levels with 4 to 5 tools each is up to about 25 subscriptions; most small teams need one or two per level.
- The graphic is also marketing for the author's newsletter (growthtoday.co); nothing was followed.
