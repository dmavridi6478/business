## What the graphic leaves out, for a corporate reader
1. **Data governance is the real selection criterion.** For each tool ask: does the plan you would buy exclude your data from model training, where is it processed, can it be deleted on exit, and does your security team approve it? Consumer plans and enterprise plans differ. [Likely] DeepSeek's hosted apps are restricted or banned in some organisations and governments; check your policy before putting company data in them, and note that running its open weights yourself is a different risk from using its hosted service.
2. **Categorisation is loose.** Cursor is a code editor, not an automation platform. Clay is data enrichment for sales and marketing. Browserbase is browser infrastructure for agents, not a business app. Poe is an aggregator of other models. Flux is a model, not an app. MCP-style connections are not in the list.
3. **Possible naming and ownership traps.** "Motion" here is the scheduling app, not the creative-analytics product connected in this account. [Guessing] Limitless (the wearable and meeting-memory product) was reported to have been acquired and to have wound down its hardware; confirm it still exists and what happened to stored data before recommending it. Several of these tools have been renamed or merged since graphics like this are made.
4. **Overlaps.** Fathom, Fireflies, Otter and Granola all record and summarise meetings; recording needs consent from everyone on the call and a retention rule. Pick one. Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic and Anyword overlap heavily; Claude or ChatGPT with your brand guidelines often replaces two of them.
5. **Tool count is not a strategy.** Sixty tools is a catalogue. A corporate programme needs three things: approved tools for confidential data, a list of banned uses, and one measured pilot per department.
6. **No evidence of "growth".** Nothing on the graphic ties any tool to revenue or cost. Define the metric before the trial.

## Quick picker by job
| Job | Start with | Check first |
|---|---|---|
| Research a market or a competitor | Perplexity or Claude with sources, NotebookLM for your own documents | Source links resolve; dates visible |
| Draft and edit business writing | Claude or ChatGPT plus Grammarly | Brand rules and facts supplied by you |
| Presentations and visuals | Canva AI, Figma AI, Adobe Firefly | Licensing and brand-safety terms of generated images |
| Video from a script | HeyGen or Synthesia | Consent for any real person's likeness; disclosure rules |
| Meetings | One recorder only (Fathom, Fireflies or Granola) | Consent, retention, who can read transcripts |
| Cross-tool automation | n8n, Make or Zapier | Credentials in a secret store; human approval before send or spend |
