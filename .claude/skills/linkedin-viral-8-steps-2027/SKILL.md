# How to go viral on LinkedIn in 2027 (8 steps)

Source: one infographic; the "7:3" ratio and format advice are the author's heuristics, not platform data. For the earlier 9-tactic card see `linkedin-virality-playbook`.

1. **Study posts that beat the average.** An outlier is a post that did several times its creator's normal numbers. Before saving one: work out what the account normally does; check the post is performing, not the famous person; note which part carried it (hook, format or claim).
2. **Rebuild the idea, don't copy the post.** Keep the structure, hook pattern and format. Replace the story, proof and angle with yours.
3. **Give every post one job before you write it.** Reach (written for people who've never heard of you), Trust (for people your reach already brought), Action (one specific next step). Mix **7 reach : 3 trust-or-ask**. "A post written because it was Tuesday does nothing for anyone."
4. **Keep the first hook line under 12 words.** The first two lines must earn the "see more". Example from the card: 18 words (bad), 16 (bad), "My first three hires left within a year." (8, good).
5. **Use the second hook line to continue.** Line 1 grabs, line 2 carries: a bridge, not a second headline.
6. **Choose the format after the goal.** Followers -> infographic or cheat sheet; profile clicks -> photo with a story attached; reach and impressions -> short quote graphic or text-only take; saves -> numbered breakdown people can use.
7. **Put something worth saving in every post.** A checklist (five things to run through), a template (exact wording to copy), or a swipe (three examples to adapt this week). Saves travel further than likes.
8. **Leverage the 30 minutes after you publish.** Reply to first comments in full sentences; send it to three people who will genuinely use it; ask commenters something back; comment on others' posts in your niche while you wait; keep replying, late comments still count.

## Pre-publish checklist
- [ ] One job chosen (reach / trust / action) and this week's mix is about 7:3
- [ ] Hook line 1 is under 12 words; line 2 continues it
- [ ] Format matches the goal
- [ ] A saveable resource is inside the post
- [ ] 30 minutes free after posting; three people lined up to send it to
- [ ] The idea is rebuilt: my story, my proof, my angle

## Cautions
"Send it to three people" is fine; engagement pods and asking colleagues to boost artificially are not, and the platform may suppress them. Do not copy a post's story or proof; credit sources.
