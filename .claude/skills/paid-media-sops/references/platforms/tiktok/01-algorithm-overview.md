# TikTok — How the Algorithm Works

## The For You Page, Ad Delivery, and Why This Platform Is Different

TikTok's algorithm is the most powerful content distribution system in social media. Unlike Meta (where the social graph matters) or Google (where search intent matters), TikTok uses an **interest graph** — it recommends content based on what you engage with, not who you follow. A brand-new account with zero followers can reach millions if the content resonates.

For advertisers, this means TikTok is simultaneously the biggest opportunity and the most unforgiving platform. Great creative gets rewarded with massive reach at low cost. Mediocre creative gets buried immediately.

## The Organic Algorithm (For You Page)

Understanding the organic algorithm matters for advertisers because **TikTok ads that perform look like organic TikToks**. The same signals that make organic content go viral are the signals that make ads perform well.

### How Content Gets Distributed

When a video is posted (organic or paid), TikTok follows a systematic testing process:

```
VIDEO POSTED
    │
    ▼
STAGE 1: Initial Test Pool (First 1-3 hours)
│  Shown to 200-500 viewers (primarily non-followers who match the content's topic)
│  Algorithm measures: watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, comments, likes
│
├── Engagement is strong → ADVANCE
│
▼
STAGE 2: Expanded Pool (Next 6-24 hours)
│  Shown to 1,000-10,000 viewers
│  Same signals measured at larger scale
│
├── Engagement holds → ADVANCE
│
▼
STAGE 3: Broad Distribution
│  Shown to 10,000-100,000+ viewers
│  Video is now "trending" in its niche
│
├── Engagement continues → VIRAL DISTRIBUTION
│
▼
STAGE 4: Mass Distribution
│  Shown to 100,000-millions of viewers
│  FYP placement across broad audiences
│
└── At any stage: if engagement drops below threshold → distribution stops
```

### Ranking Signals (In Order of Weight)

Based on TikTok's official documentation and confirmed through extensive testing:

| Signal | Weight | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch time / Completion rate** | Highest (~40-50%) | Did people watch the full video? This is the #1 signal. A 15-second video watched to completion beats a 60-second video abandoned at 5 seconds. |
| **Shares** | Very high | Shares indicate the viewer found the content valuable enough to send to someone else. In 2025-2026, shares outweigh likes significantly. |
| **Saves** | Very high | Saves indicate the viewer wants to return to this content. Strong signal of value. |
| **Comments** | High | Especially substantive comments (not just emojis). Comment quality now matters more than comment count. |
| **Likes** | Moderate | Lowest-effort engagement signal. Still matters but weighted less than shares/saves/comments. |
| **Content relevance** | High | Does the content match the viewer's demonstrated interests? Keywords in captions, on-screen text, and spoken audio are all scanned by the algorithm. |
| **Account authority** | Moderate (growing) | Accounts that consistently post high-performing content in a specific niche get preferential distribution for new posts in that niche. |

### Key Algorithm Changes in 2025-2026

1. **Follower-first testing.** As of late 2025, new videos are now tested with your followers first before being distributed to non-followers. This is a significant change — previously, content went directly to non-followers on the FYP.

2. **Saves and shares outweigh likes.** The algorithm shifted toward deeper engagement signals. A video with 500 saves and 100 shares outranks a video with 10,000 likes and 50 saves.

3. **Completion rate threshold rising.** The completion rate needed for viral distribution has risen to approximately 70% (up from ~50% in 2024). This means shorter, tighter content has an advantage unless longer content maintains high retention.

4. **"Qualified Views" (5 seconds).** TikTok now looks for 5-second views rather than just 3-second views as a quality threshold. Your hook needs to hold attention slightly longer than before.

5. **TikTok as a search engine.** Nearly 40% of Gen Z prefer searching on TikTok over Google for product research. TikTok SEO (keywords in captions, on-screen text, spoken audio) is now a direct ranking factor.

6. **US algorithm under Oracle.** Following the September 2025 ownership deal, Oracle now controls the US algorithm. The algorithm is being retrained on US-specific data, which may cause distribution fluctuations through mid-2026.

## The Ads Algorithm

The ads algorithm shares DNA with the organic algorithm but has additional considerations because it must balance advertiser goals (conversions, ROAS) with user experience (keeping people on TikTok).

### The Ad Delivery Formula

Similar to Meta and Google, TikTok uses an auction system. The winning ad is determined by **Total Value**:

```
Total Value = Bid × Estimated Action Rate × Ad Quality Score
```

**Bid:** How much you are willing to pay for the desired action (impression, click, conversion). TikTok's Smart Bidding handles this automatically for most campaign types.

**Estimated Action Rate:** TikTok's prediction of how likely this specific user is to take the desired action based on their behavioral history.

**Ad Quality Score:** How well the ad is likely to perform as content. This is where TikTok's algorithm diverges from other platforms — it heavily rewards ads that feel like native TikTok content. An ad with high watch time, shares, and saves gets a higher quality score, which means it wins auctions at a lower bid.

### What This Means for Advertisers

**The algorithm rewards ads that entertain.** This is not a platitude — it is the mechanical reality. An ad that users watch to completion, save, and share will literally cost less per impression than an ad that users skip. The quality score component of the auction formula directly translates creative quality into cost efficiency.

**TikTok penalizes ads that degrade user experience.** If users consistently skip your ad, hide it, or report it, your quality score drops. This means higher CPMs, less delivery, and eventually the ad stops serving entirely.

**The first 3 seconds are survival.** While you have more time than Meta's scroll-based feed, the first 3 seconds determine 71% of whether users keep watching. If your hook fails, the algorithm learns this quickly and reduces distribution.

### Targeting on TikTok

TikTok offers five targeting categories:

1. **Demographics:** Location, age, gender, language, household income (US only), spending power
2. **Audience targeting:** Custom Audiences, Lookalike Audiences, exclusions
3. **Interest & Behavior:** 700+ interest categories based on content engagement patterns
4. **Device targeting:** OS, device model, device price, carrier
5. **Smart Targeting:** Automatic expansion when the algorithm predicts improved results

### Broad vs. Narrow Targeting

According to TikTok's official best practices (updated September 2025):

> "Ads that reach a fairly broad audience, defined as including over 80% of the potential users in a given country, tend to outperform all others by a wide margin. Fairly broad audiences achieve 15% lower CPA and 20% higher conversion rates on average compared to narrower audiences."

**This means TikTok is closer to Meta than Google when it comes to targeting:** broad works. The algorithm's behavioral prediction is strong enough that over-constraining targeting restricts data flow and hurts performance.

**However:** Unlike Meta where broad is almost always the answer, TikTok benefits from **audience signals** as starting points. Custom Audiences from your customer list, website pixel data, or engaged video viewers give the algorithm a strong initial signal while still allowing expansion.

## Creative Is the Algorithm

This cannot be overstated. On TikTok more than any other platform, **creative quality IS the targeting.** The algorithm uses content engagement signals to find the right audience for each piece of creative. Different creative attracts different audiences.

If you run 5 different ad creatives:
- Creative A (skincare routine tutorial) → algorithm finds skincare-interested users
- Creative B (problem/solution format) → algorithm finds pain-point-aware users
- Creative C (trend-based with popular sound) → algorithm finds trend-engaged users
- Creative D (UGC testimonial) → algorithm finds social-proof-responsive users
- Creative E (founder story) → algorithm finds brand-story-responsive users

**You are not targeting audiences. You are targeting them through creative.** This is why creative volume and diversity matter more on TikTok than on any other platform.

### Creative Velocity Requirements

TikTok creative fatigues faster than any other platform. The feed is fast, users see enormous volumes of content daily, and the algorithm aggressively deprioritizes content that stops generating engagement.

| Account Spend Level | Creative Refresh Cadence | New Creatives Per Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Under $5,000/month | Every 2-3 weeks | 5-8 new creatives |
| $5,000-$20,000/month | Every 1-2 weeks | 8-15 new creatives |
| $20,000+/month | Weekly | 15-25+ new creatives |

**The biggest bottleneck on TikTok is not budget — it is creative production.** At TikTok's CPMs ($3-$7), distribution is cheap. The expensive part is producing enough creative variations to keep the algorithm fed.

## Platform-Specific Metrics

| Metric | TikTok Equivalent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CPM** | Cost Per 1,000 Impressions | TikTok CPMs average $2.60-$6.60, significantly lower than Meta ($9-$15). Seasonal peaks around Q4 reach $8-$10. |
| **CTR** | Click-Through Rate | TikTok CTR averages 0.5%-1.5%. Lower than Meta because the platform is entertainment-first — users are watching, not clicking. |
| **CVR** | Conversion Rate | Standard campaigns: 0.5%-2.4%. TikTok Shop campaigns: 5%-10%+ (in-app checkout dramatically reduces friction). |
| **Hook Rate** | 3-Second View Rate | Percentage of impressions where the user watched at least 3 seconds. Target 30%+. |
| **Qualified View Rate** | 5-Second View Rate | New in 2025-2026. 5-second threshold that indicates genuine engagement. |
| **Completion Rate** | Video Completion Rate | Percentage of viewers who watched the full video. The single most important creative health metric. Target 20%+ for 15-second ads, 10%+ for 30-second ads. |
| **Share Rate** | Shares / Impressions | The highest-signal engagement metric. Ads that generate shares get dramatically better distribution. |

## How This Connects to the SOP Framework

| SOP Concept | TikTok Connection |
|---|---|
| **CPM** (02) | TikTok offers the lowest CPMs of any major platform. This means more reach per dollar, but CPM efficiency is wasted without strong creative. |
| **CTR** (02) | Lower than Meta by nature. Evaluate alongside completion rate — a video with low CTR but high completion rate is still building brand awareness effectively. |
| **CVR** (02) | Dramatically higher for TikTok Shop (in-app checkout) than for external site traffic. The checkout friction difference is the primary driver. |
| **Creative Metrics** (05) | Completion rate and share rate are the TikTok equivalents of hook rate and hold rate. The creative diagnostic framework from `05-creative-metrics.md` applies directly. |
| **Attribution** (08) | TikTok defaults to 7dc/1dv like Meta. Same skepticism applies to view-through attribution. TikTok is a self-attributing network (SAN) — third-party attribution may show different numbers. |
| **Incrementality** (09) | TikTok reach is partially incremental to Meta (different user mindset, different content environment) but there is significant audience overlap. Validate with holdout tests. |
| **Contribution Margin** (10) | TikTok Shop introduces new margin considerations — platform fees, shipping, and commission reduce CM compared to your own site. Factor this into ROAS targets. |
