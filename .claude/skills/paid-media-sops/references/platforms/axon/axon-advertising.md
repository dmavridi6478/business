# AppLovin Axon — Ecommerce Advertising

## The Mobile Gaming Inventory That Actually Converts

Axon is AppLovin's AI-powered advertising platform that serves full-screen, high-attention ads inside mobile games — Candy Crush, Solitaire, Sudoku, puzzle games, and thousands of other apps. For ecommerce brands, Axon represents the most significant new advertising channel since TikTok, offering a massive audience pool completely outside the Meta and Google auction ecosystems.

**This is not a "nice to have" test channel.** Early adopters are reporting results that rival or exceed Meta in ROAS, with some brands scaling to six-figure daily spend within weeks of onboarding. The platform is still early, which means the brands that figure it out first have a significant competitive advantage.

**Last reviewed: March 2026.** Axon is evolving rapidly. Verify against current documentation at https://support.axon.ai before relying on this for strategic decisions.

## How Axon Works

### The Platform

AppLovin controls approximately 42% of the mobile gaming ad market and reaches over 1 billion daily active users globally. The company has been serving ads inside mobile games since 2012. In late 2024, they opened the platform to ecommerce advertisers using the same AXON AI engine that powers their gaming user acquisition business.

**What this means practically:** You are advertising to people playing mobile games on their phones. This is a massive, largely untapped audience of adults (not just kids) who spend significant time in these apps daily. These people are not on Meta or Google at that moment — they are in a different ecosystem entirely.

### The Ad Experience

This is fundamentally different from any other ad platform. The user experience is a three-part sequence:

```
PART 1: Full-Screen Video (9:16 vertical)
│  Unskippable for at least 5 seconds
│  Rewarded ad inventory gives 30+ seconds of guaranteed attention
│  Average total impression time: ~35 seconds
│
▼
PART 2: Interactive End Card
│  After the video completes, an interactive overlay appears
│  Contains product image, "Shop Now" button, and CTA
│  Generated by AppLovin's HTML end card tool or your custom HTML
│  User can browse/interact before returning to their game
│
▼
PART 3: MREC Banner (300x250)
│  A smaller banner that appears after the end card
│  Can be static image or dynamic product ad from your feed
│  Reinforces the brand/product one more time
│
= Three touchpoints per single ad impression
```

**Why this matters:** On Meta, you get a fraction of a second as someone scrolls past. On YouTube, you get 5 seconds before skip. On Axon, you get ~35 seconds of full-screen, undivided attention across three progressive touchpoints. This is closer to a TV commercial than a social media ad — but with digital tracking and optimization.

### The AXON AI Engine

AXON 2.0 processes over 2 million ad auctions per second, analyzing behavioral data from over 1 billion devices. Unlike Meta (which targets based on social graph data — likes, follows, interests) or Google (which targets based on search intent), AXON targets based on **behavioral patterns within apps**.

AXON does not have demographic data on users (age, gender, income). It uses data on user *actions* — what apps they use, how they engage with content, what ads they interact with, and crucially, what purchases they make after seeing ads (fed back via the Axon pixel).

The engine uses reinforcement learning — it gets smarter with every impression, every click, and every conversion. Early reports indicate that AXON's optimization speed is exceptional: one case study showed the algorithm moving from a 3% CTR on 500 impressions to significantly improved performance within hours as it identified patterns in responders vs. non-responders.

## How Axon Is Different From Meta

This is the most important section for a media buyer coming from Meta. **Do not copy-paste your Meta playbook.** Axon is a different environment with different rules.

| Dimension | Meta | Axon |
|---|---|---|
| **Where ads appear** | Social media feeds (Instagram, Facebook) | Full-screen inside mobile games |
| **User mindset** | Scrolling, socializing, browsing | Playing a game, captive attention, looking for a break |
| **Attention level** | Low — user is scrolling fast, you get fractions of a second | High — ad is full-screen, unskippable for 5+ seconds, often 30+ seconds of rewarded view |
| **Creative style that works** | Fast hooks, pattern interrupts, first 1-3 seconds matter most | Longer-form storytelling, product demos, the full 30 seconds matter. Hooks still matter but you have more time. |
| **Targeting** | Broad works because Meta's social graph is incredibly rich | No demographic targeting. AXON uses behavioral signals from app usage. You provide creative, pixel data, and product feed — the AI finds the audience. |
| **Attribution** | 7-day click + 1-day view (includes view-through) | **Click-only: 0-day click or 7-day click. No view-through attribution.** |
| **Typical conversion speed** | Varies — hours to days | Fast — ~80% of conversions happen within the first hour |
| **Auction competition** | Extremely competitive — every brand is on Meta | Low competition — most ecommerce brands are not here yet |
| **Campaign structure** | ASC or CBO with multiple ad sets | Simpler — campaign → ad set → creative sets |

### The Attribution Advantage

Axon only reports click-based attribution. No view-through. This is genuinely significant:

- **What Axon reports, actually happened.** Someone clicked the ad and then purchased. There is no inflation from "they saw an impression and bought 6 hours later through a different channel."
- **If ROAS looks good on Axon, it is probably real.** This is the opposite of the skepticism we apply to Meta 1dv or Google view-through data.
- **Axon may actually be *under-reporting* its value** because it does not take credit for people who saw the ad (35 seconds of full-screen video), did not click, but later purchased through another channel. Those assisted conversions are real but invisible in Axon's dashboard.
- **Use third-party attribution (Triple Whale, Northbeam, etc.) alongside Axon's dashboard.** Multiple advertisers report that Axon looks *better* in third-party tools than in its own reporting — the opposite of what typically happens with platforms.
- **Add "Game Ad" or "AppLovin" to your post-purchase survey** before launching. This is the most important validation step. If customers start selecting it, you have directional proof the channel is working regardless of what any dashboard says.

## Getting Access

As of early 2026, Axon is available through:

1. **Referral codes** from existing advertisers
2. **Direct outreach** to AppLovin's sales team
3. **Shopify App** — AppLovin has an official Shopify integration

The original requirement was $10M+ in GMV and $600K+/month in Meta spend, but access has loosened significantly with the referral program and Shopify app launch. Smaller brands can now get access, though the platform performs best with sufficient conversion volume to feed the algorithm.

**Minimum recommended:** $3,000-$5,000/month in Axon spend to generate enough data for the algorithm to optimize effectively. Below this, AXON may not have enough conversion signals to learn.

## Campaign Setup

### Campaign Types

Axon offers two core optimization strategies for ecommerce:

| Strategy | Optimize For | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost Per Purchase** | Lowest cost per conversion | Subscription brands, single-SKU or low-SKU brands, products with consistent AOV |
| **ROAS** | Return on ad spend target | Multi-SKU brands, catalogs with varied product values, brands focused on revenue efficiency |

**Choosing between them:**
- If your product line is simple (1-3 hero SKUs, consistent pricing), optimize for cost per purchase
- If your catalog has varied AOV (some $20 items, some $200 items), optimize for ROAS so the algorithm prioritizes higher-value purchases

### Optimization Windows

| Window | Best For |
|---|---|
| **0-day (same day)** | Impulse buy products, low AOV, products where the purchase decision is fast |
| **7-day** | Higher AOV products, considered purchases, products where buyers research before buying |

Most ecommerce brands should start with the 7-day window. Switch to 0-day only if your product is genuinely impulse-driven and you see that most conversions happen within hours.

### Campaign Structure

Keep it simple. Axon's AI does the heavy lifting — fragmentation hurts performance.

```
Axon Account
│
├── Campaign 1: Core (Purchase or ROAS optimization)
│   ├── Ad Set: Primary audience (all targeting handled by AXON)
│   │   ├── Creative Set 1: Hero video + end card + banner
│   │   ├── Creative Set 2: Testimonial video + end card + banner
│   │   └── Creative Set 3: Demo video + end card + banner
│   └── Budget: 70-80% of Axon spend
│
└── Campaign 2: Testing (for new creative)
    ├── Ad Set: Same targeting as Campaign 1
    │   ├── Creative Set: New creative being tested
    │   └── (Clone creative sets to test new assets cleanly)
    └── Budget: 20-30% of Axon spend
```

**The 80/20 rule (from AppLovin's own guidance):** One clear objective per campaign, one main audience cluster per ad set, and multiple creative concepts per creative set. Give AXON room to find winners without fragmenting data.

## Creative Strategy

### Axon Is Not a Social Feed — Creative Rules Are Different

The biggest mistake Meta media buyers make on Axon is treating it like Instagram. The environment is fundamentally different:

**On Meta:** Users are scrolling fast. You have 1-3 seconds to hook them. Short, punchy, pattern-interrupt creative wins. Quick cuts. Bold text overlays. Immediate product reveal.

**On Axon:** Users are watching a full-screen video between rounds of a game. They are captive for at least 5 seconds and often 30+ seconds (rewarded video). You have *time*. Longer-form storytelling, product demonstrations, and narrative arcs work better than rapid-fire hooks.

### What Works on Axon

| Creative Type | Why It Works | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Product demonstration** | You have 30 seconds to show the product doing something compelling. Use the time. | 30-45 seconds |
| **Founder/brand story** | The captive audience has time to hear *why* the product exists. Builds emotional connection. | 30-60 seconds |
| **UGC testimonial (longer form)** | Not the 15-second Meta clip — the full 30-45 second story of how the product changed something | 30-45 seconds |
| **Problem → Solution narrative** | Set up the problem (10 seconds), introduce the product (10 seconds), show results (10 seconds), CTA | 30 seconds |
| **Unboxing / first use** | Authentic "opening and trying the product" content | 30-45 seconds |

### What Does Not Work on Axon

- Ultra-short hooks designed for scroll-stopping (the user is not scrolling — they are watching)
- Heavy text overlay without any video/audio storytelling (the format is video-first)
- Creative that assumes the viewer knows who you are (they probably do not)
- Direct-response "BUY NOW 50% OFF" creative without any product story (the audience needs context first)

### Creative Specs

| Asset Type | Specs | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| **Video** | 9:16 vertical, 30 seconds or longer recommended. Minimum 15 seconds. | Yes — this is the primary asset |
| **End Card** | HTML interactive overlay. Use AppLovin's end card generator or create custom. Must include product image and CTA. | Yes — appears after video completes |
| **MREC Banner** | 300x250 pixels. Static image or dynamic from product feed. | Recommended for full coverage |
| **Subtitles** | Add to all videos — many users play with sound off | Strongly recommended |

### Creative Volume

- Launch with 3-5 creative sets minimum
- Each creative set can contain up to 10 videos, 10 HTML assets, and 10 images
- AXON tests combinations and allocates spend to winners
- Refresh creative every 2-4 weeks

### Creative Testing Process

AppLovin recommends a clean testing approach:

1. **Want to test a new asset?** Clone an existing high-performing creative set, add the new asset to the clone. This lets you measure the new asset's impact without disrupting the learning data in the original set.
2. **Do not add new creative to existing sets if the set is performing well.** The new asset resets some learning.
3. **Let new creative sets run for 5-7 days** before evaluating. AXON needs time to test the new assets across its audience.
4. **Kill creative sets with spend but no conversions** after 7-10 days and $500+ in spend (adjust threshold based on your CPA target).

### Product Feed Integration (Dynamic Catalog Ads)

If you connect your product catalog through Axon's Catalog Manager, AXON can serve dynamic product ads — showing specific products to specific users based on predicted relevance. This is similar to Meta's DPA (Dynamic Product Ads).

**Setup:**
- Sync your product catalog in Catalogs Manager (within Axon Ads Manager)
- Enable "Dynamic Catalog" in campaign settings
- AXON will automatically pull product images, titles, and prices to generate dynamic ads
- The AXON pixel on your site feeds back which products each user viewed and purchased, improving targeting over time

**Alternative:** If the catalog feed setup is too complex, provide 2-3 static 300x250 MREC banners. This ensures full ad format coverage even without dynamic ads.

## Pixel Setup

### The Axon Pixel

The Axon pixel is how AXON learns which users convert. Without it, the algorithm cannot optimize.

**Setup options:**
1. **Shopify App** (easiest) — Install the official AppLovin Shopify app. Captures events automatically.
2. **Manual pixel** — Place the Axon JavaScript pixel on your site. Configure events for PageView, AddToCart, and Purchase.
3. **Server-side** — For advanced setups, server-side event passing is available.

**Validation:** Use AppLovin's pixel validation tool at https://developers.applovin.com/en/audience-plus/pixel-validation/ to confirm events are firing correctly.

**Critical:** Ensure the Purchase event passes revenue value. Without value data, AXON cannot optimize for ROAS — it can only optimize for purchase count.

## Scaling on Axon

### The Scaling Playbook

1. **Start at $50-$100/day** with 3-5 creative sets and 7-day optimization window
2. **Let it run for 7-14 days** without changes. AXON needs a learning period.
3. **Evaluate at the 14-day mark.** Is CPA or ROAS within acceptable range?
4. **If yes:** Increase budget by 20-30% every 5-7 days. Add new creative.
5. **If no:** Check creative quality first. Then check pixel health. Then adjust optimization strategy (switch from purchase to ROAS or vice versa).
6. **Scale creative before scaling budget.** More creative gives AXON more options to test. Scaling budget without new creative leads to fatigue faster.

### Scaling Benchmarks (Directional)

These are based on early adopter reports and will evolve as the platform matures:

- Brands typically start at $50-$100/day
- Successful brands scale to $500-$1,000/day within the first month
- Top performers are running $5,000-$30,000/day
- AppLovin now accounts for 10-20% of total paid media spend for brands that successfully scale
- Early adopters report that weekly spend increases by 50%+ after initial onboarding as results prove out

## Measuring Axon Performance

### In-Platform Metrics

| Metric | What It Means | Where to Find |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | Number of times your ad was served | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| Clicks | Clicks on your ad (click-through to site) | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| CTR | Click-through rate | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| Spend | Total ad spend | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| Purchases (0DC) | Purchases within 0 days of click | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| Purchases (7DC) | Purchases within 7 days of click | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| Revenue (0DC / 7DC) | Revenue from attributed purchases | Axon Ads Manager reporting |
| ROAS (0DC / 7DC) | Return on ad spend | Axon Ads Manager reporting |

### Third-Party Validation

**Always cross-reference Axon's reporting with:**

1. **Post-purchase survey** — "How did you hear about us?" with "Game Ad" or "AppLovin" as an option. This is the most important validation for any new channel.
2. **Triple Whale / Northbeam / other attribution tools** — Multiple advertisers report that Axon looks stronger in third-party attribution than in its own dashboard (because third-party tools capture view-through impact that Axon does not report).
3. **Shopify / backend revenue** — Compare Axon-reported purchases to actual orders. Check for discrepancies.
4. **Blended MER** — Is total Marketing Efficiency Ratio improving as Axon spend increases? If yes, the channel is additive.
5. **Holdout test** — The gold standard. Suppress Axon in a control market and measure the revenue difference.

### The Incrementality Question

Because Axon serves ads inside mobile games (a completely different environment from social media or search), there is a strong argument that Axon reach is inherently incremental. These users are not on Meta or Google at the moment they see the ad. The audience overlap with existing channels is lower than it would be for, say, adding TikTok alongside Meta.

However, "different environment" does not guarantee incrementality. Validate with holdout tests and post-purchase surveys before making budget allocation decisions based on incrementality assumptions.

## How Axon Connects to the SOP Framework

| SOP Concept | Axon Connection |
|---|---|
| **CPM** (02) | Less transparent than Meta — Axon focuses on CPA/ROAS rather than showing CPM breakdowns. Monitor spend relative to impressions to calculate effective CPM. |
| **CTR** (02) | Available in the media library at the asset level. Use to compare creative performance. Lower CTR is expected vs. Meta (users are watching video, not necessarily clicking through). |
| **CVR** (02) | Post-click CVR should be comparable to or better than Meta because clicks on Axon are highly intentional (full-screen CTA, not accidental scroll-tap). |
| **Creative Metrics** (05) | Video completion rate is the primary creative metric. Axon's format guarantees high attention, so focus on whether the creative drives the *click* after watching, not whether they watched. |
| **Attribution** (08) | Click-only attribution (0DC or 7DC). No view-through. Most conservative reporting of any major ad platform. This is a strength. |
| **Incrementality** (09) | Strong incrementality argument due to unique inventory (mobile games). Validate with holdout tests and post-purchase surveys. |
| **Contribution Margin** (10) | Pass revenue values through the Axon pixel. If possible, pass contribution margin as the value to optimize AXON toward profit, not just revenue. |

## Platform Maturity Note

Axon for ecommerce launched in late 2024 and is still in early stages of its global rollout. The platform is evolving rapidly — new features, reporting improvements, and creative tools are being released monthly. What is written here reflects the state of the platform as of March 2026.

**Expect:**
- Feature parity with Meta will improve over time (better reporting, more campaign types, audience tools)
- More advertisers entering the platform will increase auction competition and potentially raise CPMs
- The first-mover advantage for brands already on the platform will erode as adoption grows
- Axon's official documentation at support.axon.ai is the best source for current capabilities

**Recommendation:** Get on the platform now, while competition is low and CPMs are favorable. Build your creative library and pixel data early. The brands that invest in learning Axon in 2026 will have a significant advantage over those who wait until 2027 when every brand is there.
