# The AI SEO growth engine (Manuel Arrufat)

Source: one wheel graphic, "how modern SEO teams win visibility in AI search" (dolnai.com; the author's tool is promoted by name only). Opinion; no data.

| # | Part | Question | The four bullets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prompt research | What questions lead to AI discovery? | Buyer conversations; category questions; competitor prompts; hidden search patterns |
| 2 | Intent intelligence | What do users actually want? | Informational needs; commercial decisions; comparison behaviour; purchase signals |
| 3 | Content opportunities | How do AI questions become assets? | AEO pages; comparison content; expert answers; topic clusters |
| 4 | Competitor discovery | Where do others win? | Competitor recommendations; missing mentions; category gaps; new opportunities |
| 5 | AI search measurement | What changes? | Prompt visibility; AI mentions; citation movement; market position |
| 6 | SEO evolution | Moving beyond keywords | From rankings to recommendations; keywords to conversations; traffic to influence |

## Using it honestly
- **[Certain]** Parts 1 and 5 depend on sampling prompts across assistants; answers vary run to run, so repeat each prompt several times and report a range, not one number.
- **[Likely]** Start with a list of 30 to 50 real buyer prompts, test them weekly in a fixed set of assistants, log whether you are mentioned and cited, and fix pages where competitors are named instead.
- **[Likely]** "From traffic to influence" is a framing, not a metric; keep tracking leads and revenue.
