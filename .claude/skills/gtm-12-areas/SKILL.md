# 12 areas of GTM (graphic by Lucia Kim, Batch 135)

| # | Area | Five elements as printed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ICP | Firmographics, technographics, pain points, buying triggers, disqualifies |
| 2 | Positioning | Category, alternatives, differentiation, value prop, point of view |
| 3 | Messaging | Hooks, proof points, case studies, objections, tone of voice |
| 4 | Pricing | Packaging, tiers, value metric, discounting, expansion |
| 5 | Outbound | Signals, list building, sequences, deliverability, multichannel |
| 6 | Content | Founder brand, LinkedIn, newsletter, lead magnets, distribution |
| 7 | Partnerships | Integrations, referrals, creators, co-marketing, tool partners |
| 8 | Sales | Discovery, demos, proposals, negotiation, closing |
| 9 | Customers | Onboarding, activation, retention, expansion, advocacy |
| 10 | Data & Stack | CRM hygiene, enrichment, automation, attribution, tooling |
| 11 | Metrics | ARR, win rate, pipeline velocity, conversion rates, CAC |
| 12 | Team | Ownership, rituals, playbooks, enablement, hiring |

## One-page health audit
Score each area 0 (nothing), 1 (ad hoc), 2 (written and used), 3 (measured and reviewed). For each area name the owner and the one piece of evidence behind the score. Rules:
- Areas 1 to 3 (who, why you, what you say) come first: a 0 or 1 there makes later areas expensive.
- A score with no evidence is a guess; put it as 1 at most.
- Fix the lowest-scoring area that blocks the next one in the order, not simply the lowest.
- Re-score quarterly.

## Limits
- The graphic is a checklist, not a method: it gives no order, weights or thresholds.
- "Fit" is missing between areas: for example pricing tiers must match ICP size, and outbound signals must match buying triggers.
- Metrics lists the outputs; leading indicators for each area are yours to choose.
