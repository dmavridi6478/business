# 15-Day AI Search Action Plan

Source: infographic by Searchable.com, "15 Day AI Search Action Plan — Your day-by-day playbook for getting cited by AI." A sequenced, day-by-day checklist rather than a conceptual framework — use this when the request is "give me a concrete plan/schedule to improve AI search visibility," not just "explain AEO/GEO."

Pairs with `seo-wheel-2026.md` (the *what* — the five disciplines) as the *how/when* — this plan sequences the AEO/GEO/AIO work from that wheel into 15 concrete days.

## Week 1 — Audit

- **Day 1 — AI Visibility Audit**: Run target queries through LLMs; note where the brand is (and isn't) visible; set the baseline.
- **Day 2 — Competitor Citation Research**: Identify who's cited for the same queries; analyze what's earning those citations; spot the content gaps.
- **Day 3 — Crawler Access Check**: Confirm AI bots have full access; review `robots.txt`; fix any blocks.
- **Day 4 — Schema Markup Audit**: Check schema implementation; add FAQ and Organisation schema; validate with Google's Rich Results Test.
- **Day 5 — Technical AEO Audit**: Full AEO audit — site speed, crawlability; fix the highest-priority issues first.

## Week 2 — Fix & Rebuild

- **Day 6 — Content Inventory**: Map every existing page; tag by topic, intent, and last-update date; identify decay candidates.
- **Day 7 — Semantic Drift Check**: Compare the site's terminology to how AI answers currently describe the topic; flag pages using outdated language; prioritize the worst offenders.
- **Day 8 — Stat and Source Refresh**: Replace outdated statistics; update sources to recent ones; add publish/last-updated dates.
- **Day 9 — Content Restructure**: Add clear headings and direct answers; use short paragraphs and lists; make content easy for AI to extract (see `seo-wheel-2026.md`'s AEO checklist — lead with the answer in 40-60 words).
- **Day 10 — Build Cited Content**: Create one genuinely new citable article; lead with the answer; back it with original data or insight — citation-worthy content has to say something not already said elsewhere.

## Week 3 — Authority & Tracking

- **Day 11 — Internal Linking**: Link new content to the site's best-performing pages; update old articles with fresh links; build clear topic clusters.
- **Day 12 — Off-Site Authority**: Publish on relevant industry forums; get featured on high-authority sites; focus on the sources AI already cites (not authority in general — authority AI engines specifically pull from).
- **Day 13 — Brand Sentiment Check**: Track how AI currently describes the brand; note inaccuracies or outdated claims; plan content specifically to correct them.
- **Day 14 — Citation Tracking Setup**: Set up ongoing monitoring across the top AI tools; track share of voice vs. competitors; build the reporting baseline for future comparison.
- **Day 15 — Review and Plan**: Compare current visibility against the Day 1 baseline; identify the biggest wins; set the next 30-day plan.

## Using this plan

- Run Day 1 for real before promising results — the whole plan is calibrated against an actual baseline, not an assumed starting point.
- Days can compress (a small site might do Days 1-3 in one sitting) but shouldn't reorder — audit before restructuring before authority-building is the logic, not an arbitrary sequence.
- Day 15's "next 30-day plan" is the point: this is one cycle of an ongoing process, not a one-time project with a finish line.
