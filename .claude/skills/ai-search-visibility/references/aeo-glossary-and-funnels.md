# AEO glossary, SEO vs AEO funnels, and the three content types

Batch 113, from three infographics: "AEO Essential Terms Periodic Table" (Naqui Shaikh, Pierview.ai), "SEO vs AEO: Two Different Funnels" (CiteRank), "The 3 Types of SEO Content Your Business Can't Ignore" (Searchable).

## 1. Glossary: 28 terms in six families

| Family (tile colour) | Terms |
|---|---|
| AI search and visibility (orange) | AEO Answer Engine Optimization; AIO AI Overviews; GEO Generative Engine Optimization |
| AI models and retrieval (red) | LLM Large Language Model; LLMO LLM Optimization; RAG Retrieval-Augmented Generation; NLP Natural Language Processing; MCP Model Context Protocol |
| Metrics and analytics (white) | BCR Brand Citation Rate; AIR Answer Inclusion Rate; SAT Source Attribution Traffic; SOM Share of Model; SOV Share of Voice; CPC Cost Per Click |
| Technical and structured data (blue) | KG Knowledge Graph; SCH Schema Markup; SERP Search Engine Results Page |
| Brand and reputation (green) | EEAT Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust; ORM Online Reputation Management; BLUF Bottom Line Up Front |
| Traditional SEO crossover (purple) | SEO; SEM; CRO; CTR; ROI; GSC Google Search Console; GA Google Analytics; PAA People Also Ask |

3 + 5 + 6 + 3 + 3 + 8 = 28. The table names the terms; it does not define how BCR, AIR, SAT or SOM are calculated. Treat any formula as your own: for example, "share of tested prompts where the brand is named" (see `llm-visibility-prompt-check`). Do not compare your numbers with anyone else's unless the definition is identical.

## 2. SEO vs AEO: one buyer, two routes

| Stage | SEO route | AEO route |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Awareness | Puts keywords into Google, scans a page of results, clicks several links | Asks the AI a question, receives one synthesised answer, sees 2-3 cited sources |
| 2 Consideration | Opens 5-10 tabs, reads, compares by hand, reaches a verdict alone | The AI has done the comparing; recommendations come built in; buyer trusts the synthesis; zero tab switching |
| 3 Decision | Returns to the strongest source, remembers the brand, acts on the chosen site | Acts on the recommendation, opens a cited link when needed, or moves on without a click |

| Who does the work? | SEO | AEO |
|---|---|---|
| Research | The buyer | The AI |
| Comparing options | The buyer | The AI |
| Who gets the nod | Buyer picks who to trust | AI picks who to cite |
| What you win | Traffic | Citations |

The "5-10 tabs" and "2-3 cited sources" figures are the graphic's illustration, not measured data. A cited source can still produce no click; plan to measure mentions and citations, not only visits.

## 3. Three types of content (Searchable)

| | Authority pages | Decision pages | Money pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience | Not ready to buy; learning, diagnosing | Understands the problem; weighing options | Knows the problem; ready to choose |
| Goal | Become the source they trust; build topical authority | Help them compare; get on the shortlist | Turn demand into clicks, enquiries, demos, trials, purchases |
| Publish | Educational answers, guides, definitions, frameworks, data-led and opinion-led explainers | Best-of lists, alternatives, competitor comparisons, review roundups, pricing and use-case comparisons; honest about who each option suits | Product, service, category, location and landing pages with clear answers, proof, pricing context, FAQs |
| Do not publish | Thin generic posts written only because a keyword exists | Fake-neutral comparisons that exist only to promote you | Commercial intent buried in a generic blog post |

Use with `llm-visibility-prompt-check`: prompts 01-06 test authority and discovery; 13-16 test decision pages; 07-12 and 17-25 test money pages and brand facts.
