# SEO in 2026 — The Five-Discipline Wheel

Source: infographic by Emilia Möller. Five disciplines around one center; "how to rank higher" = a checklist per discipline.

---

## 1. SEO — Search Engine Optimisation

**Definition:** Earning organic rankings on Google through keywords, links, and site health. The base layer every AI engine still pulls from.

**In practice:** A local gym ranks for "24-hour gyms in Manchester" and turns it into steady foot traffic.

**How to rank higher:**
- [ ] Build a strong entity: clear identity, topic clusters
- [ ] Refresh existing pages — freshness now beats sheer volume
- [ ] Audit schema, content decay, and Core Web Vitals

---

## 2. AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation

**Definition:** Structuring content so it gets served as the direct answer inside Google AI Mode, AI Overviews, and chat responses.

**In practice:** A tax firm's short explainer becomes the cited answer when someone asks Google AI Mode about UK capital gains allowances.

**How to rank higher:**
- [ ] Lead with the answer in 40–60 words
- [ ] Use question-style H2s and H3s AI can chunk
- [ ] Add FAQ and HowTo schema

---

## 3. GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation

**Definition:** Getting your brand cited or summarised by generative engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, and Claude.

**In practice:** A fintech gets named when people ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for "the best budgeting apps for freelancers."

**How to rank higher:**
- [ ] Publish factual, entity-rich content that earns E-E-A-T
- [ ] Get cited on forums, datasets, and trusted publications
- [ ] Put your own data and case studies out there — they are the most citation-worthy asset you have

---

## 4. AIO — AI Optimisation

**Definition:** Making sure your brand is included and represented accurately in the data AI models train on and pull from.

**In practice:** A software brand becomes part of a model's working knowledge through well-structured docs and an accurate Wikipedia presence.

**How to rank higher:**
- [ ] Publish structured, well-formatted content on credible sources
- [ ] Keep brand messaging factually consistent everywhere (inconsistency = models pick a version you didn't choose)
- [ ] Get featured where AI pulls from — Wikipedia, structured data repositories

---

## 5. SXO — Search Experience Optimisation

**Definition:** Optimising the full on-site experience so visitors convert once search or AI sends them your way.

**In practice:** An e-commerce brand aligns page speed, layout, and CTAs to buying intent and lifts conversion on AI-referred traffic.

**How to rank higher:**
- [ ] Build fast, mobile-friendly pages
- [ ] Align content tightly with user intent
- [ ] Clear, persuasive CTAs

---

## Using this as an audit tool

For any given piece of content or campaign, score it 1–5 across the five disciplines rather than asking one binary "is our SEO good" question. A page can be strong on SEO (ranks) and weak on AEO (never gets pulled into an AI Overview) — these are independent failure modes, not synonyms. The framework's implicit claim is that AEO/GEO/AIO are now co-equal with classic SEO, not afterthoughts — treat a plan that only addresses classic SEO as incomplete for 2026, and say so explicitly rather than assuming the user already knows this.

## Caveat (add when reporting to stakeholders)

This wheel is a planning heuristic from a single infographic source, not a peer-reviewed methodology or a set of platform-confirmed ranking factors. Present it as "a structured checklist" — not as authoritative fact about how ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Mode actually rank content, which none of these companies fully disclose.
