# 5 sites for AI prompts (@ai_slacker)

| # | Site | Slide claim | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PromptHero | millions of prompts for image, video and AI models | image-model focus; some features sit behind sign-in or paid tiers |
| 2 | FlowGPT | discover and use community-created prompts | community quality varies; slide shows a "visual interface for ChatGPT" page |
| 3 | AIPRM | browse thousands of ready-to-use ChatGPT and Claude prompts | it is a browser extension: it sees your pages and chats; read its permissions first; the freemium model has paid plans |
| 4 | Snack Prompt | ready-made prompts across marketing, coding, writing | also lists agents and workflows |
| 5 | PromptBase | marketplace of prompts, "access 330k" | a marketplace: the featured prompts on the slide cost $2.99 to $4.99 each, so "free" does not apply to all |

**Challenge:** the headline says "free", but #5 sells prompts and #3 is an upsold extension. Free prompts found online are generic and often AI-generated; they rarely beat a prompt written for your own task with examples.

## Safety rules for third-party prompts
1. Read the whole prompt before pasting; hidden instructions can ask the model to leak or change data.
2. Never paste confidential or customer data into a prompt tool you have not vetted.
3. Test on a throwaway task and compare against your own prompt.
4. Save winners into your own library (`prompt-optimizer`, `/prompt-optimize`, `prompt-engineering-patterns`).
