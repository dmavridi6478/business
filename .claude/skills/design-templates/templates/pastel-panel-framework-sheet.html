The strategy behind a content system

1 Find your niche

SSkill your unfair advantage, not your job title
MMarket who specifically needs that skill
PProblem the real pain they usually misdiagnose
VValue free content plus a paid service

3 Build every post on the 5 P's

ProblemName it; explain it better than your audience can.
PromiseShow the way out.
ProofShare results, backed by stories.
PersonalityShow why you.
PerspectiveShare what your industry gets wrong.

2 Write for all three tiers

DiehardsNodding along before you finish the sentence.
Drive-bysClick "see more" only if something grabs them.
Drop-insNever heard of you; zero context, zero trust.

4 Four formats to copy

Invite the crowd to celebrateShare the case study; see who raises a hand.
Package it as a lead magnetA simple way to surface more leads.
The classic before and afterGives readers hope change is possible.
Two-way promoA client's process, closing with their recommendation.

BonusMine your sales calls

  • Take a real sales, onboarding or coaching call.
  • Ask AI for the top 5-10 objections raised.
  • Turn those objections into content ideas.
  • Anchor each to your own experience.
