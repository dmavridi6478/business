9 Strategies
For Lead Generation

Use these to quickly generate inbound leads.

1 Authority Content

Act as an industry expert. Create content that positions you as the go-to authority in [your niche]. Share client wins and contrarian takes. Include specific results.

+ ⚙ ↑
2 Network Building

You are targeting decision makers. Send personalised connection requests that mention something specific. Quality over quantity; track acceptance rates.

+ ⚙ ↑
3 Lead Magnets

Act as a value provider. Create resources your ICP actually wants and deliver them honestly. Track conversion rates and double down on what performs.

+ ⚙ ↑
4 Engagement

Engage meaningfully on prospects' content soon after they post. Add value, not generic praise. Spend 15-20 minutes a day.

+ ⚙ ↑
5 Direct Messages

Act as a relationship builder. Start conversations, do not pitch at once. Compliment + question + value offer, under 50 words.

+ ⚙ ↑
6 Profile System

Treat your profile as a landing page for ideal clients: headline with a value proposition, ICP pain points, proof and a clear call to action.

+ ⚙ ↑
7 Repurposing

Turn one successful piece into many formats: posts, carousels, video, articles. Track which formats perform best.

+ ⚙ ↑
8 Social Proof

Collect and show testimonials, case studies and results, with permission. Keep proof fresh and specific.

+ ⚙ ↑
9 Conversion Tracking

Track every lead source and conversion point with UTM links. Review monthly: keep what works, drop what does not.

+ ⚙ ↑
