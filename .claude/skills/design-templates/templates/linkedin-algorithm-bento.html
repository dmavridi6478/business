How the feed works

Relevance + dwell time + conversation depth - claims graded by evidence.

The state of the platform

  • Reach is selective: relevance beats volume.
  • Quality of engagement outweighs raw impressions.
  • Value-led content beats promotion.
  • Thoughtful comments beat short replies.

Key takeaway: create value, spark discussion, earn time-on-post.

Checklist

  • Post when your audience is active.
  • Write an opening line that earns the "See more" click.
  • Keep it concise but substantial; use spacing.
  • Pair insight with a visual.
  • Ask a specific question ("What would you do?"), not "Thoughts?".
