How to position yourself as an expert

The MAPS framework

M = Market

The exact people you help and the problem they would happily spend money fixing.

"Business owners""Founders who want LinkedIn to bring in leads"

Try this: who would pay you tomorrow if you messaged them today?

A = Achievements

Proof only you can point to: results you have driven, things you have built or grown.

Try this: write down 3 results you would be proud to put a number on.

S = Stance

A view you will happily defend, even if others in your space disagree.

"Grow your followers first.""Attention without infrastructure is wasted."

Try this: name one popular piece of advice you disagree with, and why.

P = Process

The steps you repeat every time to get your clients' results.

Positioning → Content → Capture → Conversion

Try this: name your process; keep it to 3 to 5 steps.

Build your positioning line

"I help [market] get [result] through [process], after [achievement]."

Read it out loud. If it sounds like a CV, keep editing.
