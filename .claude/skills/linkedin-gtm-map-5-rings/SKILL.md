# The LinkedIn GTM map (five rings)

Source: an animated graphic by Megha Sharma (OneGTMLab), "The LinkedIn GTM Map: a practical guide for SaaS founders, from first post to booked demo" (Batch 129). It reads from the centre outward. Elements are as printed; the order of work and worksheet are added. Related: `linkedin-authority-two-engines`, `personal-brand-90-days`, `linkedin-prospecting-8-systems`, `linkedin-signal-outreach`, `linkedin-b2b-sales-levels`.

| Ring | Question | Elements (as printed) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Goal | What LinkedIn should do for you | Category ownership, Partner deals, Warm pipeline, Founder authority, Talent pull, Inbound demos, Investor visibility |
| 2. ICP | Who you are posting for | Pain they feel, Job title, Their workaround, Funding stage, Who signs off, Trigger event, Tech stack, Use case, Team size |
| 3. Content | What you post | Infographic, Founder story, How-I framework, Short video, Build in public, Product demo, Customer win, Carousel, Data insight, Teardown, Hot take |
| 4. Channels | Where you show up | Events, Founder profile, LinkedIn Live, Thought Leader Ads, Company page, Featured section, Newsletter, DMs, Comments |
| 5. Lead creation | How attention turns into pipeline | Free trial, Demo link, Comment keyword, Signal alert, Free audit, Follow-up DM, Lead magnet DM, Profile visit, Booked call |

## Build order: inside out
1. **Goal:** pick one or two of the seven; more than two dilutes every post.
2. **ICP:** pick three to four attributes you can actually observe (job title, trigger event, pain they feel, who signs off).
3. **Content:** two or three formats matched to the ICP's pain; keep one proof format (customer win or data insight).
4. **Channels:** founder profile and comments first; add one more channel only when the first earns conversations.
5. **Lead creation:** one path (for example free audit or demo link) with a follow-up DM and a log of where each lead came from.

## One-page worksheet
Goal (one line) | ICP (title, trigger, pain, signer) | Three content formats and their job | Two channels | One lead path | Metric: qualified conversations per week, booked calls per month.

## Cautions the graphic omits
- **Comment-keyword and lead-magnet DM flows.** Tools that auto-DM people who comment a keyword can breach LinkedIn's terms and feel like spam; check the current User Agreement and keep messages relevant and honest.
- **"Signal alert" and trigger events** mean monitoring named people and companies. Use public sources, store only what you need, and check privacy rules (GDPR/ePrivacy for EU and UK contacts) before storing or emailing.
- **Thought Leader Ads** need the author's consent to be promoted; confirm ad policy.
- **Demo offers and free audits** create obligations: define scope and time before you promise one.
- **It is a map, not a result.** The graphic gives no conversion data; measure your own.
