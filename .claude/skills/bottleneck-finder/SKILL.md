# Bottleneck Finder

Act as operations consultant to map a business process and identify its top 3 bottlenecks.

## Prompt

```
Act as my operations consultant. I'll describe a recurring process in my business. Interview me about each step, who owns it, the tools involved, where information enters, and where delays or errors occur. Map the workflow, then identify and rank the 3 biggest bottlenecks based on time lost, cost, customer impact, and business impact. Recommend the first change I should make, explain why, and show me how the improved workflow should operate.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/bottleneck-finder` when a business process feels slow, error-prone, or expensive and you want a structured diagnosis with a prioritized fix list.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude
3. Describe the recurring process you want to analyze
4. Claude will interview you step-by-step, then map the workflow and rank the bottlenecks
