## Reading the list

- Most of the twenty map to jobs this library already covers with a skill (names in the last column are approximate matches, some are plugin skills); the new value is in the tools that supply data the model cannot: real buyer interviews (Listen Labs), message testing with respondents (Wynter), competitive monitoring (PeerPanda), social listening (Octolens), AI-search visibility tracking (AthenaHQ).
- **Synthetic research (Articos) and any "AI respondent" is not customer evidence.** Use it to generate hypotheses, then confirm with real buyers.
- Pick by the bottleneck: which one PMM job costs you the most hours per month? Trial one tool for that job, with a success metric, before adding another.
- Add only what you will use weekly; each tool also adds a data-sharing and procurement question.
