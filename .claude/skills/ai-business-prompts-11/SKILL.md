# Eleven business-building prompts

Sources (Batch 120): a @buildbefore30 carousel, "5 AI prompts I actually used in 2026", and an @ai_slacker carousel, "Claude can build a passive income system: one that keeps working while you sleep". Prompts quoted verbatim; replace the [brackets]. The "passive income" framing is marketing: every income stream here needs setup, maintenance, support and marketing, and many fail. Use the prompts to plan and to test demand cheaply, not as a promise of income.

## A. Five prompts (build before 30)

| # | Goal | Prompt (verbatim) | What to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find real business ideas that actually work | Find 10 real problems that people or small businesses are already willing to pay for. For each, identify the target customer, an affordable solution, a simple MVP, pricing, and how I could find my first three customers. Prioritize ideas I can test within seven days. | A model cannot see real demand: check each problem against search data, forums and five conversations |
| 2 | Create a complete website for your business | Act as a senior UX designer, brand strategist and frontend developer. Create a premium, responsive website for [business]. First define the target audience, value proposition, visual direction and user journey. Avoid generic AI templates. Then build the complete website with polished typography, authentic visuals, mobile optimization and working interactions. | Check accessibility, performance, legal pages and that no claim or testimonial is invented |
| 3 | Create high-quality visuals for your brand | Create [number] photorealistic, high-quality images for [business]. The images should match my brand, feel authentic and not look like typical AI stock photos. Include different scenes, settings and angles that I can use for my website and social media. Save the images in high resolution and in WebP format. Suggest a consistent visual style and color palette based on my brand. | Label AI imagery where the law or platform requires; never show fake customers or results |
| 4 | Get a brutally honest review of your website | Act as a critical UX, conversion and product marketing expert. Review my website as if you were a first-time visitor. Identify confusing messaging, weak trust signals, unnecessary friction and missed conversion opportunities. Rank the most important improvements by impact. Don't flatter me. Give specific, actionable fixes and explain what to test before redesigning everything. | Test the top fix with real visitors before redesigning |
| 5 | Validate your idea before you invest time and money | I have this product idea: [idea]. Help me validate demand before investing heavily. Identify my riskiest assumptions, define three potential customer segments, create a simple landing-page experiment, propose pricing tests and recommend what user actions to measure. Give me clear criteria for deciding whether to continue, change direction or stop. | Set the stop criteria before the test, not after |

## B. Six passive-income prompts (ai_slacker)

| Goal | Prompt (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| Automate the business | Act as a business systems architect. Design an automation framework for my passive income business that includes AI workflows, customer onboarding, payment processing, email marketing, analytics, customer support, documentation, CRM, content scheduling, and reporting. Clearly identify which processes should be automated and which require periodic human oversight. Restrictions: scalable automation only. |
| Build your passive income plan | Act as a world-class entrepreneur and financial planner. Build a complete operating system for creating multiple passive and semi-passive income streams based on my skills, interests, available time, risk tolerance, and financial goals. Analyze my current situation, recommend the highest-leverage opportunities, validate each business model, and create a step-by-step execution plan. |
| Monetize your content | Act as a creator economy strategist. Build a complete system for using content platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, newsletters, blogs, and podcasts to create multiple income streams. Include audience growth strategy, content repurposing workflows, affiliate marketing, sponsorships, digital products, memberships, consulting, and email marketing. Explain how each income stream supports the others. |
| Design digital products | Act as a digital product entrepreneur. Design a complete strategy for creating digital assets that can generate recurring income over time. Recommend products such as online courses, eBooks, templates, prompt packs, software, memberships, printables, stock resources, and licensing opportunities. For each asset, include a creation roadmap, pricing strategy, marketing plan, delivery system, and maintenance requirements. |
| Diversify your income streams | Act as a financial strategist. Build a diversified portfolio of passive and semi-passive income streams based on businesses that reduce dependence on a single source of income. Recommend an order for launching each income stream, explain how they complement one another, and identify the risks associated with each model. Restrictions: balanced, long-term strategy only, optimized for financial resilience. |
| Find the right income ideas | Act as an elite business strategist. Generate 30 passive and semi-passive income ideas that fit my skills and resources. For each opportunity, include startup cost, implementation time, technical difficulty, scalability, recurring revenue potential, maintenance requirements, level of competition, and long-term profit potential. Rank each opportunity by launch speed, sustainability, and automation potential. |

## Rules (added)
- **No guaranteed income.** Do not repeat income claims. Ask the model for failure rates and what the first ninety days look like.
- **Give context.** Both carousels say the more specific you are (skills, time, budget, audience), the better the output; add your numbers and constraints.
- **Validate before building.** Run prompt A5 before A2, A3 or any B prompt that has you build assets.
- **Money and legal.** The "financial planner" and "financial strategist" roles are personas, not advice; check taxes, contracts, consumer and data-protection law, and platform rules (affiliate disclosure, sponsorship labels, AI labels) with a qualified person.
- **Automation needs oversight.** Payments, refunds, support and email sending need a human in the loop.
