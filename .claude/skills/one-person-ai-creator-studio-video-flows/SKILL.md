# One-person AI creator studio and AI social media team (two videos)

Source: two vertical TikTok videos (about 98 s and 102 s) by ReStructure AI. Frames were read; **there is no transcript**, so only what is visible on screen is recorded. Brand names appear as tool labels, and no logos were copied.

## Video 1, "The One-Person AI Creator Studio"
A 16-flow system diagram. Visible flows:
- **Voice memo flow:** voice memo, Claude, ElevenLabs, "you approve" (Slack).
- **Hook video flow:** 3 selfies, Higgsfield, Descript, ElevenLabs.
- **Global dubbing:** the video is dubbed into ten languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, German, French, Japanese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Indonesian, Italian) on screen.
- **Planner:** Metricool schedules YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook at the best time per country.
- **Fan voice and sales:** an "AI assistant" persona (Maya) answers fans on an ElevenLabs voice and chat agent; products shown at "$497 course" and "$2,000/mo coaching"; booking via calendar; revenue panel.

## Video 2, "AI Social Media Team That Runs Your Marketing"
Departments on the diagram: paid ads, lead capture, nurture and retargeting, reputation, data and observability. Visible loops:
- **Ads:** Ads Manager, Claude, Higgsfield, ElevenLabs, scan of competitor ads, generate 20 ads, a phone "Approvals" screen, then a live test.
- **Leads:** Instagram, Facebook and TikTok comments and DMs tagged and routed.
- **Voice agent:** a new lead triggers a call from an AI voice agent within seconds.
Caption on screen: "Claude writes. Jev decides. ElevenLabs talks. You just approve."

## Read critically
- **[Certain]** The videos show a diagram and mockups, not results; no revenue, conversion or cost figure is evidenced.
- **[Certain]** Cloned or synthetic voices, AI sales calls and auto-replies to fans have consent, disclosure and telemarketing rules (for example TCPA in the US, GDPR in the EU); an AI "person" selling coaching can mislead buyers.
- **[Likely]** Mass competitor-ad scanning and auto-generation can copy other brands' creative; review for IP.
- Build with approval gates (`os-approval`), and use `one-person-marketing-team-map`, `n8n-workflow-patterns` and `brand-brain` as the implementable parts. Nothing was built, connected or spent here.
