# Use AI to turn your knowledge into a digital product
Do not write a 200-page ebook: package one specific transformation into a small, useful product. Give AI your expertise, buyer, and transformation. If the input is vague the output will be too.
**Prompt 1 - shape the product**
My knowledge/experience: [KNOWLEDGE].
Buyer: [BUYER].
Problem: [PROBLEM].

Generate 5 small digital product ideas. Each must have: one clear defined audience, a short delivery format, what is included, what is excluded, and how the buyer can use it in under one week.
**Prompt 2 - design the delivery**
Take this product: [PRODUCT]. Create the structure, worksheet/checklist components, examples, and a simple 7-day implementation plan. Avoid filler. Mark any claim that needs evidence from me.
**Second pass:** ask what it assumed; what is missing; attack its own answer; verify facts, figures, prices.
**Digital-product test:** specific buyer; specific problem; specific outcome; practical format; real examples or evidence.
**Sell** a defined result, a repeatable process and a clear quality check; do not sell "I know how to prompt AI". Examples: niche checklists, templates, SOP packs, interview kits, calculators, research frameworks, mini-courses. Validate with a small cohort first (see `llm-council-5-advisors` example).
