# "Claude for Sales" free courses (graphic)

Source: one infographic, "Claude for Sales: a collection of free courses to master sales with AI. Yes, it's free", ending "COMMENT 'COURSE' FOR FREE ACCESS". Every lesson card reads "9 screens, 20 min".

## What I could and could not verify
- **[Certain]** The graphic gives no author, handle, link or provider.
- **[Certain]** A web search on 16 October 2026 found no listing for these lesson titles. The only related pages were syndicated promotions for a different set of 13 Anthropic Academy courses (Claude 101, AI Fluency, Claude Code in Action, API and MCP), none about sales.
- **[Likely]** The "course" is a lead magnet: the free access is delivered after a comment, so the real product is the author's follow-up. Nothing was commented or requested.
- Arithmetic: the tracks add to 60 + 60 + 40 = 160 minutes (about 2 h 40 min). Each lesson is "9 screens", so it reads as a short slideshow rather than hours of video.

## Use the curriculum without the course
Run `/sales-lesson <1-8>` for the prompt and checklist of a lesson, or `/sales-30-day-plan` for lesson 08. Related skills already here: `claude-sales-prompts-20`, `claude-sales-by-role`, the commands `/sales-method` and `/objection`, `outbound-sales-system-5stage`, `perplexity-sales-research-10`.

## Safe use
- Lessons 02, 04 and 06 send messages to real people. Draft with Claude, send yourself, and keep opt-out and consent rules (GDPR, ePrivacy, CAN-SPAM) in mind; "24/7 capture and nurture" needs a lawful basis for every contact.
- Do not paste customer call recordings or CRM exports into any tool you have not cleared; strip names first (lesson 07).
- Never claim results a prospect cannot verify.
