# Keyword research strategy: 7 steps, 10 types, 10 methods, 8 metrics

Source: infographic "Complete SEO Keyword Research Strategy in 2026" (Ume Laila). Batch 114. The framework is standard SEO practice; the card gives no data, so nothing below is a benchmark.

## 7 steps

| # | Step | Output | Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Understand your business | One-paragraph offer, audience and goal | Write what you sell, to whom, and what a visit is worth |
| 2 | Find seed keywords | 10-30 broad terms | Products, problems, jobs-to-be-done, customer words |
| 3 | Expand keyword ideas | Long list | Tools, SERP suggestions, competitors, communities |
| 4 | Analyze and evaluate | Metrics per keyword | See the 8 metrics below |
| 5 | Group and prioritize | Clusters ranked | One cluster = one intent = one page |
| 6 | Map to content | Keyword-to-URL map | New page, update, or merge; avoid two pages for one intent |
| 7 | Track and refine | Monthly review | Rankings, clicks, conversions; drop what does not move |

## 10 keyword types

Short-tail, long-tail, branded, non-branded, informational, navigational, commercial, transactional, local, question.

## 10 research methods

Brainstorming, Google SERP, Search Console, AI search (ask the answer engines what they cite), keyword tools, competitors, content gap, customers (sales calls, support tickets), communities (Reddit, forums, groups), trends.

## 8 metrics

| Metric | Read it as |
|---|---|
| Search volume | Demand ceiling, not traffic you will get |
| Traffic potential | What the top page actually earns; usually more useful than volume |
| Keyword difficulty (0-100 in Ahrefs and Semrush) | The two scores are different models and not comparable; use one tool consistently |
| Search intent | What the SERP rewards: guide, list, product page, tool |
| SERP analysis | Who ranks, what format, what features (snippets, AI overviews) |
| CPC | A rough proxy for commercial value |
| Trends | Seasonality and direction |
| Relevance | Does it fit what you sell? |

## Prioritisation framework

**High-potential keyword = Relevant + good traffic potential + manageable difficulty + matches search intent + business value.**
