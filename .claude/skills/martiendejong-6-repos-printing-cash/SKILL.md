## Uncomfortable summary
- **[Certain]** A free repo is not a business: none of the slides shows a single paying customer or invoice. "Printing cash" is the hook, not evidence.
- **[Likely]** The price anchors ($1,500, $27,000, $2,000) are what the author imagines a client would pay; real clients pay for outcomes, not repos.
- Before reselling anything built on a repo, read its licence and the terms of every model or data source it calls.
