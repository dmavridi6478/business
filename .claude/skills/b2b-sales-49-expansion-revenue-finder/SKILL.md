# 49. Expansion Revenue Finder

Stage 7 of 7: Onboarding and expansion. Skill 49 of 50 in the B2B sales set. The title comes from the Prosp "Claude Skills for B2B Sales" graphic; the method below is original to this repository (the graphic's own skill file was not obtained). Follow `b2b-sales-standard` for evidence tags, drafts-only and privacy rules.

## When to use

Use for one account with usage data, org facts and the customer's stated needs.

## Inputs

- Required: what the account uses and what it has bought
- Required: usage data against limits
- Required: the customer's stated needs or requests, quoted
- Required: the products or tiers available to sell
- Optional: the customer's org structure
- Optional: results delivered so far
- If a required input is missing, ask for it. Do not guess or fill it with an example.

## Method

1. List the current footprint: products, users, sites, teams.
2. List expansion hypotheses of three kinds: more of the same (users, volume), new teams or sites, and new products. Each needs evidence from the inputs.
3. For each, state the customer's likely benefit in their terms and the evidence they have asked or need it.
4. Check readiness: is the customer getting value now? If not, fix value before expanding.
5. Rank by evidence strength and value to the customer, not by price.
6. Write the approach for the top two: who to talk to, the question to ask, and the draft message needing approval.
7. List the risks: over-selling, support load, contract terms.

## Output

Use these headings, in this order:

- `## Current footprint`
- `## Expansion hypotheses with evidence`
- `## Readiness check`
- `## Ranking`
- `## Approach for the top two`
- `## Risks`
- `## Assumptions and what could be wrong`

## Quality checks

The output fails if any of these is false:

- Every hypothesis cites evidence from the inputs.
- Readiness is checked before expansion.
- The ranking is not by price.
- Messages are labelled drafts.
- Every number not taken from the inputs carries a `[calc: ...]` or `[assumption]` tag (see `b2b-sales-standard`).
- Nothing is sent, posted, priced or signed; anything outward is labelled "draft, needs approval".

## Do not

- Do not upsell an unhappy customer.
- Do not invent a need.
- Do not recommend features the customer cannot use.
