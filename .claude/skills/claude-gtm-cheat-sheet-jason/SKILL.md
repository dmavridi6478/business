## What to do with it

1. Ask the author for a text version, or re-share a higher-resolution image; then run `/gtm-model-pick`-style review on the actual prompts.
2. For the structure, the four-layer loop matches what is already here: `gtm-outbound-engine`, `gtm-5-stages-data-layer`, `gtm-funnel-2026`, `claude-connectors-by-category`, `claude-sales-prompts-20`, `cowork-sales-prompts-10`, `outbound-sales-system-5stage`.
3. Treat tool and model recommendations as vendor-influenced. Do not connect a tool because a poster lists it: check what data it reads and writes, and who approves any send.
4. Outreach built from such prompts still needs your own list rules, consent and opt-out handling (for example GDPR and PECR in the EU and UK).
