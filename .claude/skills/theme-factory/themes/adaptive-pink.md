# Adaptive Pink

A before/after comparison look: a cool grey left panel for "traditional" and a pink-to-lilac right panel for "the new way", with a hot-pink second headline line and numbered pill badges alternating pink and violet.

Source: sampled from "Most marketing teams measure performance. Very few measure adaptability." (Andrea Rubik). Colours approximated.

## Color Palette

- **Background**: `#F8F9FE`
- **Navy Ink**: `#0F1633` - Headline line 1, titles
- **Hot Pink**: `#F0479A` - Headline line 2, odd badges, icons
- **Violet**: `#6C5CE0` - Even badges, "broader focus" label
- **Grey Panel**: `#EEF1F8` - Left ("traditional") column
- **Lilac Panel**: `#FBEFFC` → `#F3E9FD` - Right ("new") column gradient
- **Card White**: `#FFFFFF` - Row cards
- **Slate Text**: `#5A6178` - Descriptions

## Typography

- **Headers**: Aptos Display Bold (source: Inter Bold)
- **Body Text**: Aptos

## Best Used For

Old-vs-new comparisons, "most teams do X, few do Y" posts, scorecards, framework introductions, change-management decks.
