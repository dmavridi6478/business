# Warm Creator Carousel

A warm cream-and-cocoa palette with a brown-to-peach gradient headline — the "numbered tool list" carousel look used by creator-education accounts. Friendly, premium, readable on a phone.

Source: sampled from an uploaded carousel ("Best 8 AI Automation Tools", @theromanknox). Colours are sampled/approximated from the screenshots, not an official brand kit.

## Color Palette

- **Cream Canvas**: `#FDFBF8` - Slide background (add a faint 4 % hexagon/line pattern)
- **Cocoa**: `#4B2A24` - Headline start, body text
- **Peach Glow**: `#F2A15F` - Headline gradient end, accents
- **Apricot Pill**: `#FBBF85` - Page pill, "swipe" button fill
- **Blush Badge**: `#FBE8D9` - Number badge fill (with `#E9B99A` 1 px border)
- **Sand Rule**: `#E1B29E` - Divider rule under each subtitle
- **Asterisk Orange**: `#F7A865` - The `✱` bullet
- **Cover Espresso**: `#0C0304` → `#8D6F64` - Radial gradient for the dark cover slide

Headline treatment: `background: linear-gradient(90deg, #4B2A24 0%, #8A4F38 45%, #F2A15F 100%); -webkit-background-clip: text; color: transparent;`

## Typography

- **Headers**: Aptos Display Bold (source uses a geometric sans like Poppins ExtraBold; tracking −0.04em)
- **Body Text**: Aptos (source: Poppins Medium)

## Best Used For

Social carousels (LinkedIn/Instagram/TikTok, 4:5 at 1080×1350), "Top N tools" lists, lead-magnet slides, workshop handouts, community/course promotion.
