# Concentric Rainbow

Soft pastel concentric rings (blue core → green → yellow → peach → pink) on off-white, with small white label pills scattered on the rings and a bold serif headline. Friendly, instantly readable "start at the centre" metaphor.

Source: sampled from "5 Layers of Operational Excellence" (Eric Partaker). Colours approximated.

## Color Palette

- **Paper**: `#F5F5F5` - Background
- **Core Blue**: `#C9DDF8` - Ring 1 (centre)
- **Mint**: `#D9F0DF` - Ring 2
- **Butter**: `#FFF1C2` - Ring 3
- **Peach**: `#FCDCC4` - Ring 4
- **Rose**: `#F6C9C6` - Ring 5 (outer)
- **Ink**: `#111111` - Headline, ring titles
- **Label Pill**: `#FFFFFF` with `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.15)` shadow
- **Footer Blue**: `#1C77E9` - Call-to-action bar

## Typography

- **Headers**: Aptos Display Black (source: a heavy slab/serif such as Roboto Slab Black)
- **Body Text**: Aptos Semibold for ring labels, Aptos for pills

## Best Used For

Maturity models, inside-out frameworks, capability maps, "start here" roadmaps, LinkedIn framework posts.
