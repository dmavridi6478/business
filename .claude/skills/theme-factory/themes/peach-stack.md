# Peach Stack

A warm, airy cream-to-peach gradient with softly tinted 3D slabs stacked into a pyramid. Calm, premium SaaS feel; lets many small tool logos breathe.

Source: sampled from "The 5-layer GTM engine" (Cold IQ). Colours approximated from the image.
