# Prosp Dark Grid

A near-black org-chart grid: a CEO/lead card at the top with dashed connectors fanning out to 10 column heads, each column a stack of small outlined job cards alternating cyan and orange borders, and a full-width orange CTA bar. Dense but ordered.

Source: sampled from "Claude Team for MARKETING — 50 marketing jobs" (prosp). Colours approximated.

## Color Palette

- **Charcoal**: `#272928` - Background
- **Card**: `#1E2021` - Job card fill
- **Signal Orange**: `#FB5F22` - Headline accent word, CTA bar, alternate card border
- **Electric Cyan**: `#24CDEE` - Card borders, connectors
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` - Headline, card titles
- **Grey Text**: `#9AA0A3` - Card sub-labels
- **Mascot Cream**: `#F4D9C0` - Illustration/mascot fill

## Typography

- **Headers**: Aptos Display Black (source: a heavy grotesk such as Archivo Black)
- **Body Text**: Aptos Semibold, small

## Best Used For

"N jobs / N prompts" roundups, team or org charts, capability grids, skills libraries, lead-magnet posts.
