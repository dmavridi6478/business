# How to actually run GTM in 2026 (Alex Vacca, Frontal)

Source: one hourglass infographic ("from a team that's built GTM systems for 275+ B2B companies", a self-reported claim). The top half says "this doesn't matter", the bottom half "this does".

## This doesn't matter (as drawn)
Rewriting copy when the list or offer is the problem; open rates (Apple Mail "opens" emails nobody read); impressions from people who could never buy; meetings booked that never turn into pipeline; personalizing the email for the wrong person; most of the GTM advice you read online; more SDRs sending more cold email; one template sent to 10,000 people; which team gets credit for the lead; adding another tool to the stack; a 12-step email sequence; spam trigger word lists.

## This does
| Area | Item (tool shown) |
|---|---|
| AI search | Mentions on the sites AI answers cite; pages that answer what buyers ask AI |
| Outbound | Writing the week they raise or hire (Clay); email, LinkedIn and calls to top accounts (lemlist); authenticated domains, inboxes ramped slowly (Instantly); verified emails and mobiles, gaps filled by others (Prospeo) |
| Content | Founder and team posts answering what buyers ask on sales calls; people at target accounts who engage with your posts, sent to outbound |
| Ads | Ads shown to your outbound list before the first email; the founder's own posts run as ads to target accounts |
| RevOps | One clean CRM that every channel feeds and reads; every channel judged on the same pipeline number |

## Read critically
- **[Certain]** The tool logos shown (Clay, lemlist, Instantly, Prospeo) mark a vendor-network list, not a neutral test.
- **[Likely]** The "doesn't matter" half is sound on open rates (Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates them) and on list or offer quality outranking copy tweaks. "More tools never matter" overreaches: a missing enrichment or deliverability tool can be the real bottleneck.
- **[Likely]** "Ads to your outbound list before the first email" needs consent and a lawful basis for matching contact lists in ad platforms.
