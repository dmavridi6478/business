# Seven LinkedIn profile essentials (Reno Perry)

Source: one infographic, credited "By Reno Perry". The rules in the table are the sheet's; the checks and the scoring are my additions. The related five-hack sheet by the same author is in `linkedin-job-search-5-hacks`.

| Essential | The sheet says | Check it yourself |
|---|---|---|
| Profile photo | Photo affects first impressions and "can increase views up to 21x"; use a high-quality headshot (400 x 400 px), clean background, good lighting, dress as for a client meeting or interview | The 21x figure is the author's and unverified; treat it as "a photo helps" |
| Headline | More than a job title; a hook. Under 220 characters; keywords tied to your target role and industry; speak to your value ("Helping X do Y") | Does it contain the exact job titles recruiters type? |
| Custom URL | `linkedin.com/in/yourname`; avoid random characters or numbers; add it to resume, email signature and business cards | One minute to fix |
| About | A positioning statement, not a resume summary. First person; open with something personal or a clear value; show how you create real impact; weave in your career story; end with what you are looking for or how to reach you. Pro tip: speak to the next opportunity you want, not the last one you had | Read it aloud; is the target role named? |
| Skills | They power LinkedIn search. Add up to 100 relevant skills; prioritise those in your ideal job descriptions; refresh every 3 to 6 months | Copy ten skills from three target job ads and compare |
| Content activity | Active profiles build visibility; comment weekly on industry content; share insights, wins, resources; stay consistent | A weekly slot, not daily |
| Recommendations | Social proof; aim for 5+ strong ones; request them after projects or milestones; ask people to mention outcomes, leadership and how you work | Ask for specific outcomes, never write them for others to sign without their edit |

## Score yourself (my addition)

Give each essential 0 (missing), 1 (present, generic) or 2 (specific, evidenced). Total /14: under 8 means fix photo, headline, About and skills first; those are what a recruiter sees before anything else.

## Cautions

- The "$200K-500K job in 90 days" banner in the sample profile is the author's own coaching ad, not evidence for the method.
- Keyword matching helps search, but do not claim skills you cannot demonstrate in an interview.
