# GTM Strategy Framework 2026

**Invoke:** `/gtm-strategy`

## Purpose
Apply the 2026 Go-To-Market framework to motion selection, tool stack decisions, and identifying the weakest link in the growth engine.

## Core Principle

> GTM isn't a launch plan. It's the system behind why a product will succeed in a market.
> The real work isn't just taking a product to market — it's making sure the market has a reason to care.

**When one link is weak, the entire growth engine feels it.**

## The 7 GTM Building Blocks

Work through these in order. A break at any stage collapses everything after it.

| # | Block | The Question It Answers |
|---|-------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Market** | Are we going after the right opportunity? |
| 2 | **Customer** | Do we know exactly who we're building for? |
| 3 | **Problem** | Are we solving something that truly matters? |
| 4 | **Positioning** | Why should customers choose us? |
| 5 | **Product** | Does what we've built deliver on that promise? |
| 6 | **Sales** | Can we turn that value into a buying decision? |
| 7 | **Growth** | Can we make it repeatable and scalable? |

## The 7 GTM Motions

| Motion | How It Drives Growth | Primary Tools |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Inbound-Led** | Earn demand through content, search and AI discovery | Semrush, Ahrefs, HubSpot |
| **Outbound-Led** | Create demand through targeted, signal-based outreach | Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Clay |
| **Product-Led** | Let users experience value before they buy | TasteQ, Amplitude, Pendo |
| **Partner-Led** | Grow through referrals, integrations and co-selling | PartnerStack, Crossbeam, Impartner |
| **Community-Led** | Build trust through peers, conversations and advocacy | Slack, Reddit, Discord |
| **Event-Led** | Create high-intent interactions through webinars & shared experiences | Riverside, Luma, Zoom |
| **Account-Led** | Orchestrate marketing and sales around high-value accounts | Clay, 6sense, Demandbase |

## Amplification Channels
Every motion is amplified by: **Search · Social · Creators · AI Answers · Paid**

## Usage

When invoked:
1. **Diagnose** — ask which of the 7 blocks is weakest (or run through all 7 questions)
2. **Select motion** — match business stage and strengths to the right GTM motion(s)
3. **Stack tools** — recommend the tool stack for the selected motion
4. **Channel plan** — identify which amplification channels fit the motion

Ask: *"What stage is your business at, and which GTM question do you most need to answer?"*
