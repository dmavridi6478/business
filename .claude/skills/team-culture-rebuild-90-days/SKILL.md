# Rebuild team culture in the first 90 days

Source: an infographic by Michaela Schuler, "How to rebuild team culture in the first 90 days: a 6-step roadmap for leaders after major change" (Batch 124). Its closing line: "Day 90: the culture is not finished, but the direction is set." The six steps and their actions are summarised below in my own words; the order on the mountain graphic goes 1 to 6 from the bottom.

| Step | Name | Actions (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create clarity | Name and repeat what matters now; say what stops, not only what starts; clarify who decides what; name the culture you are building; show people where they sit in it |
| 2 | Align leadership | Align as one leadership team; one story and one shared language; match decisions to your words; give team leads the translation; agree what you will and will not reinforce |
| 3 | Build team rhythms | Set clear channels (where to look, where to ask); weekly all-hands with real Q&A; steady one-to-ones you do not cancel; recognise in the moment, not once a year; rebuild one team on purpose |
| 4 | Create trust | Go first and name what you do not know; hold the unscripted conversations; close the loop, even when the answer is no; treat silence as not agreement; ask people to challenge your thinking |
| 5 | Build accountability | One clear owner per priority; keep commitments visible; measure the new priorities, not the old KPIs; protect focus and capacity; remove three things that eat the week |
| 6 | Make it stick | Live it rather than announce it; what is not repeated is replaced; build culture on ordinary Tuesdays; turn habits into the default; make retrospectives a habit, not a reaction |

## A 30-60-90 sequence (my suggestion)
- **Days 1 to 30: clarity and alignment.** Steps 1 and 2. Output: a one-page statement of priorities, what stops, who decides what, and the leadership team's agreed message. Test it by asking five people at different levels to repeat it back.
- **Days 31 to 60: rhythms and trust.** Steps 3 and 4. Output: the meeting and channel system running, and three visible examples of a loop closed (including a "no").
- **Days 61 to 90: accountability and habit.** Steps 5 and 6. Output: owners and measures for each priority, three things removed from the week, a first retrospective.

## What to measure (pick four, ask the same questions each month)
1. Share of people who can state the top priorities unprompted.
2. Share who say they know who decides what.
3. Share who say they could challenge a decision without penalty.
4. Meeting load per person (hours per week) and focus time protected.
5. Commitments made versus kept, by owner.
6. Regretted attrition and engagement pulse (anonymous, minimum group size).

## Where the graphic overreaches
- **Ninety days sets direction, not culture.** The graphic says so itself. Culture shows in behaviour under pressure, which takes longer to observe.
- **No evidence base is shown.** The steps are experienced leadership practice, not a tested protocol. Adapt them to your context and review.
- **Change fatigue and legitimate grievance.** After layoffs or a merger, some distrust is a rational response to events. Acknowledge what happened; do not treat resistance as a culture problem. Where redundancies, reorganisation or monitoring are involved, employment and consultation law applies; take advice.
- **Leaders are the instrument.** If leaders do not change their own behaviour, steps 2 and 4 fail first. Ask for feedback on yours.
- **Do not announce "the new culture".** Step 6 warns against it for a reason: announcements without changed decisions raise cynicism.
