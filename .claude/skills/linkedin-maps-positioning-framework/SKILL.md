# MAPS: position yourself as an expert on LinkedIn

Source: one infographic, "How To Position Yourself As An Expert On LinkedIn - The Maps Framework", by Josh Sanders ("Want help with yours? Take the free positioning quiz via the link in my post"; the quiz was not taken). Transcribed 15 October 2026. The infographic's example uses Chris Donnelly's LinkedIn (180k to 750k in 6 months); that result is the author's, unverified here.

## The four letters

| Letter | Definition | Weak vs strong | Try this |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M = Market** | The exact people you help and the problem they would happily spend money fixing | "Business owners" vs "Founders who want LinkedIn to bring in leads" | Who would pay you tomorrow if you messaged them today? (The narrower your market, the easier your content is to write.) |
| **A = Achievements** | Proof only you can point to: results you drove, things you built or grew | - | Write down 3 results you would be proud to put a number on, e.g. "40 new clients in 12 months" or "grew revenue from 200k to 1M". Lead with the result your market cares about most; save the rest for About |
| **P = Process** | The steps you repeat every time to get your clients' results | Graphic's example: Positioning, Content, Capture, Conversion | Name your process so people can picture how you will help. Keep it to 3-5 steps so it is easy to explain on a call or in a post |
| **S = Stance** | A view you will happily defend, even if others in your space disagree | "Grow your followers first." vs "Attention without infrastructure is wasted." | Think of one popular piece of advice you disagree with (e.g. "you need to post every day to grow") and why. Back it with a result or story so people know you have tested it |

## Build the positioning line

Template: **"I help [market] get [result] through [process], after [achievement]."**
Example on the graphic: "I help founders turn LinkedIn into a lead gen system, after helping grow Chris Donnelly's audience from 180k to 750k in 6 months." Read it aloud; if it sounds like a CV, keep editing.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "I'm a marketing consultant." | "I help B2B SaaS founders turn LinkedIn into booked sales calls." |
| "I'm a business coach." | "I help agency owners scale past 1M without working weekends." |
| "I run a recruitment agency." | "I help Series A startups hire their first sales team in 30 days." |
| "I'm a freelance designer." | "I design landing pages that turn LinkedIn traffic into booked calls for coaches." |

## Where to use it

Use the same line everywhere so people start to recognise it. Banner: your offer in one line. Headline: who you help plus your biggest result. About: open with the positioning line. Posts: mention your market and stance in hooks and calls to action.

## Cautions

- **Do not claim an achievement you cannot show.** Every number needs a client who would confirm it; get permission before naming one.
- A stance must be something you believe and can evidence, not a manufactured controversy.
- Test the line: send it to 5 people in your market and ask them to say back what you do. If they cannot, it is not positioned yet.
