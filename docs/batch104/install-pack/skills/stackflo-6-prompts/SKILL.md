# StackFlo 6 prompts (cover says 5, the carousel has 6)
1. **Reading into 2 minutes** - "Summarise this into the 5 things I actually need to know. Remove repetition and background information. End with any actions I need to take." (`/read-5-things`)
2. **Emails** - "Write a reply to this email. Keep it professional but natural. Be concise and avoid corporate language. Ask me for any missing information first." Paste the email underneath. (`/reply-email`)
3. **Notes to plan** - "Turn these notes into a clear action plan. Separate: What needs doing / Who needs to do it / Priority / Next step." (`/action-plan-from-notes`)
4. **Research** - "I need to understand [TOPIC]. Give me the 20% of information that covers 80% of it. Explain it simply, then tell me what to research next." (`/research-80-20`)
5. **Bad first draft** - "Critique this draft before rewriting it. Tell me: What's unclear / What's unnecessary / What's missing / What could be stronger. Then rewrite it." Do not ask AI to "make it better". (`/grade-and-rewrite-loop`)
6. **Save first** - "Before answering, ask me the questions you need to give me the best possible result." (`/clarify-first`)
