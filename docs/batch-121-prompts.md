# Batch 121 prompts (plain text, copy and paste)

The four infographics contain no verbatim prompts (the only quoted text is the example banned-words line, "Never say: leverage, tapestry, groundbreaking."). Every prompt below is original, built from their content.

## 1. Custom instructions for a marketer (step 1 of the Claude setup)

Write my Claude custom instructions. I am a [role] at [company], serving [audience]. Our tone is [description]. Give me: a four-sentence identity block, five tone rules each with a "do" and a "don't" example, and a banned-words list of at most fifteen words with a one-line reason for each (start with: leverage, tapestry, groundbreaking). Keep it under 250 words. Do not invent facts about the company; mark anything missing as [ADD: ...].

## 2. Projects plan (step 2)

I work on [functions: content, email, research or others]. For each function, propose one Claude Project: the exact files to upload, files to leave out (confidential, personal data, outdated), a naming and dating rule, and a one-line project instruction. Tell me which project to build first and how I will measure the time saved over two weeks.

## 3. Reusable template (step 3)

Turn this task into a reusable prompt template: [describe the task and paste your best past output]. Give me the input fields, the prompt with placeholders, the output format, three quality checks, and a five-output review routine. Tell me what the template cannot do reliably and what a human must check.

## 4. Connector plan with least privilege (step 4)

I want to connect Claude to [Drive folder, Gmail and Calendar, CRM]. For each connector list: the narrowest access to start with (read-only, one folder or label), what it could read, write or send, the data it must never touch, who must approve it in my company, and how I will review and revoke it. Put the connectors in the order to add them.

## 5. TAM mapping from won and lost deals

Here is a summary of my closed-won and closed-lost deals: [paste counts and key fields]. Derive an ICP as testable rules, state the sample size and the survivorship bias in using only my CRM, and propose one hypothesis segment I have never sold to. Then split the market into segments by region, industry and size, choose path A (blast first, enrich repliers) or B (enrich first with signals and stack) for each with the reason, define tiers T1, T2 and T3 with explicit rules, and list personas for T1 accounts. Add outreach and data-protection checks per region. Mark every cost or count as an assumption. Slash command: `/tam-map`.

## 6. Who owns what in launches

Our launch of [product] involves brand, product marketing, demand generation, lifecycle and customer marketing. For each, list what it owns in this launch, the one deliverable it must hand to the next team, and the metric it is judged on. Show where two teams could both think they own the same task and propose who decides. Use our current team structure: [describe], and say where one person covers several functions.

## 7. Presentation builder

Plan a [length]-minute presentation for [audience] to achieve [decision]. Choose one or two of: the 10-20-30 rule, the Golden Circle, the Rule of Three, the STARR method, or What? So what? Now what?, and explain why you rejected the others. Give a slide-by-slide outline with message titles, one source per number, speaker timing, a 30-second opening and a close with a decision, an owner and a date. Use only this content: [paste]. Mark gaps as [ADD: ...]. Slash command: `/presentation-frameworks`.
