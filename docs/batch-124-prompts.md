# Batch 124 prompts (plain text, copy and paste)

The six images (five new, plus the 60-tool graphic that was already covered in Batch 123) contain no verbatim prompts, only a formula for each of two columns in the GTM table (quoted in prompt 5). Every prompt below is original.

## 1. LinkedIn authority audit
I am a [role] selling [offer] to [audience]. I post [frequency] and spend about [hours] a week on LinkedIn. Treat LinkedIn authority as two engines: relationship-led (personal brand, genuine engagement, community) and algorithm-led (clear positioning, consistent messaging, content systems). Score me 0 to 2 on: specific positioning, matching profile, five target accounts commented on weekly, warm introductions tracked, two or three content themes, a posting rhythm held for eight weeks, inbound conversations logged with source. Give a weekly time split for my stage with the reason and the outcome measures to review after 90 days. Do not quote any survey percentage as a benchmark. No automation that pretends to be me.

## 2. AI citation question map
My site is [URL] and my business is [description]. List the ten questions buyers ask before they buy (costs, comparisons, alternatives, limits, FAQs), in their exact wording. For each, say whether a page on my own site answers it directly in the first two sentences, and what proof (data, method, expert input, date) would make it citable. Then write a 25-prompt visibility test set and the log columns: assistant, date, mentioned, cited, source, sentiment. Mark any statistic you cite as verified, provisional or unverified.

## 3. 90-day culture plan after a major change
Our team of [size] went through [merger / reorganisation / layoffs / new leader]. What has been said so far: [summary]. First, state in two sentences what people have probably concluded and which grievances are legitimate. Then, for each of six steps (create clarity, align leadership, build team rhythms, create trust, build accountability, make it stick), give two or three actions for us, an owner, a date and the signal that it worked. Add a 30-60-90 plan, four monthly measures (anonymous, minimum group size), three things to remove from the team's week, and what leaders must change in their own behaviour. Flag where employment or consultation law may apply.

## 4. Pressure-test a culture message
Here is the message leadership plans to send about the new culture: [paste]. Check it against these tests: does it say what stops as well as what starts; does it match decisions already made; would a team lead know how to translate it; does it ask for challenge; does it promise more than 90 days can deliver. Rewrite it in plain language and list three decisions leaders should make visibly in the next two weeks.

## 5. GTM strategy versus engineering split
My offer: [offer]. Target: [customer]. Current motion: [channels and tools]. First write the positioning sentence in this form: "We help [customer] get [outcome] with [offer]. Unlike [alternative], we [difference]." Then list my open decisions and sort them into strategy (offer, price, positioning, brand, channel) and engineering (data, integrations, automation, sending setup), naming the strategy decision that blocks the most engineering. Then specify one outbound motion as: [target criteria] → enrich and verify → send and follow up → log outcomes, with the data fields, the hand check of 50 records, the proof asset per industry, and the weekly report back to strategy. Flag privacy and outreach-law checks. Do not choose tools yet.

## 6. Pick items from a 48-item marketing department list
My function: [description]. Channels: [list]. Ad spend: [amount]. Tools connected: [list]. From these six sections (strategy and GTM, paid media, Meta and Google agents, LinkedIn and personal brand, content SEO and CRO, scoring and stack), pick at most six items I need, and for each say which skill or command already in my library covers it. For anything that touches an ad account propose a read-only first week, a spend cap set in the ad platform itself, a human approval step for every change, and a change log. Do not describe the contents of any item you only know by title.
