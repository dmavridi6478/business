# Batch 111 - prompts in plain text (copy and paste)

Replace every [BRACKET] before sending. Slash commands live in `.claude/commands/`, skills in `.claude/skills/`. Section A is transcribed from the sales-email infographic (source wording). Sections B to D are written for this repo from the infographics' structures; they are not the authors' text.

## A. Nine sales-email prompts - `/sales-email <1-9>` (source: SalesDaily.co infographic)

A1. The Personalised Opener
Purpose: skip the "hope you're well" trap and prove you did your homework.
Here's my prospect's LinkedIn profile: [paste]. Write 3 opening lines for a cold email that reference something specific about their career, recent activity, or company. No flattery. Make it sound like I actually read their page.

A2. The Simplifier
Purpose: shorter emails get more replies.
Rewrite this sales email at a 5th grade reading level. Cut it to under 80 words. Remove every word that doesn't earn its spot. Keep one clear CTA: [paste draft]

A3. The Subject Line Factory
Purpose: test 10 options instead of guessing one.
Here's my email body: [paste]. Give me 10 subject lines using these angles: curiosity, a specific number, their company name, a pain point, and a bold claim. Keep each under 6 words.

A4. The Feature-To-Benefit Translator
Purpose: features describe your product; benefits describe their life after buying.
I'm selling [product] to a [prospect's title] at a [company type]. Here are my product's features: [list]. Rewrite each one as a benefit statement this buyer would actually care about. Start each with "You" or "Your team."

A5. The Objection Follow-Up
Purpose: address the real blocker instead of "circling back".
My prospect said: "[exact objection]". Their role is [title] at a [company type]. Write a follow-up email that acknowledges their concern without agreeing, reframes the value around their specific pain point, and ends with a soft CTA. Under 100 words.

A6. The Breakup Email
Purpose: remove all pressure with a final message.
I've sent [number] emails to [prospect's title] at [company] over [timeframe] with no reply. Write a final email that's honest, zero-pressure, and gives them an easy out. Make it feel human, not passive-aggressive. Under 60 words.

A7. The Case Study Snippet
Purpose: one case study becomes ammunition for every persona.
Here's a full case study: [paste]. Write a 2-sentence version for a [specific role] that leads with the metric they care about most. Then write a different 2-sentence version for a [different role]. No buzzwords.

A8. The Spam And Tone Audit
Purpose: catch spam-trigger language and robotic tone before sending.
Here's my sales email: [paste]. Flag any words or phrases likely to trigger spam filters. Rate the tone from 1-10 on warmth and confidence. Then rewrite it with your fixes applied.

A9. The Full Sequence Builder
Purpose: a working five-email draft in minutes.
I sell [product/service] to [ICP]. The main pain point I solve is [pain]. Build a 5-email outbound sequence. Email 1: pain-focused. Email 2: social proof. Email 3: insight or contrarian take. Email 4: direct ask. Email 5: breakup. Keep each under 80 words.

## B. Fifty marketing verbs - `/marketing-verb <VERB>` (structure from the Emilia Moller infographic; prompt wording is ours)

B1. RESEARCH (30 sources -> one answer)
Using only these sources, answer [question]. Cite each claim to a source; list what the sources disagree on.

B2. ANALYZE (raw CSV -> actual insights)
Analyse this CSV. State the 5 findings that would change a decision, with the numbers behind each.

B3. SUMMARIZE (100 pages -> what matters)
Summarise to one page: decisions needed, risks, numbers. Quote anything you are unsure of.

B4. COMPARE (you vs 10 competitors)
Compare us with these 10 on [criteria] as a table. Mark every cell 'verified' or 'inferred'.

B5. EXTRACT (50 calls -> recurring patterns)
From these transcripts extract recurring pains, phrases and objections with call counts.

B6. INTERVIEW (calls -> pains + objections)
Turn this call into: top 3 pains, top 3 objections, buying triggers, exact quotes.

B7. BRAINSTORM (5 directions, not 50 ideas)
Give 5 clearly different directions for [goal]; for each the bet, the risk and the cheapest test.

B8. CHALLENGE (make the case against your strategy)
Argue against this strategy as its smartest critic. Then say which objection is strongest.

B9. CRITIQUE (ask what is wrong, not what is good)
List what is wrong with this draft, ranked by damage. No praise.

B10. VALIDATE (pressure-test before you build)
List the 5 assumptions this idea needs to be true and the fastest evidence for each.

B11. SIMULATE (see it as your customer)
Act as [persona]. React to this page line by line: what you believe, doubt, skip.

B12. POSITION (find what only you can say)
From our proof and the competitor claims, find the statement only we can make and can prove.

B13. SEGMENT (find audiences hidden in your data)
Cluster these customers into 3-5 segments; name each by behaviour, size and value.

B14. PERSONALIZE (one message -> every audience)
Rewrite this message for each of these audiences; keep the offer identical, change the angle.

B15. MEETING (notes -> actions + owners)
From these notes list decisions, actions with owner and date, open questions.

B16. EDIT (good -> actually worth reading)
Edit for clarity and cut 20%. Keep my voice. Show what you removed.

B17. SURVEY (responses -> key findings)
Summarise these responses: top findings, the minority view, one surprising result.

B18. REPURPOSE (1 webinar -> 20 assets)
From this transcript produce: 5 posts, 3 emails, 1 article outline, 5 hooks, 3 quotes.

B19. SCRIPT (idea -> short-form video)
Write a 30-45 second script: hook, 3 beats, CTA, on-screen text.

B20. HOOK (generate + rank opening lines)
Give 15 opening lines across 5 hook types; rank the top 3 and say why.

B21. HEADLINE (generate + score alternatives)
Give 10 headlines; score each 1-5 for clarity, specificity, curiosity.

B22. DEDUPE (messy list -> clean data)
Deduplicate by email then company+name; show merge rules and the count removed.

B23. BRIEF (idea -> execution-ready brief)
Turn this idea into a brief: goal, audience, message, deliverables, KPI, deadline, owner.

B24. PLAN (goal -> tasks + deadlines)
Break this goal into tasks with owner, dependency and date; flag the critical path.

B25. ORGANIZE (chaos -> structured system)
Propose a folder/tag/naming system for these assets and map every file into it.

B26. CALCULATE (model CAC, ROI + scenarios)
Model CAC, payback and ROI for base/low/high; state every assumption in a table.

B27. FORMULA (describe it -> Excel formula)
Write the Excel formula for [description]; explain it and give a test row.

B28. CLEAN (messy spreadsheet -> usable data)
Normalise these columns (case, dates, currency); list every change you made.

B29. CLUSTER (1,000 responses -> themes)
Cluster these responses into themes; give count, share and 2 verbatim quotes per theme.

B30. CLASSIFY (sort leads, feedback or content)
Classify each row into [labels]; add a confidence flag and list the unclear rows.

B31. SCORE (rank ideas against your criteria)
Score these ideas on [criteria, weights]; show the maths; give the ranked list.

B32. AUDIT (find gaps before you publish)
Audit this page against [checklist]; list gaps by severity with the fix.

B33. VERIFY (check claims + assumptions)
List every factual claim in this draft and mark it supported, unsupported or wrong.

B34. DISPROVE (find evidence you are wrong)
Find the strongest evidence that this claim is false. If none, say so.

B35. SYNTHESIZE (20 sources -> one POV)
Synthesise these 20 sources into one point of view; show where it rests on thin evidence.

B36. DEBRIEF (transcript -> insights + actions)
Debrief this meeting: what we learned, what changed, next 3 actions.

B37. MINE (reviews -> exact customer language)
Mine these reviews for exact phrases customers use for problem, outcome and objection.

B38. FAQ (customer questions -> answers)
Group these questions, then write answers from the approved docs only; flag any gap.

B39. DASHBOARD (data -> interactive dashboard)
Build a single-file HTML dashboard from this data with 5 KPIs, 2 charts and a filter.

B40. SEO (find content gaps + opportunities)
From this keyword and competitor data list content gaps ranked by opportunity.

B41. AEO (find questions your brand should answer)
List the questions buyers ask AI assistants in this category that our site does not answer.

B42. PITCH (research -> sales deck)
Turn this research into a 10-slide outline: problem, proof, offer, next step.

B43. VISUALIZE (data -> charts + interactive visuals)
Pick the right chart for each question in this data and build it; state what it shows.

B44. BUILD (idea -> interactive tool)
Build a small calculator/checker for [job] as one HTML file; list inputs and formula.

B45. CREATE (prompt -> finished PPT/XLSX/DOCX)
Create the finished file (pptx/xlsx/docx) from this brief using the repo's file skills.

B46. CONNECT (stop copy-pasting your context)
Which of my connected tools hold this context? Pull it directly instead of asking me to paste.

B47. REMEMBER (give ongoing work a Project)
Set up a Project: instructions, reference files, voice rules for [ongoing work].

B48. REUSE (turn repeat workflows into Skills)
I repeat this workflow weekly. Write it as a skill with trigger, steps, output format.

B49. MONITOR (competitors -> meaningful changes)
Check these competitor pages and report only meaningful changes since last time.

B50. SCHEDULE (recurring work -> runs itself)
Propose a schedule and the exact prompt for [recurring job]; I approve before it is created.
