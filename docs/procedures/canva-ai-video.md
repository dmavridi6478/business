# Procedure: AI Canva Video Production

## When this applies

Producing video with Canva's built-in AI tools — animation, screen-record
narration, AI voiceover, and text-to-video — instead of the heavier stack
in the `video` skill (Hyperframes/Remotion, Veo/Runway/Kling, HeyGen/
Synthesia). Canva's suite is the right call when the job needs no-code,
inside-one-tool production; reach for the `video` skill's stack instead
when the job needs programmatic/templated batch rendering, a custom AI
avatar, or a dedicated AI-generation model with more control than a text
prompt gives.

## The 5 formats, and when to use each

| # | Format | How | Best for |
|---|--------|-----|----------|
| 1 | **Animated design** | Design a static post → select elements → Animate → pick a style (breathe, drift, fade, pan, rise, wipe) → export as MP4 loop, 5-15s | TikTok/Reels/Shorts — animated designs consistently outperform statics in reach |
| 2 | **Talking presentation** | Open a presentation → Present and Record → webcam captures your face over the slides → AI auto-captions → export MP4 | Explainer videos, thought leadership, LinkedIn video — professional talking-head with no green screen or studio |
| 3 | **Video editing** | Import a recorded MP4 → add branded elements, AI auto-captions (95%+ English accuracy), brand-kit logo, background music → export | Repurposing recorded content, branded shorts. For complex cuts/effects, pair with CapCut (per the `video` skill) rather than forcing it in Canva |
| 4 | **AI voiceover** | In any Canva video/presentation → Add Audio → AI Voiceover → type the script → pick from 40+ voices/languages/accents → auto-synced to the timeline | Faceless educational content, product demos, e-learning |
| 5 | **Text-to-video** | New design → AI Video → describe the clip (subject, mood, duration, aspect ratio) → Canva generates an animated clip, no footage needed | TikTok backgrounds, YouTube intros, abstract visuals/environments — currently less reliable for people, so don't rely on it for anything with a human subject |

Decision shortcut: have footage already? → format 3. Need a presenter but no
camera crew? → format 2. Need scale without recording anything? → format 1
or 5 (5 for backgrounds/abstract, 1 for branded posts). Need narration over
existing visuals? → format 4.

## Platform export specs

| Platform | Spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Instagram Reels / Shorts | 1080×1920, 9:16 | vertical, under 60s optimal |
| YouTube standard | 1920×1080, 16:9 | landscape, any length |
| Instagram feed video | 1080×1080, 1:1 | square or portrait |
| LinkedIn video | 1920×1080 or 1:1 | landscape or square |
| Facebook video | 1280×720, 16:9 | landscape preferred |

Canva's Magic Switch handles most format conversions automatically —
design the primary format first, then switch. Always preview each output
before downloading; video switching needs more manual checking than static
design switching does.

## The 4-week system (turns this into a recurring content pipeline)

- **Week 1** — Build 5 branded animated designs (format 1). Five video
  pieces, no recording.
- **Week 2** — Record your best presentation with Present and Record
  (format 2), then clean it up with format 3. One LinkedIn video.
- **Week 3** — Turn your best carousel into a 60-second AI-voiceover
  explainer (format 4) for YouTube Shorts. No recording session needed.
- **Week 4** — Generate a text-to-video clip (format 5) for a TikTok
  background, layer voiceover and brand overlay on top.

Feeds naturally into this repo's `/content-pipeline` command once a piece
is scripted — Canva AI video is a production method to slot into that
pipeline's design/schedule stages, not a replacement for it.
