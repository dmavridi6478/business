## LinkedIn: the 15 prompts (condensed, from SME AI Issue 03)

Phase 1. Audit
1. "I am pasting my full LinkedIn profile below. You are a senior LinkedIn strategist who works with B2B service businesses. Read it as my ideal client would on a first visit. Tell me: why would they scroll past? What is confusing? What is missing? What would make them click away in under 10 seconds? Be specific. Zero sugar-coating. Do not rewrite anything yet. Just diagnose. Think before answering."
2. "Based on my profile and the audit above, define my ideal client in precise detail: job title, company size, the specific problem I solve, the words they type into LinkedIn search, the fear behind that search, the outcome they are buying. I will use this as the brief for every rewrite."

Phase 2. Rewrite
3. "Using the ideal client brief above, rewrite my headline. Under 220 characters. Lead with the outcome, name the audience. Write 3 versions: direct, curiosity-led, result-forward. No buzzwords."
4. "Rewrite my About section: open with the exact problem my ideal client has, in their words; show I have solved it with one specific result; explain my method in 2 sentences; end with one CTA. Maximum 260 words. First person. No corporate language."
5. "Rewrite each experience entry: one sentence of context, then 3 result bullets as [action verb] + [what I did] + [measurable outcome]. Remove duty language. Use only numbers I have given you; where I have none, ask me."
6. "Design my Featured section with 3 items: a free resource, a piece of social proof or a case study, and a link to my offer. Give each a title and a 160-character description."
7. "Identify 8 to 12 keywords my ideal client types into LinkedIn search. Show where each belongs (headline, about, experience, skills) without keyword stuffing."

Phase 3. Content
8. "Define 4 content pillars for my niche. For each: topic, audience pain, best format, 3 post ideas for this week."
9. "Give me 5 repeatable hook structures for my audience: template, why it works, one example."
10. "I will paste 5 posts that performed well in my niche. For each: hook structure, emotional trigger, format, why it was rewarded. Then 3 ideas that reuse the pattern in my own words."

Phase 4. Connections (share only the fields you need; check your tool's data terms first)
11. "Here is a list of my connections (name, title, company). My ideal client is [brief]. Sort into A (message this week), B (nurture), C (not now). Explain each A."
12. "For each A-list person write a first message under 60 words that references something specific I can verify about their role or company. No pitch. Do not ask for anything."
13. "Write a 3-message follow-up sequence: open a conversation; 5 days later add value; 7 days after that make one low-friction offer. Direct, human, not salesy."
14. "Write 5 reply templates (interested but busy, tell me more, not now, wrong person but can refer, how much does it cost), each under 80 words, each moving one step forward."

Phase 5. Voice
15. "Define my LinkedIn brand voice from my profile, ideal client and the writing samples I paste: five words, what I never say, default format, one opinion I consistently hold, the feeling I want a first-time reader to have. Output a one-page voice brief I can paste at the start of every session."
