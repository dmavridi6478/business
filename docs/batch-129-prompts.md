## 1. Five GTM questions
Company: [..]. Offer: [..]. Answer in one page: Who (ICP, with the won and lost deals that support it), What (value proposition and the buyer words behind it), Where (territories and segments, ranked with reasons), How (direct or indirect, sales and marketing activities needed) and Not what (a stop list with an owner). Mark every guess. Then audit three layers: foundation, demand activation, revenue growth loops, each pass, partial or fail with one test.

## 2. LinkedIn GTM map
Product: [..]. ICP guess: [..]. Founder time: [hours] a week. Build a five-ring map from the centre out: one or two goals, three or four observable ICP attributes, two or three content formats tied to the ICP's pain plus one proof format, two channels, and one lead path with a follow-up and a source log. Add the weekly metric. Do not suggest auto-DM flows that breach platform terms.

## 3. AI governance scorecard
Organisation: [..]. AI systems and vendors: [..]. For each of 25 governance decisions in visibility, ownership, human authority, evidence and continuous governance, score 0, 1 or 2 from what I describe, quoting evidence or "not stated". Name the weakest group, the three largest exposures and a 30-day action with an owner type for each. Flag where law or contracts decide.

## 4. Offer negotiation plan
Role [..], location [..], offer [..]. Set rock bottom, target and stretch (mark market figures as assumptions to verify). List the non-salary items to ask for, in order. Write scripts for: asked for expectations first, asked current pay, "above our band", low offer. Build a four-year total-comp comparison with equity discounted conservatively. List twelve pre-signing checks. Flag local pay-history and pay-transparency rules.

## 5. Offer comparison table

Build a table of base, expected bonus (with the payout history if known), signing bonus and clawback, equity value in dollars at a conservative price with vesting and cliff, benefits and notice period. Show four-year totals at target and at a pessimistic case, and list the three clauses to clarify in writing.

## 6. Free-tool privacy check
